The figures above are for Delta Comfort and Delta Main fares on domestic flights within the US, without Medallion status, a Delta SkyMiles American Express Card, or an active military exemption. They are charged each way, so a round trip carrying one bag in each direction is ninety dollars.

## The date on your ticket may matter more than the date today

Delta states that its published baggage fees apply to all ticketing on or after today, and keeps a separate page for tickets issued earlier.

That is worth reading twice. The fee you actually pay is attached to **when you bought the ticket**, not when you fly. If you booked several months ago, the schedule that governs your bag may not be the one on this page or Delta's.

It is the same structure Alaska uses, and it is the most common reason two people quoting Delta's bag fee will both be right and still disagree.

## The weight limit depends on your cabin, not your bag

Fifty pounds is the limit for Main, Comfort and Premium Select. Delta One and Delta First allow seventy pounds per piece.

The size limit is sixty-two inches total — length plus width plus height added together, not the longest side. People measure the longest side and get caught.

## Who does not pay

SkyMiles Medallion members and eligible Delta SkyMiles American Express cardholders check the first bag free. Eligible Basic Card Members get the second free as well on domestic Delta flights.

The exclusion that catches people: **the card benefit does not apply on partner-operated flights**. A ticket sold by Delta but flown by a partner falls outside it, and that is not obvious from the boarding pass.

## Paying with miles

At most domestic airports, SkyMiles members can pay standard checked bag fees with miles rather than cash during the twenty-four hour check-in window, on delta.com, the Fly Delta app, or an airport kiosk.

Excess and overweight bags cannot be paid with miles. Neither can bags on flights leaving from airports outside the qualifying list.

## One route-specific rule

Travel to or from Key West is limited to one checked bag, and Delta is explicit that this **supersedes all exceptions** — including Medallion status and card benefits.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the Delta bag fee each way or round trip?**

Each way. Delta's page states the figure is per direction, so a round trip with one checked bag in each direction is 90 dollars rather than 45.

**Who gets a free checked bag on Delta?**

SkyMiles Medallion members and eligible Delta SkyMiles American Express cardholders get the first bag free. Eligible Basic Card Members also get a second bag free on domestic Delta flights. The benefit does not apply on partner-operated flights.

**Why was I charged a different amount than Delta publishes?**

Delta applies the published schedule to tickets issued on or after today and maintains a separate page for tickets issued earlier. If you booked months ago, an older fee schedule may govern your ticket.
