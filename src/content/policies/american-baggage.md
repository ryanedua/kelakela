American updated its bag allowances and fees on 18 May 2026. The figures above are the current schedule for travel within and between the US, Puerto Rico, the US Virgin Islands, Canada, the Caribbean (except Cuba and Haiti), Mexico, Central America (except Panama) and Guyana.

Fees are per person, per direction.

## Five dollars for using the app

American charges **five dollars less per bag if you pay online** rather than at the airport. Forty-five against fifty for the first bag; fifty-five against sixty for the second.

It sounds trivial. On a round trip for two people with a bag each, it is twenty dollars, for the work of tapping a button before you leave the house.

Basic Economy pays five dollars more again, on both bags. So the spread between the best case (standard fare, paid online) and the worst (Basic Economy, paid at the counter) is ten dollars per bag per direction — forty dollars on a round trip with one bag each way for two people.

## Pay for a bag, then get upgraded, and the money is gone

This one is worth knowing before it happens.

American states plainly that if you pay for checked bags and **later buy or receive an upgrade that includes free checked bags**, the fees you already paid are **not refunded**.

So the sequence matters. Pay for the bag, then take the upgrade offer at the gate, and you have paid twice for the same bag — once in cash and once inside the upgrade you bought.

Bag fees paid at the airport are non-refundable generally. American does allow a refund request online if you believe you were charged incorrectly, which is a different thing from changing your mind.

## How many bags you can actually check

Ten bags on American-operated domestic, trans-Atlantic and trans-Pacific flights. Five if you are travelling to, through or from Mexico, the Caribbean, Central America, South America or Brazil — and those regions carry seasonal restrictions on top.

Cuba is its own case: two bags maximum, seventy pounds each. And since 26 August 2025, even Executive Platinum, Platinum Pro and oneworld Emerald members are capped at **two free bags** on Cuba routes. Elite status does not lift that ceiling.

## What this page does not tell you

American's weight and size limits sit behind a collapsed section on its own page, and are not recorded here because they were not stated in the source we checked. Check American's page directly for those before you pack.

That is a smaller gap than it sounds — 50 lb and 62 linear inches is the industry standard, and American is unlikely to differ — but "unlikely to differ" is not the same as "we checked", and this site does not print figures it did not read.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is American's bag fee cheaper if I pay online?**

Yes, by 5 dollars per bag. The first bag is 45 online against 50 at the airport, and the second is 55 against 60. On a round trip with two bags that is 20 dollars for using the app instead of the counter.

**Does Basic Economy cost more for bags on American?**

Yes. Basic Economy pays 5 dollars more for both the first and second checked bag, on top of any airport surcharge.

**Can I get a refund if I pay for a bag and then get upgraded?**

No. American states that if you pay for checked bags and later buy or receive an upgrade that includes free bags, the fees you already paid are not refunded. Bag fees paid at the airport are non-refundable in general.
