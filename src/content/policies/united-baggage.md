Most airline baggage pages open with a number. United's does not.

Ask United what a checked bag costs and it hands you a calculator, which wants your route and the date you bought your ticket before it will answer. That is not evasion for its own sake — it reflects a fee structure with too many axes to print — but it does mean that any site quoting a flat United bag fee is quoting a figure United itself does not publish.

So this page carries what United states outright, and stops there.

## Why the number moves

United raised bag fees twice in 2026, and both rises attach to **when the ticket was bought**, not when you fly:

- **3 April 2026** — domestic, short-haul US to Latin America, and US routes to Canada, the Caribbean and Mexico.
- **12 May 2026** — many US routes to Africa, Asia, Europe and South America.

A ticket bought on 2 April and flown in August pays the old rate. One bought on 4 April for the same seat pays the new one. This is the same ticketing-date structure Alaska and Delta use, and it is the reason quoted fees disagree so persistently across the internet.

## Basic Economy has no carry-on

This is the part that costs people real money at the gate.

United Basic Economy includes a **personal item only**. Not a personal item and a carry-on — just the personal item. If you turn up with a roller bag, it gets checked, and you pay:

- **50 dollars** if you check it at the lobby
- **75 dollars** if you get as far as the gate

For tickets bought before 3 April 2026, those were 40 and 65.

The gate price is the one to notice. Twenty-five dollars is the cost of not reading the fare rules until you are already in the boarding queue — and by then there is no cheaper option.

This is a genuine difference between United and its rivals. American and Southwest both include a carry-on on their cheapest fares; United does not.

## The weight limit takes the higher of two numbers

United sets the weight cap by cabin **and** by MileagePlus status, then uses whichever is more generous.

Economy and Premium Economy are 50 lb. Business, First and Polaris are 70 lb. But Premier Silver and above get 70 lb regardless of cabin — so a Premier Silver member in Economy carries the Business allowance.

Star Alliance Gold is the exception that catches people: 70 lb in Business, but only 50 lb in Economy.

Size is 62 inches total, or 30 x 20 x 12, including handles and wheels.

## What to do instead of trusting a number

Open United's calculator with your actual route and your actual purchase date. It is the only source that will give you your fee rather than a fee.

If you are shopping fares, price the Basic Economy ticket **plus 50 dollars** if you are travelling with anything larger than a backpack. The gap to a standard Economy fare is often smaller than that.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does United charge for a checked bag?**

United does not publish a single figure. Its baggage page routes you to a calculator that needs your route and the date you bought the ticket. Any site quoting one flat United bag fee is quoting something United itself does not print.

**Why does United's bag fee depend on when I bought the ticket?**

Because United raised fees twice in 2026 and applied each rise to tickets purchased on or after a set date. Fees went up on 3 April 2026 for domestic and nearby routes, and again on 12 May 2026 for many long-haul routes.

**Can I bring a carry-on with United Basic Economy?**

No. Basic Economy includes a personal item only. If you bring a carry-on you must check it: 50 dollars at the lobby or 75 at the gate for tickets bought on or after 3 April 2026, against 40 and 65 for tickets bought earlier.
