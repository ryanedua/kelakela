The figure above is JetBlue's best case: Blue, Blue Basic, Blue Extra or EvenMore, flying within the US and nearby, on an off-peak date, paid more than twenty-four hours before departure.

Change any one of those four things and the price changes. That is unusual — most carriers move the fee on one axis, maybe two. JetBlue moves it on four.

## The four things that change the price

**Fare brand.** Blue Plus includes the first bag. Mint includes two at seventy pounds. Mosaic 2-4 includes two. Everyone else pays.

**Route.** US and nearby is one schedule. Transatlantic is a different one, and the ranking inverts — see below.

**Date.** JetBlue publishes a peak calendar and it is not a handful of holidays. Most of June, July and August 2026 is peak. So are Thanksgiving and the winter holidays.

**When you pay.** More than twenty-four hours out: forty-five dollars. Inside twenty-four hours: forty-nine. The second bag goes from fifty-nine to sixty-nine. Prepaying saves up to ten dollars a bag, and it is the only one of the four you control on the day.

## Transatlantic inverts the ranking

On US routes, Blue Basic pays the same as Blue and Blue Extra — forty-five dollars, nobody is special.

Fly transatlantic and Blue Basic suddenly pays **seventy-five dollars** while Blue, Blue Plus, Blue Extra and EvenMore all include the first bag **free**.

So the same fare brand is unremarkable on one route and the worst option on another. If you book Blue Basic to London on the strength of what it costs to Florida, the first bag alone erases most of the saving.

## Oversize and overweight stack

Most airlines charge one penalty for a bag that breaks the rules. JetBlue charges both.

Oversize (63–80 inches) is a hundred and fifty dollars. Overweight (51–99 lb) is a hundred and fifty dollars. A bag that is **both** pays **three hundred**, on top of the standard bag fee. JetBlue states this explicitly.

Over eighty inches or over ninety-nine pounds is refused outright.

## The card benefit has a hole in it

JetBlue Plus and Premier cardholders get the first bag free — on **JetBlue-operated flights only**. Codeshares and partner-operated flights are excluded.

That is the same carve-out Delta and Alaska use, and it is invisible on your boarding pass. The flight number says JetBlue. The metal says otherwise, and the metal decides.

## Some destinations have their own rules

Ecuador, the Dominican Republic, Guyana, Haiti and Trinidad & Tobago each carry separate limits: lower weight ceilings, caps on how many bags you may check, third bags addable only at the airport. Haiti, Guyana and Trinidad drop to two bags maximum between 16 November and 15 January.

If you are flying to any of them, the general table above is not your table.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I get JetBlue's cheapest bag fee?**

Pay more than 24 hours before departure and travel off-peak. Prepaying saves up to 10 dollars per bag. Inside 24 hours the first bag rises from 45 to 49 dollars and the second from 59 to 69.

**What counts as a peak date on JetBlue?**

JetBlue publishes a calendar, and it is wide. Most of June, July and August 2026 are peak, along with Thanksgiving, the winter holidays, and stretches of spring. If you are flying in summer, assume peak pricing.

**Does Blue Basic include a checked bag?**

Not on US and nearby routes, where it pays the same 45 dollars as Blue and Blue Extra. On transatlantic routes Blue Basic is the odd one out: it pays 75 dollars while Blue, Blue Plus, Blue Extra and EvenMore all include the first bag free.
