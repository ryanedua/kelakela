# Delta eCredit: How to Use, Check Balance & Expiration in 2026

A Delta eCredit is the travel credit issued when you cancel a non-refundable ticket. It holds the unused value of your original ticket and can be applied toward a future Delta flight booking. Knowing exactly how to find it, apply it, and avoid losing it to expiration can save you hundreds of dollars. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** if you are having trouble locating or redeeming your eCredit.

## What Is a Delta eCredit?

A Delta eCredit is a digital credit stored in your SkyMiles wallet. Delta issues one when you cancel a non-refundable ticket voluntarily, when you rebook to a cheaper flight and a fare difference credit is generated, or in certain disruption situations as goodwill compensation.

It is not a cash voucher. You cannot withdraw it, transfer it to another person, or convert it to SkyMiles. It can only be applied toward the purchase of a future Delta flight for the original passenger.

## How to Find Your Delta eCredit Balance

**If you have a SkyMiles account**: Log in to delta.com, click your name or profile icon, and navigate to My Wallet. Under Credits and Certificates you will see all active eCredits along with their values and expiration dates. The Fly Delta app shows the same information under the wallet section.

**If you booked as a guest without a SkyMiles account**: Go to delta.com/redeem. Search using one of three methods: your 13-digit ticket number (found in your original booking confirmation email under Flight Receipt, starts with 006), the credit card used to purchase the original ticket, or your SkyMiles account number if you have since created one.

Once you locate the eCredit, enter the first and last name of the original ticket holder to validate it and add it to your session for use.

## How to Apply a Delta eCredit at Checkout

**Method 1: Apply at delta.com/redeem first**

Go to delta.com/redeem, find your eCredit, check the box next to it, and click Continue. The system will redirect you to the flight search with your eCredit pre-loaded. Search for and select your new flight, and the eCredit will automatically apply during checkout.

**Method 2: Apply during checkout**

Search for and select your new flight on delta.com as normal. During the payment step, look for the Use eCredits option in the Payment section. Click it to pull up your available credits, select the one you want to apply, and confirm. The credit is applied instantly and the remaining balance due is shown.

If the flight costs more than your eCredit value, you pay the difference with a credit card. Delta requires a valid credit card on file for all transactions even when the eCredit covers the full fare, for identity verification purposes.

If the flight costs less than your eCredit value, Delta issues the remaining balance as a new eCredit with the same expiration date as the original.

**Method 3: Apply by phone**

If the online process is not working or your eCredit is not appearing in your wallet, call **+1-(866)-332-0756**. Have your ticket number or SkyMiles account number ready. An agent can locate the credit, verify your identity, and apply it to a new booking manually.

## eCredit Expiration Rules

Delta eCredits expire one year from the original ticket purchase date. This is not the cancellation date. If you bought a ticket in March 2025 and cancelled it in December 2025, your eCredit still expires in March 2026.

When you partially use an eCredit and a residual balance is reissued, the residual credit carries the same expiration date as the original. The clock does not reset to give you a new year from the redemption date.

Check your expiration date in My Wallet before booking a new trip. If the credit is close to expiring, book something soon even if your travel plans are uncertain. You can always change the booking later (on a Classic or Extra fare) without losing the value.

If your eCredit has already expired, call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to ask about reinstatement options. Delta handles these on a case-by-case basis and does not guarantee extension, but it is worth asking.

## Can You Use an eCredit for Award Flights?

Delta eCredits cannot be applied to award tickets booked with SkyMiles. They apply only to cash-priced revenue flights. If you want to use SkyMiles for a flight, that is a separate transaction. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to confirm compatibility before starting a booking if you plan to use both forms of payment.

## eCredit vs Travel Credit: What Is the Difference?

Delta eCredits and Delta Travel Credits are two different things despite similar names.

An eCredit is issued from a cancelled non-refundable ticket. It is tied to the original passenger and has a one-year expiration from purchase date.

A Delta Travel Credit may be issued as compensation for disruptions, as part of a promotion, or through certain Delta credit card benefits. Travel credits may have different expiration terms and usage conditions. Always check the specific terms when you receive either type.

To find out exactly what type of credit you have and what its terms are, log in to My Wallet or call **+1-(844)-833-1535**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where do I find my Delta eCredit?**
Log in to your SkyMiles account and go to My Wallet. If you booked as a guest, visit delta.com/redeem and search by ticket number, credit card, or SkyMiles number.

**How long does a Delta eCredit last?**
One year from the original ticket purchase date. The expiration does not reset when you use or partially use the credit. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to confirm the expiration on your specific credit.

**Can I use a Delta eCredit for someone else's ticket?**
No. eCredits are non-transferable and must be used by the original ticketed passenger only.

**What happens if my new flight costs less than my eCredit?**
The remaining balance is reissued as a new eCredit with the same expiration date as the original.

**Can I transfer a Delta eCredit to SkyMiles?**
No. eCredits cannot be converted to SkyMiles and can only be applied to future Delta flight purchases.
