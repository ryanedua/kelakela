# Delta eCredit for Someone Else: What's Allowed in 2026

This is one of the most frequently asked questions about Delta eCredits, and the answer has a critical nuance. Delta eCredits are non-transferable — you cannot give your credit to another person or have them use it independently for their own booking. However, you can use your eCredit on a booking that includes a companion as long as you are also on the same reservation. Understanding this distinction prevents wasted credits and frustrated attempts to use them for someone else. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** if you need help setting up a multi-passenger booking using an eCredit.

## The Non-Transferable Rule

Delta's eCredit policy is explicit: eCredits are non-transferable and can only be used by the original ticketed passenger. The credit is tied to the name and SkyMiles account associated with the cancelled ticket.

This means:

You cannot give your eCredit to a spouse, parent, child, or friend.

You cannot sell your eCredit.

You cannot apply your eCredit to a booking made entirely in someone else's name.

The system enforces this at checkout. When applying an eCredit, Delta validates the passenger name against the credit. A booking in a different passenger's name will not accept the credit.

## What You Can Do: Booking Yourself and a Companion

Here is the important exception that many passengers miss. Delta's official guidance notes that eCredits can be used to book a flight for a companion as long as the credit holder is also on the same reservation.

In practice: if your eCredit is for John Smith, John can book a round trip for himself and his partner Jane using that eCredit. The eCredit pays for John's portion. Jane's ticket is paid for separately. Both are on the same booking confirmation.

This is not transferring the credit — John must be a passenger on the flight. But it does allow you to book for a travel companion using your credit without having to travel separately.

To set this up: go to delta.com/redeem, add your eCredit to the session, then search for flights with two passengers. Add both passengers and the eCredit will apply to the primary passenger's (your) fare. The companion's fare is paid separately.

If the online flow does not handle this correctly, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to complete a multi-passenger booking with eCredit by phone.

## Credits Issued for a Child's Cancelled Ticket

If you cancelled a ticket that was originally in your child's name — for example, a ticket purchased for a family trip — the eCredit is associated with that child's ticket and can be used for a new booking for the same child.

To apply a child's eCredit to a new booking, go to delta.com/redeem and search by the original ticket number (from the child's booking confirmation email). The credit appears associated with the child's name. Use it to book a new flight including the same child passenger.

If the child's ticket was on your SkyMiles account rather than a separate account, the credit should appear in your wallet but will be tagged to the child's name. The new booking must include the child as a passenger.

Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** if you encounter difficulty applying a credit that is in a child's name.

## What to Do If You Have an eCredit You Cannot Use

If you have an eCredit from a cancelled booking and you no longer have travel plans, your options are limited:

**Book a placeholder**: Use the credit to book a future Delta flight before it expires, even if your plans are not firm. Choose a Classic or Extra fare so you can cancel or change later. You only need to book before expiration — travel can occur after.

**Call about exceptions**: Call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** and explain your situation. Delta handles extension and exception requests case by case. While transfers are not standard policy, Delta sometimes grants goodwill extensions for documented extenuating circumstances such as medical issues.

**Accept the expiration**: If the credit expires unused and no exception is granted, the value is lost. This is why booking a placeholder is the most practical option for credits you cannot use soon.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use my Delta eCredit to pay for someone else's ticket?**
Not independently. The credit must be applied to a booking that includes you as a passenger. You can book for a companion on the same reservation.

**Can I transfer a Delta eCredit to a family member?**
No. eCredits are non-transferable to any other person.

**What if I cancelled a ticket for my child — can I use that eCredit for their new ticket?**
Yes, for a new booking for the same child. Apply the credit via delta.com/redeem using the original ticket number.

**Can I use someone else's Delta eCredit to book my own flight?**
No. Credits can only be applied to bookings that include the original passenger whose name is on the credit.

**Is there any way to use a Delta eCredit if I can no longer travel myself?**
Book a placeholder flight for future use before expiration. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** to ask about goodwill exceptions.
