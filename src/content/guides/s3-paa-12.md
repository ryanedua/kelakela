# Can Delta change my flight without my permission? — 2026 Answer

Yes, Delta can make schedule changes to your flight without your prior consent. However, if the change is significant (3+ hours domestic or 6+ hours international for tickets issued after October 28, 2024), you are entitled to a full cash refund or free rebooking if you choose not to accept it. Minor changes do not trigger refund rights. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 after any Delta-initiated change.
