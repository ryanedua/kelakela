# How Do I Use My Delta eCredit? Step-by-Step 2026





Using a Delta eCredit online takes about two minutes. The fastest path is through the Payment section at checkout on delta.com — click Use eCredits, select the credit, and confirm. For guest bookings or credits not linked to your SkyMiles account, go to delta.com/redeem first and search by your 13-digit ticket number (starting with 006, from your original booking confirmation email). Add the credit to your session and then proceed to flight search. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** if the credit is not appearing or cannot be applied.





## Key Rules to Know





eCredits are non-transferable — they can only be used by the original ticketed passenger. If the eCredit is larger than the flight cost, the balance is reissued as a new eCredit with the same original expiration date. Delta requires a credit card on file even when the eCredit covers the full fare, for identity verification.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I use my Delta eCredit?**
Use your Delta eCredit at checkout on delta.com by clicking Use eCredits in the Payment section, or pre-load it at delta.com/redeem using your 13-digit ticket number before searching for flights. You need to be logged in to your SkyMiles account or have your ticket number ready. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 if the credit is not appearing.
