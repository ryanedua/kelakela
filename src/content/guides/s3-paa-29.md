# Does Delta refund miles if flight is cancelled? — 2026 Answer

Yes. If Delta cancels your award flight, your SkyMiles are fully redeposited to your account at no charge within 24 to 72 hours. Cash taxes and fees are also refunded within 7 business days. If you voluntarily cancel a US/Canada-originating non-Basic award ticket before departure, miles are also redeposited with no fee. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 if the redeposit has not occurred within 72 hours.
