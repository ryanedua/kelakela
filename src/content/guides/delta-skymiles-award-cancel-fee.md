# Delta SkyMiles Award Cancellation Fee 2026: Complete Guide

Delta's SkyMiles award cancellation policy is one of the most flexible among major US frequent flyer programs. For award tickets originating in the US and Canada, miles are returned in full at no charge when you cancel before departure — no redeposit fee, no administrative charge, no penalty. The key rules are knowing when fees do apply, what happens if you no-show, and how to handle cancellations on international-origin awards. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to cancel an award ticket or confirm the rules for your specific booking.

## The Core Policy: Free Redeposit from US and Canada

Delta's SkyMiles Program Rules clearly state that for award tickets originating in the United States or Canada, members can cancel and have miles redeposited without a redeposit or change fee. This applies to all Medallion tiers and to general SkyMiles members without elite status, with one exception: Basic experience award fares.

When you cancel a qualifying award ticket before departure, your miles return to your SkyMiles account within 24 to 72 hours. Cash taxes and fees paid on the award booking are refunded to your original payment method within 7 business days.

This policy makes Delta SkyMiles awards relatively low-risk to book in advance. You can lock in scarce award space and cancel later if plans change, with the full miles returned and no financial penalty.

## When the Redeposit Fee Applies

**International-originating routes for non-Medallion members**: For award tickets on flights departing from outside the US and Canada — for example, a Paris to New York award — non-Medallion members may face a $150 per-person redeposit fee on cancellations. This fee is not consistently applied on all international-originating routes, but it is the stated policy for some markets.

Medallion members (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) have the international-origin redeposit fee waived regardless of where the award originates.

**Basic experience award fares**: Delta Basic experience award fares carry a mileage cancellation charge. The exact amount is deducted before any miles are redeposited to your account. The remaining miles after the charge are redeposited. This is analogous to the cash cancellation fee on Basic revenue tickets.

If you want full cancellation flexibility on your award ticket, avoid booking Basic experience award fares.

## What Happens to Cash Taxes and Fees

When you cancel an award ticket and miles are redeposited, the cash portion you paid — government taxes, airport fees, and any surcharges — is also refunded to your original payment method. This is separate from the mile redeposit and follows the standard refund timeline of 7 business days for credit cards.

If you paid taxes with a Delta eCredit rather than a credit card, the tax refund returns as a new eCredit rather than cash. This is an important nuance if you used eCredits to cover the cash portion of an award booking.

## No-Show on an Award Ticket: Miles Are Forfeited

If you do not cancel your award ticket before the scheduled departure time, Delta registers a no-show and the miles are forfeited. No redeposit occurs for award no-shows.

This is why it is essential to cancel before departure even if you realize you cannot travel at the last minute. A cancellation made one minute before departure still preserves your miles. A no-show does not.

If extenuating circumstances caused the no-show — a documented medical emergency, for example — call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and explain the situation. Delta handles no-show exceptions case by case with documentation.

## When Delta Cancels Your Award Flight

If Delta cancels your award flight, your miles are automatically redeposited at no charge and all cash taxes and fees are refunded. This is a mandatory outcome and applies regardless of Medallion status or which market the award originated in.

In most cases, Delta sends an automatic redeposit notification within a few hours of the cancellation. If you do not receive confirmation of the redeposit within 72 hours of the cancellation, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and ask an agent to manually process the redeposit.

## Partner Airline Award Cancellations

Award tickets booked on SkyTeam partners through Delta SkyMiles may have different cancellation rules and fees set by the individual carrier. Partner award cancellations generally cannot be processed online and require a phone call.

Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to cancel a partner airline award. Confirm the specific partner's redeposit terms before initiating the cancellation, as fees and timelines vary by carrier.
