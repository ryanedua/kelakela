# What is Delta basic economy cancellation policy? — 2026 Answer

Delta Main Basic (Basic Economy) tickets cannot be changed and are not refundable to cash after the 24-hour risk-free window. Cancel before departure for an eCredit minus the applicable cancellation fee (varies by route: $99 Caribbean, $199 Asia-Pacific, $330 South America on newer tickets). No-shows forfeit the entire ticket value. If Delta cancels the flight, full cash refund applies regardless of fare type. Call +1-(866)-332-0756.
