# Delta Trip in a Bag & Basic Economy: Change Rules Explained 2026

Delta's Trip in a Bag is a fare bundle designed for the lightest of light packers — passengers who can fit everything they need for a trip into a personal item that slides under the seat in front of them. It offers a lower price than standard Main Basic in exchange for giving up the full-size carry-on allowance. The change and cancellation rules are identical to all other Main Basic fare types, which means no changes after the 24-hour window and eCredit minus a fee if you cancel. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to confirm details about your specific Trip in a Bag booking.

## What Is Delta Trip in a Bag?

Trip in a Bag is a Main Basic bundled fare that includes only a personal item (underseat bag) rather than a full-size carry-on plus personal item. The fare is priced lower than standard Main Basic to reflect the reduced baggage allowance.

The onboard experience is identical to any other Main Basic ticket — same seat class, same complimentary snacks and beverages, same in-flight entertainment. What changes is exclusively the baggage allowance.

Personal item size requirements are the same as standard Delta policy: the bag must fit under the seat in front of you. Small backpacks, laptop bags, briefcases, and small totes typically qualify. A standard rolling carry-on does not.

## Change Rules: Same as All Main Basic

Trip in a Bag follows the same change rules as all Main Basic (Basic Economy) tickets:

**Within 24 hours of purchase**: Cancel for a full cash refund to your original payment method with no fee. This applies regardless of the bundle type.

**After 24 hours and before departure**: Changes are not permitted. The only option is to cancel for an eCredit minus the applicable cancellation fee.

**After departure or no-show**: Full forfeiture. No eCredit is issued.

There is no special flexibility associated with the Trip in a Bag bundle. The lower price reflects reduced baggage, not reduced restrictions. The change rules are as strict as any other Main Basic ticket.

If you realize after booking that you need overhead bin space, you have two options: cancel within 24 hours of purchase for a full refund and rebook in a standard Main Basic or Classic fare, or accept the personal-item-only constraint and pack accordingly.

## Cancellation Fee by Route

When cancelling a Trip in a Bag ticket after the 24-hour window, the applicable fee varies by route origin:

US and Canada originations: fee deducted from ticket value per fare rules (built into the specific fare class).
Caribbean routes (to/from US, Canada, Mexico, Central America): $99.
Asia-Pacific routes (tickets issued on or after September 26, 2024): $199.
South America routes (tickets issued on or after June 25, 2025): $330.

Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to confirm the exact fee for your specific Trip in a Bag ticket before cancelling so you know how much eCredit to expect.

## How to Cancel a Trip in a Bag Ticket

**Via delta.com**: Log in to My Trips, find your booking, and click Cancel. The system displays the eCredit amount after the fee deduction before you confirm. Review and confirm the cancellation.

**Via the Fly Delta app**: Open My Trips, select the booking, and tap Cancel. Same fee and eCredit display before confirmation.

**By phone**: Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and provide your booking confirmation number. An agent will confirm the fee and eCredit amount and process the cancellation.

## Trip in a Bag vs Standard Main Basic vs Main Classic

**Trip in a Bag**: Personal item only. Lowest price. No changes. Same no-change and cancellation rules as standard Main Basic.

**Standard Main Basic**: Personal item plus full-size carry-on on most routes. Slightly higher price than Trip in a Bag. Same no-change and cancellation rules.

**Main Classic**: Personal item plus full-size carry-on. Advance seat selection. SkyMiles earning. No change fee (fare difference only). Full eCredit if cancelled before departure.

The price difference between Trip in a Bag and Classic is usually $50 to $120 on domestic routes. For trips where you need overhead bin space, seat selection, or any flexibility, Classic provides substantially better value for a modest premium.
