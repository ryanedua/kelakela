# Delta Flight Booking Customer Service 2026: Phone, App & Online Guide

Most Delta flights can be booked in minutes on delta.com or the Fly Delta app. But there are situations where the online tools fall short — complex multi-city routes, SkyMiles award searches, applying multiple eCredits, group travel, or special service requests. For those situations, calling a Delta booking specialist is the most efficient path. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to reach Delta's booking line directly and get help with any flight-related request.

## How to Book a Delta Flight Online

**Via delta.com**: Go to delta.com and enter your origin, destination, travel dates, and number of passengers. Select Main Cabin or premium cabin, choose your fare tier (Basic, Classic, or Extra), and complete checkout with a credit card or eCredit. Booking online avoids the phone ticketing fee.

**Via the Fly Delta app**: The app mirrors delta.com functionality and adds extras like real-time flight status, mobile boarding passes, and same-day change requests. Download on iOS or Android. Log in with your SkyMiles account for stored payment information and eCredit access.

**SkyMiles award bookings online**: Log in to your SkyMiles account, toggle Shop with Miles before searching, and browse available award flights. The award calendar shows the lowest mileage options by date. Book entirely with miles or use Miles + Cash for a combination payment.

## When to Call Delta Customer Service for Booking

There are specific situations where a phone call produces better outcomes than online self-service.

**Complex multi-city itineraries**: Online tools handle point-to-point and standard round-trips well. Multi-city routes with three or more cities, open-jaw itineraries, or unusual connections are better handled by a phone agent who can build the routing manually.

**Partner airline SkyMiles awards**: Delta's online award search shows Delta-operated flights and some partner availability, but partner award space is not always visible through the standard search. Phone agents can search partner award inventory directly, particularly for SkyTeam partners like Air France, KLM, and Korean Air, which can reveal availability the website misses.

**Applying multiple eCredits**: If you have several eCredits from different cancelled bookings and want to combine them toward a single purchase, phone agents can apply multiple credits manually. The online checkout typically allows only one or two eCredits per transaction.

**Group travel**: Delta's standard booking flow handles up to 9 passengers. For 10 or more, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and ask to be connected to Delta Group Travel. Group bookings receive different rate structures, name flexibility after booking, and deposit options.

**Special service requests**: Unaccompanied minor services, wheelchair assistance, medical equipment carriage, service animal travel, and special meal requests that require coordination across multiple segments are handled more reliably by phone agents who can document each request against every segment of your itinerary.

**Waiver code applications**: If you are rebooking under an active travel waiver and the online tool is not reflecting the $0 fee, a phone agent can apply the waiver code manually and complete the rebooking.

## The Phone Ticketing Fee

Delta charges a ticketing service fee for new bookings made by phone or at the airport ticket counter rather than through self-service channels. The fee is approximately $35 per ticket and applies to revenue flights booked through agents.

This fee is waived in specific situations: when the booking cannot be completed online due to a technical issue, for certain SkyMiles award bookings, for passengers with documented disabilities who cannot use self-service tools, and in some cases for bookings involving complex itineraries not supported by the online system.

To avoid the ticketing fee, always attempt to book at delta.com or through the Fly Delta app first. If the online system cannot complete your booking, call **+1-(866)-332-0756** and explain the situation. The agent may waive the fee if the online channel genuinely cannot handle the request.

## Booking with eCredits by Phone

If you have eCredits that are not appearing in your online wallet or that you want applied to a new booking in combination with cash or miles, phone booking is often the cleanest path.

Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and have your ticket number (a 13-digit number starting with 006, found in your original booking confirmation email) or SkyMiles number ready. The agent will locate your credits, verify your identity, and apply them directly to the new booking during the call.

## What to Have Ready Before You Call

To make the call as efficient as possible, prepare the following before dialing.

Your travel details: origin, destination, preferred travel dates, number of passengers, and preferred cabin class or fare tier.

Your SkyMiles account number if you want miles to accrue or award redemption.

Your eCredit ticket numbers or SkyMiles account for credit application.

Your preferred payment method for any balance due.

For special service requests, relevant documentation such as a medical note for equipment carriage or a prescription for service animals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Delta's customer service number for booking a flight?**
Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to reach a Delta booking specialist, available 24/7.

**Can I book a Delta flight with SkyMiles by phone?**
Yes. Phone agents can access partner award availability not always visible online and handle complex SkyMiles booking requests.

**Is there a fee to book a Delta flight by phone?**
Yes, approximately $35 per ticket. Avoid this by booking at delta.com or through the Fly Delta app.

**Can I book a group flight through Delta customer service?**
Yes. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and ask for Delta Group Travel for parties of 10 or more.

**What can Delta customer service help with that the website cannot?**
Partner airline award searches, multiple eCredit applications, group rates, special service requests, and manual waiver code applications.
