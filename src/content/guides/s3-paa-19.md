# Does Delta give hotel vouchers for delays? — 2026 Answer

Yes for overnight delays caused by factors within Delta's control such as mechanical problems, crew issues, or operational decisions. Delta provides a complimentary hotel room near the departure airport, round-trip ground transportation, and dinner and breakfast meal vouchers. Weather delays do not require hotel accommodation. Request at the gate not given automatically. Call +1-(866)-332-0756.
