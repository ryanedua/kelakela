# A Complete Guide to Contacting Delta Air Lines

Delta Air Lines can be contacted by phone at +1-(866)-332-0756 (24 hours, 7 days), through the Fly Delta app self-service and messaging feature, via My Trips on delta.com, through airport gate agents and service desks, and through written forms at delta.com/refunds and delta.com/customerrelations. The right method depends on what you need: phone for urgent and complex issues, app self-service for standard changes and cancellations, messaging for non-urgent written inquiries, and the airport desk when you are traveling today.

Travelers can contact Delta Air Lines through five main channels: by phone, through the Fly Delta app and its messaging feature, through My Trips on delta.com, at the airport, and through written forms for complaints and refund submissions. Each channel reaches Delta through a different mechanism and serves a different category of need. Choosing the wrong one adds wait time and sometimes creates the need for a second contact.

The best method depends on what you need help with. Phone is the right choice for complex situations, cash refund requests after Delta-initiated cancellations, supervisor escalation, and same-day emergencies where immediate resolution matters. The Fly Delta app handles most standard changes, cancellations, standby additions, and status checks faster than any other channel — with no hold time at any hour. Messaging serves non-urgent written inquiries and situations where documentation matters. The airport is the fastest channel when you are physically present and time is short.

One important distinction: the phone numbers on this page are Delta Air Lines' official customer service contacts. Independent travel-assistance services sometimes advertise numbers that appear alongside airline information in search results. These are not Delta. The official Delta customer service number is **+1-(866)-332-0756)**, and any Delta contact information should be verified against delta.com before use.

## How to Contact Delta Air Lines by Phone

The main Delta customer service number is **+1-(866)-332-0756)**. This single line handles the full range of passenger requests: new reservations, existing booking changes and cancellations, disruption rebooking, cash refund requests, eCredit and travel credit questions, SkyMiles award ticket changes, baggage follow-up, and supervisor escalation. There is no separate published number for individual departments — all contacts go through this line and are routed internally.

A secondary line — **+1-(855)-321-3528)** — routes through different call capacity and sometimes has a shorter queue than the primary line during peak periods. During urgent situations, call both simultaneously from two devices and speak with whichever agent answers first.

**How to navigate the IVR**: Press 0 immediately when the automated menu begins — before it finishes its opening sentence. This bypasses the IVR on most Delta phone configurations and puts you directly in the live agent queue. If the system does not respond to 0, say "agent" or "representative" clearly. Repeat at each subsequent prompt until connected.

**When calling internationally**: Dial +1-(866)-332-0756 using your country's international exit code. On a mobile phone, entering +1-(866)-332-0756 directly works from any country — the + automatically replaces the exit code. US toll-free numbers are billed at international rates when dialed from abroad; check your carrier plan or use delta.com to find a local number for your country.

### When Calling Delta Is the Best Option

Calling is the right first choice when the situation requires real-time human judgment, authority, or immediate action that cannot wait for a messaging response. Specifically:

A complicated reservation problem involving multiple passengers, complex routing, or booking conditions that the online tools surface incorrectly. Urgent same-day changes where alternatives are filling by the minute — every moment in the messaging queue is a moment another passenger is booking the seat you want. A disrupted itinerary where the automatic rebooking is inadequate and you need an agent to search, compare, and confirm alternatives in real time. A cash refund request after a Delta-initiated cancellation, where the first-sentence framing — "I want a full cash refund, not a travel credit" — must happen before the agent processes anything else. Any situation that requires supervisor authority: a waiver code that is not applying, a refund dispute, a policy exception.

Calling is not always the fastest option for straightforward requests. A voluntary flight change on a Classic ticket takes under 2 minutes through My Trips with no queue. Use the phone when human judgment or authority is genuinely needed — not as a default for every issue.

### What to Prepare Before Calling Delta

The fastest calls start with all relevant information visible before dialing. Nothing slows a Delta call more than searching through email for a confirmation number while an agent waits.

Before calling **+1-(866)-332-0756)**:

Your **6-character booking confirmation code** — the alphanumeric record locator (e.g., ABC123) in the subject line of your booking confirmation email. State this in the first sentence when an agent answers.

The **13-digit ticket number** beginning with 006, found under Flight Receipt in the confirmation email. Required for eCredit lookups and refund tracking.

**Passenger names exactly as they appear on the booking** — particularly important for multi-passenger reservations.

**The specific outcome you want** — not the backstory, the result. "Rebook on DL 456 departing at 6 PM today" or "Full cash refund to the original payment method" or "Cancel for a travel credit — confirm the amount before processing."

**Flight details**: flight number, departure city, destination, scheduled date and time. If rebooking, the specific alternative flight details too.

**Your SkyMiles number** for Medallion routing priority and miles crediting on any new booking.

**A credit card** for fare differences on changes, or for any booking that requires a card on file.

**Any previous case reference number** from a prior call on the same issue — prevents starting from zero on follow-up calls.

## How to Speak With a Live Delta Representative

Delta's customer service line connects to live agents 24 hours a day. The path to a live person is the same regardless of call reason: dial **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and press 0 immediately when the IVR begins.

Once connected, choose the correct framing for your situation. The agent's first task when they answer is to understand what you need. Give it to them immediately:

"My confirmation is ABC123. I need to rebook on DL 456 at 6 PM today — Delta cancelled my original flight."

"My confirmation is ABC123. I want to cancel this booking for a travel credit. Can you confirm the credit amount before processing?"

"My confirmation is ABC123. Delta made a qualifying significant schedule change — departure moved 4 hours. I decline the new schedule and want a cash refund."

These opening statements give the agent everything they need to act. Compare them to "I need help with my flight" — which puts the agent in diagnostic mode for the first 2 to 3 minutes before any resolution work starts.

### How to Explain Your Problem to the Agent

Follow this structure for any Delta call:

**State the reservation first**: "My confirmation is ABC123." This gets the agent into your booking record within seconds.

**Explain what happened**: "Delta cancelled my July 25 flight from Atlanta to Los Angeles." Or "My departure time was moved from 2 PM to 7 PM — a 5-hour shift." Or "My eCredit is not applying at checkout." One sentence, factual, no narrative buildup.

**Explain what has already been attempted**: "I tried to rebook through My Trips but the alternative shown doesn't work for my schedule." Or "I submitted a refund request at delta.com/refunds on July 20 but have not received confirmation." This prevents the agent from directing you back to a channel you have already tried.

**State the resolution you are requesting**: "I want to rebook on DL 456 at 6 PM today." Or "I want a full cash refund to my original Visa card — not a travel credit." Be specific. Agents process specific requests faster than open-ended ones, and a specific stated preference prevents the wrong outcome from being processed by default.

### What to Do If the First Representative Cannot Resolve the Issue

If the first agent provides incorrect information, offers only a travel credit when you are entitled to cash, or cannot process the specific resolution you are requesting:

**Ask for a supervisor calmly and specifically**: "I understand this may be outside what you can authorize. Could I speak with a supervisor who has more authority over [specific type of request]?" This framing works better than frustrated demands — it acknowledges the agent's limitations without escalating the tone.

**Get a case or reference number before ending the call**: "Can I get a case or reference number for this conversation?" This is the single most important piece of information for any follow-up. With a case number, the next agent or supervisor picks up from where the previous one left off. Without it, you start from zero.

**Document the conversation immediately after**: Write down the agent's name, employee ID, the time of the call, what was discussed, what was offered, and what was refused. This documentation is necessary for formal escalation at delta.com/customerrelations or through the DOT.

**Follow up through the correct channel**: For refund disputes, submit at delta.com/refunds with the case reference number. For formal complaints, use delta.com/customerrelations. For refunds not processed within 7 business days, file at transportation.gov/airconsumer.

## Other Ways to Contact Delta Air Lines

### Delta Messaging

The Fly Delta app messaging feature — under Help in the app navigation — connects to a live Delta agent with full booking system access. The key difference from phone is response time: 15 to 30 minutes for the first reply, with back-and-forth extending total resolution time to 30 to 60 minutes on complex issues.

Messaging earns its place for non-time-critical situations. Policy questions about your fare type — what your cancellation produces, whether a waiver covers your specific booking, what the fee would be for a particular change — resolve through messaging without the frustration of a queue. The agent looks up your booking and responds in writing, which also creates a documented answer you can reference later.

Complaint submissions belong in messaging more than on the phone. A written exchange creates a transcript that strengthens any escalation to delta.com/customerrelations or the DOT. Verbal conversations have to be reconstructed from memory; messaging exchanges are already documented.

### Fly Delta Mobile App

The Fly Delta app is the fastest channel for the majority of standard passenger requests. It operates entirely independently of the phone system, has no hold time at any hour, and processes most self-service actions in under 2 minutes.

From the app, passengers can view all reservations under My Trips, check real-time flight status with inbound aircraft tracking, change eligible flights, cancel bookings and receive travel credits, add same-day standby for earlier departures, download and manage boarding passes, apply travel credits at checkout, and track delayed baggage. Push notifications deliver gate changes, delay announcements, and cancellation alerts the moment they enter Delta's system — often before the airport departure board updates.

For new users: download the Fly Delta app and sign in with your SkyMiles number to link all bookings automatically. Guest bookings can be accessed through a trip lookup using the confirmation code.

### My Trips and Online Self-Service

My Trips on delta.com is the desktop equivalent of the app's self-service functions. Log in with your SkyMiles account or use the guest lookup at delta.com/trips with your last name and confirmation code.

Standard tasks that resolve through My Trips without any call or messaging: reviewing the complete booking itinerary, changing flights on eligible Classic and Extra fare tickets, cancelling bookings and checking the resulting travel credit amount, applying travel credits at checkout during a new booking, selecting or changing seat assignments on eligible bookings, checking flight status by flight number, and downloading boarding passes within 24 hours of departure.

My Trips does not process: cash refund requests that require DOT-rule override (these need phone), changes to Basic fare tickets after 24 hours (not possible on any channel), or complex multi-city itinerary modifications that exceed the online tool's routing logic.

### Airport Customer Service

Airport staff are the most powerful real-time resource available to travelers who are physically at the airport. The gate agent at a specific departure has direct authority over that flight's standby list, seat assignments, and boarding decisions in a way that no phone agent can replicate from a call center.

Airport assistance is the right first channel when you are traveling today. For same-day standby, missed connections, rebooking after a disruption when alternatives are boarding right now, and hotel and meal voucher requests for overnight controllable delays — the airport is faster than the phone during active disruptions because gate agents are processing passengers physically while the phone queue is absorbing volume from hundreds of simultaneous callers.

Go to the departure gate of the flight you want to board — not your original gate, not the main check-in counter. The gate agent managing an active departure has the most direct authority over that specific flight.

The Delta customer service counter in the terminal handles more extended conversations: post-disruption rebooking when no immediate departure is involved, baggage filing, and situations requiring documentation.

### Written Forms and Feedback

Delta's written contact channels create timestamped records and trigger formal response timelines.

**delta.com/refunds**: For cash refund requests after qualifying events (Delta-initiated cancellations, significant schedule changes). Include your confirmation code, the qualifying event, and an explicit statement: "I request a full cash refund under the 2024 DOT final rule. I do not consent to a travel credit."

**delta.com/customerrelations**: For formal complaints, reimbursement requests after disruptions, unresolved cases from previous contacts, and any situation where you need a written record of Delta's response with a case number.

**transportation.gov/airconsumer**: For cash refunds not processed within 7 business days of the qualifying event. DOT complaints trigger mandatory airline review and have a high resolution rate for legitimate refund claims.

## How to Contact Delta About a New Booking

For standard new bookings on common routes, delta.com and the Fly Delta app provide real-time fare comparison, flexible date search, and immediate confirmation — faster and more transparent than calling.

Calling **+1-(866)-332-0756)** adds value for new bookings when: the itinerary is complex (multi-city, multiple passengers with different routing needs, accessibility requirements), you want a specific fare class confirmed before committing, the booking involves SkyMiles award redemption, or you have questions about specific fare conditions that delta.com does not answer clearly.

Before calling about a new booking, have ready: your intended travel dates and destination, at least two or three alternative departure options in case your first choice is unavailable, the number of passengers and any accessibility requirements, your cabin preference (Main Cabin, Comfort+, First Class, Delta One), and your SkyMiles number for credit and potential Medallion priority routing.

## How to Contact Delta About an Existing Reservation

For most existing reservation management, My Trips on delta.com or the Fly Delta app is the correct starting point — not the phone. Check My Trips first and only call if the specific action you need is not available there.

Situations that route through My Trips without a call: voluntary flight changes on Classic and Extra fares, cancellations on all eligible fares, travel credit application at checkout, same-day standby additions, boarding pass retrieval, and itinerary viewing.

Situations that require calling **+1-(866)-332-0756)**: cash refund requests after qualifying events, Basic fare ticket situations after 24 hours, multi-city complex modifications, SkyMiles award ticket destination changes, waiver code application when the system charges incorrectly, and supervisor escalation for disputes.

If the booking cannot be found in My Trips: confirm the booking is linked to your SkyMiles account, or use the guest lookup at delta.com/trips with your last name and confirmation code. If the booking was made through a third-party platform, it may not appear in delta.com My Trips — contact the original booking platform for management.

### What If a Travel Agency Issued the Ticket?

When a ticket is issued by a corporate travel agency, an employer's travel program, or a third-party platform, the modification rights may sit partially or entirely with the issuing agency rather than Delta.

Before calling Delta about a travel agency-issued ticket, verify: who issued the ticket (check the confirmation email sender and the ticket number prefix — Delta tickets start with 006), whether the booking appears in My Trips on delta.com, and whether the agency's own booking management portal can process the change you need.

Delta can view travel agency bookings and can rebook you after a Delta-initiated cancellation regardless of who issued the ticket. For voluntary changes and cancellations on agency-issued tickets, the agency may need to initiate the modification in their own booking system. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and ask: "Can you confirm whether you have modification authority over this booking, or does the change need to go through the issuing agency?"

## How to Contact Delta About a Flight Change

For voluntary flight changes on Main Classic and Extra fare tickets, the fastest channel is My Trips on delta.com or the Fly Delta app — no hold time, no IVR, under 2 minutes for most standard date and time changes. The only cost is a fare difference if the new flight is priced higher than the original. No change fee applies.

Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for flight changes when: the ticket is a Main Basic fare (cannot be changed after 24 hours on any channel, only cancelled), the routing is complex, the fare difference shown online appears incorrect, a travel waiver should be covering the change but the system is charging anyway, or the itinerary involves an award ticket destination change.

For same-day changes on the day of travel: the Fly Delta app handles Same-Day Standby (free) and Same-Day Confirmed ($75, waived for Gold/Platinum/Diamond Medallion) requests beginning at midnight on the day of travel. These options are not available on Main Basic fare tickets.

For Delta-initiated significant schedule changes — departure or arrival shifting by 3 or more hours on domestic routes, 6 or more hours internationally, or involving airport changes, added connections, carrier changes, or cabin downgrades — the passenger is entitled to free rebooking or a cash refund. Call +1-(866)-332-0756) and decline the new schedule before accepting anything.

## How to Contact Delta About a Cancellation

For voluntary cancellations on Main Classic tickets, My Trips on delta.com or the Fly Delta app processes the cancellation in under 2 minutes and issues the travel credit immediately. The credit amount is shown before confirmation — review it before accepting.

For voluntary cancellations on Main Basic (Basic Economy) tickets, My Trips also handles these — but the credit issued is the ticket value minus the applicable cancellation fee. Verify the specific fee and resulting credit before confirming.

For refundable Main Extra tickets, cancellation at any time before departure produces a full cash refund to the original payment method. This can be processed through My Trips.

When Delta cancels a flight, the situation is different. Fare restrictions — including non-refundable designations and Basic Economy limitations — are overridden when Delta initiates the cancellation. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and choose between free rebooking on a specific alternative flight or a full cash refund under DOT rules. Request cash explicitly before the agent processes anything else.

## How to Contact Delta About a Refund

Refund contacts split by situation:

**Cash refund after a Delta-initiated cancellation**: Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** immediately. Say before anything else: "Delta cancelled my flight. I want a full cash refund to my original payment method under the 2024 DOT final rule. I do not consent to a travel credit." Submit simultaneously at delta.com/refunds with the same language in the notes field.

**Cash refund after a qualifying significant schedule change**: Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** before accepting the new itinerary. "Delta moved my departure by [X hours], exceeding the qualifying threshold. I decline the new itinerary and want a full cash refund."

**Travel credit balance and expiration check**: Log in to delta.com under My Profile → Certificates, eCredits and Vouchers, or go to delta.com/redeem with your 13-digit ticket number. Or call **+1-(855)-321-3528)**.

**Refund status follow-up**: Call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** with your confirmation code and the date the request was submitted. Check with your card issuer first — they can confirm pending credits before they post.

### Information Needed for a Refund Follow-Up

When calling about an existing refund request, have ready: the 13-digit ticket number, the refund request reference or case number from the original submission, the date the request was submitted, the original payment method (card type and last four digits), the passenger name on the ticket, and the cancelled itinerary details (flight number, date, route).

### What to Do When a Refund Takes Longer Than Expected

Credit card refunds must process within 7 business days under DOT rules. Other payment methods within 20 calendar days. If the deadline has passed:

Confirm the request was actually submitted — check your inbox for a confirmation email from Delta or WorldTrips with a reference number. If no confirmation exists, the request may not have gone through.

Contact your card issuer first — ask whether a pending credit from Delta Airlines is in their processing queue. Issuers sometimes hold airline credits 3 to 5 days before posting to your statement.

If the card issuer confirms nothing incoming after 7 business days: call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and ask for the refund transaction reference number. With this you can confirm whether Delta has processed on their end.

Avoid submitting a duplicate refund request for the same booking — it creates confusion in Delta's system and can delay the original. Follow up using the original reference number.

If Delta cannot confirm processing: file at transportation.gov/airconsumer. DOT complaints trigger mandatory airline review with a high resolution rate.

## How to Contact Delta About Baggage

The first and most important baggage contact is not a phone call — it is a report filed in person at the Delta Baggage Service Office at the arrival airport before you leave the baggage claim area.

Go to the Baggage Service Office — typically located near the carousel exits in the baggage claim hall — and file a Property Irregularity Report before exiting the terminal. You receive a 13-character file reference number (beginning with DL) that is required for all subsequent tracking and follow-up. Without this number, every subsequent contact starts from a slower search process.

For online tracking after filing: go to delta.com and use the baggage tracking tool under Travel Information. Enter your file reference number and last name. Set up SMS or email update notifications through the portal.

For phone follow-up: call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and say "baggage" when prompted. Have your file reference number, confirmation code, and a description of the bag ready.

### What to Do Before Leaving the Airport

Do not leave the arrival airport without: the 13-character baggage file reference number from the report, the contact information of the Baggage Service Office agent who filed the report, photographs of any visible damage taken before touching the bag's contents, all baggage claim tag stickers from your boarding pass, and receipts for any essential items you immediately purchased due to the delay.

Damage reports must be filed within 24 hours for domestic flights and 7 days for international flights. After these windows, damage claims are generally not accepted. If your bag is damaged, photograph it first, then report immediately before leaving the terminal.

## Contacting Delta During Delays and Cancellations

The first two minutes after a Delta delay or cancellation announcement determine how many alternatives you have. Start all channels simultaneously and use whichever resolves first.

Open the Fly Delta app immediately and check My Trips for automatic rebooking. If the rebooking is acceptable — same day, reasonable routing — accept it in the app without calling. This is the fastest possible resolution.

If the automatic rebooking is not acceptable or does not appear: call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and press 0 immediately. You may be on hold — that is fine. Start the queue while simultaneously checking the app and, if at the airport, moving toward the gate of the alternative flight you want.

For overnight controllable delays: the hotel and meal voucher request goes to the gate agent or Delta service counter in person — not the phone. Agents issue physical vouchers on the spot. Calling to authorize hotel accommodation still requires you to go to the airport to receive the documentation, so skip the call step and go directly.

### Which Contact Option Is Best for Same-Day Travel?

The right channel depends on where you are and how much time you have.

**At the airport, flight still boarding or departing in under 60 minutes**: Gate agent at the specific departure gate. No queue, direct system access, can physically place you on a boarding list. This is faster than any phone call for immediate same-day situations.

**At the airport, flight cancelled, multiple future options to evaluate**: Phone **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and gate agent simultaneously. Whichever resolves first.

**Not at the airport, issue developing while in transit**: Fly Delta app for automatic rebooking check and standby addition, phone for everything that requires a human decision.

**App showing options but none are adequate**: Phone. Agents can see and book alternatives that have not yet surfaced in the app's search results during fast-moving disruption events.

**Overnight disruption, need accommodation**: Airport service counter directly. Hotel, ground transportation, and meal vouchers are issued there.

## How to Contact Delta From Outside the United States

Calling the US number from abroad: dial +1-(866)-332-0756 using your country's international exit code, or enter +1-(866)-332-0756 directly on a mobile phone — the + replaces the exit code automatically. US toll-free numbers are billed at international rates when dialed from outside the US. This is not a free call from other countries.

For local numbers in major markets: go to delta.com, navigate to Contact Us, and select your country from the dropdown. Delta maintains local numbers for the United Kingdom, Canada, Mexico, Japan, South Korea, Australia, Germany, France, Brazil, China, and other significant markets. Local numbers avoid international charges and may offer local language service.

For time zone considerations: Delta's lowest wait times on the US line are Tuesday through Thursday, 5 to 7 AM Eastern — which corresponds to 11 AM to 1 PM Central European Time, 6 PM to 8 PM Japan Standard Time, and 7 PM to 9 PM Australia Eastern Standard Time.

The Fly Delta app is available in every country with internet access and handles most standard requests with no call and no international charges — the most practical channel for international travelers managing non-urgent issues.

## Best Time to Call Delta Air Lines

Delta call volume follows a weekly pattern with predictable peaks and troughs. Understanding this pattern is one of the most practical tools available for reducing wait time.

Consistently short waits: Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday mornings between 5 AM and 7 AM Eastern. Typical wait under 5 minutes. These windows capture the end of the overnight quiet period before the morning business travel surge, with full daytime staffing ramping up.

Good secondary windows: Tuesday through Thursday evenings from 8 PM to 11 PM Eastern, and Saturday mornings from 9 AM to 11 AM Eastern.

Consistently long waits: Monday mornings from 7 AM to noon (post-weekend surge), Friday afternoons from 1 PM to 6 PM (pre-weekend changes), and any time within 2 hours of a major weather announcement or mass cancellation — when dozens of markets generate simultaneous call surges.

Holiday periods: Thanksgiving week (Wednesday before and Sunday after are the two busiest travel days of the year), Christmas week (December 22 to 26), New Year's period (December 30 to January 2), and the day before and after July 4 all see elevated volume. Call before 7 AM Eastern on these days for the shortest holiday waits.

### Ways to Reduce Long Phone Waits

Accept the callback option when offered — it holds your queue position without requiring you to stay on hold. The callback arrives within the announced window; answer immediately when it does.

Use My Trips on delta.com or the Fly Delta app for any issue those tools can handle. Most voluntary changes and cancellations complete in under 2 minutes with no wait, regardless of how congested the phone system is.

## Which Delta Contact Method Should You Choose?

### Use Online Self-Service When:

Reviewing reservation information, seat assignments, or itinerary details. Making a voluntary flight change on an eligible Classic or Extra fare. Cancelling a booking and checking the resulting travel credit amount. Applying a travel credit at checkout on a new booking. Checking real-time flight status. Adding same-day standby.

Traveling today and an issue develops at the airport. Facing check-in or boarding difficulties. Dealing with a missed connection due to a Delta delay. The flight is cancelled and an alternative is boarding soon. Reporting baggage that did not arrive. Requesting hotel and meal vouchers for an overnight controllable delay.

## Final Thoughts

There is no single contact method that is best for every Delta passenger in every situation. The right channel is the one that matches what you need with what that channel does well.

Phone is the tool for urgency, complexity, and authority. It connects you in real time to someone who can make a decision — but it comes with a queue that varies from under 5 minutes to over an hour depending on when you call and what is happening at Delta's hubs.

Messaging creates a written record and requires no hold time — but the 15 to 30 minute response window makes it the wrong tool for same-day emergencies where every minute matters.

My Trips and the Fly Delta app resolve most standard requests faster than any human channel — a 90-second self-service change versus a 20-minute phone call accomplishes the same outcome for most voluntary modifications.

The airport desk wins every time you are physically there during an active disruption. No phone queue. No messaging lag. A live person with direct flight-specific authority standing in front of you.

Use the right tool for the situation. When in doubt about which one that is, start with the Fly Delta app — it either resolves the issue directly or tells you clearly that you need to call.
