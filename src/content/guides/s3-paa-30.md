# Can I change my Delta award ticket for free? — 2026 Answer

Yes for US and Canada-originating non-Basic award tickets. Delta allows changes to origin, destination, routing, date, and time without a change or redeposit fee. Only the mileage difference applies if the new flight costs more miles. Medallion members have fees waived on all routes including international. Basic experience award fares have change restrictions. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 for destination changes, which cannot be done online.
