# What is a Delta significant schedule change? — 2026 Answer

For tickets issued on or after October 28, 2024: a departure or arrival time shift of 3 or more hours for domestic flights or 6 or more hours for international flights. Also qualifying: a change in departure or arrival airport, the addition of connections, a change in operating carrier, or a cabin downgrade. A qualifying change entitles you to a full cash refund or free rebooking. Call +1-(866)-332-0756.
