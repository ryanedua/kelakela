# Frontier Customer Service Not Responding? What to Try Next

If Frontier customer service is not responding, work through these escalation steps: try +1-(866)-228-6201 as an alternative line, use the Frontier app for self-service, contact @Frontier on X for public escalation, submit at flyfrontier.com/refunds in writing, or file a DOT complaint at transportation.gov/airconsumer for cash refund disputes.

## Step 1: Try the Secondary Line

If **+1-(866)-227-2948)** is overloaded, try **+1-(866)-228-6201)**. Frontier operates multiple customer service routing lines. During peak periods, one line may have shorter wait times than the other. Call both simultaneously from two devices if the situation is urgent, and speak to whichever agent answers first.

## Step 2: Fly Frontier App and flyfrontier.com Self-Service

Flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets: flyfrontier.com My Trips then Change Flight. eCredit application: flyfrontier.com/miles then book. Cancellation for eCredit: My Trips then Cancel. Standby addition: Frontier app then My Trips then Same-Day Change then Standby. Refund submission: flyfrontier.com/refunds then complete form.

If the phone line is not responding and your issue falls into one of these categories, the app resolves it with no wait.

## Step 3: Escalate on X

Frontier monitors @Frontier on X and responds to public mentions and direct messages faster than the web feedback form during high-volume periods.

What to post: @Frontier My flight [confirmation code] was cancelled on [date]. I have been unable to reach customer service. I am requesting a full cash refund per DOT rules. Can you assist?

Include contact details in a follow-up direct message. Frontier social media team has escalation authority and can initiate refund processing and arrange priority callbacks.

## Step 4: Submit a Written Refund Request

Submit at flyfrontier.com/refunds. Include your confirmation code, flight date and route, event type (Frontier-initiated cancellation), and explicit request: I am requesting a full cash refund to my original payment method under the 2024 DOT final rule. I do not consent to receiving an eCredit.

A written submission creates a timestamped record that supports escalation if Frontier does not process the refund within 7 business days for credit card payments.

## Step 5: File a DOT Complaint

Go to transportation.gov/airconsumer. File a complaint if Frontier has cancelled your flight, you have requested a cash refund, and Frontier has not processed it within 7 business days for a credit card.

Include your flight details, the qualifying event, your refund request with dates, and Frontier response or non-response. DOT complaints trigger a mandatory Frontier response. The airline compliance rate is high. This is the most powerful escalation available to passengers.

## Frequently asked questions

**What do I do if Frontier customer service is not answering?**
Try +1-(866)-228-6201 as an alternative line. Use the Frontier app for self-service. Contact @Frontier on X. Submit a written refund request at flyfrontier.com/refunds.

**How do I escalate a Frontier issue that an agent could not resolve?**
Ask for a supervisor during the call. If unresolved, call +1-(866)-228-6201. For cash refund disputes, submit at flyfrontier.com/refunds and file a DOT complaint at transportation.gov/airconsumer if not resolved within 7 business days.

**Does contacting Frontier on X actually work?**
Yes. @Frontier on X responds to direct messages and public mentions faster than the web contact form. Particularly effective for unresolved cancellation refund requests.

**How do I file a DOT complaint against Frontier?**
Go to transportation.gov/airconsumer. Complete the complaint form with your flight details, the qualifying event, your refund request, and Frontier's response. Effective for cash refund disputes after qualifying Frontier cancellations.

**What if Frontier gives me a credit when I asked for a cash refund?**
Call +1-(866)-228-6201 and repeat the cash request. Submit at flyfrontier.com/refunds in writing. File a DOT complaint at transportation.gov/airconsumer if unresolved after 7 business days.
