# Can I Get a Refund on a Delta Basic Economy Ticket in 2026?

Whether you can get a refund on a Delta Basic Economy ticket depends entirely on timing and circumstances. The short answer: sometimes yes, sometimes partial eCredit, sometimes nothing. Knowing which situation you are in determines what to do. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to understand your specific options.

## Refund Scenarios for Delta Basic Economy

**Scenario 1 â€” Within 24 hours of purchase**: Cancel for a full cash refund to your original payment method regardless of fare type. No fee. The 24-hour window starts the moment your purchase is confirmed and applies to all tickets purchased directly from Delta.

**Scenario 2 â€” After 24 hours, voluntary cancellation**: Cancel before departure for an eCredit minus the applicable cancellation fee. The fee varies by route: $99 for Caribbean, $199 for Asia-Pacific, $330 for South America on newer tickets. For US domestic tickets, the fee is built into the fare rules.

**Scenario 3 â€” Delta cancels your flight**: Full cash refund to your original payment method regardless of fare type, under the 2024 DOT rule. No fee, no eCredit conversion. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** and request cash specifically.

## How to Cancel a Basic Economy Ticket

To cancel a Basic Economy ticket before departure: log in to My Trips on delta.com, select your booking, and click Cancel. The system shows the eCredit amount after the fee deduction before you confirm.

If the online system is not working, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to cancel by phone. An agent will confirm the fee and eCredit amount before processing.

If you miss the flight without cancelling, the entire ticket value is forfeited as a no-show. Always cancel before departure, even one minute before, to preserve the residual eCredit.

If your ticket was purchased through a third-party platform (Expedia, Traveloka, travel agency), contact that platform to cancel. Delta cannot process refunds for third-party-issued tickets.




## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I get a refund on a Delta Basic Economy ticket?**
Within 24 hours of purchase: full cash refund regardless of fare type. After 24 hours: eCredit minus the applicable cancellation fee (varies by route). If Delta cancels your flight for any reason: full cash refund under DOT rules regardless of fare type. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 for assistance.

**What is the Delta Basic Economy cancellation fee?**
The fee varies by route. For Caribbean routes: $99. For Asia-Pacific (tickets issued after September 26, 2024): $199. For South America (tickets issued after June 25, 2025): $330. For US/Canada domestic routes, the fee is deducted from the ticket value per fare rules. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 for your specific fee.

**Does Delta Basic Economy ever qualify for a full cash refund?**
Yes, in three situations: within 24 hours of purchase (full cash refund), during an active travel waiver (cancellation fee typically waived), or when Delta cancels your flight (full cash refund under DOT rules regardless of fare type).
