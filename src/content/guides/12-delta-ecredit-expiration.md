# Delta eCredit Expiration: How Long It Lasts & What to Do If It Expires

A Delta eCredit represents real money — the value of a ticket you already paid for. Understanding when your eCredit expires is critical to ensuring you do not forfeit those travel funds. Many passengers are surprised to learn that the expiration countdown begins at purchase, not at cancellation. This guide explains everything you need to know about Delta eCredit expiration in 2026.

## How Long Does a Delta eCredit Last?

Delta eCredits expire **one year from the date the original ticket was purchased**. This is a common source of confusion, because many passengers assume the expiration runs from the date they cancelled the ticket or from the original travel date.

Here is a clarifying example:

- You purchased a Delta ticket on **March 1, 2025**.
- You traveled on the ticket but cancelled one leg, receiving a partial eCredit.
- Or you cancelled the entire ticket in June 2025.
- Your eCredit expires on **March 1, 2026** — one year from purchase, not from the June cancellation.

This means that if you wait a long time before canceling, you may have significantly less time to use the resulting eCredit than you expect.

## Where to Find Your eCredit Expiration Date

### Delta.com My Wallet

1. Log in to your SkyMiles account at delta.com.
2. Navigate to **My Wallet** or your account dashboard.
3. Active eCredits appear with their current balance and expiration date clearly listed.

### eCredit Certificate Email

When Delta issues an eCredit, you receive an email with the certificate details, including the expiration date. Searching your email for "Delta eCredit" or "travel certificate" can surface this information if you have lost track of it.

### By Phone

Call Delta at **++1-866-332-0756** to look up the expiration date of an eCredit by your ticket number or SkyMiles account.

## What Happens When a Delta eCredit Expires

Once the expiration date passes, a Delta eCredit is typically **forfeited**. The credit cannot be used for future bookings and does not automatically roll over or renew. Delta's standard policy does not include automatic extensions.

This means the balance is lost unless Delta agrees to an exception, which brings us to what you can do if this has already happened.

## What to Do If Your Delta eCredit Has Expired

### Step 1: Contact Delta Customer Care

If your eCredit expired recently, call Delta at **++1-866-332-0756** and explain your situation. Delta does not guarantee extensions for expired credits, but customer service representatives have occasionally issued goodwill exceptions — particularly if:

- The expiration was recent (within the last 30 to 90 days)
- The expiration occurred during a period of travel disruption or emergency
- You are a Medallion elite member with a history of loyalty to Delta

### Step 2: Ask About Reissuance

In some cases, Delta may reissue a new eCredit with a short validity window as a goodwill gesture. This is not a guaranteed outcome, and results depend on individual circumstances.

### Step 3: Submit Feedback in Writing

If a phone call does not resolve the issue, submit a written request through Delta's customer care portal at delta.com. Documenting your situation formally can sometimes get a different result than a phone call alone.

## How to Avoid Losing an eCredit to Expiration

The best strategy is to book your next trip before the expiration date — even if your travel dates are flexible.

### Strategy 1: Book a Refundable Ticket

Use the eCredit to purchase a refundable fare for a future date you think you might travel. If your plans change, cancel the refundable ticket and the value returns as another eCredit, potentially with a new expiration date. Check the terms of the refundable fare before relying on this approach.

### Strategy 2: Set a Calendar Reminder

Add a reminder 60 days before your eCredit expiration date. This gives you ample time to plan and book travel before the deadline.

### Strategy 3: Use Partial eCredits on Smaller Purchases

You do not need to use an eCredit on a single large booking. Apply partial eCredit value to a lower-cost flight or an upgrade purchase to drain the credit before it expires, rather than losing the full balance.

## Do Delta eCredits from Specific Situations Last Longer?

In some cases, eCredits issued under special circumstances — such as those issued during the pandemic travel disruptions — had extended expiration terms. Delta periodically adjusts eCredit policies during extraordinary events.

For eCredits issued under standard cancellations in 2026, the one-year-from-purchase rule applies. If you received an eCredit during a period of special policies (such as pandemic waivers), check the expiration date in your wallet to confirm whether extended terms apply to your specific credit.

## Using Multiple eCredits Before They Expire

If you have multiple eCredits with different expiration dates, apply the one expiring soonest first. At the payment step on delta.com, enter each certificate number separately. Delta's checkout allows multiple eCredits on a single booking.

Prioritizing credits by expiration date ensures you do not inadvertently let an older, smaller credit expire while using a newer one.

## Tips for Managing Delta eCredits

- **Check your wallet regularly.** Staying aware of what eCredits you have and when they expire prevents unpleasant surprises.
- **Never assume the expiration date.** Always verify the date in your wallet or from the certificate email — it is measured from purchase, not cancellation.
- **Book early if plans are uncertain.** Even a tentative booking using your eCredit preserves its value while you confirm details.
- **Call if your credit expired recently.** There is no guarantee, but Delta representatives have some discretion in exceptional cases.

## Contact Delta Customer Support

For help with eCredit expiration dates, expired credit inquiries, or assistance booking a flight using your eCredit, contact Delta Air Lines at **++1-866-332-0756** or visit the My Wallet section at delta.com.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When does a Delta eCredit expire?**

Most Delta eCredits expire one year from the date the original ticket was purchased. This is not one year from the cancellation date or the original travel date — it is one year from when the ticket was bought. Check the expiration date in your Delta wallet at delta.com.

**What happens if my Delta eCredit expires?**

Once a Delta eCredit expires, it is typically forfeited and can no longer be used. Delta does not automatically extend expired eCredits, but contacting Delta at ++1-866-332-0756 to explain your situation may result in a goodwill extension in some circumstances.

**Can I extend a Delta eCredit before it expires?**

Delta does not offer a standard process to extend an eCredit before expiration. The best approach is to use it before the expiration date. If you cannot travel, use the eCredit to book a refundable ticket for a future date, then cancel if needed to retain its value.

**How do I check when my Delta eCredit expires?**

Log in to delta.com and go to 'My Wallet' or your account dashboard. Active eCredits appear with their current balance and expiration date. You can also call Delta at ++1-866-332-0756 to confirm an eCredit's expiration date.
