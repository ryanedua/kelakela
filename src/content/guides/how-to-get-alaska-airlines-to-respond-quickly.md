This means East Coast travelers calling during their afternoon are hitting Alaska's peak West Coast morning volume simultaneously.

Alaska's merger with Hawaiian Airlines in 2025 and the subsequent Sabre PSS unification in April 2026 generated elevated customer contacts related to loyalty program transitions, booking system changes, and the Atmos Rewards rebrand. ## Use Alaska's Callback Feature

Alaska's phone system offers a **virtual hold callback** during high-volume periods. When the system detects a long queue, it presents the option to receive a callback rather than waiting on hold. You confirm your callback number, hang up, and Alaska's system calls you back when an agent becomes available while preserving your queue position.

The callback feature is not offered on every call — it activates based on real-time queue thresholds — but it is consistently available during peak-volume periods when wait times are longest. When offered, accepting the callback is almost always the better option. Keep your phone accessible after requesting it and answer promptly to avoid losing your position.

## Use the Alaska Airlines App for Self-Service

Alaska's **mobile app** provides self-service tools that handle many common requests without any customer service contact. During flight disruptions, the app pushes notifications to affected passengers with rebooking options on alternative flights, allowing you to select and confirm a new itinerary instantly without waiting in any queue.

Beyond disruption rebooking, the Alaska app manages flight status, gate changes, mobile boarding passes, seat upgrades, and Mileage Plan account activity. For the majority of routine information needs, the app provides the answer immediately without requiring contact with a live agent.

The self-service rebooking tool is particularly valuable during major disruptions when phone queues spike to their longest. Opening the app and selecting your preferred alternative flight the moment you receive a disruption notification gives you first access to available seats before other affected passengers fill them.
