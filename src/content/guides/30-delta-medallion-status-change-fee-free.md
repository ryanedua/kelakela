# Delta Medallion Members: Free Flight Changes & Cancel Rules

Delta's Medallion program rewards loyalty with more than lounge access and upgrade priority. Medallion status also provides meaningful flexibility benefits when it comes to changing and canceling flights — benefits that matter most when travel plans go sideways. Here is a clear breakdown of how Medallion status affects your flight change and cancellation options in 2026.

## Background: Delta's General Change Fee Policy

Before examining the Medallion-specific benefits, it helps to understand the baseline. Delta eliminated change fees on most tickets starting in 2020. In 2026, this means:

- **No change fee** on Main Classic, Main Extra, Comfort+, First Class, Delta Premium Select, and Delta One
- Fare differences still apply when switching to a more expensive flight
- **Main Basic does not allow voluntary changes**, regardless of status

This baseline applies to all passengers — Medallion and non-elite alike. Medallion status builds on top of this baseline to provide additional benefits.

## Same-Day Confirmed Change Fee Waiver

This is the most significant Medallion-specific change benefit. Delta charges **$75** for same-day confirmed flight changes (where you guarantee yourself a seat on an earlier or different same-day flight). For non-Medallion passengers on eligible fare classes, this $75 fee applies.

**All Medallion tiers have this fee waived:**

- Diamond Medallion — $75 fee waived
- Platinum Medallion — $75 fee waived
- Gold Medallion — $75 fee waived
- Silver Medallion — $75 fee waived

This means if your morning meeting ends early and you want to take an earlier flight home, Medallion members can book the confirmed same-day change for free, while non-elite passengers pay $75.

Same-day standby is free for all passengers regardless of status, but it is not guaranteed. The same-day confirmed change guarantee is the benefit that Medallion members get for free.

## Award Ticket Redeposit Fee Waiver

When you cancel a SkyMiles award ticket, Delta typically charges a fee to redeposit your miles back into your account. Medallion status significantly reduces or eliminates this fee:

- **Diamond Medallion** — No redeposit fee
- **Platinum Medallion** — No redeposit fee
- **Gold Medallion** — Reduced or no fee (verify current terms at delta.com)
- **Silver Medallion** — Reduced fee (verify current terms)
- **Non-elite members** — Standard redeposit fee applies

This benefit adds meaningful value when award travel plans change. Elite members can book and cancel award tickets freely, while non-elite members pay a penalty each time they need to cancel.

## Main Basic and Medallion Status

Medallion status does not change the fundamental fare rules for Main Basic tickets. Specifically:

- **Direct flight changes** are still not permitted on Main Basic, even for Diamond members
- **Cancellation yields an eCredit**, not a cash refund, for voluntary cancellations — regardless of Medallion tier
- **24-hour cancellation** for a full cash refund still applies, as it does for all passengers

Where Medallion status does make Main Basic better:

- **Complimentary seat upgrades** — Medallion members receive preferred seat assignments in economy and upgrade priority for First Class on Main Basic tickets
- **Priority boarding** — Medallion members board early regardless of fare class
- **Complimentary checked bags** — Medallion members receive free checked bags regardless of fare class

These status benefits mean the restrictions of Main Basic matter much less for elite members than for non-elite travelers.

## Upgrade Priority and Change Interaction

Medallion members requesting upgrades to First Class are waitlisted at booking and cleared close to departure. If you change your flight, your upgrade waitlist position transfers to the new flight, but your place in the waitlist queue may be affected depending on how close to departure the change is made.

When making a same-day confirmed change to an earlier flight, your upgrade request typically carries forward and is cleared based on your Medallion tier and the upgrade waitlist for the new flight.

## How Medallion Status Affects Rebooking After Disruptions

When Delta cancels a flight or makes a significant schedule change, Medallion members have the same rights as all other passengers — including the right to a full cash refund or free rebook under DOT rules. Status does not change these fundamental rights, but it does affect the rebooking experience:

- **Higher upgrade priority** on alternative flights, making a rebook more comfortable
- **More proactive Delta service** — higher-tier members may receive earlier personal notifications and priority in rebooking queues
- **Lounge access** during long layovers created by disruptions (for lounge-eligible tiers)

## Tips for Medallion Members Managing Flight Changes

- **Always use same-day confirmed changes for free.** The $75 fee waiver adds up significantly over a year of frequent travel.
- **Book award tickets with confidence.** The redeposit fee waiver for Platinum and Diamond members means award bookings carry no penalty for changes of plan.
- **Book Main Basic to save money when your status covers the restrictions.** If your Medallion status gives you seats, bags, and boarding priority, Main Basic is a better value for elite members than for general travelers.
- **Confirm your redeposit fee tier before canceling an award.** Fee structures can change; verify the current terms for your Medallion level on delta.com or by calling **++1-866-332-0756** before canceling a valuable award booking.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do Delta Medallion members pay flight change fees?**

Delta eliminated change fees on most tickets in 2020, so Medallion members and non-elite members alike pay no change fee on eligible fare classes (Main Classic and above). Medallion members additionally have the $75 same-day confirmed change fee waived, which non-elite members still pay.

**Do Medallion members pay award redeposit fees when canceling?**

Delta Medallion members at Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Diamond tiers may have reduced or waived award redeposit fees. Diamond and Platinum members typically pay no redeposit fee. Specific fee amounts for each tier should be verified on delta.com, as they are subject to change.

**Can Medallion members cancel a Main Basic ticket and change flights?**

Medallion status does not override the fare class rules for Main Basic tickets. On Main Basic, direct flight changes are still not permitted for Medallion members. However, Medallion members can cancel for an eCredit and rebook. Their status provides seat and boarding benefits regardless of fare class.

**What change benefits do Silver Medallion members get?**

Silver Medallion members receive the same-day confirmed change fee waiver (waiving the standard $75 fee), complimentary upgrades when available, and early boarding. Standard change fee eliminations on Main Classic and above apply to all passengers, including Silver members.
