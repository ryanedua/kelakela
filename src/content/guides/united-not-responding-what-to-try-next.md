# United Customer Service Not Responding? What to Try Next

If United customer service is not responding, work through these escalation steps: try +1-(855)-235-1859 as an alternative line, use the United app for self-service, contact @United on X for public escalation, submit at united.com/refunds in writing, or file a DOT complaint at transportation.gov/airconsumer for cash refund disputes.

## Step 1: Try the Secondary Line

If **+1-(855)-235-1859)** is overloaded, try **+1-(855)-235-1859)**. United operates multiple customer service routing lines. During peak periods, one line may have shorter wait times than the other. Call both simultaneously from two devices if the situation is urgent, and speak to whichever agent answers first.

## Step 2: Fly United App and united.com Self-Service

Flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets: united.com My Trips then Change Flight. eCredit application: united.com/redeem then book. Cancellation for eCredit: My Trips then Cancel. Standby addition: United app then My Trips then Same-Day Change then Standby. Refund submission: united.com/refunds then complete form.

If the phone line is not responding and your issue falls into one of these categories, the app resolves it with no wait.

## Step 3: Escalate on X

United monitors @United on X and responds to public mentions and direct messages faster than the web feedback form during high-volume periods.

What to post: @United My flight [confirmation code] was cancelled on [date]. I have been unable to reach customer service. I am requesting a full cash refund per DOT rules. Can you assist?

Include contact details in a follow-up direct message. United social media team has escalation authority and can initiate refund processing and arrange priority callbacks.

## Step 4: Submit a Written Refund Request

Submit at united.com/refunds. Include your confirmation code, flight date and route, event type (United-initiated cancellation), and explicit request: I am requesting a full cash refund to my original payment method under the 2024 DOT final rule. I do not consent to receiving an eCredit.

A written submission creates a timestamped record that supports escalation if United does not process the refund within 7 business days for credit card payments.

## Step 5: File a DOT Complaint

Go to transportation.gov/airconsumer. File a complaint if United has cancelled your flight, you have requested a cash refund, and United has not processed it within 7 business days for a credit card.

Include your flight details, the qualifying event, your refund request with dates, and United response or non-response. DOT complaints trigger a mandatory United response. The airline compliance rate is high. This is the most powerful escalation available to passengers.

## Frequently asked questions

**What do I do if United customer service is not answering?**
Try +1-(855)-235-1859 as an alternative line. Use the United app for self-service. Contact @United on X. Submit a written refund request at united.com/refunds.

**How do I escalate a United issue that an agent could not resolve?**
Ask for a supervisor during the call. If unresolved, call +1-(855)-235-1859. For cash refund disputes, submit at united.com/refunds and file a DOT complaint at transportation.gov/airconsumer if not resolved within 7 business days.

**Does contacting United on X actually work?**
Yes. @United on X responds to direct messages and public mentions faster than the web contact form. Particularly effective for unresolved cancellation refund requests.

**How do I file a DOT complaint against United?**
Go to transportation.gov/airconsumer. Complete the complaint form with your flight details, the qualifying event, your refund request, and United's response. Effective for cash refund disputes after qualifying United cancellations.

**What if United gives me a credit when I asked for a cash refund?**
Call +1-(855)-235-1859 and repeat the cash request. Submit at united.com/refunds in writing. File a DOT complaint at transportation.gov/airconsumer if unresolved after 7 business days.
