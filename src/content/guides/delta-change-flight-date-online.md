# How to Change Your Delta Flight Date Online in 2026

Changing a Delta flight date online takes about five minutes and costs nothing in change fees for most ticket types. Delta eliminated change fees on Classic and Extra fares from the US and Canada, so the only cost you may face is a fare difference if the new flight is priced higher than the original. Here is exactly how to do it step by step, what to expect at each stage, and when to call instead of clicking. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** if the online flow is not completing or your ticket type requires agent assistance.

## Step-by-Step: How to Change Your Delta Flight Date on delta.com

**Step 1 — Log in to your account**: Go to delta.com and sign in with your SkyMiles number and password. If you booked as a guest, have your booking confirmation number and last name ready.

**Step 2 — Go to My Trips**: Click your name in the upper right corner and select My Trips, or navigate directly to delta.com/mytrips. Your upcoming bookings will be listed.

**Step 3 — Select your booking**: Find the flight you want to change and click on it. The booking detail page will open showing your current itinerary.

**Step 4 — Choose Change Flight**: Look for the Change Flight option on the booking detail page. If this button is not visible, your ticket type may not support online changes (for example, Basic Economy or third-party tickets). In that case, call **+1-(866)-332-0756**.

**Step 5 — Search for new dates**: A search interface will open within your booking. Enter your preferred new departure date. Results show available flights for that date along with any fare difference that applies.

**Step 6 — Review the fare difference**: The system displays the price difference between your original ticket and the new flight. If the new flight is more expensive, you pay the difference at checkout. If it is less expensive, the difference will be issued as an eCredit. No standalone change fee appears for Classic or Extra tickets.

**Step 7 — Confirm and pay**: Select the new flight, review the summary, and confirm the change. Pay any fare difference with a card or eCredit. A new confirmation email will be sent immediately.

## How to Change Your Flight Date in the Fly Delta App

Open the app and tap My Trips at the bottom of the screen. Select your booking and tap Modify Flight or Change Flight. The in-app flow mirrors the website process. The app has the added advantage of showing same-day change options and standby availability directly within the trip view.

## Which Tickets Can Be Changed Online

**Main Classic**: Full online date change available. No change fee. Fare difference may apply.

**Main Extra**: Full online date change available. No change fee. Fare difference may apply. If the new flight costs less, the difference is issued as a cash refund since Extra is fully refundable.

**Comfort+, First Class, Delta One Classic/Extra**: Online changes available. No change fee for Classic and Extra tiers in premium cabins. Fare difference applies if moving to a more expensive flight.

**Main Basic (Basic Economy)**: Online date changes are not permitted after the 24-hour risk-free window. The ticket must be cancelled for an eCredit minus the applicable cancellation fee, and a new ticket purchased separately.

**Award tickets booked with SkyMiles**: Non-Basic award tickets can generally be changed online. A mileage difference may apply if the new award costs more.

## What Happens to the Fare Difference

**New flight costs more**: You pay the difference at checkout. This is the only cost for changing a Classic or Extra ticket.

**New flight costs the same**: No payment required. The change is free.

**New flight costs less**: The difference is issued as an eCredit for Classic tickets. For Extra tickets, the difference is refunded to your original payment method since Extra fares are fully refundable.

## Same-Day Date Changes

If your new travel date is today and you want to move to an earlier or later departure on the same calendar day, use the Same-Day Change feature rather than the standard change flow.

Same-Day Confirmed: $75 fee for non-Medallion passengers, waived for Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion members. Guarantees a seat on the new flight. No fare difference if staying in the same cabin.

Same-Day Standby: Free for all eligible passengers. You join the waitlist for an earlier flight without giving up your original seat. No guarantee of getting on.

Access both options through the Fly Delta app under Same-Day Change, or call **+1-(866)-332-0756)**.

## When to Call Instead of Clicking

If the Change Flight button does not appear in My Trips, if the online change flow returns an error, or if your ticket involves a complex multi-city routing or codeshare segments, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for agent assistance. Agents can complete changes that the online system does not support for your specific ticket and can apply manual waiver codes during disruptions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a fee to change a Delta flight date online?**
No change fee for Classic and Extra. Only a fare difference if the new flight costs more.

**Can I change a Delta flight date on the same day of travel?**
Yes via Same-Day Confirmed ($75) or Same-Day Standby (free) in the Fly Delta app.

**Can I change a Delta Basic Economy flight date online?**
No. Basic Economy cannot be changed after the 24-hour window. Cancel for an eCredit and rebook. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)**.

**What if the new flight costs less than what I paid?**
The fare difference is issued as an eCredit for Classic tickets. Extra tickets receive a cash refund.

**Can I change my Delta flight if I booked through a travel agency?**
Contact the agency. Delta cannot modify third-party issued tickets through My Trips.


**What if the new flight I want costs less than what I paid?**
If you change to a less expensive flight, the fare difference is issued as an eCredit for future use. Delta does not refund fare differences to the original payment method for voluntary date changes on non-refundable tickets.

**Can I change my Delta flight date if I booked through a travel agency?**
If you booked through a third-party agency, contact that agency to change your flight. Delta cannot modify tickets issued by third-party sellers through the standard My Trips flow.
