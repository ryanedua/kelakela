# How to Speak With a Alaska Representative About Your Booking

To speak with a Alaska representative about your booking, call +1-(855)-321-3341 with your 6-character confirmation code ready. Press 0 to bypass the IVR. State your confirmation code and the specific change you need in the first sentence. For booking issues that can be resolved without a call, the Alaska Airlines app and alaskaair.com My Trips handle most standard changes instantly.

Speaking with a Alaska representative about your booking is most effective when you arrive at the call prepared. Agents resolve bookings faster when they have the confirmation code in the first sentence and a specific request to work toward. Call **+1-(855)-321-3341)** with your confirmation code ready before you dial.

## What to Have Ready Before You Call

Your **6-character booking confirmation code** from your confirmation email. This is the single most important thing to have ready. The agent enters it immediately to pull up your reservation.

The **names of all passengers** on the booking, exactly as they appear on the confirmation.

Your **preferred outcome**: the specific flight you want to move to, or a cancellation for eCredit, or a cash refund. Knowing what you want before you call shortens the resolution time significantly.

A **credit card** in case a fare difference applies to a change.

Your **Mileage Plan number** if you hold MVP status — this routes your call with higher priority.

## What to Say When the Agent Answers

State your confirmation code and your request in the first sentence:

"My confirmation code is ABC123. I need to move my July 25 flight from the 2 PM to the 6 PM Atlanta to Los Angeles departure."

"My confirmation is ABC123. Alaska cancelled my July 25 flight and I want a full cash refund to my original payment method."

"My confirmation is ABC123. I need to apply my eCredit to rebook on the July 28 departure instead."

Agents who receive specific requests process them faster than agents who spend the first few minutes asking clarifying questions about what the passenger actually needs.

## When You Do Not Need to Call

Many booking-related tasks do not require a live representative:

Voluntary flight date and time changes on Classic and Extra tickets: alaskaair.com My Trips → Change Flight.
eCredit application at checkout: alaskaair.com/redeem or the Payment section at checkout.
Cancellation for eCredit: My Trips → Cancel.
Seat changes on eligible bookings: My Trips → Manage Seats or the Alaska Airlines app.
Same-day standby additions: Alaska Airlines app → My Trips → Same-Day Change.

Use these channels first. Call **+1-(855)-321-3341)** only when the self-service tools cannot complete your specific request.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I speak with a Alaska representative about my booking?**
Call +1-(855)-321-3341 with your 6-character booking confirmation code ready. Press 0 to bypass the IVR. State your confirmation code and specific request in the first sentence.

**What information do I need before calling Alaska about my booking?**
Your 6-character confirmation code, passenger names, preferred outcome (rebook, cancel, refund), and a credit card if a fare difference might apply.

**Can I change my Alaska booking without calling a representative?**
Yes for most standard changes via the Alaska Airlines app or alaskaair.com My Trips. Call +1-(855)-321-3341 when online tools cannot complete your request.

**What booking issues require speaking with a Alaska representative?**
Complex multi-city changes, waiver code application issues, award ticket destination changes, cash refund requests after Alaska cancellations, and supervisor escalations.

**What should I say to a Alaska representative when calling about my booking?**
Lead with your confirmation code and the specific outcome: 'My confirmation is ABC123. I need to change my July 25 flight to the 6 PM departure.'
