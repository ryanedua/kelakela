# What is the Delta eCredit expiration date? — 2026 Answer

Delta eCredits expire one year from the original ticket purchase date — not the cancellation date. You only need to book a new flight before the expiration date, not complete the travel. Residual credits from partial use keep the same original expiration date. Log in to My Wallet on delta.com or call +1-(855)-321-3528 to check your specific expiration date.
