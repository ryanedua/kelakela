# Calling Frontier vs. Using the App: Which Is More Reliable?

The Frontier app is more reliable for self-service tasks — flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets, standby additions, eCredit application, and cancellations — because it works 24 hours with no hold time and makes no verbal errors. Calling +1-(866)-227-2948 is more reliable for complex issues, disruption rebooking, cash refunds, and anything requiring a judgment call from a human agent.

Phone and the Frontier app are not competing tools — they complement each other. Each one handles a different set of tasks reliably and falls short on others. Knowing which to use first, and when to switch, eliminates the frustration of being stuck in the wrong channel. Call **+1-(866)-227-2948)** when the app cannot complete your request.

## Where the App Is More Reliable

**Speed and availability**: The Frontier app works 24 hours with no hold time. There is no queue, no IVR, and no callback to wait for. For tasks it can handle, it is faster than any phone interaction.

**Accuracy on standard changes**: When you change a Classic or Extra ticket through the app, the fare difference is calculated automatically and the new itinerary is confirmed in under 2 minutes. No verbal miscommunication, no hold music, no repeating your confirmation code three times.

**Standby and same-day changes**: Adding yourself to the standby list for an earlier flight is faster on the app than by phone. You can see your real-time standby position and receive push notification when you clear.

**Tasks the app handles reliably**: Voluntary flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets. Cancellation for eCredit on eligible fares. eCredit application at checkout. Same-day standby addition. Boarding pass retrieval. Flight status and gate checks.

## Where the Phone Is More Reliable

**Judgment calls and exceptions**: When your situation does not fit the standard parameters — a waiver code that is not applying, a cash refund request after a Frontier cancellation, an unusual routing change — a live agent can apply judgment and override system defaults that the app cannot.

**Disruption rebooking**: During major cancellations, the app may lag behind the phone system in available inventory. Phone agents can see and book alternatives that have not yet surfaced in the app's search results.

**Complex itineraries**: Multi-city, award tickets, group bookings, and codeshare segments may not be fully modifiable through the app. Phone handles these reliably.

**Escalation**: The app has no path to a supervisor. When escalation is needed, the phone is the only channel.

## The Practical Rule

Try the app first for any self-service task. If the app presents a suitable option, complete it — you will be done in under 2 minutes. If the app cannot complete the request or the options it shows are inadequate, call **+1-(866)-227-2948)**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is calling Frontier or using the app more reliable?**
The app is more reliable for self-service tasks. Calling +1-(866)-227-2948 is more reliable for complex situations requiring human judgment — disruption rebooking, cash refunds, supervisor escalations.

**Can the Frontier app do everything the phone can?**
No. The app cannot process cash refunds for qualifying cancellations, apply manual waiver codes, complete award ticket destination changes, or escalate to a supervisor.

**When does the Frontier app fail and require a phone call?**
When it does not offer a Change Flight option for your ticket type, when it does not surface adequate disruption alternatives, or when you need something outside standard self-service scope.

**Does the Frontier app work during flight disruptions?**
Partially. Use the app first to check automatic rebooking. If the alternatives are inadequate, call +1-(866)-227-2948.

**Is it faster to change a Frontier flight by phone or app?**
By app for standard changes — under 2 minutes, no hold time. By phone for changes the app cannot complete.

- Disruptions: try app first, then phone if alternatives are inadequate
