# How long does a Delta refund take? — 2026 Answer

Credit card refunds from Delta process within 7 business days under DOT rules. Other payment methods such as cash or check take up to 20 calendar days. Delta typically processes faster than these mandatory windows. If your refund has not appeared after 7 business days on a credit card, call +1-(855)-321-3528 with your booking confirmation to check the status.
