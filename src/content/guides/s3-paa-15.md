# What happens if I miss my Delta flight? — 2026 Answer

If you miss your Delta flight without cancelling first, it is registered as a no-show. The full ticket value is forfeited for non-refundable fares and all remaining segments including return flights are automatically cancelled. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 immediately before the flight departs if possible. Cancelling before departure, even one minute before, is always better than a no-show.
