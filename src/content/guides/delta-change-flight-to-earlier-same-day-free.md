# How to Change to an Earlier Delta Flight on the Same Day for Free in 2026

Getting on an earlier Delta flight than the one you booked is straightforward if you know which tool to use. Delta offers two same-day change options: Standby, which is free, and Confirmed, which costs $75 but guarantees your seat. Neither charges a fare difference as long as you stay in the same cabin. The right choice depends on how certain you need to be about getting on the earlier flight. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to request either option by phone, or use the Fly Delta app for the fastest self-service experience.

## Option 1: Same-Day Standby (Free)

Same-Day Standby places you on the waitlist for an earlier Delta flight on the same travel day at no cost. You keep your original confirmed seat and boarding pass throughout the process. If a seat becomes available on the earlier flight before it departs, you board. If not, you board your original flight as planned.

**Cost**: $0 for all eligible passengers regardless of Medallion status.

**How it works**: Your name joins the standby list for the target earlier flight. Medallion members are placed ahead of non-Medallion passengers on the list. Within the same status tier, passengers who requested standby earlier generally have priority.

**Key advantage**: No fare difference, no fee, and your original seat is safe throughout.

**How to request it**:
- Fly Delta app: Go to My Trips, tap your flight, select Same-Day Change, then Standby.
- Airport: Go to the check-in counter or gate and ask to be added to the standby list for an earlier flight.
- Phone: Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and ask for same-day standby on your preferred earlier departure.

Add yourself to standby as early in the day as possible. Earlier requests generally receive better list placement.

## Option 2: Same-Day Confirmed ($75, Waived for Gold/Platinum/Diamond)

Same-Day Confirmed guarantees a seat on a specific earlier Delta flight. When you confirm the change, you receive a new boarding pass immediately. Your original booking is replaced with the confirmed new departure.

**Cost**: $75 for Silver Medallion members and non-Medallion passengers. Waived for Gold Medallion, Platinum Medallion, and Diamond Medallion members.

**How it works**: A confirmed seat must be available in your original fare class on the new flight. If the flight is full in your fare class, Confirmed is not available for that departure.

**Key advantage**: Guaranteed seat with no waiting or uncertainty.

**How to request it**:
- Fly Delta app: Go to My Trips, tap your flight, select Same-Day Change, then Confirmed. The app shows available flights and whether confirmed space is available.
- Airport: Ask the check-in counter or gate agent for a same-day confirmed change to your preferred earlier flight.
- Phone: Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to request confirmed placement by phone.

## For Medallion Members: Always Use Confirmed

If you hold Gold, Platinum, or Diamond Medallion status, the $75 Same-Day Confirmed fee is waived. For these elite members, Confirmed is the clear choice: guaranteed seat, no fee, and you board the earlier flight without any waiting or uncertainty. Standby provides no meaningful advantage when the Confirmed fee is zero.

Silver Medallion members pay the $75 fee for Confirmed but receive priority placement on the Standby list over non-Medallion passengers, which improves the odds of clearing standby without paying.

## No Fare Difference: The Biggest Advantage

The most financially important aspect of same-day changes — often overlooked — is that no fare difference applies when you move to an earlier flight in the same cabin class. If you booked a $250 Main Classic ticket and the earlier flight you want is currently selling for $480, you do not pay the $230 difference. You pay $0 (Standby) or $75 (Confirmed).

This is a significant advantage over a standard ticket change, where the fare difference would apply at full price. Same-day changes are specifically designed to let you adjust within-day travel timing without a price penalty.

## Who Is Not Eligible

Delta Main Basic (Basic Economy) passengers are excluded from both Same-Day Standby and Same-Day Confirmed. If you hold a Basic ticket and want to move to an earlier flight, you would need to purchase a new ticket for the earlier departure.

Long-haul international flights generally do not qualify for same-day changes. The option applies primarily to domestic US routes and certain short-haul international routes. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** to verify eligibility on your specific international route.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change to an earlier Delta flight the same day for free?**
Yes via Same-Day Standby — free for all eligible passengers. No fare difference. No guarantee.

**Does changing to an earlier Delta flight charge a fare difference?**
No, when staying in the same cabin class.

**How do I request Same-Day Standby for an earlier flight?**
Fly Delta app under Same-Day Change, at the airport counter, or call **+1-(866)-332-0756)**.

**What if I'm a Medallion member?**
Same-Day Standby is free for all. Same-Day Confirmed fee is waived for Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion members — use Confirmed for the guaranteed seat.

**Can I change to an earlier international Delta flight the same day?**
Generally no for long-haul routes. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** to check.


**Does changing to an earlier Delta flight on the same day cost a fare difference?**
No. Neither Same-Day Standby nor Same-Day Confirmed charges a fare difference when the new flight is in the same cabin as the original booking. This is a key advantage of same-day changes over standard changes.

**How do I request Same-Day Standby for an earlier flight on Delta?**
Open the Fly Delta app, go to My Trips, select your flight, and tap Same-Day Change. Select Standby for your preferred earlier departure. Alternatively, request at the airport check-in counter or by calling +1-(866)-332-0756.

**What if I'm a Medallion member — is the same-day change free?**
Same-Day Standby is free for all passengers including Medallion members. Same-Day Confirmed ($75) is waived for Gold Medallion, Platinum Medallion, and Diamond Medallion members. For these elite members, Same-Day Confirmed is both free and guaranteed — always the better choice.

**Can I change to an earlier Delta international flight the same day?**
Delta's same-day change options apply primarily to domestic US routes. Long-haul international flights generally do not qualify. Call +1-(855)-321-3528 to check eligibility on your specific international route.
