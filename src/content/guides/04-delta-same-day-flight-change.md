# Delta Same-Day Flight Change: Confirmed vs Standby Guide

Sometimes your plans shift on the day of travel. Whether an early meeting ends ahead of schedule or traffic makes your original departure time risky, Delta's same-day change options give you flexibility to board an earlier or different flight. Understanding the difference between a same-day confirmed change and same-day standby — and knowing the fees involved — helps you move quickly and make the right call.

## What Is a Delta Same-Day Flight Change?

A same-day flight change allows you to switch to a different flight on the same calendar day as your originally scheduled departure. Delta offers two distinct versions of this option:

- **Same-Day Confirmed Change** — You pay a fee (or qualify for a waiver) and are guaranteed a seat on the new flight before your original flight departs.
- **Same-Day Standby** — You are added to a waitlist for an earlier flight at no charge, but you are only confirmed if a seat becomes available.

Both options are subject to availability and must be requested on the actual day of your ticketed flight.

## Same-Day Confirmed Change: How It Works

A same-day confirmed change is the more reliable of the two options. Once you pay the fee and select a new flight, your seat is confirmed. Your original booking is updated and you no longer have to worry about availability.

### Cost of a Same-Day Confirmed Change

Delta charges **$75** for same-day confirmed changes on eligible tickets. This fee applies at the time of the request and is charged per person.

### Who Gets the Fee Waived

Several passenger categories receive complimentary same-day confirmed changes:

- **Diamond Medallion members** — free
- **Platinum Medallion members** — free
- **Gold Medallion members** — free
- **Silver Medallion members** — free
- **Delta One cabin passengers** — free
- **First Class passengers** — free
- **Delta Premium Select passengers** — free

### Eligible Fare Classes

Same-day confirmed changes are available for passengers booked in Main Classic and higher fare classes. Main Basic passengers are not eligible for same-day confirmed changes but can use same-day standby.

### Rules and Restrictions

- The new flight must be on the same calendar day as your original departure.
- The origin and destination must remain the same. You cannot use a same-day change to reroute to a different city.
- A fare difference is not charged — you pay only the flat $75 fee regardless of the new flight's price.
- Requests must be made at least one hour before the departure of the new flight.

## Same-Day Standby: How It Works

Same-day standby is a no-cost option that allows you to wait for a seat to open up on an earlier same-day flight. Unlike a confirmed change, standby does not guarantee you a spot. You keep your original reservation and only move to the earlier flight if space is available.

### Who Can Use Same-Day Standby

Same-day standby is available to all Delta passengers, including those on Main Basic fares. There is no fee to be added to the standby list.

### How Standby Works at the Airport

When you request same-day standby, Delta adds your name to the standby list for the earlier flight. The order in which standby passengers are confirmed is based on:

- Medallion status (higher-status members are confirmed first)
- Fare class
- Check-in time

If a seat becomes available — typically due to other passengers not checking in or operational adjustments — Delta will confirm you and notify you via the app, a text message, or a gate announcement.

### Your Original Seat Is Protected

A key advantage of same-day standby is that your original flight seat remains held until you are confirmed on the new flight. If you are not confirmed on the earlier flight, you simply board your original flight as planned.

## Confirmed Change vs Standby: Which Should You Choose?

| | Same-Day Confirmed | Same-Day Standby |
|---|---|---|
| Fee | $75 (waived for Medallion/premium) | Free |
| Seat guarantee | Yes | No |
| Eligibility | Main Classic and above | All fare classes |
| Risk | None after payment | May not get on |
| Best for | Tight connections or urgent arrival | Flexible passengers |

If you have a meeting, connection, or event that depends on arriving earlier, choose the same-day confirmed change. If you are flexible and simply prefer to get home sooner if possible, same-day standby is a cost-free way to try.

## How to Request a Same-Day Change

### Online at Delta.com

1. Log in to your SkyMiles account.
2. Go to **My Trips** and select your upcoming flight.
3. Look for the same-day change option and select your preferred earlier flight.
4. Confirm the fee (or verify your waiver eligibility) and complete the change.

### Fly Delta App

Open the app, navigate to your trip, and tap the same-day change option if available. The app shows standby and confirmed options and walks you through the process.

### Airport Kiosk or Counter

Delta kiosks at most airports can process same-day standby requests. For confirmed changes, an airport agent at the Delta counter can assist and process payment if needed.

### By Phone

Call Delta at **++1-866-332-0756** to speak with an agent who can process both confirmed changes and standby requests. This is useful if you are managing a complex itinerary or need help choosing between options.

## Tips for Same-Day Changes

- **Request early.** The earlier you request a standby or confirmed change, the better your chances of getting on a preferred flight, especially during busy travel periods.
- **Monitor the app.** Delta's app provides real-time standby list updates, so you can see your position and how quickly the list is moving.
- **Know your status.** Medallion members should always check their waiver eligibility before paying a same-day fee, as the $75 charge may not apply.
- **Check your connecting flights.** If your trip has connections, confirm that changing to an earlier first leg does not disrupt your connecting flights before making the change.

## Contact Delta Customer Support

For same-day flight change requests, fee waivers, or standby list inquiries, contact Delta Air Lines at **++1-866-332-0756** or use the Fly Delta app for the fastest self-service experience.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does a Delta same-day confirmed flight change cost?**

Delta charges $75 for a same-day confirmed flight change on most tickets. This fee is waived for Diamond, Platinum, Gold, and Silver Medallion members, as well as passengers booked in Delta One, First Class, and Delta Premium Select.

**What is the difference between a same-day confirmed change and same-day standby on Delta?**

A same-day confirmed change guarantees your seat on the new flight for a fee. Same-day standby places you on a waitlist for an earlier flight at no charge, but your original seat is not given up until you are confirmed on the new flight.

**How do I request a same-day flight change on Delta?**

You can request a same-day change online at delta.com, through the Fly Delta app under 'My Trips,' at an airport kiosk, or by calling Delta at ++1-866-332-0756. Changes must be requested on the day of your original flight.

**Can Delta Main Basic passengers use same-day standby?**

Yes, Main Basic passengers can request same-day standby at no charge. However, they are not eligible for same-day confirmed changes, which require a Main Classic or higher fare.
