# Missed Your Connecting Delta Flight? Here Is How to Get Rebooked

If a Delta delay caused you to miss your connecting flight, go to the gate of the next available Delta departure to your destination immediately — do not go to the check-in counter. Simultaneously call +1-(866)-332-0756. Delta is responsible for rebooking you at no cost when its delay caused the missed connection. For overnight controllable delays, request hotel accommodation at the gate.

When a Delta delay causes a missed connection, the rules are clear: Delta is responsible, Delta rebooks you at no cost, and for controllable overnight situations, Delta provides hotel accommodation. The challenge is speed — the next available flight to your destination fills quickly as every other affected passenger competes for the same seats. Move immediately. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** the moment you land and simultaneously run toward the gate.

## The Moment You Land: What to Do

**Do not go to the main check-in counter**. The main counter handles a general queue. You need to be at the gate of the specific flight you are trying to board.

**Go directly to the gate of the next departure to your destination**. The gate agent managing that flight has direct authority over that flight's seats and standby list. They can add you physically to the list for a flight boarding in 15 minutes — something no phone agent can guarantee.

**Call +1-(866)-332-0756) simultaneously**. If the gate line is long, calling covers your position in both channels. Whichever resolves first wins.

**Tell the agent immediately**: "A Delta delay caused me to miss my connection. I need rebooking to [final destination] at no additional cost. Delta is responsible for this missed connection."

## What Delta Owes You

**Free rebooking**: On the next available Delta flight to your final destination. No fare difference. No change fee. This applies to all fare types including Basic Economy — the missed connection overrides fare restrictions.

**Meal vouchers**: If the controllable delay has passed 3 hours and you are waiting for the next departure.

**Hotel and ground transport**: If the controllable delay causes an overnight stay. Ask the gate agent at the connection airport immediately when it becomes clear you will not make it that day.

**Partner airline rebooking**: If no adequate Delta option exists in a reasonable timeframe, request rebooking on a SkyTeam partner airline. Not offered automatically — ask specifically.

## If Delta's Delay Did Not Cause the Miss

If you caused the missed connection — you were slow getting off the plane, took too long in customs, or arrived late to the original airport — Delta's responsibility is reduced. Agents have goodwill discretion but are not obligated to rebook you for free. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** immediately, explain honestly, and ask what options are available for your fare type.

## Frequently asked questions

**What should I do if I miss my connecting Delta flight?**
Go directly to the gate of the next Delta departure to your destination. Simultaneously call +1-(866)-332-0756. Delta rebooks you at no cost when its delay caused the miss.

**Is Delta responsible if I miss a connection because of their delay?**
Yes. Delta rebooks you at no cost on the next available flight when its delay caused your missed connection on the same itinerary.

**Does Delta provide a hotel if I miss a connection and am stranded overnight?**
Yes for controllable delays. Hotel, ground transportation, and meal vouchers. Ask the gate agent immediately. Not required for weather.

**What if there are no Delta flights to my destination until tomorrow?**
Delta rebooks you the next day. Request hotel, transport, and meal vouchers from the gate agent for the overnight. Ask about SkyTeam partner options for a sooner departure.

**Can Delta rebook me on another airline if I miss my connection?**
Yes when no adequate Delta alternative is available. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 and ask specifically about SkyTeam partner rebooking.
