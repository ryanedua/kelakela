# How to Use Your Delta eCredit Online in 2026 (Step-by-Step)

Using a Delta eCredit online is straightforward once you know where to look and what order to follow the steps in. The process takes about two minutes whether you start at the payment step during checkout or pre-load the credit at delta.com/redeem first. Either path works, and choosing depends on your situation — logged-in SkyMiles members can go straight to checkout, while guest bookers or those with credits not linked to an account will need the redeem flow first. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** if you encounter any issues or if the credit is not appearing where it should.

## Method 1: Apply at Checkout (Fastest for SkyMiles Members)

This method works when you are logged in to your SkyMiles account and your eCredit is linked to that account.

**Step 1**: Log in to your SkyMiles account on delta.com before searching for flights.

**Step 2**: Search for your flight using the standard flight search. Enter origin, destination, and dates. The fare selection and seat selection process is identical to a normal booking.

**Step 3**: Proceed to checkout. On the payment page, look for the Use eCredits section. This typically appears above or below the credit card entry fields.

**Step 4**: Click Use eCredits. A list of your available credits will appear with their values and expiration dates. Check the box next to the credit you want to apply.

**Step 5**: The credit amount is deducted from your total. If a balance remains, enter your credit card to pay the difference. If the credit covers the full amount, enter a credit card anyway — Delta requires one for verification even when the credit covers everything.

**Step 6**: Confirm and complete the booking. A confirmation email will show the eCredit applied and any card charge.

## Method 2: Apply via delta.com/redeem First (For Guest Bookers or Credits Not in Wallet)

This method is for passengers who booked as a guest without a SkyMiles account, or whose credit is not appearing in their wallet.

**Step 1**: Go to delta.com/redeem.

**Step 2**: Search for your eCredit using one of three options: your 13-digit ticket number (found in your original booking confirmation email under Flight Receipt, starts with 006), the credit card used to purchase the original ticket, or your SkyMiles account number.

**Step 3**: Enter the first and last name of the original ticket holder to validate the credit. Click Add.

**Step 4**: Your eCredit is now loaded in the session. Check the box next to the credit and click Continue. You are redirected to the flight search with the credit pre-loaded.

**Step 5**: Search for and select your new flight. The eCredit will apply automatically at the checkout step. Pay any remaining balance with a card.

## Using Multiple eCredits on One Booking

If you have multiple eCredits — for example, from two separately cancelled bookings — you can apply more than one to a single new booking.

In Method 1, the checkout page shows all available credits in your wallet. Select each one you want to apply.

In Method 2 at delta.com/redeem, you can add multiple credits to the session before proceeding to the flight search. Add each one separately, then continue to flight selection.

If the online checkout limits the number of credits you can apply or they are not all appearing, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and an agent can apply each credit manually to a phone booking. Note that a phone ticketing fee may apply for bookings made by agent instead of self-service online.

## What Happens When the Credit Is More Than the Flight Cost

If your eCredit is larger than the cost of the flight you are booking, Delta issues the remaining balance as a new eCredit after the booking is confirmed. The residual credit carries the same expiration date as the original — the clock does not reset from the date of use.

For example: you have a $400 eCredit expiring March 1, 2027. You book a $180 flight. The $220 remaining balance is reissued as a new eCredit, also expiring March 1, 2027.

Plan your next use of the residual credit accordingly so it does not expire unused.

## Troubleshooting: What to Do If the Credit Won't Apply

**Credit not showing in wallet**: Check that you are logged in to the correct SkyMiles account. If the credit was linked to a different account or a guest booking, use delta.com/redeem instead.

**Ticket number not found at redeem page**: Locate your original booking confirmation email and find the 13-digit ticket number starting with 006 in the Flight Receipt section. Enter the number without spaces or dashes.

**System shows a fee when applying**: If the checkout shows a change or cancellation fee when applying a credit, do not proceed — call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to have an agent investigate whether a waiver should apply.

**Credit appears but cannot be selected**: Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for manual application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I apply a Delta eCredit at checkout?**
Click Use eCredits in the Payment section, select your credit, and confirm. Pay any remaining balance with a card.

**What if my Delta eCredit doesn't appear at checkout?**
Log in to your SkyMiles account or use delta.com/redeem to search by ticket number. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** if it still won't appear.

**Can I use multiple Delta eCredits on one booking?**
Yes. Select each credit in the checkout or pre-load multiple at delta.com/redeem. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for manual application if needed.

**Do I need a credit card even if my eCredit covers the full fare?**
Yes. Delta requires a card on file for all bookings even when the eCredit covers everything.

**What happens to the remaining balance if my eCredit is more than the flight cost?**
The balance is reissued as a new eCredit with the same original expiration date.
