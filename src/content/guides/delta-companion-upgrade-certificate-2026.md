# Delta Companion Certificate 2026: Complete Guide to Rules and Best Use

The Delta Companion Certificate is one of the most valuable perks tied to Delta's co-branded American Express credit cards. Used correctly, it lets you bring a companion on a round-trip flight for the cost of taxes and fees only — which on a domestic First Class ticket can mean a $1,200 value for less than $20. Used incorrectly, or without understanding the rules, it can leave you with a forfeited certificate and no compensation. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** when booking a Companion Certificate to ensure the reservation is set up correctly the first time.

## Which Cards Include a Companion Certificate

Four Delta American Express cards include a Companion Certificate as an annual benefit upon card renewal:

The Delta SkyMiles Platinum American Express Card (personal) provides a certificate valid for Main Cabin and Comfort+ domestic, Caribbean, and Central American round-trip travel.

The Delta SkyMiles Reserve American Express Card (personal) provides a certificate valid for First Class, Comfort+, and Main Cabin domestic, Caribbean, and Central American round-trip travel.

The Delta SkyMiles Platinum Business American Express Card and Delta SkyMiles Reserve Business American Express Card each provide certificates with the same terms as their personal counterparts.

The certificate is issued after card renewal and payment of the annual fee. It typically appears in your SkyMiles wallet within a few weeks of renewal.

## The Critical Expiration Rule

The most important rule that catches passengers off guard is the expiration requirement. Unlike a Delta eCredit where you only need to book by the expiration date, a Companion Certificate requires that travel be fully completed before the expiration date. You must board, fly, and arrive at your destination before the certificate expires.

If your certificate expires July 31, 2026, you cannot book a trip departing July 28 with a return arriving August 2. The return must land on or before July 31.

There is no grace period and no extension. An unused or partially used certificate at expiration is forfeited with no carryover to the following year. Plan your trip well in advance to allow time for booking and to ensure the return falls within the window.

## Booking a Companion Certificate

You must book the primary paid ticket and the companion certificate ticket simultaneously on the same reservation. You cannot apply a Companion Certificate to a ticket that has already been purchased or exists as a separate booking.

The primary ticket must be purchased with your eligible Delta American Express card. Both the primary passenger and the companion must be on the exact same flights and dates.

To book, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and tell the agent you want to use a Delta Companion Certificate from your eligible Amex card. Have your card and SkyMiles account information ready. As of 2026, the certificate can also be applied toward the flight portion of a Delta Vacations package in addition to standalone flights.

## What the Companion Pays

The companion travels for the cost of government-imposed taxes and fees only. On most domestic US routes this amounts to $5.60 to $30 depending on the origin and destination. On Caribbean and Central American routes the taxes are higher due to destination country fees.

The companion pays nothing for the airfare itself — only the mandatory government taxes. The primary cardholder pays the full fare for their own ticket plus both passengers' taxes.

## Miles and Status Earning

The primary paid ticket earns SkyMiles and Medallion Qualifying Dollars, Miles, and Segments normally based on the fare purchased. The companion ticket does not earn SkyMiles of any kind or count toward Medallion status accrual. If earning miles matters to the companion, they should note this before traveling.

## Upgrade Eligibility

You cannot change the cabin class of a Companion Certificate booking at the time of ticketing — for example, you cannot pay the difference to move from Main Cabin to First Class after issuing the certificate. The certificate must be used for the cabin class for which it was issued.

However, if either passenger holds Delta Medallion status, both the primary ticket holder and the companion are eligible for complimentary upgrades based on the higher status tier of the two passengers. A Gold Medallion member traveling with a general member companion can bring both on the upgrade list as though both were Gold Medallion members.

Post-purchase paid upgrades using cash or SkyMiles may also be offered through My Trips at Delta's discretion based on availability.

## Cancellation Rules

If you cancel a Companion Certificate booking, the certificate is reissued to your SkyMiles wallet with its original expiration date intact. You do not get a new certificate or an extended window. The reissued certificate must still be used with travel completed before the original expiration.

The primary ticket is refunded based on its fare rules — Classic or Extra fares produce a full eCredit or cash refund, Basic produces an eCredit minus a fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which Delta credit cards include a Companion Certificate?**
Delta Platinum Amex (personal and business) and Delta Reserve Amex (personal and business) each include one upon annual renewal. Reserve certificates cover First Class; Platinum certificates cover Main Cabin and Comfort+.

**Do I have to complete travel before the certificate expires?**
Yes. Unlike eCredits, travel must be fully completed before expiration. Booking before the date is not sufficient.

**Can I use my Companion Certificate for someone who is not traveling with me?**
No. The primary cardholder must travel on the same itinerary as the companion.

**Does the companion earn SkyMiles?**
No. The companion ticket does not earn miles or Medallion credit.

**Can I upgrade a Companion Certificate booking?**
You cannot upfare at booking. Complimentary Medallion upgrades may apply for both passengers based on the higher-status member's tier.


**Does the companion on a Delta Companion Certificate earn SkyMiles?**
No. The companion ticket issued by the certificate does not earn SkyMiles or count toward Medallion status. The primary paid ticket earns miles normally.

**Can I upgrade a Companion Certificate booking to First Class or Delta One?**
You cannot upfare the companion certificate at the time of booking. However, if you or your companion hold Medallion status, both passengers may be eligible for complimentary upgrades on the same itinerary based on the status of the higher-tiered member.
