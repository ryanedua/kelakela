# Delta No-Show Policy 2026: Do You Get an eCredit After Missing a Flight?

No. Delta does not issue eCredits for no-shows. If you miss your flight without cancelling before departure, your ticket value is forfeited — not converted to a credit, not held for later use. The distinction between a pre-departure cancellation (which produces an eCredit) and a no-show (which produces nothing) is one of the most financially significant in Delta's policy. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** the moment you realize you will miss your flight, even if it is only minutes before departure.

## What Is a Delta No-Show?

A no-show occurs when a passenger does not board their flight and does not cancel or contact Delta before the flight's departure time. The key element is the lack of action before the plane leaves. Once the departure time passes without the passenger on board and without any prior cancellation, the system registers a no-show.

The no-show designation triggers two automatic consequences: the ticket value is forfeited, and all subsequent segments in the itinerary are cancelled.

## No eCredit for No-Shows: Why This Matters

For non-refundable Main Classic tickets, a pre-departure cancellation produces a full eCredit with no fee. That same ticket, if no-shown, produces nothing. The passenger loses the entire amount paid.

For Main Basic tickets, a pre-departure cancellation produces an eCredit minus the cancellation fee. A no-show produces nothing at all — not even the reduced eCredit.

For Main Extra tickets, a pre-departure cancellation produces a full cash refund. A no-show forfeits the refundable value.

The math is always the same: any pre-departure cancellation beats a no-show. Even the worst possible pre-departure outcome (Basic ticket, high cancellation fee, minimal residual eCredit) is better than a no-show, because at least something is preserved.

## The Return Flight Cancellation Trap

The most damaging consequence of a no-show that passengers consistently underestimate is the automatic cancellation of all remaining itinerary segments. This happens quickly after the original departure time.

A passenger who no-shows the outbound flight of a round trip does not just lose the outbound ticket value. They also lose their return flight. Both cancellations happen automatically, and by the time the passenger realizes the scope of the loss, rebooking the return may require purchasing a new ticket at current prices.

Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** immediately if you realize you are going to miss a flight. The most time-critical action is telling Delta before departure — this prevents the no-show flag and gives agents the ability to protect your subsequent segments.

## Pre-Departure Cancellation: The Alternative to a No-Show

Any cancellation made before the flight's scheduled departure time counts as a pre-departure cancellation, not a no-show. This includes:

Cancelling one day before departure via delta.com.
Cancelling two hours before departure via the Fly Delta app.
Cancelling 10 minutes before departure by calling **+1-(866)-332-0756)**.
Cancelling at the airport counter while the flight is still boarding.

All of these preserve the ticket value in whatever form the fare type allows: full eCredit for Classic, eCredit minus fee for Basic, cash refund for Extra.

The only exception is after the boarding door closes or after the flight actually departs. At that point, a cancellation request is treated as a no-show.

## Goodwill Exceptions: Rare but Possible

Delta agents have discretion to apply goodwill accommodations in genuine extraordinary circumstances. Situations where passengers have received partial value recovery after a no-show include: documented medical emergencies requiring immediate care, documented car accidents en route to the airport, and severe infrastructure events that prevented access to the airport.

These outcomes are not guaranteed. Calling immediately after the missed flight (not hours later) and explaining the situation honestly and briefly gives the best chance of any goodwill accommodation. Documentation helps — a police accident report, hospital discharge paperwork, or similar documentation supports the request.

## When a Missed Flight Is Not a No-Show

If a Delta flight delay caused you to miss a connection — for example, your Atlanta to New York flight was delayed and you missed your New York to London connection — this is not treated as a no-show. Delta is responsible for the disruption and rebooks you on the next available flight to your final destination at no cost.

Tell the gate agent or call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** immediately and explain: "My [flight number] was delayed and I missed my connection to [destination]. I need to be rebooked."

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do you get an eCredit if you no-show a Delta flight?**
No. A no-show results in full forfeiture of ticket value for non-refundable fares. No eCredit is issued.

**What is the difference between a no-show and a pre-departure cancellation?**
Pre-departure cancellation preserves eCredit value. No-show after departure results in complete forfeiture and automatic cancellation of all remaining segments.

**Is there any way to get an eCredit after a no-show on Delta?**
In rare goodwill cases for genuine unforeseeable emergencies. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** immediately and explain honestly.

**Does Delta cancel my return flight if I no-show the outbound?**
Yes. All subsequent segments are automatically cancelled. Call immediately to protect remaining bookings.

**What if a Delta delay caused me to miss my connection?**
Not a no-show — Delta is responsible. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for free rebooking.


**What is the difference between a no-show and a pre-departure cancellation on Delta?**
A pre-departure cancellation (even one minute before the flight) preserves your ticket value as an eCredit (minus any cancellation fee for Basic). A no-show after the flight departs results in complete forfeiture — no eCredit, no refund, and automatic cancellation of all remaining itinerary segments.

**What happens if a Delta delay caused me to miss my connection — is that a no-show?**
No. If a Delta flight delay caused you to miss your connection, Delta is responsible and rebooks you on the next available flight at no cost. This is not treated as a no-show. Tell the gate agent or call +1-(866)-332-0756 and explain that a Delta delay caused the missed connection.
