# Delta First Class Upgrade 2026: Complimentary, Paid & Miles Options

Getting upgraded to Delta First Class is one of the most sought-after perks in domestic air travel. Whether you are a Medallion elite member waiting for a complimentary upgrade to clear or a general member willing to pay for the premium seat, there are multiple paths available in 2026. Understanding how each method works and when upgrades are most likely to be available helps you board in the front cabin more often. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** if you need help with an upgrade request or if an option is not appearing for your booking.

## Complimentary Upgrades for Medallion Members

Delta's complimentary upgrade program gives Medallion elite members priority access to First Class and Comfort+ seats when they become available on eligible flights. This is the most valuable upgrade path since it costs nothing beyond the original economy fare.

**How it works**: Medallion members are automatically added to the complimentary upgrade waitlist for eligible domestic and short-haul international flights when they book a qualifying fare (Classic or Extra — not Basic). The list clears based on Medallion status tier, with Diamond members receiving the highest priority.

**Clearance order**: Diamond Medallion clears first, followed by Platinum, Gold, and Silver. Within the same tier, passengers are ranked by check-in order, fare class, and SkyMiles account tenure. Checking in the moment the 24-hour window opens gives you the best position within your tier.

**When upgrades clear**: For Diamond members, upgrades can clear as early as 24 hours before departure. For lower tiers, clearance often happens closer to departure or at the gate. Upgrades that do not clear before boarding may still clear at the gate if First Class seats remain open after confirmed passengers have boarded.

**Which flights qualify**: Complimentary upgrades apply to Delta and Delta Connection-operated domestic flights and certain short-haul international routes. Long-haul international flights to Europe, Asia, and other distant destinations use a separate Global Upgrade Certificate system for Medallion benefits rather than complimentary upgrades.

## Paid Upgrades Using Cash

Non-Medallion passengers and Medallion members who want guaranteed upgrade confirmation rather than waiting for complimentary clearance can purchase upgrades with cash.

Delta surfaces paid upgrade offers through My Trips on delta.com and the Fly Delta app in the days leading up to departure. The price varies based on route length, flight demand, and how far in advance the offer appears.

To access paid upgrade offers: log in to delta.com, go to My Trips, select your upcoming flight, and check for any upgrade or bid offers. The system will show the available seat (Comfort+ or First Class) and the cash price. Confirm the purchase and your seat is confirmed immediately.

Not all flights or fare types display upgrade offers. If you do not see an offer for a flight you want to upgrade, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to ask whether any upgrade inventory is available for purchase.

## Upgrade with Miles

SkyMiles members can use miles to upgrade from Main Cabin to First Class on eligible flights through the Upgrade with Miles feature.

To use this feature: log in to delta.com, go to My Trips, and look for the Upgrade with Miles option on your upcoming flight. If upgrade space is available and your fare is eligible, the system shows the mileage required. Select the upgrade and the miles are deducted from your account at confirmation.

The mileage cost varies by route and availability. Shorter domestic routes typically cost fewer miles than longer routes. The Upgrade with Miles feature is available to Delta SkyMiles American Express cardholders and other eligible SkyMiles members on Delta-operated flights.

## Global and Regional Upgrade Certificates for Diamond Members

Diamond Medallion members who select Upgrade Certificates as their Choice Benefit receive Global Upgrade Certificates (for Delta One on international routes) or Regional Upgrade Certificates (for domestic First Class). These certificates confirm an upgrade at booking rather than entering a waitlist, making them significantly more valuable than standard complimentary upgrades.

Certificates are valid for the Medallion year associated with the Choice Benefit selection. Travel must be completed before the certificate expires.

## Basic Fares and Upgrade Ineligibility

Delta Main Basic and all other Basic experience fares — Basic Business, Delta First Basic, Delta Premium Select Basic — are not eligible for paid or complimentary upgrades. Purchasing a Basic fare to save money and then upgrading separately is not possible.

To be upgrade-eligible, you must hold a Classic or Extra fare in the cabin you booked. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to confirm your fare's upgrade eligibility before purchasing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do Medallion members get a free Delta First Class upgrade?**
By automatically entering the complimentary upgrade waitlist when booking a qualifying fare. Upgrades clear based on status tier starting up to 24 hours before departure.

**Can non-Medallion passengers upgrade to Delta First Class?**
Yes, through cash paid upgrades in My Trips or through Upgrade with Miles for eligible SkyMiles members.

**How do I use SkyMiles to upgrade to Delta First Class?**
Log in to My Trips on delta.com and look for the Upgrade with Miles option. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** if it is not appearing.

**Do Basic fares qualify for upgrades?**
No. Basic experience fares are not eligible for paid or complimentary upgrades.

**When do complimentary upgrades clear for Medallion members?**
Starting up to 24 hours before departure for Diamond, progressively closer to departure for lower tiers, with gate clearance possible on the day of travel.


**Do Basic fares qualify for Delta First Class upgrades?**
No. Delta Main Basic and all other Basic experience fares are not eligible for paid or complimentary upgrades. Classic and Extra fares are upgrade-eligible.
