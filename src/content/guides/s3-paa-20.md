# How do I check my Delta eCredit balance? — 2026 Answer

Log in to your SkyMiles account at delta.com, go to My Wallet, and check Credits and Certificates. Each active eCredit shows its value and expiration date. On the Fly Delta app go to Account then My Wallet then Certificates and eCredits. If you booked as a guest, use delta.com/redeem with your 13-digit ticket number starting with 006. Call +1-(855)-321-3528 if the credit is not appearing.
