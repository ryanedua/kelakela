# Delta Main Basic Cancellation Policy 2026

Delta Main Basic is the airline's most restrictive fare type, and cancellation rules reflect that. If you bought a Main Basic ticket hoping to cancel later for a full refund, you will not get one. Understanding exactly what happens when you cancel, when the cancellation fee applies, and how to avoid losing the full ticket value can save you a significant amount of money. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** if you need to cancel and want to know what you will receive before you proceed.

## What Is Delta Main Basic?

Delta Main Basic is the entry-level economy fare, formerly known as Basic Economy. It offers the lowest price in the Main Cabin but comes with strict restrictions on changes, cancellations, seat selection, baggage, and SkyMiles earning. As of 2026, Delta has extended the Basic tier to Comfort+, First Class, Premium Select, and Delta One as well, but the cancellation rules for all Basic fares follow the same general framework.

Main Basic passengers are not eligible for priority boarding, Sky Club access, mileage accrual toward Medallion Status, paid or complimentary upgrades, preferred seats, or Same-Day Confirmed or Same-Day Standby changes.

## The 24-Hour Risk-Free Cancellation Window

The one full refund opportunity available on a Main Basic ticket is the 24-hour risk-free cancellation window. If you cancel within 24 hours of booking and your travel does not begin within that window, you receive a complete refund to your original payment method with no cancellation fee. This applies regardless of fare type, including Main Basic.

This window is your only chance at a full cash refund on a Main Basic ticket purchased directly from Delta. Once 24 hours pass, the cancellation fee structure takes over. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** immediately if you realize you need to cancel shortly after booking.

## What Happens When You Cancel After the 24-Hour Window

After the risk-free period expires, Delta Main Basic cancellation works as follows for tickets originating in the United States and Canada.

You may cancel your ticket before the scheduled departure. Delta will issue you an eCredit for the remaining value of the ticket after deducting the applicable cancellation fee. The eCredit is tied to your SkyMiles account and can be applied toward a future Delta booking.

If you do not cancel before departure, the entire ticket value is forfeited. Delta treats this as a no-show and issues no eCredit and no refund of any kind. Cancelling before the flight departs, even at the last minute, is always better than not cancelling at all.

## Cancellation Fees by Region

The cancellation charge on a Delta Main Basic ticket varies depending on where your travel originates.

For travel originating in the United States and Canada, the cancellation fee is deducted from the ticket value before the eCredit is issued. The amount is not a fixed flat fee but is built into the fare rules of your specific ticket.

For travel originating in South America, the cancellation fee is $330 for tickets issued on or after June 26, 2025, and $199 for tickets issued before that date. Ticket changes are not permitted on South American routes regardless of when the ticket was purchased.

For travel originating in the Caribbean to the US, Canada, Mexico, or Central America, the cancellation fee is $99.

For travel originating in Asia and the Pacific, the fee is $199 for tickets issued on or after September 26, 2024. No change or cancellation fees apply for tickets issued before that date.

For travel originating in Korea, the refund is returned to the original payment method after deducting 450,000 KRW. For Indonesia, the fee is 4,000,000 IDR.

To confirm the exact fee that applies to your specific ticket, call **+1-(855)-321-3528** and have your booking confirmation ready.

## What You Receive: eCredit, Not Cash

When you cancel a Main Basic ticket and a residual value remains after the fee, Delta issues you an eCredit. This is not a cash refund. The eCredit is stored in your SkyMiles wallet and is valid for one year from the original ticket purchase date, not from the date of cancellation.

The eCredit can be applied toward any future Delta flight booking for the same passenger. It is non-transferable, meaning you cannot use your eCredit to pay for another person's ticket. If you book a cheaper flight than the eCredit value, the remaining balance is reissued as a new eCredit with the same original expiration date.

If you need the credit applied to a booking and are having trouble with the online process, call **+1-(844)-833-1535** to complete the redemption by phone.

## Can You Change a Main Basic Ticket Instead of Cancelling?

For most routes, the answer is no. Ticket changes are not permitted on Main Basic fares after the 24-hour risk-free window. The option you have is to cancel for an eCredit and then use that credit to purchase a new ticket on a different date or routing.

For tickets issued before November 6, 2025, a waiver code was required to apply the unused ticket value toward a new booking. For tickets issued on or after November 6, 2025, this process is handled automatically through updated fare rules in the booking system.

## What If Delta Cancels Your Flight?

The Main Basic cancellation restrictions only apply to voluntary changes initiated by the passenger. If Delta cancels your flight for any reason, those restrictions do not apply.

When Delta cancels a flight, every passenger regardless of fare type is entitled to free rebooking on the next available Delta flight, or a full cash refund to the original payment method under DOT rules. The cancellation fee and eCredit-only rule are waived entirely. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** after a Delta cancellation to choose between rebooking and a cash refund.

## How to Cancel a Delta Main Basic Ticket

The simplest way to cancel is through the Fly Delta app or delta.com. Log in to My Trips, select your booking, and follow the cancellation prompts. The system will display whether your booking qualifies for an eCredit and how much will remain after the cancellation fee.

If the online cancellation is not working or your ticket was purchased through a third-party platform, call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to cancel by phone. Third-party bookings may require contacting the original seller rather than Delta directly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I cancel a Delta Main Basic ticket for a refund?**
No cash refund is available after the 24-hour risk-free window. You may cancel before departure to receive an eCredit minus the cancellation fee. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to process the cancellation and confirm the fee amount.

**What is the Delta Main Basic cancellation fee in 2026?**
The fee varies by route. For South America originations it is $330 for tickets issued on or after June 26, 2025. For Caribbean routes it is typically $99. For Asia-Pacific routes it is $199 for tickets issued after September 26, 2024. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528** for the exact fee on your booking.

**What happens if I no-show on a Delta Main Basic ticket?**
If you fail to cancel before departure, the full ticket value is forfeited with no eCredit issued. Always cancel before the flight departs to preserve residual value.

**Can Delta Main Basic tickets be changed instead of cancelled?**
Changes are not allowed after the 24-hour window for most routes. Cancel for an eCredit, then apply that credit to a new booking. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to explore your options.

**Does the cancellation fee apply if Delta cancels my flight?**
No. If Delta cancels your flight, you are entitled to free rebooking or a full cash refund regardless of fare type. The Main Basic restrictions apply only to voluntary passenger changes.


**Does the Delta Main Basic cancellation fee apply if Delta cancels my flight?**
No. If Delta cancels your flight, the Basic Economy restriction does not apply. You are entitled to free rebooking or a full cash refund under DOT rules regardless of fare type.
