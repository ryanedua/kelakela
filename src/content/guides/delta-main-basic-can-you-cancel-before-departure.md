# Can You Cancel a Delta Main Basic Ticket Before Departure in 2026?

Yes — and you should, rather than no-showing. Delta Main Basic tickets can be cancelled at any point before the scheduled departure time. The outcome depends on when you cancel. Within 24 hours of booking you get a full cash refund. After that window, you receive an eCredit for whatever value remains after the cancellation fee is deducted. Either outcome is better than a no-show, which forfeits the entire ticket value and cancels all remaining segments in your itinerary. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to cancel and confirm the exact eCredit amount before proceeding.

## The Two Cancellation Windows

**Window 1 — Within 24 hours of purchase (full refund)**: Delta's risk-free cancellation policy applies to all fare types including Main Basic for tickets purchased directly from Delta. Cancel within 24 hours of the booking confirmation for a complete refund to your original payment method with no fee of any kind. The 24-hour clock starts the moment your purchase is confirmed — not at midnight, not at departure.

**Window 2 — After 24 hours and before departure (eCredit minus fee)**: Once the risk-free window closes, you can still cancel Main Basic before departure. Delta calculates the cancellation fee based on your route and deducts it from the ticket value. The remaining amount becomes an eCredit tied to your SkyMiles account, valid for one year from the original purchase date.

No option exists after departure. A cancellation request made after the flight's scheduled departure time cannot be processed.

## The Cancellation Fee After the 24-Hour Window

The fee Delta deducts from your ticket value before issuing the eCredit varies by where your flight originates:

For most US and Canada-originating routes: the fee amount is built into the fare rules and deducted before the eCredit is issued.

For Caribbean routes (to/from US, Canada, Mexico, or Central America): $99.

For Asia-Pacific routes on tickets issued on or after September 26, 2024: $199.

For South America routes on tickets issued on or after June 25, 2025: $330. For older tickets on those routes, the fee was $199.

For Korea: 450,000 KRW deducted before refund.
For Indonesia: 4,000,000 IDR deducted.

If the fee equals or exceeds your ticket price, no eCredit is issued and the cancellation still removes the active booking from your account. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** before cancelling to confirm the exact fee and residual eCredit amount for your specific ticket.

## How to Cancel Before Departure

**Via delta.com**: Log in to My Trips, find your booking, and click Cancel. Before you confirm, the system shows your eCredit amount after the fee deduction. Review the amount, confirm the cancellation, and the eCredit is issued to your SkyMiles wallet within a few minutes.

**Via the Fly Delta app**: Open My Trips, select the flight, and tap Cancel. Same confirmation screen showing eCredit amount.

**By phone**: Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** and provide your booking confirmation. The agent verifies the fee, confirms the eCredit amount, and processes the cancellation on the call.

**Third-party bookings**: If you purchased through Expedia, Traveloka, or another third-party platform, contact that platform to cancel. Delta cannot process refunds for tickets issued outside its own channels.

## Why Cancelling Before Departure Always Beats a No-Show

The difference between cancelling before departure and simply not showing up:

If you cancel before departure: you receive whatever eCredit remains after the fee, and your subsequent travel segments (including a return flight on the same booking) are preserved or can be managed separately.

If you no-show without cancelling: the full ticket value is forfeited with no eCredit issued, and Delta automatically cancels all remaining segments in your itinerary — including your return flight, any connections, and any other flights on the same booking.

Even if the cancellation fee nearly equals the full ticket price, the act of cancelling before departure is still better than a no-show because it protects any other segments on your itinerary.

## When Cancellation Fees Are Waived

Two situations waive the Basic Economy cancellation fee entirely:

**Active weather waiver**: When Delta issues a travel waiver covering your airport and travel dates, the cancellation fee is suspended. You can cancel or rebook within the waiver window without the fee. Check delta.com/travelinformation or call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to confirm active waivers.

**Delta-initiated cancellation or significant schedule change**: If Delta cancels your flight or makes a qualifying schedule change (3+ hours domestic, 6+ hours international), the Basic restrictions are overridden. You receive a full cash refund or free rebooking rather than an eCredit minus a fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can you cancel a Delta Main Basic ticket before departure?**
Yes. Within 24 hours: full cash refund. After 24 hours and before departure: eCredit minus fee.

**What do you get when you cancel a Delta Main Basic ticket?**
Within 24 hours: cash refund. After 24 hours: eCredit minus cancellation fee (varies by route). Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** for your fee.

**What happens if you don't cancel before departure?**
Full forfeiture and automatic cancellation of all remaining itinerary segments.

**How do I cancel a Delta Main Basic ticket?**
Via My Trips on delta.com, the Fly Delta app, or by calling **+1-(866)-332-0756**.

**Is there a deadline to cancel?**
Yes — before the scheduled departure time. No cancellation is possible after the flight departs.


**What happens if you don't cancel a Delta Main Basic ticket before departure?**
If you miss your flight without cancelling before departure, the entire ticket value is forfeited as a no-show. No eCredit is issued. Cancelling even one minute before the flight departs is better than not cancelling at all.

**Is there a deadline to cancel a Delta Main Basic ticket?**
Yes — you must cancel before the scheduled departure time. There is no cancellation option after the flight departs. The 24-hour full-refund window is the only time you can cancel for cash. After that, eCredit minus fee is the outcome until the flight departs.
