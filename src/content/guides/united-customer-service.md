# United Customer Service Guide

United Airlines customer service can be reached by phone at +1-(855)-235-1859, chat, app, and online forms. This guide explains how to contact support for booking, cancellations, refunds, baggage, and urgent issues.

## How to Contact United Customer Service

United Airlines customer service is available 24 hours a day. The right channel depends on what you need — most standard changes and cancellations are fastest through My Trips on united.com or the United app, while urgent issues and cash refund requests require a phone call.

| Channel | Best For | Response |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Phone +1-(855)-235-1859 | Urgent issues, refunds, escalation | Immediate + wait |
| My Trips / United app | Changes, cancellations, standby, status | Under 2 min |
| Agent on Demand | Day-of-travel digital assistance at select airports | Real-time |
| App messaging | Non-urgent questions, written record | 15–30 min |
| Airport gate agent | Same-day rebooking, standby | Immediate |
| united.com/refunds | Written refund submission | 7 business days |
| Customer Care (united.com) | Complaints, reimbursements | Varies |

### Phone Support Numbers

**Main customer service**: **+1-(855)-235-1859)** — reservations, flight changes, cancellations, refunds, baggage follow-up, SkyMiles, and escalations. Available 24/7. Press **0 immediately** when the automated menu begins.

Accept the callback option when the queue is long — it holds your position without keeping you on hold. Answer immediately when the callback arrives.

### Live Chat and Mobile App

The United app offers two contact modes beyond self-service:

**Agent on Demand**: available at select United airports and through the app. Connects to a live United agent via video or chat interface for real-time day-of-travel assistance. Fastest at airports where the feature is active.

**App messaging**: United app → Help → messaging. A live United agent — not a chatbot — responds in 15 to 30 minutes with full booking system access. Best for non-urgent questions and situations where a written record is useful.

## Speak With a Live United Representative

**By phone**: call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** and press 0 immediately when the IVR begins. Say "agent" if 0 does not work. Accept the callback option if the queue is long.

**At the airport**: go to the departure gate of the flight you want to board — not the check-in counter, not your original gate. The gate agent has direct authority over that specific flight's standby list and boarding decisions. During mass cancellations when phone queues exceed 45 minutes, gate agents continue processing passengers in person.

**By app**: United app → Help → Agent on Demand (where available) or messaging (15 to 30 minutes).

**What to say when connected**: your 6-character United confirmation code and the specific outcome you need — in the first sentence, before anything else.

- Rebooking: "My confirmation is ABC123. United cancelled my flight. Rebook me on UA 456 at 5 PM today."
- Cash refund: "My confirmation is ABC123. United cancelled my flight. I want a full cash refund — not a travel credit."
- Flight change: "My confirmation is ABC123. Change my July 25 Chicago to LA from 3 PM to the 7 PM departure."

Specific requests process significantly faster than open-ended problem descriptions.

## Reservations and Booking Assistance

**New bookings**: united.com and the United app for standard routes — real-time fares, immediate confirmation, no queue. Call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** for complex multi-city itineraries, MileagePlus award bookings with partner airlines, or accessibility requirements at time of booking.

**Existing booking review**: My Trips on united.com shows the full itinerary, fare type, seat assignments, and any schedule updates without a call.

**Booking not found**: log in to confirm the booking is linked to the correct MileagePlus account, or use the guest lookup at united.com/trips with last name and confirmation code. Third-party bookings may not appear in My Trips — contact the original platform first for voluntary changes.

**Third-party reservations**: United can display a third-party booking when you have the United confirmation code, but voluntary changes and cancellations may require the issuing agency. Ask the United agent whether they have modification authority before assuming.

## Flight Changes and Same-Day Help

**Advance voluntary change (eligible fares)**: My Trips → Change Flight. No change fee — only a fare difference if the new flight costs more. Under 2 minutes, no queue.

**Basic Economy after 24 hours**: no changes available on any channel. Cancellation only — call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** to confirm the exact fee and credit amount before committing.

**Same-day standby**: United app → My Trips → Same-Day Change. Free for eligible passengers from the morning of travel. Position updates in real time in the app.

**Same-day confirmed change**: fee applies for non-status passengers. Waived for qualifying MileagePlus Premier members. Via app or **+1-(855)-235-1859)**.

**United-initiated significant schedule change** (3+ hours domestic, 6+ hours international, airport change, added connections, carrier change, or cabin downgrade): call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** before accepting the new schedule. You are entitled to free rebooking or a full cash refund under DOT rules.

**Travel waiver not applying online**: call **+1-(855)-235-1859)**. The agent applies the waiver code manually — this cannot be resolved through My Trips.

**Agent on Demand at the airport**: for same-day changes and disruption questions when physically at an eligible airport, Agent on Demand connects you to a live agent via the United app without joining the phone queue.

## Cancellation and Refund Support

**Voluntary cancellation (refundable fare)**: My Trips → Cancel. Full cash refund to original payment method.

**Voluntary cancellation (non-refundable fare)**: My Trips → Cancel. Travel credit issued — shows amount before confirming.

**Basic Economy after 24 hours**: My Trips → Cancel, but call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** first to confirm the exact cancellation fee and resulting credit.

**Within 24 hours of purchase (any fare)**: full cash refund, any channel. Clock starts at exact purchase moment.

**United-initiated cancellation — cash refund**: call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** and say before anything else: "United cancelled my flight [confirmation code]. I want a full cash refund to my original payment method — not a travel credit." Submit at **united.com/refunds** simultaneously as a written backup.

**Refund status follow-up**: check your card issuer first — they hold airline credits 3 to 5 days before posting. If nothing incoming after 7 business days for a credit card, call with your case reference number. If still unresolved: **transportation.gov/airconsumer**.

**Travel credit balance**: log in to your MileagePlus account on united.com. Credits, expiration dates, and applicable bookings are shown.

**Refund vs travel credit vs reimbursement**:
- **Refund**: return of ticket value after a qualifying event → united.com/refunds or phone
- **Travel credit**: value retained after voluntary cancellation of a non-refundable fare → MileagePlus account
- **Reimbursement**: out-of-pocket expenses during a controllable disruption → Customer Care at united.com with receipts

## Baggage Claim and Delayed Bags

**Delayed or missing bag**: go to the **United Baggage Service Office** in the baggage claim area before leaving the terminal. File a Property Irregularity Report. Receive your file reference number — required for all tracking and follow-up. Do not leave the airport without it.

**Online tracking**: united.com baggage tracker or United app with the file reference number. Set up notifications for automatic status updates.

**Phone follow-up**: call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** and say "baggage" when the IVR prompts. Have the file reference number ready.

**Damaged bag**: photograph exterior damage before touching the contents and before leaving the baggage carousel area. Report at the Baggage Service Office within 24 hours of domestic arrival and 7 days of international arrival. After these windows, claims are generally not accepted.

**Missing contents**: same reporting windows — 24 hours domestic, 7 days international. United's liability for electronics, jewelry, cash, and fragile items in checked bags is limited — check travel insurance or credit card baggage protection for these categories.

**Formally lost bag (5+ days)**: call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** to initiate a formal lost baggage claim. Liability limits: domestic limit under DOT rules, approximately $1,700 internationally under the Montreal Convention.

**Interim expenses during delay**: keep receipts for essential purchases. Submit at united.com Customer Care with the baggage file reference number.

## SkyMiles and Loyalty Program Support

**MileagePlus award bookings and changes**: call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** and identify it as a MileagePlus award inquiry. Partner airline award changes may require phone processing.

**Premier status progress**: log in to united.com and check your MileagePlus account. Current Premier Qualifying Points (PQP) and Premier Qualifying Flights (PQF) toward each Premier tier are displayed. Basic Economy fares earn reduced or no status credit — book standard or higher fare classes to accrue Premier status.

**Travel credit balance**: MileagePlus account on united.com shows all active credits, expiration dates, and eligible bookings.

**Premier status identification**: state your Premier tier in the first sentence when calling — "I am a Premier 1K member, my confirmation is ABC123." This is a priority routing instruction that changes how the call is handled.

## International Customer Service Numbers

Local numbers by country at **united.com → Contact Us → select country**. United maintains dedicated lines for:

UK, Germany, Japan, Australia, Mexico, Brazil, China, and other major markets. Local numbers avoid international calling charges and may offer local language service.

**Dialing the US number internationally**: enter **+1-(855)-235-1859)** directly on any mobile — the + replaces the exit code automatically. US toll-free numbers are billed at international rates by local carriers.

**United app internationally**: works in any country with internet access at no additional charge for self-service, flight status, and messaging.

**Optimal call timing from international time zones**: Tuesday through Thursday, 5 to 7 AM Eastern — 11 AM to 1 PM Central European Time, 6 PM to 8 PM Japan Standard Time, 7 PM to 9 PM Australia Eastern Standard Time.

**Partner-operated flights**: United codeshares with Star Alliance partner airlines on many international routes. Changes to partner-operated segments may require partner airline coordination — ask the United agent which applies to your specific segment.

## Faster Response Tips

**Call during off-peak windows**: Tuesday through Thursday, 5 to 7 AM Eastern — typically under 5 minutes to a live agent. Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons.

**Press 0 immediately**: the moment the IVR recording begins — do not wait for the menu. Pressing 0 during the opening message bypasses the full IVR tree on most configurations.

**Accept the callback**: holds your queue position without holding. Answer immediately when it arrives — missed callbacks return to the end of the queue.

**Use My Trips first**: if the action is available in My Trips, it is faster than calling. Standard changes and cancellations complete in under 2 minutes with no queue.

**State confirmation code and outcome first**: eliminates the diagnostic phase of every call. Lead with the 6-character code and the exact result you want.

**Do not open duplicate cases**: follow up using the original case reference number. Multiple submissions create conflicting records and slow resolution.

**Use Agent on Demand at the airport**: where available, bypasses the general phone queue for day-of-travel situations.

**Go to the gate during disruptions**: gate agents at active departures process standby and boarding in real time while phone queues are at their longest.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the United Airlines customer service phone number?**
The main United Airlines customer service number is +1-(855)-235-1859, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Press 0 immediately when the automated menu begins. Find local international numbers at united.com under Contact Us.

**How do I speak to a live person at United Airlines?**
Call +1-(855)-235-1859 and press 0 immediately when the IVR starts. Say 'agent' if 0 does not work. At the airport, go to the departure gate of the flight you want to board. The United app offers Agent on Demand at select airports and messaging for live agent assistance.

**How do I get a refund from United Airlines?**
Call +1-(855)-235-1859 immediately after a United-initiated cancellation and say: 'I want a full cash refund to my original payment method — not a travel credit.' Submit a written backup at united.com/refunds simultaneously.

**What should I do if United cancels my flight?**
Open the United app and check My Trips for automatic rebooking. If it works, confirm it. If not, call +1-(855)-235-1859 immediately and go to the airport gate if you are there. You are entitled to free rebooking or a full cash refund under DOT rules.

**How do I report a delayed or missing bag to United?**
Go to the United Baggage Service Office in the baggage claim area before leaving the terminal. File a Property Irregularity Report and get your file reference number. Online tracking: united.com baggage tracker. Phone: +1-(855)-235-1859, say 'baggage.'

**What is the best time to call United customer service?**
Tuesday through Thursday, 5 to 7 AM Eastern — typically under 5 minutes to a live agent. Avoid Monday mornings, Friday afternoons, and the first two hours after any major disruption announcement.

**How do I contact United Airlines from outside the US?**
Go to united.com then Contact Us then select your country for a local number. Or dial +1-(855)-235-1859 using your country's international exit code. The United app works internationally at no additional charge.
