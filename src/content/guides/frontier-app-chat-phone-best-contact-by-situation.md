# Frontier App, Chat, or Phone: The Best Contact Method for Every Situation

Use the Frontier app for self-service tasks with no hold time. Use phone at +1-(866)-227-2948 for urgent issues, cash refunds, and complex changes. Use chat for non-urgent questions with a 15 to 30 minute response window. Use the airport gate agent for same-day emergencies. The right channel depends entirely on how urgent your situation is and what authority you need.

Frontier offers four main contact channels: the phone line, the Frontier app (self-service and chat), the airport desk or gate agent, and social media. Each one is the best option in specific situations and the wrong choice in others. Here is the complete situational guide. Call **+1-(866)-227-2948)** when you need an immediate human decision.

## By Situation: The Complete Guide

### Flight Cancellation — Same Day

**Best**: Phone **+1-(866)-227-2948)** + Frontier app simultaneously. Check the app for automatic rebooking first — if acceptable, accept it without calling. If not, call immediately while at the airport gate if possible.

**Why not chat**: Chat response times of 15 to 30 minutes are too slow when you need rebooking in the next hour.

---

### Voluntary Flight Change — Not Urgent

**Best**: Frontier app or flyfrontier.com My Trips → Change Flight. Under 2 minutes, no hold time.

**Call instead when**: Basic Economy, award tickets, multi-city bookings, or when the app does not show a Change option.

---

### Cash Refund After Frontier Cancellation

**Best**: Phone **+1-(866)-227-2948)**. Say immediately: "Frontier cancelled my flight. I want a full cash refund to my original payment method." Submit at flyfrontier.com/refunds simultaneously as a written backup.

**Why not app or chat**: Cash refunds after qualifying Frontier-initiated cancellations are processed by live agents with authority to approve DOT-mandated refunds.

---

### eCredit Balance Check or Application

**Best**: flyfrontier.com/miles for lookup, then apply at checkout → Payment → Add a Certificate, eCredit or gift card. No call needed.

**Call when**: The credit is not appearing at flyfrontier.com/miles or is not applying at checkout. Call **+1-(866)-228-6201)**.

---

### Same-Day Standby Request

**Best**: Frontier app → My Trips → Same-Day Change → Standby. Immediate, real-time list position visible.

**Also**: airport gate agent or call **+1-(866)-227-2948)** if the app option is not visible.

---

### Missed Connection Due to Frontier Delay

**Best**: Gate agent at the connection airport — immediately. Simultaneously call **+1-(866)-227-2948)** if the gate agent line is long.

**Why not app**: The app cannot resolve a missed connection in real time with the human judgment and system overrides available to gate agents.

---

### Policy Question or Non-Urgent Inquiry

**Best**: Frontier app chat or flyfrontier.com. Response time 15 to 30 minutes via chat is acceptable for questions with no same-day deadline.

---

### Complaint or Compensation Request

**Best**: flyfrontier.com/contact-us for written record. @Frontier on X for faster public escalation. Phone for immediate escalation to a supervisor.

---

### Supervisor Escalation

**Best**: Phone **+1-(866)-227-2948)**. No other channel has a supervisor escalation path.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best way to contact Frontier for a flight change?**
Frontier app or flyfrontier.com for standard Classic and Extra changes. Call +1-(866)-227-2948 for changes the app cannot complete.

**What is the best way to contact Frontier for a cancellation?**
Frontier app for standard cancellations. Phone +1-(866)-227-2948 for cash refund requests after Frontier-initiated cancellations.

**What is the best way to contact Frontier during a flight disruption?**
Frontier app for automatic rebooking check, then phone +1-(866)-227-2948 and gate agent simultaneously if needed.

**Is Frontier chat or phone better for an eCredit question?**
Self-service at flyfrontier.com/miles or checkout is fastest. Call +1-(866)-228-6201 if the credit is not working online.

**When is the airport desk better than calling Frontier?**
For same-day travel emergencies — cancelled flight, missed connection, standby — the gate agent at your departure has direct system access and no queue.

## Quick Reference

- Urgent: phone **+1-(866)-227-2948)** — press 0 immediately
- Self-service: Frontier app or flyfrontier.com
- Same-day airport: gate agent at your departure gate
- eCredit follow-up: **+1-(866)-228-6201)**
- Escalation: phone supervisor or @Frontier on X
