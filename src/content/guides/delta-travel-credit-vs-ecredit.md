# Delta Travel Credit vs eCredit: Key Differences Explained (2026)

Delta uses the terms eCredit and travel credit, and they are not the same thing. Both show up in your SkyMiles wallet, both can be used toward future flights, but they originate differently, expire under different rules, and in some cases have different usage restrictions. Knowing which type you have and what the specific terms are can prevent you from losing money to an unexpected expiration or usage rule. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to confirm exactly what type of credit you have and when it expires.

## What Is a Delta eCredit?

A Delta eCredit is a digital flight credit issued when you cancel a non-refundable Delta ticket. It represents the unused value of your original ticket, minus any applicable cancellation fee for Basic fares, and is stored in your SkyMiles wallet.

Key characteristics of a Delta eCredit:

It is valid for one year from the original ticket purchase date, not the cancellation date. A ticket purchased in February 2025 that was cancelled in August 2025 still expires in February 2026.

It is non-transferable. Only the original ticketed passenger can use it to book a new flight.

It can be applied at checkout on delta.com or delta.com/redeem. You only need to book by the expiration date — travel can occur after.

If you partially use an eCredit, the remaining balance is reissued as a new eCredit with the same original expiration date. The clock does not reset.

## What Is a Delta Travel Credit?

A Delta travel credit is a broader category that can come from several sources other than ticket cancellations. Common sources include compensation issued after a flight disruption such as a delay or cancellation, promotional offers from Delta or Delta American Express partnerships, goodwill credits issued by agents for service issues, and in some cases credits from refunded ancillary fees.

Travel credits may have different validity periods than eCredits. Some promotional credits have shorter windows of 6 months or less. Compensation credits may have validity tied to the date of issuance rather than a purchase date. The specific terms depend on why and how the credit was issued.

Usage restrictions also vary. Some travel credits are fully flexible and can be used like eCredits by any passenger on a booking. Others have passenger-specific restrictions similar to eCredits.

## How to Tell Which Type You Have

Log in to your SkyMiles account at delta.com and navigate to My Wallet, then Credits and Certificates. Each credit is listed individually with its type, value, and expiration date. Look at the description next to each credit — it will typically indicate whether it is an eCredit from a cancelled ticket, a promotional credit, or a compensation credit.

If the type is unclear or you want to confirm usage rules, call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** and give the agent your SkyMiles account number. They can look up each credit, confirm its type, expiration, and any usage restrictions.

## Where Each Type Can Be Used

Both eCredits and travel credits can generally be applied during checkout at delta.com in the payment section. In most cases you can combine them with a credit card for any balance remaining.

eCredits are limited to Delta and Delta Connection carrier-operated and ticketed flights. They cannot be used on partner airline bookings.

Travel credit usage depends on the specific credit terms. Most apply to Delta flights. Some promotional credits may have destination or fare class restrictions. Check the fine print in My Wallet or ask an agent.

## Can You Combine Them on One Booking?

In most cases yes. Delta's checkout system allows multiple credits to be applied to a single booking. If you have both an eCredit and a travel credit, you can stack them to cover more of the purchase price.

If the online checkout does not allow combining them — which can happen when credits have different passenger associations or special restrictions — call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and an agent can apply multiple credits manually during a phone booking.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between a Delta eCredit and a travel credit?**
An eCredit comes from a cancelled non-refundable ticket and expires 1 year from purchase date. A travel credit can come from promotions, compensation, or other sources and may have different validity terms. Check My Wallet to see which type you have.

**Can I use a Delta travel credit or eCredit for someone else's ticket?**
eCredits are non-transferable. Travel credit transferability depends on the specific credit terms. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** to confirm.

**Where can I see my Delta credits and their expiration dates?**
Log in to delta.com, go to My Wallet, then Credits and Certificates.

**Can I combine a Delta eCredit and a travel credit on the same booking?**
Yes in most cases. If the online checkout does not allow it, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to have an agent apply both manually.

**Do Delta travel credits expire?**
eCredits expire 1 year from the original purchase date. Travel credits may have different expiration terms depending on the source. Always check in My Wallet.
