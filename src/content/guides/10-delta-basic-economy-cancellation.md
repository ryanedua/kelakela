# Delta Basic Economy Cancellation: What Happens & Your Options

Delta's Main Basic fare is designed to be the lowest-cost option in the economy cabin, but that low price comes with trade-offs — and one of the biggest is limited flexibility when it comes to cancellation. If you are holding a Main Basic ticket and your plans have changed, here is exactly what happens when you cancel and what options remain available to you.

## What Is Delta Main Basic?

Main Basic is Delta's entry-level economy fare. It offers the essential flying experience — a seat on the plane, carry-on and personal item allowance, and in-flight amenities — at the lowest price point within economy. In exchange, Main Basic passengers accept restrictions on:

- Advance seat selection
- Same-day confirmed changes
- Refund and cancellation flexibility

Understanding these restrictions before booking is essential, and if you are already holding a Main Basic ticket and need to cancel, this guide explains your path forward.

## What Happens When You Cancel a Delta Main Basic Ticket

### Voluntary Cancellation: eCredit, Not Cash

If you decide to cancel a Main Basic ticket voluntarily — meaning Delta has not changed or cancelled the flight — the result is an **eCredit** for the unused value of your ticket. You do not receive a cash refund.

The eCredit:
- Equals the unused ticket value (generally the full fare minus any service fee, though Delta has waived these fees in recent years)
- Is issued to the email address associated with your booking or SkyMiles account
- Expires **one year from the original ticket purchase date**
- Can only be used by the passenger named on the original ticket
- Can be applied toward future Delta flights at checkout on delta.com or the Fly Delta app

### The 24-Hour Exception: Full Cash Refund

There is one important exception to the eCredit-only rule: if you cancel within **24 hours of the original booking** and the flight departs at least **7 days from your booking date**, you are entitled to a full cash refund. This 24-hour risk-free cancellation rule applies to all Delta fare classes, including Main Basic.

This is the most important window to be aware of. If you booked a Main Basic ticket and have any uncertainty about your travel plans, cancel within 24 hours to preserve your full cash back.

### When Delta Cancels or Significantly Changes Your Flight

If Delta cancels your Main Basic flight or makes a significant schedule change, the rules shift in your favor. Under DOT regulations, you are entitled to a **full cash refund** regardless of fare type. This right applies even to Main Basic tickets, which are otherwise non-refundable.

In this situation:
- Do not automatically accept the rebooking Delta may offer
- Go to **My Trips** on delta.com and look for the refund option
- Or call Delta at **++1-866-332-0756** to explicitly request a cash refund

## Can You Change a Delta Main Basic Ticket?

The short answer is no — not in the traditional sense. Delta Main Basic does not allow voluntary flight changes on most domestic routes. If your plans change and you want to fly on a different date, your options are:

1. **Cancel the Main Basic ticket** and receive an eCredit
2. **Use the eCredit** to book a new flight with the dates that work for you (you may pay a fare difference if the new flight costs more)

This two-step process effectively gives you the outcome of a flight change, but without the ability to directly rebook the way you could with a Main Classic or higher fare.

## How to Cancel a Delta Main Basic Ticket

### Online via Delta.com

1. Go to delta.com and log in to your SkyMiles account.
2. Navigate to **My Trips**.
3. Select the Main Basic booking you want to cancel.
4. Click **Cancel Flight**.
5. Review the eCredit summary and confirm the cancellation.
6. Your eCredit will be emailed to the address on file.

### Via the Fly Delta App

Open the app, tap **My Trips**, select the flight, and tap the cancel option. The process mirrors the desktop version and issues the eCredit automatically.

### By Phone

Call Delta customer support at **++1-866-332-0756** if you prefer to speak with someone, especially if your situation involves a schedule change or a recent booking within the 24-hour window.

## Using Your eCredit After Canceling

Once you have canceled your Main Basic ticket and received an eCredit:

- Go to delta.com and search for your new flights as normal.
- At the payment step, select **Certificate, eCredit, or Gift Card**.
- Enter your eCredit certificate number.
- The credit is applied to the new booking. Pay any remaining balance with a card.

If the new ticket costs less than your eCredit value, the remaining balance stays on the eCredit for future use. If the new ticket costs more, you pay the difference.

## What If Your eCredit Expires Before You Can Use It?

Once a Delta eCredit expires, it is typically forfeited. Delta does not automatically extend credits. If your eCredit is close to expiration and you cannot book travel in time, contact Delta at **++1-866-332-0756** to ask whether any exceptions or extensions are available. While outcomes are not guaranteed, Delta has occasionally provided goodwill accommodations in exceptional circumstances.

## Tips for Main Basic Ticket Holders

- **Use the 24-hour window.** If you have any doubt about your trip right after booking, cancel immediately and rebook when your plans are confirmed.
- **Check for travel waivers.** If weather or another disruption affects your route, a travel waiver may let you rebook without any penalty, even on Main Basic.
- **Track your eCredit expiration.** Set a reminder on your phone for 30 days before the expiration date so you have time to book new travel.
- **Consider Main Classic next time.** If flexibility matters to your travel style, the modest price difference between Main Basic and Main Classic is worth it.

## Contact Delta Customer Support

For questions about canceling a Main Basic ticket, checking your eCredit, or requesting a refund after a Delta-initiated change, contact Delta Air Lines at **++1-866-332-0756** or visit the My Trips section at delta.com.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I cancel a Delta Main Basic ticket and get a refund?**

For voluntary cancellations, Delta Main Basic tickets receive an eCredit, not a cash refund. You can get a full cash refund if you cancel within 24 hours of booking (with departure at least 7 days out), or if Delta cancels or significantly changes your flight.

**How do I cancel a Delta Main Basic fare?**

Log in to delta.com, go to 'My Trips,' select the Main Basic booking, and choose 'Cancel Flight.' Your eCredit will be issued to the email address associated with your booking or SkyMiles account.

**Does Delta Main Basic allow any changes before cancellation?**

Delta Main Basic does not allow standard flight changes on most domestic routes. If your plans change, your primary option is to cancel and receive an eCredit for the unused ticket value.

**How long does the Delta Main Basic eCredit last?**

The eCredit issued for a canceled Main Basic ticket is valid for one year from the date the original ticket was purchased. The expiration date is shown in your Delta wallet on delta.com.
