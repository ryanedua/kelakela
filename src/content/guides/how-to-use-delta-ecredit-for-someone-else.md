# Using a Delta eCredit for Someone Else: What's Possible in 2026

Delta eCredits cannot be transferred. That is the firm rule. But the practical question most passengers are actually asking — "can I use my credit to help book travel that includes someone else?" — has a more nuanced answer. If you are traveling on the same booking as the other person, yes, your credit can help pay for that trip. If you want to book a ticket entirely for someone else without being on it yourself, no, the credit cannot be used for that. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to set up a multi-passenger booking where your eCredit applies to your portion.

## The Non-Transferable Rule (What You Cannot Do)

Delta's eCredit policy is clear: eCredits cannot be transferred to another person. This means:

You cannot give your eCredit to your spouse, parent, child, or friend for them to use independently.

You cannot apply your eCredit to a booking that is solely in someone else's name.

You cannot sell your eCredit or use it as a gift.

The system enforces this at the payment step — it validates the passenger name against the credit holder. A booking with only a different passenger's name will be rejected.

## What You Can Do: Book for Yourself and a Companion

Here is the legitimate workaround that Delta explicitly allows. You can use your eCredit on a booking that includes both you and another passenger, as long as you are also traveling on the same flights.

Here is how it works in practice:

Your eCredit (in your name, $350 for example) can pay for your portion of a booking that includes you and your partner. Your partner's fare is paid separately by card. Both of you are on the same booking confirmation.

You are not transferring the credit — you are using it for your own ticket on a shared booking. The companion benefits indirectly because the trip is booked together and you both travel on the same flights.

**To do this online**: Go to delta.com/redeem, add your eCredit to the session, then search for flights with 2 passengers (or however many are traveling). Proceed through the booking for the full party. At checkout, your eCredit applies to your fare and the remainder is paid by card.

**If the online flow doesn't handle multi-passenger eCredit bookings correctly**: Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and have an agent complete the booking by phone. Agents can apply eCredits manually during a phone booking.

## Using a Child's eCredit for the Child's New Ticket

If you previously cancelled a ticket that was in your child's name, the resulting eCredit is associated with the child's name and original ticket number. This credit can be used to book a new ticket for the same child.

To apply a child's eCredit: go to delta.com/redeem and search by the child's original ticket number (13 digits starting with 006, found in the original booking confirmation email). Enter the child's name to validate the credit. Book a new flight that includes the child as a passenger.

If the child is part of a family booking, you as the adult booker can handle the transaction. The credit is still applied to the child's fare specifically.

If you encounter difficulty applying the credit online for a child's booking, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for phone assistance.

## Using Multiple eCredits on the Same Booking

For a multi-passenger booking where multiple passengers have individual eCredits, each credit must be applied to its corresponding passenger's fare. You cannot pool credits across passengers.

Example: You have a $300 eCredit and your travel partner has a $250 eCredit. You can each apply your own credit to your respective fare on the same booking. Your $300 applies to your ticket. Their $250 applies to their ticket. Neither credit can be used for the other person's fare.

Online checkout may limit the ability to apply multiple passenger-specific eCredits. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to have an agent handle a booking where multiple passengers each want to use their own individual eCredits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use my Delta eCredit to pay for someone else's ticket?**
Only if you are also on the same booking. You can apply your credit to your portion of a shared reservation. You cannot book a standalone ticket in someone else's name using your credit.

**Can I give or transfer my Delta eCredit to a family member?**
No. eCredits are non-transferable to any other person.

**Can I use a Delta eCredit to pay for my child's ticket?**
Yes, if the credit is in the child's name. Apply it via delta.com/redeem using the child's original ticket number. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** if the online process doesn't work.

**What if my partner has an eCredit and I want to use it for my ticket?**
Your partner can apply their credit to their own fare on a shared booking where you are both passengers.

**Can multiple eCredits from different passengers be pooled on one booking?**
No. Each credit applies only to the specific passenger it belongs to.


**Can I apply multiple eCredits from different passengers to one booking?**
No. Each eCredit is tied to a specific passenger. For a booking with two passengers, each passenger's eCredit can only be applied to their own fare. Passenger A's eCredit cannot pay for Passenger B's ticket.
