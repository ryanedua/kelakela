# Fastest Way to Reach a Live Agent Through Frontier Customer Service

Call +1-(866)-227-2948 and press 0 immediately when the IVR begins. Say agent if pressing 0 does not work. Call between 5 and 7 AM Eastern on weekdays for the shortest wait. At the airport for same-day emergencies, the gate agent is faster than the phone line. Elite members reach live agents significantly faster through priority routing.

## Skip the IVR Immediately

When you call **+1-(866)-227-2948)**, press 0 immediately when the IVR begins. If 0 does not connect you to an agent, say agent or representative clearly. If the system asks for your reason for calling, say cancel or change — these phrases often route faster than more specific descriptions. Repeat as needed until connected.

## If the Wait Is Long: Use the Callback

After bypassing the IVR, if the agent queue has a long wait, listen for the callback offer. Accepting it keeps your position in queue without requiring you to stay on hold. Answer immediately when the callback arrives — missed callbacks return to the end of the queue.

## Same-Day Emergencies: Go to the Gate Agent

If you are at the airport right now with an urgent issue, the gate agent at the flight you want to board is faster than the phone line during disruptions. Gate agents at an active departure have direct system access and can physically add you to standby for a flight boarding in 20 minutes — something a phone agent cannot do.

Go to the gate of your target flight, not the main check-in counter.

## Elite Members: Identify Your Tier Immediately

If you hold Silver, Gold, Platinum, or Diamond Elite status, say your tier immediately when the agent answers. Elite members are processed with higher priority. Diamond Elite members may have a dedicated number in their FRONTIER Miles account dashboard.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I get to a live agent on the Frontier customer service line fast?**
Call +1-(866)-227-2948 and press 0 immediately when the IVR begins. Say 'agent' if pressing 0 does not work. Repeat until connected.

**What is the fastest time of day to reach Frontier customer service?**
Tuesday through Thursday, 5 to 7 AM Eastern. Wait times are typically under 5 minutes during this window.

**If I am at the airport, is the phone or the gate agent faster?**
For same-day travel emergencies, the gate agent at your specific gate is almost always faster. Gate agents have direct system access with no phone queue.

**Does Frontier have a dedicated line for Elite members?**
Elite members receive priority routing on +1-(866)-227-2948. Diamond Elite members may have a dedicated number in their FRONTIER Miles account. Identify your status tier immediately when connected.

**What if I cannot reach a live Frontier agent?**
Try the secondary number +1-(866)-228-6201, use the Frontier app for self-service, or go to the airport gate agent for in-person resolution.
