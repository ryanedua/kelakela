# Can I Change My Delta Flight for Free? The 2026 Answer

Yes — for the vast majority of Delta tickets, you can change your flight without paying any standalone change fee. Delta permanently eliminated change fees for Main Cabin and above tickets originating from the US and Canada in 2020, and this policy remains fully in force in 2026. The only cost that might apply is a fare difference if the new flight you want is priced higher than what you originally paid. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to change your flight by phone or check the change options in My Trips on delta.com.

## Which Tickets Change for Free

**Main Classic**: Change any time before departure. No change fee. Only fare difference if the new flight is more expensive. If the new flight is cheaper, the difference becomes an eCredit.

**Main Extra**: Change any time before departure. No change fee. If the new flight is cheaper, the difference is refunded as cash since Extra is fully refundable.

**Comfort+, First Class, Premium Select, Delta One Classic and Extra**: Same no-change-fee rule applies across all these cabin classes for Classic and Extra tiers.

**Award tickets (non-Basic, US/Canada-originating)**: Change with no redeposit or change fee. Only the mileage difference applies.

## Which Tickets Cannot Be Changed for Free

**Main Basic (Basic Economy)**: After the 24-hour risk-free purchase window, changes are not permitted. The fee is not just waived for Basic — the change option itself does not exist. Cancel for an eCredit minus the cancellation fee and rebook separately.

**Same-day confirmed changes**: A $75 fee applies for non-Medallion and Silver Medallion passengers who want a confirmed seat on a different same-day departure. This fee is waived for Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion members.

## How to Change Your Delta Flight Online

Log in to delta.com, go to My Trips, select your booking, and click Change Flight. The system shows available alternatives and the fare difference for each. If the difference is zero or negative, the change costs nothing to complete. Confirm and receive an updated booking confirmation.

If the online tool is not working or your ticket involves a complex routing, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)**.

## Same-Day Changes: What They Cost

On the day of travel, two options exist for moving to an earlier or later same-day departure:

Same-Day Standby: Free. You join the waitlist for the earlier flight. No fee, no fare difference. No guarantee.

Same-Day Confirmed: $75 for non-Medallion and Silver Medallion passengers. Guaranteed seat. No fare difference when staying in the same cabin. Fee waived for Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change my Delta flight for free?**
Yes for Classic and Extra. No standalone change fee — only fare difference if the new flight costs more.

**What is the fare difference?**
The price gap between your original ticket and the new flight. Nothing else is charged.

**Is changing a Delta flight free on the day of travel?**
Same-Day Standby is free. Same-Day Confirmed costs $75 (waived for Gold/Platinum/Diamond Medallion).

**Can I change a Basic Economy Delta ticket for free?**
No after the 24-hour purchase window. Cancel for eCredit minus fee and rebook.

**How do I change online?**
My Trips on delta.com → select booking → Change Flight.


**What is the fare difference when changing a Delta flight?**
The fare difference is the price gap between your original ticket and the new flight you want. If the new flight costs the same or less, you pay nothing or receive a credit back. If it costs more, you pay the difference — no additional penalty.

**How do I change my Delta flight for free online?**
Log in to My Trips on delta.com, select your booking, click Change Flight, pick your new flight, and confirm. If the fare difference is $0 or favorable, the change costs nothing.
