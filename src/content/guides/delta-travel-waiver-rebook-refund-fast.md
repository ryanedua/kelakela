# Delta Travel Waiver: How to Rebook or Get a Refund Fast (2026)

When severe weather, a security situation, or a major operational disruption hits, Delta issues a travel waiver that gives affected passengers the ability to change their plans without paying the usual fees. Understanding exactly how a waiver works and what it entitles you to can be the difference between getting on a new flight within hours or spending days waiting at a gate. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** as soon as a waiver is announced and your flight falls within the covered window.

## What Is a Delta Travel Waiver?

A Delta travel waiver is a temporary policy that Delta activates when a significant disruption is expected to affect flight operations at specific airports. Common triggers include hurricanes, nor'easters, major snowstorms, ice storms, severe thunderstorm systems, and international security situations.

When a waiver is active, passengers with tickets to, from, or through the covered airports and within the specified travel date range can do the following without paying change fees or fare differences: rebook on an alternative Delta flight within the waiver window, postpone travel to a later date within the allowed period, or cancel and receive an eCredit for the unused value of their ticket.

Delta typically issues between 15 and 25 or more weather waivers per year in addition to event-specific waivers for situations like the ongoing Middle East security waiver covering Tel Aviv flights through December 2026. Each waiver has its own set of rules including which airports are covered, which travel dates qualify, and the deadline by which rebooking must be completed.

To check whether an active waiver applies to your flight right now, visit **delta.com/travelinformation** or call **+1-(866)-332-0756**.

## Waiver vs Delta Cancellation: Know the Difference

This is the most important distinction to understand before you act.

A travel waiver gives you the option to rebook or cancel voluntarily. If you choose to cancel voluntarily under a waiver, the standard outcome is an eCredit, not a cash refund.

If Delta cancels your flight entirely, your rights change. Under US Department of Transportation rules, when an airline cancels a flight for any reason, passengers are entitled to a full cash refund to the original payment method, regardless of the fare type and regardless of whether a waiver is active. The same applies if Delta makes a schedule change of three hours or more.

In short: waiver plus your voluntary cancellation equals eCredit. Delta cancels your flight equals cash refund right. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** immediately after a Delta cancellation to request your cash refund rather than accepting an automatic eCredit.

## How to Rebook Under a Delta Travel Waiver

Rebooking under a waiver is time-sensitive. Alternative flights fill quickly when hundreds of passengers are rebooking simultaneously. Move early and target off-peak dates and times within the waiver window, which tend to have more available seats.

**Step 1: Confirm your flight is covered.** Check delta.com/travelinformation to verify your origin, destination, and travel date fall within the waiver terms. Each waiver specifies a ticket purchase deadline and a range of affected travel dates.

**Step 2: Choose your new flight.** Identify available Delta flights within the waiver rebooking window. Under most waivers, rebooking must be in the same cabin of service as your original ticket. If the same class of service is unavailable, Delta agents can rebook you in the next available class within the same cabin at no additional charge.

**Step 3: Rebook online or by phone.** Log in to My Trips on delta.com or use the Fly Delta app. Select your booking and look for the option to change your flight. The system should show a zero-dollar change fee when a waiver applies. If a fee appears or the self-service option is unavailable, call **+1-(866)-332-0756** and ask the agent to apply the waiver code manually.

**Step 4: Confirm your bags.** If you have already checked bags, notify the agent about your baggage when rebooking so they can note the new flight assignment on your bag record.

## How to Get a Refund Under a Delta Travel Waiver

Your refund options depend on whether you are acting voluntarily or whether Delta has cancelled your flight.

**Voluntary cancellation under a waiver** gives you an eCredit for the unused value of your ticket. This eCredit can be used toward future Delta flights and is typically valid for one year from the issue date. To cancel voluntarily, go to My Trips on delta.com, select your booking, and choose to cancel. You can also call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to process the cancellation by phone.

**Delta-initiated cancellation** entitles you to a full cash refund. If your flight is cancelled by Delta, do not accept an eCredit automatically. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** and specifically request a cash refund to your original payment method. Agents process these under DOT rules and the refund typically appears within seven business days for credit cards.

**Schedule changes of three hours or more** also trigger the cash refund right. If Delta reschedules your flight and the new departure is three or more hours different from the original, you can decline the new schedule and request a full cash refund.

## What If Your Airport Is Not Covered by the Waiver?

Waivers are issued for specific airports. If severe weather or another disruption is affecting your area but Delta has not included your airport in the active waiver, you still have options.

Call **+1-(855)-321-3528** and ask for a goodwill accommodation. Delta agents occasionally apply waiver-like flexibility to passengers whose airports are adjacent to the covered zone, particularly when the weather event is widespread. This is not guaranteed but is worth requesting.

Alternatively, you can wait rather than voluntarily cancelling. If Delta ends up cancelling your flight due to the disruption even without an official waiver for your airport, you become entitled to a full cash refund. Voluntarily cancelling early for an eCredit locks you into the less favorable outcome.

## Rebooking Under a Waiver for Codeshare and Partner Flights

If your itinerary includes a codeshare segment operated by a SkyTeam partner such as Air France, KLM, Korean Air, or Virgin Atlantic, the waiver process is more complex. Changes need to be reflected across both airline systems.

Contact Delta first at **+1-(866)-332-0756** since Delta is the ticket-issuing carrier. Let the agent know your flight involves a codeshare partner so they can coordinate the rebooking across both systems simultaneously.

## Delta Vacations Waiver Policy

If your disrupted travel was booked as a Delta Vacations package rather than a standalone flight, the process is slightly different. Under the Delta Vacations Travel Advisory Waiver Policy, you can rebook within the waiver guidelines at no additional cost if the new reservation costs the same or more. If the new reservation costs less, the difference is refunded as a Delta Vacations travel certificate valid for one year. If you choose to cancel entirely, you receive a Delta Vacations travel certificate for the full original package value rather than a cash refund, unless you purchased the Delta Vacations Travel Protection Plan, which may offer additional options.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is a Delta travel waiver?**
A Delta travel waiver is a temporary policy issued during disruptions such as severe weather, security situations, or major operational events. It allows affected passengers to rebook or cancel without paying change fees or fare differences. Check delta.com/travelinformation for active waivers.

**Do I get a cash refund or eCredit with a Delta waiver?**
If you voluntarily cancel under a waiver, you receive an eCredit. If Delta cancels your flight entirely, you are entitled to a full cash refund under DOT rules. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** immediately after a Delta cancellation to request the cash refund.

**How do I know if my flight is covered by a Delta waiver?**
Check delta.com/travelinformation for covered airports and travel dates. If your airport is not listed, call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to ask about goodwill accommodations.

**Can I rebook to a higher cabin class under a Delta waiver?**
Generally no. Delta waivers allow fee-free rebooking in the same cabin of service as originally booked. A fare difference applies for upgrades. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to confirm what is available for your ticket.

**What happens to my checked bags if I rebook under a Delta waiver?**
Bags not yet loaded will be transferred to your new flight. Bags already loaded on a subsequently cancelled flight will be returned to the baggage carousel. Always notify the Delta agent about your bags when rebooking.

**How long do I have to use my Delta eCredit after a waiver cancellation?**
Most eCredits are valid for one year from the issue date. Call **+1-(844)-833-1535** to confirm the exact expiration on your specific eCredit.
