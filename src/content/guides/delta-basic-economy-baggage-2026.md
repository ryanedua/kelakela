# Delta Basic Economy Baggage Rules 2026: What's Free and What Costs Extra

Delta's Main Basic fare, formerly called Basic Economy, has more generous carry-on rules than most US airlines. Unlike Spirit or Frontier, which charge for full-size carry-ons on their lowest fares, Delta lets Main Basic passengers bring a full-size carry-on at no extra cost. The catch is boarding order — Basic passengers board last, which means overhead space is often gone by the time they reach the cabin. Understanding the rules, the practical limitations, and how to avoid extra charges starts before you arrive at the airport. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** if you have questions about bag fees on your specific booking.

## Carry-On Allowance for Delta Main Basic

Delta Main Basic includes one full-size carry-on bag and one personal item at no additional charge. This applies on most routes including domestic US flights, flights to Canada, Mexico, the Caribbean, and most international routes.

**Carry-on size limit**: 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 35 x 23 cm), with a maximum linear total of 45 inches. This is the same size limit applied to all fare classes on Delta. Weight limits are not enforced on most domestic routes but apply on some international routes — Singapore limits carry-ons to 7 kg and Beijing/Shanghai to 10 kg.

**Personal item**: Must fit under the seat in front of you. Acceptable items include purses, small backpacks, laptop bags, and camera bags. The personal item does not count against your carry-on allowance.

**Exception — Delta Connection flights on aircraft with 50 seats or fewer**: Passengers on these smaller regional jets may only bring one personal item in the cabin due to limited overhead bin space. Full-size carry-ons are checked at the gate at no charge and returned at the jet bridge upon arrival.

**Exception — Transatlantic routes**: On some Delta flights to Europe from the US, Main Basic passengers may be restricted to a personal item only. No carry-on bag is allowed on certain transatlantic Main Basic tickets. Check your fare rules at booking or call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to verify before you pack.

## The Boarding Order Problem

Main Basic passengers board in the last zone, typically Zone 6, 7, or 8 depending on the aircraft and route. By the time Basic passengers reach the cabin, overhead bins near the front and middle of the aircraft are usually full, particularly on popular domestic routes from Atlanta, New York, Los Angeles, or Chicago.

When overhead bin space is gone, the gate agent will gate-check your carry-on bag. It is placed in the hold and returned to you at the jet bridge upon arrival — you do not have to go to baggage claim for a gate-checked bag.

Gate-checking does not cost anything additional for Main Basic passengers if the bag is within the standard carry-on size. However, it does mean your bag goes into the hold, which changes your deplaning experience.

**How to avoid gate-checking**: Arrive at the gate early and ask the gate agent whether a voluntary gate-check option is available, which sometimes moves you to an earlier boarding position. Hold a Delta SkyMiles American Express card that includes Zone 5 boarding, which gets you on the plane three groups ahead of most Basic passengers. Book early morning flights where aircraft are less full and bins are emptier. Choose a soft-sided bag that compresses more easily into remaining space.

## Checked Bag Fees for Delta Main Basic

Main Basic does not include free checked bags on most routes. Fees vary by route and direction.

**US domestic routes**: First checked bag approximately $45 each way. Second bag approximately $55 each way. These fees are paid at online check-in or at the airport counter.

**Transatlantic routes (US to Europe)**: First checked bag approximately $75 each way for Main Basic on most routes.

**Transpacific routes**: First checked bag may be included on some transpacific routes. Verify your specific route's baggage allowance at booking since transpacific rules differ from other international routes.

**Overweight and oversize fees**: Standard checked bags must be under 50 lb (23 kg) and 62 linear inches (157 cm). Bags between 51 and 70 lb incur an overweight fee of approximately $100 or more. Bags over size limits incur additional oversize fees.

## How to Get Free Checked Bags on Main Basic

**Delta SkyMiles American Express card**: Eligible cardholders — Gold, Platinum, Reserve, and their business versions — receive the first checked bag free on Delta flights including Main Basic. The benefit extends to up to 8 companions on the same reservation. On a round trip with a checked bag, the card's annual fee often pays for itself in the first trip.

**Medallion status**: Diamond, Platinum, Gold, and Silver Medallion members receive free checked bags based on their status tier, including on Main Basic tickets.

**Military travelers**: Active duty US military members traveling on orders may receive additional baggage allowances. Verify your specific entitlement at delta.com or by calling **+1-(855)-321-3528**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Delta Basic Economy include a carry-on bag?**
Yes. One full-size carry-on and one personal item are included at no charge on most routes. Late boarding means bins may be full and gate-checking is common.

**How much is a checked bag on Delta Basic Economy?**
Approximately $45 each way for domestic US flights. Approximately $75 each way for transatlantic routes. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** for the fee on your specific route.

**Can I get a free checked bag on Delta Basic Economy?**
Yes, with an eligible Delta SkyMiles American Express card or Medallion elite status.

**What is the carry-on size limit for Delta Basic Economy?**
22 x 14 x 9 inches, the same as all fare classes. Maximum 45 linear inches total.

**Is there a personal item allowance on Delta Basic Economy?**
Yes. One personal item fitting under the seat in front of you is included at no charge.
