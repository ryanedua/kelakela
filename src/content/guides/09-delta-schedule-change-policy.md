# Delta Schedule Change Policy: When You're Entitled to a Refund

Airlines regularly adjust their flight schedules, and Delta is no exception. Whether it is a minor time shift or a complete rerouting, a Delta schedule change can significantly affect your travel plans. The key question most passengers want answered is: when does a schedule change entitle me to a free rebook or a full refund? This guide explains the policy clearly.

## What Is a Delta Schedule Change?

A Delta schedule change occurs when Delta modifies the details of a ticketed flight after the original booking was made. Schedule changes are distinct from same-day operational delays and are usually announced days, weeks, or even months in advance.

Common types of schedule changes include:

- Departure or arrival time shifts
- Changes to connecting airports or routing
- Equipment changes that affect seating or cabin class
- Flight number or operating carrier changes
- Flight cancellations scheduled in advance

Not all schedule changes trigger refund rights. The key is whether the change is considered **significant** under Delta's policy and DOT guidance.

## What Qualifies as a Significant Schedule Change

### DOT Guidance on Significant Changes

The U.S. Department of Transportation has issued guidance stating that passengers are entitled to refunds when airlines make significant changes to their itineraries. DOT has identified the following as significant changes:

- Departure or arrival time changes of **3 hours or more** for domestic flights and **6 hours or more** for international flights (as a baseline, though Delta's own thresholds may be lower)
- Changes to the departure or arrival airport
- Increases in the number of connections
- Changes to connecting airports
- Downgrades to a lower class of service
- Changes affecting passengers with disabilities

### Delta's Own Standards

Delta's customer commitment and internal policies may treat changes of 30 minutes or more as warranting a rebooking option for affected passengers. The specific threshold can depend on the circumstances and may vary. As a general rule, if a schedule change materially affects your ability to travel as planned — particularly if you miss a connection or an important event — it is worth contacting Delta to review your options.

## What You Can Do When Delta Changes Your Schedule

When Delta sends a schedule change notification, you typically have three options:

### Option 1: Accept the New Itinerary

If the new schedule still works for your plans, simply accept the updated itinerary. Delta will update your booking automatically, and no further action may be needed. In some cases, accepting the new itinerary triggers a confirmation email.

### Option 2: Rebook on a Different Flight

If the new times do not work for you, you can rebook on an alternative Delta flight at no charge. This option is available when a schedule change qualifies as significant. Log in to delta.com, go to **My Trips**, select the affected reservation, and choose the rebook option. You can choose from available Delta flights on your same route.

### Option 3: Request a Full Refund

If you would prefer not to travel at all given the new schedule, you may be entitled to a full cash refund — even on a non-refundable ticket — if the change is significant. Refund requests can be submitted through **My Trips** on delta.com or by calling Delta at **++1-866-332-0756**.

## How Delta Notifies You of Schedule Changes

Delta sends schedule change notifications through the following channels:

- **Email** to the address on file in your booking
- **Fly Delta app** push notifications (if you have the app installed and notifications enabled)
- **Text message** if you have opted in to SMS notifications

It is important to keep your contact information current in your SkyMiles account and in any reservation you make, even without logging in. A schedule change notification you miss because of outdated contact details does not affect Delta's ability to honor the original booking terms, but it can reduce your response time.

## Minor Schedule Changes and Your Options

Not all schedule changes trigger automatic refund or rebook rights. Minor changes — such as a departure time shift of 10 to 20 minutes — typically do not rise to the level of a significant change. In these cases:

- You can accept the change without any action required.
- If the minor change causes a connection problem or another issue, contact Delta to explain the situation. Agents may have flexibility to accommodate you.

## What Happens to Your Connecting Flights

If a schedule change causes a connection time to drop below Delta's minimum connection time — or eliminates a viable connection entirely — Delta will typically rebook the affected segment automatically. Review the full revised itinerary when you receive a schedule change notification to ensure all connections still work.

If your revised connection is uncomfortably tight and Delta has not adjusted it, call **++1-866-332-0756** to ask for an alternative routing.

## Special Situations: Award Tickets and Partner Bookings

### Award Tickets

If your SkyMiles award ticket is affected by a significant schedule change, the same refund and rebook options apply. Miles redeposit fees may be waived when the reason for cancellation is a significant schedule change. Confirm this with a Delta agent when you call.

### Third-Party Bookings

If you booked through a travel agency or online travel platform, Delta's schedule change notification may go to that third party rather than directly to you. Verify that your contact information is current with the booking platform, and monitor your reservation directly through delta.com to stay informed.

## Tips for Responding to a Delta Schedule Change

- **Act quickly.** Rebooking options are available on a first-come, first-served basis. The best alternative flights fill up after a large schedule change affects many passengers.
- **Check the full itinerary.** A change to one segment can affect your connections. Review all legs of your trip.
- **Request the refund in writing.** If you call to request a refund, follow up via email through Delta's customer care portal for a written record.
- **Check whether a travel waiver applies.** During major disruptions, Delta may issue waivers that provide even more flexibility than standard schedule change policies.

## Contact Delta Customer Support

For questions about a specific schedule change, refund eligibility, or rebooking options, contact Delta Air Lines at **++1-866-332-0756** or log in to delta.com and go to My Trips.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What counts as a significant Delta schedule change?**

Delta considers changes significant when they substantially affect departure or arrival times, change your origin or destination airport, alter your routing in a materially different way, or downgrade your cabin class. DOT guidance supports refund eligibility for changes of 30 minutes or more.

**Can I get a refund if Delta changes my flight time by 30 minutes?**

A schedule change of 30 minutes or more may qualify you for a refund or free rebook under DOT guidance and Delta's customer commitment policies. Contact Delta at ++1-866-332-0756 to request your options if an automatic refund or rebook option is not presented.

**How does Delta notify passengers of schedule changes?**

Delta notifies passengers of schedule changes via email, the Fly Delta app, and text message if opted in. You will typically receive a notification with your updated itinerary and options for accepting the change, rebooking, or requesting a refund.

**What should I do if I receive a Delta schedule change notification?**

Review the change carefully. If the new times do not work for you, go to 'My Trips' on delta.com, select the affected flight, and choose to rebook on a different flight or request a refund. You can also call Delta at ++1-866-332-0756 for assistance.
