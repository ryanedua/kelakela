# What are my rights if Delta cancels my flight? — 2026 Answer

When Delta cancels your flight, you are entitled to: (1) a full cash refund to your original payment method or free rebooking on the next available Delta flight, (2) meal vouchers for controllable delays of 3+ hours, (3) hotel accommodation for overnight controllable cancellations, and (4) rebooking on SkyTeam partners when no Delta alternative is available. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 immediately after a cancellation to claim your rights.
