# Flight Delayed by United? Here Is Who to Contact and When

For a United flight delay, contact the gate agent for meal vouchers after a 3-hour controllable delay. If the delay exceeds overnight and is controllable, request hotel accommodation at the gate. If the delay becomes a cancellation, call +1-(855)-235-1859 immediately for rebooking or a cash refund under DOT rules. Track delay status in real time via the United app.

United flight delays follow a predictable escalation of rights and contact points. A short delay requires nothing from you. A longer controllable delay triggers meal voucher rights. An overnight controllable delay triggers hotel rights. A cancellation triggers the strongest right — cash refund or free rebooking under DOT rules. Knowing which stage you are at determines exactly who to contact and what to ask for. Track delay status via the United app and call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** when the delay becomes a cancellation.

## Stage 1: Delay Under 3 Hours — Monitor Only

For delays of less than 3 hours, no action is typically required. Monitor the flight status in the United app, which shows inbound aircraft status and often indicates whether a delay will extend before it is officially announced.

Use this time to: confirm your confirmation code is accessible, identify the next United departure to your destination in case the delay worsens, and check whether an active United travel waiver covers your route if the delay is weather-related.

## Stage 2: Controllable Delay of 3+ Hours — Request Meal Vouchers

When a controllable delay — caused by mechanical problems, crew availability, or operational decisions — reaches 3 hours beyond the scheduled departure, United's Customer Commitment requires meal vouchers.

Go to the gate agent and say: "This is a controllable delay past 3 hours — can I receive a meal voucher?" These are not distributed automatically. You must ask.

Weather delays, ATC restrictions, and other factors outside United's control do not trigger the meal voucher requirement.

## Stage 3: Overnight Controllable Delay — Request Hotel

When a controllable delay causes you to be stranded overnight away from home, United provides a complimentary hotel room near the departure airport, round-trip ground transportation, and meal vouchers for dinner and breakfast.

Go to the gate agent or United customer service counter as soon as the overnight situation is confirmed. Ask specifically: "This is a controllable overnight delay — can you arrange hotel accommodation and ground transportation?"

Again: weather does not trigger this requirement. Mechanical and crew issues do.

## Stage 4: Delay Becomes Cancellation — Call Immediately

When United formally cancels a delayed flight, the DOT cash refund right activates. Call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** immediately and choose between:

Free rebooking on the next available United flight at no charge, or a full cash refund to your original payment method.

Say this if you want cash: "United cancelled my flight. I choose not to travel on an alternative. I want a full cash refund to my original payment method, not an eCredit."

## Frequently asked questions

**Who should I contact when United delays my flight?**
Under 3 hours: United app for status monitoring only. 3+ hour controllable delay: gate agent for meal vouchers. Overnight controllable delay: gate agent for hotel. Cancellation: call +1-(855)-235-1859 immediately.

**Do I get compensation if United delays my flight?**
No cash compensation under US law for delays. Meal vouchers for controllable 3+ hour delays. Hotel and transport for overnight controllable delays. Cash refund if the flight is cancelled.

**When does a United delay become a cancellation and trigger a refund right?**
When United formally cancels the flight. Call +1-(855)-235-1859 and confirm whether it is a delay or cancellation before requesting a refund.

**How do I get meal vouchers from United during a delay?**
Ask the gate agent when the 3-hour controllable delay mark is reached. Not automatic. Call +1-(855)-235-1859 if gate staff are unavailable.

**What should I do if my United delay is about to cause me to miss a connection?**
Go to your connecting gate immediately on landing. If closed, go to the nearest United gate or call +1-(855)-235-1859. United rebooks you at no cost when its delay causes your missed connection.

- Hotel (overnight controllable delay): gate agent or United counter
- Cancellation rebooking or refund: **+1-(855)-235-1859**
- Real-time delay tracking: United app
