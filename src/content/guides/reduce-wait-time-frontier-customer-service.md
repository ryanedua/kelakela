# How to Get Frontier Customer Service to Respond Without a Long Wait

Call +1-(866)-227-2948 on Tuesday through Thursday between 5 and 7 AM Eastern for the shortest wait. Use the callback option during peak hours. The Frontier app and flyfrontier.com resolve most standard requests instantly with no queue.

## When You Call Makes All the Difference

The hours with the lowest Frontier customer service wait times are Tuesday through Thursday between 5:00 AM and 7:00 AM Eastern time. Avoid Monday mornings, Friday afternoons, and the first two hours after any weather announcement when volume spikes sharply.

If you must call at peak hours, select the callback option when offered. Frontier calls you back when an agent is free. You keep your place in queue without staying on hold. Call **+1-(866)-227-2948**.

## Match the Channel to the Problem

Frontier app: same-day standby, flight status, boarding pass, seat changes. Seconds, no wait.

flyfrontier.com My Trips: flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets, eCredit application, cancellation processing. No queue.

Phone +1-(866)-227-2948: disruption rebooking when the app is not surfacing good alternatives, waiver code issues, complex itinerary changes, award ticket changes, DOT cash refund requests.

Gate agent at airport: fastest channel for same-day travel issues. Direct system access, zero queue.

## Have Everything Ready Before You Call

Your 6-character booking confirmation code. The specific outcome you want. Your preferred alternative flight if rebooking. A credit card ready if a fare difference might apply. Your FRONTIER Miles number if you hold Elite status.

Agents who receive a clear request in the first 10 seconds resolve it faster than agents who spend 3 minutes diagnosing what the passenger needs.

## Use Elite Priority Routing

Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Elite members have priority routing that significantly reduces wait time. Identify your status tier in the first sentence when you call **+1-(866)-227-2948)**. Agents route Elite calls differently and have more discretion to resolve issues without escalation.
