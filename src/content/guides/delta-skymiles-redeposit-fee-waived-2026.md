# Delta SkyMiles Redeposit Fee Waived in 2026: Complete Rules

Delta's SkyMiles redeposit fee policy is one of the most passenger-friendly in US aviation for most members. If you cancel a non-Basic SkyMiles award ticket before departure on a US or Canada-originating route, your miles come back with no fee. This applies whether you have elite status or not. Medallion members get the additional benefit of having fees waived even on international-origin routes where non-elite members face a $150 charge. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to cancel an award ticket and confirm the rules that apply to your specific booking.

## The Waiver: Who Gets It and When

**All SkyMiles members (including non-Medallion) on US/Canada-originating routes**: Cancel any non-Basic award ticket before departure with no redeposit fee. Miles return to your account within 24 to 72 hours. This is the base policy and covers the vast majority of Delta award bookings made by US-based passengers.

**All Medallion members on all routes (including international origin)**: Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion members have redeposit fees waived on award cancellations regardless of where the travel originates. Even if you are cancelling a Tokyo to New York award (international-origin), no redeposit fee applies if you hold any Medallion tier.

**Delta-cancelled award flights**: When Delta cancels your award flight, your miles are redeposited with no fee regardless of origin or Medallion status. This is a mandatory DOT-aligned protection.

## When the Fee Is Not Waived

**International-origin routes for non-Medallion members**: If you hold no Medallion status and are cancelling an award ticket that originates outside the US and Canada, Delta's stated policy is a $150 per-person redeposit fee. This applies to routes beginning in Europe, Asia, Latin America, and other international markets.

The $150 fee is not consistently applied on every international-origin route, but it is the published policy. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** before cancelling an international-origin award to confirm whether the fee applies to your specific booking.

**Basic experience award fares**: Delta Basic experience award fares carry a mileage cancellation charge before redeposit. The amount deducted depends on the specific fare rules. This applies regardless of Medallion status — even Diamond Medallion members face a mileage charge on Basic award cancellations.

**No-shows**: If you miss your award flight without cancelling before the scheduled departure time, your miles are forfeited entirely. No redeposit occurs for no-shows. This is a complete loss — not a fee but a forfeiture. Cancel before the flight departs even if only minutes before.

**Some partner airline awards**: Awards booked on SkyTeam partner airlines through Delta SkyMiles may carry their own cancellation fees set by the individual partner carrier. These vary by airline and route.

## Why the Waiver Matters for Award Booking Strategy

The practical value of the redeposit fee waiver is significant. It makes it rational to book award space early even when plans are uncertain, because you can cancel at any time before departure and get your miles back with no penalty.

This is particularly valuable during periods when scarce award space appears — booking early to secure the space and then cancelling if plans change later has no downside cost. The miles return promptly and can be used for the next booking.

For non-Medallion members booking international-origin routes, the $150 fee creates a modest disincentive to book speculatively, but for US-originating routes, the strategy of early booking and late cancellation is essentially cost-free.

## How to Cancel a SkyMiles Award and Get the Redeposit

**Via delta.com**: Log in to My Trips, select the award booking, and choose Cancel. The system should display the redeposit amount (number of miles) before you confirm. For US/Canada-originating non-Basic awards, the redeposit should show the full mileage amount.

**By phone**: Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** with your booking confirmation number. Tell the agent you want to cancel the SkyMiles award and confirm there is no redeposit fee for your booking. The agent can complete the cancellation on the call.

**After cancellation**: Miles redeposit within 24 to 72 hours. Log in to your SkyMiles account to confirm the redeposit appears in your activity history. Cash taxes and fees refund within 7 business days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the Delta SkyMiles redeposit fee waived in 2026?**
Yes for US/Canada-originating non-Basic awards for all members, and for all routes for Medallion members.

**Do Medallion members pay a redeposit fee?**
No. All Medallion tiers have redeposit fees waived on all routes.

**What is the redeposit fee for non-Medallion members on international awards?**
$150 per person on some international-origin routes. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to confirm before cancelling.

**When is the redeposit fee not waived?**
Basic experience award fares (mileage charge applies), no-shows (full forfeiture), and some partner airline awards.

**How long does Delta take to redeposit miles?**
24 to 72 hours. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** if not received within 72 hours.


**Do Medallion members pay a redeposit fee when cancelling Delta award tickets?**
No. All Medallion tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) have the redeposit fee waived on all routes, including international-origin award cancellations. This is a valuable Medallion benefit that makes award bookings effectively risk-free to cancel.

**How long does it take for Delta to redeposit miles after an award cancellation?**
24 to 72 hours. Cash taxes and fees refund within 7 business days. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 if miles have not redeposited within 72 hours of the cancellation.
