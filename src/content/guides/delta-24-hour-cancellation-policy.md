# Delta 24-Hour Cancellation Policy 2026: Full Refund Rules Explained

The 24-hour risk-free cancellation window is the single most passenger-friendly policy Delta offers. For 24 hours after you book, any fare type can be cancelled for a complete refund to your original payment method with no fee, no questions, and no exceptions based on how restrictive the ticket is. If you made a mistake, found a better price, or simply changed your mind, this window is your safety net. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** if you need to cancel within the window and want to confirm the refund before proceeding.

## How the 24-Hour Policy Works

Delta's 24-Hour Risk-Free Cancellation policy guarantees a full refund if you cancel your ticket within 24 hours of the booking being confirmed. The refund goes back to the exact payment method used at purchase — your credit card, debit card, or other form of payment. No conversion to eCredit, no deduction of fees.

The clock starts the moment Delta confirms your purchase, not at midnight and not at the time of the flight. If you booked at 11:22 PM on a Thursday, your window closes at 11:22 PM on Friday.

The policy applies to all fare types purchased directly from Delta: Main Basic, Main Classic, Main Extra, Comfort+, Delta First, Delta Premium Select, Delta One, Basic Business, and all other cabin and tier combinations.

## What Qualifies

**Direct Delta bookings only**: The 24-hour refund applies to tickets purchased on delta.com, through the Fly Delta app, or directly with a Delta reservations agent by phone. Tickets booked through Expedia, Traveloka, a travel agency, or any third-party platform are not covered by Delta's own 24-hour policy. Those bookings are subject to the third-party platform's own cancellation rules.

**Any fare type**: Basic Economy, premium cabin Basic fares, Classic, Extra, and refundable fares are all covered within the 24-hour window.

**Flight must be more than 7 days from purchase**: The DOT's regulatory basis for the 24-hour rule technically requires the flight to be at least 7 days after the booking date. Delta generally applies the policy broadly and extends it to shorter-notice bookings as well, but if your departure is within 7 days of purchase, call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to confirm your window before assuming the refund applies.

## How to Cancel Within the 24-Hour Window

**Via delta.com**: Log in to My Trips, select your booking, and choose Cancel. If you are within the 24-hour window, the system will display a full refund amount with no fee deducted. Confirm the cancellation and the refund is initiated immediately.

**Via the Fly Delta app**: Open My Trips in the app, select the booking, and tap Cancel. The app displays the refund amount before you confirm. If within the window, the full ticket value is shown.

**By phone**: Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and give your booking confirmation number. Tell the agent you are within the 24-hour risk-free window and want to cancel for a full refund. The agent will confirm the timestamp and process the refund during the call.

## How Long Does the Refund Take?

After cancelling within the 24-hour window, Delta initiates the refund immediately on their end. The time for the refund to appear on your statement depends on your financial institution.

For credit cards, the refund typically posts within 7 business days. Some banks process it faster, appearing in 3 to 5 days.

For debit cards and other payment methods, the timeframe may be up to 20 calendar days.

If you have not received the refund after the expected window, call **+1-(855)-321-3528** with your booking confirmation number and ask for a refund status update.

## Using the 24-Hour Window to Price Shop

Delta's own Low Fare Commitment notes that if you find a lower fare after booking, you can cancel within 24 hours and rebook at the lower price. This is entirely within policy and a legitimate way to use the risk-free window.

For example, if you book on Monday morning and find a $100 cheaper fare for the same route Monday afternoon, cancel the original booking within the window and rebook at the lower price. You receive a full refund on the first ticket and pay the lower price on the new one. No fees apply as long as both actions happen within 24 hours of the original purchase.

## After the 24-Hour Window Closes

Once the risk-free window closes, your refund rights depend entirely on which fare type you booked.

Main Basic: eCredit minus cancellation fee for pre-departure cancellations. Full forfeiture for no-shows.

Main Classic: Full eCredit, no fee, for pre-departure cancellations.

Main Extra: Full cash refund to original payment method at any time before departure.

All fare types: Full cash refund if Delta cancels your flight or makes a qualifying significant schedule change.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Delta's 24-hour cancellation policy apply to Basic Economy?**
Yes. All fare types including Main Basic are covered if purchased directly from Delta within the 24-hour window.

**When does the 24-hour cancellation window start?**
At the exact moment your purchase is confirmed. Not at midnight, not at departure.

**Does Delta's 24-hour policy apply to third-party bookings?**
No. Only tickets purchased directly from Delta on delta.com, the Fly Delta app, or by phone.

**What if my flight departs within 24 hours of booking?**
Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to confirm whether the risk-free window applies to your specific booking.

**How do I cancel within the 24-hour window?**
Via My Trips on delta.com, the Fly Delta app, or by calling **+1-(866)-332-0756)**.
