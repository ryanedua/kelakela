# Delta Medallion Status: Flight Change and Cancel Benefits in 2026

There is a common misconception that free flight changes on Delta are a Medallion benefit. They are not. The no-change-fee policy for Classic and Extra tickets applies to every Delta passenger regardless of elite status — it was introduced in 2020 for everyone. What Medallion status actually provides in terms of change and cancellation benefits is more specific: the waiver of the $75 Same-Day Confirmed fee for qualifying members, award redeposit fee waivers across all routes, and priority on standby lists. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for any change or cancellation request where Medallion benefits may apply to your booking.

## What Every Passenger Already Gets (Not a Medallion Benefit)

**No change fee on Classic and Extra tickets**: Delta permanently eliminated change fees for Main Cabin and above tickets in 2020. This applies to all passengers — Silver Medallion, Diamond Medallion, and no status at all — booking Classic or Extra fares originating in the US and Canada.

**Free Same-Day Standby**: All eligible passengers (Classic and above fare classes on qualifying domestic routes) can join the standby list for an earlier same-day flight at no cost. This is not a Medallion benefit — it is available to all.

**No redeposit fee on US/Canada-originating award cancellations**: All SkyMiles members including those without Medallion status can cancel US and Canada-originating non-Basic award tickets without a redeposit fee.

These are baseline policy benefits for all passengers. Medallion status does not create them — they already exist.

## What Medallion Status Actually Adds for Changes and Cancellations

**Same-Day Confirmed fee waiver for Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion**: The $75 Same-Day Confirmed change fee is waived for Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion members on eligible domestic routes. For these elite members, Same-Day Confirmed is both free and guaranteed — a meaningful benefit when you arrive at the airport early and want to move to an earlier flight without cost or uncertainty.

Silver Medallion members pay the same $75 as general members for Same-Day Confirmed changes.

**Award redeposit fee waiver for all Medallion tiers**: All Medallion members (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) have the $150 redeposit fee waived on award cancellations for all routes including international-origin bookings. Non-Medallion members face this fee on some international-origin routes.

**Priority on Same-Day Standby lists**: When standby lists are populated, Medallion members are ranked above non-Medallion passengers. The clearance priority is Diamond, then Platinum, then Gold, then Silver, then general members. Within the same tier, earlier requests rank higher.

**Upgrade eligibility and priority**: Medallion complimentary upgrades to Comfort+ and First Class on eligible domestic and short-haul international flights are a Medallion status benefit. Basic fares are excluded from this benefit, but Classic and Extra fares in economy make Medallion members eligible for the complimentary upgrade waitlist.

## What Medallion Status Does Not Change

**Basic Economy change restrictions**: Main Basic tickets cannot be changed after the 24-hour risk-free window regardless of Medallion status. Gold Medallion holders cannot change a Basic ticket any more than a general member can. The fare-level restriction overrides the status benefit.

**Standard advance changes on Classic/Extra**: Since there is already no change fee for these fares, Medallion status provides no additional benefit for standard advance changes. All passengers save equally here.

**Cancel-before-departure eCredit outcome**: Cancelling a Classic ticket before departure produces a full eCredit for all passengers regardless of status.

## The Practical Value of Status for Flight Flexibility

For a Medallion member who frequently makes same-day adjustments, the $75 Confirmed fee waiver alone provides significant value. If you make two same-day confirmed changes per month, that is $150 saved monthly — meaningful for frequent business travelers.

The award redeposit fee waiver benefits Medallion members who book award tickets speculatively and cancel international-origin awards. Without status, a $150 redeposit fee on an international-origin award cancellation is a real cost. With any Medallion tier, it is zero.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do Medallion members get free flight changes on Delta?**
Classic and Extra changes are free for all passengers. Medallion status specifically waives the $75 Same-Day Confirmed fee for Gold/Platinum/Diamond.

**Which Medallion tiers have the same-day confirmed change fee waived?**
Gold, Platinum, and Diamond. Silver still pays the $75 fee.

**Do Medallion members pay award cancellation fees?**
No. All Medallion tiers have award redeposit fees waived on all routes.

**Can Medallion members change Basic Economy tickets?**
No. Basic Economy restrictions apply regardless of Medallion status.

**Do Medallion members get priority on standby lists?**
Yes. Diamond first, then Platinum, Gold, Silver, then general members. Within tiers, earlier requests rank higher.


**Do Medallion members pay award cancellation fees on Delta?**
No. All Medallion tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) have award redeposit fees waived on all routes, including international-origin awards. Non-Medallion members pay a $150 redeposit fee on some international-origin award cancellations.

**Do Medallion members get priority on same-day standby lists?**
Yes. Medallion members are placed higher on Same-Day Standby lists than non-Medallion passengers. Diamond members receive the highest priority, followed by Platinum, Gold, Silver, and then general members. Within the same tier, earlier standby requests are ranked higher.
