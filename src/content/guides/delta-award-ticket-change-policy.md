# Delta Award Ticket Change & Cancellation Policy 2026

Delta's award ticket policy is one of the most flexible among major US airlines for North America-originating bookings. Most SkyMiles award tickets can be changed or cancelled before departure without paying a redeposit or change fee, with only the mileage difference applying for changes. The key exceptions are Basic experience award fares and some international-origin routes where non-Medallion members still face fees. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for award changes or to confirm the rules on your specific booking.

## The Core Rule: No Fees for US/Canada-Originating Awards

For award tickets originating in the United States or Canada, Delta's SkyMiles Program Rules allow members to change the origin, destination, routing, date, and time — or cancel entirely — without a redeposit or change fee. This applies to all Medallion tiers and to general SkyMiles members without elite status, for non-Basic awards.

The only cost when changing a North American-origin award ticket is the mileage difference. If the new flight requires more miles than the original, the additional miles are deducted from your account. If the new flight requires fewer miles, the difference is redeposited.

This flexibility makes Delta SkyMiles awards particularly useful for locking in scarce award inventory early and then adjusting later if better options become available or plans change.

## Changes vs Cancellations

**Changes** are generally faster and simpler than cancellations for award tickets. Instead of cancelling and redepositing miles before rebooking, you modify the existing award ticket to the new date, routing, or destination. The mileage difference is applied immediately and your confirmed seat is maintained while the change is processed.

For date changes on the same route, go to My Trips on delta.com and select the award booking. Date change options appear if award space is available on the new date. If the online option is not available for your routing, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)**.

Destination changes and routing changes cannot be made online and require a phone call.

**Cancellations** result in full mileage redeposit to your SkyMiles account and a refund of cash taxes and fees. Redeposit typically processes within 24 to 72 hours. Cash refunds follow the standard 7-business-day window for credit cards.

## Redeposit Fees: When They Apply

**US and Canada-originating non-Basic awards**: No redeposit fee for any Medallion tier or general SkyMiles member. Cancel before departure and miles return at no cost.

**International-originating awards**: For awards originating outside the US and Canada — for example, a flight from Paris to Atlanta booked with SkyMiles — non-Medallion members may face a $150 per-person redeposit fee on cancellations. This fee is not consistently applied on all routes, but it is the policy for some markets. Medallion members (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) have the redeposit fee waived regardless of origin.

**Basic experience award fares**: Delta Basic experience award fares follow the same restrictions as Basic revenue tickets. Changes may not be permitted and cancellations result in a mileage cancellation charge rather than a full redeposit. The amount deducted before redeposit depends on the specific fare rules.

## No-Shows on Award Tickets

If you miss your award flight without cancelling before departure, the miles are forfeited. No redeposit is issued for a no-show on an award ticket. Cancel before the flight departs to preserve your miles, even if only minutes before departure.

If the no-show resulted from a Delta-caused cancellation or significant delay, contact **+1-(866)-332-0756)** — miles should be redeposited at no charge when Delta initiated the disruption.

## Partner Airline Award Changes

Award tickets booked on SkyTeam partner airlines through Delta SkyMiles — Air France, KLM, Korean Air, etc. — may have different change and cancellation rules set by the individual partner carrier. Partner awards generally cannot be changed or cancelled online and require a phone call.

Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for all partner airline award changes. Confirm the specific partner's rules before initiating any change, as fees and restrictions vary by carrier.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change a Delta award ticket without a fee?**
Yes for US/Canada-originating non-Basic awards. Only the mileage difference applies.

**What is the Delta award ticket redeposit fee?**
No fee for US/Canada-originating non-Basic awards. $150 for some international-originating routes for non-Medallion members.

**What happens to my miles if I cancel a Delta award ticket?**
Miles are redeposited within 24 to 72 hours. Cash taxes refund within 7 business days.

**Can I change a Delta Basic award ticket?**
Changes are generally not permitted. Cancellation results in a mileage deduction before redeposit.

**What if Delta cancels my award flight?**
Miles are fully redeposited at no charge. Cash fees are refunded. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** if redeposit is not received within 72 hours.
