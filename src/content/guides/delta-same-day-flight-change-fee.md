# Delta Same-Day Flight Change: Confirmed vs Standby Guide 2026

If your plans shift on the day of travel and you want to move to an earlier or later Delta flight, you have two options: Same-Day Confirmed and Same-Day Standby. One costs $75 and guarantees your seat. The other is free but depends on availability. Knowing which to use and whether your ticket qualifies can save you both time and money at the airport. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to start a same-day change or check your eligibility before you get to the gate.

## Same-Day Confirmed Change

Same-Day Confirmed lets you move to a different eligible Delta flight on the same travel day and receive a confirmed seat assignment before you board. You are guaranteed a spot on the new flight as long as a seat in your original fare class is available.

**Fee**: $75 for general SkyMiles members, Silver Medallion members, and non-members. The fee is waived entirely for Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion members on eligible routes.

**No fare difference**: As long as the new flight is in the same cabin class as your original booking, no fare difference is charged. This is the major advantage of the same-day window. Moving to an earlier business-class flight on the same day does not cost you the price difference between the two departures.

**How to request it**: Open the Fly Delta app, go to Same-Day Change, browse available flights, and select Confirmed. You can also request it at the airport check-in counter or Delta Sky Priority desk, or by calling **+1-(866)-332-0756**.

**Timing**: Same-day change requests can typically be made starting at midnight on the day of travel through the departure of the alternate flight you want to move to. Check in early at the airport if you want the widest range of options.

## Same-Day Standby

Same-Day Standby places you on the waitlist for an earlier Delta flight on the same travel day. Your original reservation is kept active. If a seat opens up before the earlier flight departs, Delta assigns you to it at no charge.

**Fee**: Free for all eligible passengers. No charge applies even if you successfully get a seat on the standby flight.

**How it works**: You keep your original booking and boarding pass. You are added to the standby list for the earlier flight. If a seat opens, you receive a new boarding pass. If no seat opens, you board your original flight as planned.

**How to request it**: Through the Fly Delta app under Same-Day Change, at the airport, or by calling **+1-(844)-833-1535**. You can add yourself to the standby list and then monitor assignment notifications in the app.

**Confirmed vs Standby decision**: Use Confirmed when you need to be on a specific earlier flight with certainty. Use Standby when you are flexible, willing to wait at the gate, and want to save the $75 fee.

## Who Is Eligible

Both Same-Day Confirmed and Same-Day Standby are available for domestic Delta flights on Main Cabin Classic, Main Cabin Extra, Comfort+, First Class, and Delta One. Availability also extends to certain short-haul international routes including Canada and Puerto Rico.

**Not eligible**: Delta Main Basic (Basic Economy) fares are excluded from both same-day options. If you booked the lowest Main Basic fare, you cannot use Same-Day Confirmed or Same-Day Standby. A new ticket would need to be purchased for the earlier flight.

Delta Medallion status affects the fee but not the availability window. All eligible fare classes can use same-day options regardless of elite status; Medallion status only determines whether the $75 confirmed fee is waived.

## How to Do a Same-Day Change

**Via the Fly Delta app** is the fastest method. Open the app, tap My Trips, select your flight, and look for Same-Day Change. Available flights will display. Choose Confirmed or Standby and follow the prompts.

**At the airport** works at any Delta staffed counter or kiosk starting from when check-in opens. Approach the check-in counter or Sky Priority desk and ask for a same-day confirmed change or standby list addition.

**By phone** is the best option if you want to confirm eligibility before arriving at the airport or if the app is not working. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** and have your booking confirmation ready.

## International Flights

Delta's same-day change policy does not apply to most long-haul international routes. If your flight is an international departure other than to Canada, Puerto Rico, or certain nearby destinations, same-day options are generally not available.

For international itineraries, any change to a different departure time or date typically involves the standard change rules: no fee for Classic and Extra, fare difference may apply. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to confirm what same-day options, if any, exist for your specific international route.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does a Delta same-day confirmed change cost?**
$75 for general members and non-Medallion passengers. Waived for Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion members on eligible routes.

**Is Delta same-day standby free?**
Yes. Same-Day Standby is free for all eligible passengers. No fee is charged even if you get a confirmed seat.

**Can I do a same-day change on a Delta Basic Economy ticket?**
No. Main Basic fares are not eligible for Same-Day Confirmed or Same-Day Standby.

**Does a same-day change charge a fare difference?**
No fare difference applies if the new flight is in the same cabin as the original booking. This is the key benefit of using the same-day option rather than a standard change.

**Can I do a same-day change on an international Delta flight?**
Generally no for long-haul routes. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to verify eligibility on your specific route.
