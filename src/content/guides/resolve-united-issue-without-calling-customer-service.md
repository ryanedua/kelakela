# Can You Resolve a United Issue Without Calling Customer Service?

Yes — most United issues can be resolved without calling. Voluntary flight changes, cancellations for eCredit, standby additions, eCredit application, boarding passes, and flight status all work through the United app or united.com with no hold time. Calling +1-(855)-235-1859 is necessary for cash refunds after cancellations, waiver code issues, award ticket changes, and supervisor escalation.

Most United customer service interactions that passengers assume require a phone call can be completed through the United app or united.com in under 2 minutes with no hold time. Here is a full breakdown of what works without calling — and the narrower list of situations where **+1-(855)-235-1859)** is actually necessary.

## What You Can Do Without Calling

**Change your flight**: united.com My Trips → Change Flight, or United app → My Trips → Change. Works for Classic and Extra tickets. Under 2 minutes. Available 24 hours.

**Cancel for eCredit**: My Trips → Cancel on either platform. The system calculates and confirms your eCredit before you confirm the cancellation. Instantly processed.

**Apply an eCredit**: At checkout on united.com, Payment section → Add a Certificate, eCredit or gift card. Or locate your eCredit first at united.com/redeem using your 13-digit ticket number.

**Add to same-day standby**: United app → My Trips → select your flight → Same-Day Change → Standby. Track your real-time list position in the app.

**Request a refund in writing**: united.com/refunds — submit your booking confirmation, flight date, and the reason for the refund. Creates a timestamped written record.

**Check flight status**: united.com/flight-status or United app flight status tab. Enter your flight number. Shows current departure time, gate, and delay status including inbound aircraft status.

**Download a boarding pass**: United app or united.com → My Trips → Check In → boarding pass. Available 24 hours before departure.

**Change your seat**: My Trips → Manage Seats on united.com or the United app. Works for seats that are available for selection on your fare type.

**Submit a complaint**: united.com/CustomerRelations — for formal written complaints after a disruption, requesting goodwill compensation, and documentation of incidents.

## What Still Requires Calling

**Cash refund after a United-initiated cancellation**: The DOT-required cash refund is processed by a live agent. Submit at united.com/refunds in writing as a backup, but call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** to request cash explicitly.

**Waiver code not applying online**: When an active United travel waiver covers your flight but the online system charges a fee, a live agent applies the waiver code manually.

**Award ticket destination changes**: Complex award modifications, especially those involving MileagePlus partner airlines, require a phone agent.

**Multi-city itinerary changes**: Changes to complex routings that the online tool cannot display correctly.

**Supervisor escalation**: The app and website have no escalation path. Call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** and ask for a supervisor.

## Frequently asked questions

**What United issues can I resolve without calling customer service?**
Voluntary flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets, cancellation for eCredit, standby additions, eCredit application, boarding passes, seat changes, flight status, and refund request submissions — all work through the United app or united.com.

**How do I change my United flight without calling?**
united.com My Trips → Change Flight, or United app → My Trips → Change. Under 2 minutes for most Classic and Extra tickets.

**How do I cancel my United flight without calling?**
My Trips on united.com or the United app → Cancel. The system shows your outcome before you confirm.

**How do I apply a United eCredit without calling?**
At checkout on united.com, Payment section → Add a Certificate, eCredit or gift card. Or locate the credit first at united.com/redeem.

**What United issues still require calling customer service?**
Cash refunds after United cancellations, waiver code manual application, award ticket destination changes, complex multi-city changes, and supervisor escalation. Call +1-(855)-235-1859.
