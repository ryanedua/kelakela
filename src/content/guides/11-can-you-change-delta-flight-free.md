# Can You Change a Delta Flight for Free? (2026 Rules)

Travel plans change. Whether it is a rescheduled meeting, a family situation, or simply a better flight time opening up, the ability to change a flight without a penalty is a significant part of the booking experience. Delta made major changes to its fee structure starting in 2020, and in 2026 the rules remain largely in favor of passengers — with one critical exception.

## Delta's No Change Fee Policy: What Changed

In October 2020, Delta became one of the first major U.S. carriers to permanently eliminate change fees on most tickets. This was a structural change to Delta's policy, not a temporary waiver. As of 2026, this policy remains in effect.

Before 2020, Delta charged up to $200 for domestic ticket changes and up to $500 for some international changes. Those fees are gone for eligible fare classes.

## Which Delta Fare Classes Allow Free Changes

The no change fee policy applies to:

- **Main Classic** — free to change, fare difference applies
- **Main Extra** — free to change, fare difference applies
- **Delta Comfort+** — free to change, fare difference applies
- **First Class** — free to change, fare difference applies
- **Delta Premium Select** — free to change, fare difference applies
- **Delta One** — free to change, fare difference applies

For all of these fare classes, you pay no change fee to modify your flight. However, if the new flight you choose is more expensive than your original booking, you pay the difference in fare. If the new flight is cheaper, Delta issues the difference as an eCredit.

## The Exception: Delta Main Basic Cannot Be Changed

**Delta Main Basic does not allow voluntary flight changes.** This is the one fare class excluded from the no change fee policy.

If you hold a Main Basic ticket and need to adjust your travel dates, your options are:

1. Cancel the Main Basic ticket and receive an eCredit for the unused value.
2. Rebook a new ticket separately.

This two-step approach effectively achieves the same outcome as a change but without the convenience of a direct change transaction.

## How Fare Differences Work

Even though the change fee is waived, fare differences are still charged when the new flight costs more than your original ticket. Here is how it works:

**Example:** You originally paid $180 for a Main Classic ticket from Atlanta to New York. You want to change to a flight that costs $230 in the same fare class. Delta will charge you the $50 difference. No change fee is added.

If the new flight costs less than your original ticket — say $150 — Delta does not charge you anything, and the $30 difference may be issued as a Delta eCredit for future use, depending on how the change is processed.

## Changing Flights with Medallion Status

Delta Medallion elite members (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) benefit from the same no change fee policy on all eligible fares. Additionally:

- **Same-day confirmed changes** have the $75 fee waived for all Medallion tiers.
- **Same-day standby** is free for all passengers, regardless of status.
- **Upgrade waitlist priority** improves significantly for higher-tier Medallion members when changing to a flight with upgrade availability.

Medallion status does not eliminate fare differences when changing to a higher-cost flight.

## How to Change a Delta Flight Online

### Step-by-Step on Delta.com

1. Log in to your SkyMiles account at delta.com.
2. Navigate to **My Trips** and select your upcoming reservation.
3. Click **Change Flight**.
4. Enter your new preferred travel dates and destination (if permitted by your fare class).
5. Select your new flight from the available options.
6. Review the fare difference summary — no change fee will appear for eligible fares.
7. Confirm the change.

### Using the Fly Delta App

Open the app, go to **My Trips**, select the trip to change, and tap the change flight option. The process mirrors the website experience and allows you to search for and confirm a new flight directly from your phone.

### By Phone

If your itinerary is complex — multiple passengers, connecting flights, international routing — it may be easier to call Delta at **++1-866-332-0756**. An agent can walk you through the available change options and handle fare difference calculations for you.

## Changing International Flights

Delta's no change fee policy also applies to most international tickets, but the specific rules can vary depending on the route, partner airlines involved in the itinerary, and the fare rules of the ticket. For partner-operated international segments, change policies from those operating carriers may apply.

When changing an international ticket with connecting segments on partner airlines, calling Delta at **++1-866-332-0756** is often more efficient than attempting the change online.

## What If You Want to Change Destination

Changing your destination is a more significant modification than changing your travel date. Delta allows destination changes in some situations, subject to:

- The availability of your fare class on the new route
- Fare differences between the original and new routing
- Any restrictions in the original fare rules

Contact Delta or use the online change tool to explore destination change options for your specific booking.

## Tips for Flight Changes

- **Compare prices before changing.** If flights on your new preferred dates are significantly more expensive, consider whether the fare difference is worth the change versus canceling and rebooking at a later date.
- **Check for same-day change options.** If you want to move to an earlier flight on the same day of travel, use same-day standby (free) or a same-day confirmed change ($75 for non-Medallion passengers).
- **Know your fare class before assuming free changes.** Main Basic is easily confused with Main Classic. Double-check your booking confirmation to confirm which fare you purchased.
- **Watch the new ticket price direction.** If you are changing to a cheaper flight, you may receive an eCredit rather than cash back. Keep this in mind when planning.

## Contact Delta Customer Support

For help changing your flight, understanding fare difference calculations, or navigating a complex multi-leg itinerary change, contact Delta Air Lines at **++1-866-332-0756** or visit delta.com.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Delta charge a fee to change a flight in 2026?**

Delta eliminated change fees on most domestic and international tickets in 2020. In 2026, passengers on Main Classic, Main Extra, Comfort+, First Class, Delta Premium Select, and Delta One can change their flights without a change fee. Fare differences still apply. Main Basic tickets cannot be changed.

**Can I change a Delta Main Basic ticket for free?**

No. Delta Main Basic tickets do not allow voluntary flight changes on most routes. If you need to adjust your travel dates, you must cancel the Main Basic ticket and receive an eCredit, then rebook a new flight separately.

**Does changing a Delta flight cost money if I have Medallion status?**

Medallion members can change their flights without a change fee on all eligible fare classes. For same-day confirmed changes, Medallion members also have the $75 fee waived. Fare differences on new bookings still apply regardless of status.

**How do I change my Delta flight online?**

Log in to delta.com, go to 'My Trips,' select your upcoming flight, and choose 'Change Flight.' Select a new flight and confirm. Any fare difference will be displayed before you finalize the change.
