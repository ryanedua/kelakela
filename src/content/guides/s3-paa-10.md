# How Do I Rebook a Cancelled Delta Flight? (2026 Guide)









When Delta cancels your flight, two channels work simultaneously: check the Fly Delta app for automatic rebooking and call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** at the same time. Whichever resolves first is the one you use. Delta often automatically rebooks cancelled flight passengers on the next available service and sends a push notification through the app. If the automatic rebooking does not work for your schedule, reject it and call to request a different alternative or a full cash refund under DOT rules. Act immediately — alternative seats fill within hours during major cancellation events.

## Key Rules to Know









You have two rights after a Delta cancellation: free rebooking on the next available Delta flight (including on SkyTeam partners when no Delta alternative is available), or a full cash refund to your original payment method. The refund right applies regardless of your fare type and regardless of why Delta cancelled. State clearly which you want — Delta often presents eCredits or automatic rebooking first.

## Quick Reference

- Rebooking and cash refund requests: **+1-(866)-332-0756**
- Refund status follow-up: **+1-(855)-321-3528**
- Partner airline rebooking: **+1-(844)-833-1535**
