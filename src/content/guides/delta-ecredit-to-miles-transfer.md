# Delta eCredit to SkyMiles: Can You Convert or Transfer? (2026)

One of the most common questions Delta passengers ask is whether they can convert an eCredit into SkyMiles miles or transfer the credit to someone else. The answer to both is no. Delta eCredits exist as their own form of travel currency with specific rules about where they can go and who can use them. Understanding these limits before your credit expires helps you get full value from it. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** if you have questions about your specific eCredit or want to explore what options are available.

## eCredits Cannot Be Converted to SkyMiles

Delta does not offer any mechanism to convert eCredits into SkyMiles miles. The two systems are entirely separate. eCredits represent a cash-equivalent credit toward revenue flight purchases. SkyMiles is a mileage currency earned through flights, credit card spending, and partner activity and redeemed for award bookings.

There is no exchange rate, no conversion form, and no workaround that turns an eCredit into miles. If someone suggests a method to do this, it does not exist in Delta's systems.

If you want SkyMiles for your next flight, you earn them by booking a revenue flight using your eCredit — the mileage accrual happens based on the ticket you buy, not based on how you pay for it. So if you use a $400 eCredit to book a Main Classic ticket, you earn the SkyMiles associated with that flight in the normal way.

## eCredits Are Non-Transferable

Delta eCredits are tied to the original ticketed passenger. Only the person whose name is on the eCredit can use it to book a future flight. You cannot give an eCredit to a family member, sell it, or apply it to someone else's booking.

There is one nuance worth knowing: you can book a flight for a travel companion using your eCredit as long as you are also on the same booking. If you book a round trip for yourself and a companion and use your eCredit to pay for your own portion, that is permitted. But you cannot use your eCredit to pay for a separate booking in someone else's name.

## What eCredits Can Be Used For

Delta eCredits can be applied toward any of the following:

Any Delta-operated revenue flight (Main Basic, Classic, Extra, Comfort+, First Class, Delta One) booked through delta.com or delta.com/redeem.

Any Delta Connection carrier-operated flight ticketed by Delta.

Combination payment with a credit card when the flight price exceeds the eCredit value.

Combination with other eCredits you hold under the same passenger name.

## What eCredits Cannot Be Used For

Partner airline award bookings. Award tickets booked on Air France, KLM, Korean Air, or other SkyTeam partners through Delta's award booking system cannot be paid with eCredits.

SkyMiles award flights. eCredits do not apply to award tickets regardless of the carrier.

Baggage fees, seat upgrades, or ancillary services separate from the base ticket price in most cases.

Third-party travel platforms. eCredits can only be redeemed on delta.com or delta.com/redeem, not through Expedia, Traveloka, or other booking sites.

## What to Do If You Cannot Use Your eCredit Before It Expires

If your eCredit is approaching its one-year expiration and you have no travel plans, the best strategy is to book a placeholder flight before the expiration date. You only need to book — not travel — before the credit expires. Delta opens its schedule 331 days in advance, giving you the ability to book a flight nearly 11 months ahead.

Book a refundable Classic or Extra fare as the placeholder so you can cancel or change it later when your actual travel plans crystallize. If you book a non-refundable Classic ticket, you can still change the date later without a fee, with only a fare difference applying.

Call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** if you want to explore options for an eCredit that is about to expire or has already expired. Extensions are not standard policy but may be considered case by case for extenuating circumstances.

## If Your eCredit Should Have Been a Cash Refund

In some cases, a passenger receives an eCredit when they should have been entitled to a cash refund — for example, if Delta cancelled their flight or made a qualifying significant schedule change that was incorrectly processed as a voluntary cancellation.

If you believe your eCredit originated from a situation where a cash refund was owed, call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** and ask an agent to review the original cancellation reason. If a qualifying flight cancellation or significant schedule change is documented, the agent can escalate the request to convert the eCredit to a cash refund even if time has passed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I convert a Delta eCredit to SkyMiles?**
No. There is no conversion path between eCredits and miles in Delta's systems.

**Can I transfer my Delta eCredit to another person?**
No. eCredits are non-transferable and can only be used by the original ticketed passenger.

**Can I use a Delta eCredit for an award flight?**
No. eCredits apply only to revenue flights priced in dollars, not to award bookings.

**What can I use a Delta eCredit for?**
Any Delta-operated or Connection revenue flight booked through delta.com, in combination with a card or other eCredits.

**What if my eCredit should have been a cash refund?**
Call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** to review the original cancellation reason and explore cash conversion.


**Can I use a Delta eCredit to book an award flight with SkyMiles?**
No. Delta eCredits cannot be applied toward award tickets booked with SkyMiles. eCredits apply only to revenue flights priced in dollars.

**What happens to my eCredit if Delta goes through a schedule change?**
If your original ticket was cancelled due to a qualifying Delta schedule change, the credit may have been issued in error — you may be entitled to a cash refund instead. Call +1-(855)-321-3528 to review the original cancellation reason and potentially convert to cash.
