# Does Delta Customer Service Work on Weekends?

Yes, Delta customer service is available on weekends at +1-(866)-332-0756. Saturday mornings (9 to 11 AM Eastern) tend to have shorter wait times than Sunday afternoons and evenings, which see elevated volume as weekend travelers encounter issues before Monday. The Fly Delta app handles most self-service tasks on weekends with no wait at any hour.

Delta customer service operates on weekends exactly as it does on weekdays — 24 hours a day with no reduced schedule or weekend-only limitations. The difference between weekend and weekday is not whether agents are available but what the wait times look like at specific hours. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** on any day of the week.

## Weekend Hours: No Reduction

Delta's customer service line at **+1-(866)-332-0756)** is fully staffed on Saturdays and Sundays. There is no Saturday afternoon close, no Sunday morning message directing you to call back Monday, and no reduced-service weekend window. Live agents handle the full range of requests on weekends — rebooking, refunds, eCredit questions, waiver applications, and supervisor escalations.

## Saturday: Generally the Quieter Weekend Day

Saturday mornings between 9 and 11 AM Eastern are among the quieter weekend windows for customer service calls. Most weekend travelers are already at their destination by Saturday, and the pre-travel call surge that happens on Fridays has passed.

Saturday afternoons are moderately busy as some travelers begin thinking about Sunday departures. Saturday evenings pick up slightly as Sunday travelers start encountering issues.

Best time to call on Saturday: **9 to 11 AM Eastern**.

## Sunday: Elevated Afternoon Volume

Sunday is the busier of the two weekend days. Sunday afternoons (2 to 7 PM Eastern) see elevated call volume as weekend travelers encounter disruptions, last-minute changes, and pre-Monday travel concerns. The Sunday evening window (6 to 9 PM Eastern) can be particularly busy as travelers scramble before Monday morning flights.

Sunday mornings before noon Eastern are generally quieter and comparable to moderate weekday windows.

Best time to call on Sunday: **before noon Eastern**.

## Weekend Self-Service: No Wait at Any Hour

The Fly Delta app and delta.com handle most self-service tasks on weekends with no hold time: voluntary flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets, cancellations, eCredit application, standby additions, flight status, and boarding passes.

For weekend issues that do not require a human agent, the app is faster than calling regardless of Saturday or Sunday.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Delta customer service slower on weekends?**
Wait times vary. Saturday mid-mornings are comparable to weekday off-peak. Sunday afternoons are busier. Neither is as consistently slow as Monday morning or Friday afternoon.

**What is the fastest way to reach Delta customer service on a weekend?**
