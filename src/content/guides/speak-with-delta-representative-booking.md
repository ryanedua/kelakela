# How to Speak With a Delta Representative About Your Booking

To speak with a Delta representative about your booking, call +1-(866)-332-0756 with your 6-character confirmation code ready. Press 0 to bypass the IVR. State your confirmation code and the specific change you need in the first sentence. For booking issues that can be resolved without a call, the Fly Delta app and delta.com My Trips handle most standard changes instantly.

Speaking with a Delta representative about your booking is most effective when you arrive at the call prepared. Agents resolve bookings faster when they have the confirmation code in the first sentence and a specific request to work toward. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** with your confirmation code ready before you dial.

## What to Have Ready Before You Call

Your **6-character booking confirmation code** from your confirmation email. This is the single most important thing to have ready. The agent enters it immediately to pull up your reservation.

The **names of all passengers** on the booking, exactly as they appear on the confirmation.

Your **preferred outcome**: the specific flight you want to move to, or a cancellation for eCredit, or a cash refund. Knowing what you want before you call shortens the resolution time significantly.

A **credit card** in case a fare difference applies to a change.

Your **SkyMiles number** if you hold Medallion status — this routes your call with higher priority.

## What to Say When the Agent Answers

State your confirmation code and your request in the first sentence:

"My confirmation code is ABC123. I need to move my July 25 flight from the 2 PM to the 6 PM Atlanta to Los Angeles departure."

"My confirmation is ABC123. Delta cancelled my July 25 flight and I want a full cash refund to my original payment method."

"My confirmation is ABC123. I need to apply my eCredit to rebook on the July 28 departure instead."

Agents who receive specific requests process them faster than agents who spend the first few minutes asking clarifying questions about what the passenger actually needs.

## When You Do Not Need to Call

Many booking-related tasks do not require a live representative:

Voluntary flight date and time changes on Classic and Extra tickets: delta.com My Trips → Change Flight.
eCredit application at checkout: delta.com/redeem or the Payment section at checkout.
Cancellation for eCredit: My Trips → Cancel.
Seat changes on eligible bookings: My Trips → Manage Seats or the Fly Delta app.
Same-day standby additions: Fly Delta app → My Trips → Same-Day Change.

Use these channels first. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** only when the self-service tools cannot complete your specific request.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I speak with a Delta representative about my booking?**
Call +1-(866)-332-0756 with your 6-character booking confirmation code ready. Press 0 to bypass the IVR. State your confirmation code and specific request in the first sentence.

**What information do I need before calling Delta about my booking?**
Your 6-character confirmation code, passenger names, preferred outcome (rebook, cancel, refund), and a credit card if a fare difference might apply.

**Can I change my Delta booking without calling a representative?**
Yes for most standard changes via the Fly Delta app or delta.com My Trips. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 when online tools cannot complete your request.

**What booking issues require speaking with a Delta representative?**
Complex multi-city changes, waiver code application issues, award ticket destination changes, cash refund requests after Delta cancellations, and supervisor escalations.

**What should I say to a Delta representative when calling about my booking?**
Lead with your confirmation code and the specific outcome: 'My confirmation is ABC123. I need to change my July 25 flight to the 6 PM departure.'
