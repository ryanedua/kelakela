# Delta Flight Cancelled: What Are You Entitled to in 2026?

When Delta cancels your flight, you have more rights than most passengers realize — and fewer than some assume. The DOT rules that took effect in 2024 significantly strengthened passenger refund rights, but US law still does not require cash compensation for the inconvenience itself. What you are entitled to depends partly on why Delta cancelled and partly on what choices you make in response. Knowing exactly what to ask for and when prevents you from leaving money and accommodations on the table. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** immediately after a cancellation notification and work through each entitlement on the call.

## Entitlement 1: Full Cash Refund or Free Rebooking

This is the baseline entitlement under DOT rules and applies regardless of the reason for the cancellation, your fare type, or whether you have a refundable ticket.

**Cash refund**: If Delta cancels your flight and you choose not to travel, you are entitled to a full refund of the ticket price, plus any prepaid seat upgrades, checked bag fees, and preferred seat fees, returned to your original payment method. Credit card refunds must process within 7 business days. You must ask for this — Delta often presents eCredits or rebooking first.

**Free rebooking**: If you want to travel, Delta will rebook you on the next available Delta flight at no additional charge. No fare difference, no change fee.

Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and state your preference: "Delta cancelled my flight. I want [a full cash refund to my original payment method] OR [free rebooking on the next available flight]."

## Entitlement 2: Rebooking on SkyTeam Partner Airlines

If no adequate Delta alternative is available in a reasonable timeframe, Delta's Customer Commitment includes the option to rebook you on a SkyTeam alliance partner airline at no cost. Partners include Air France, KLM, Korean Air, and ITA Airways.

This is not offered automatically. You must specifically request partner rebooking when there are no acceptable Delta alternatives. Tell the agent: "There are no Delta flights that work for my schedule. Can you rebook me on a SkyTeam partner instead?"

## Entitlement 3: Meal Vouchers (Controllable Cancellations Only)

When Delta cancels a flight due to a reason within the airline's control — mechanical problems, crew issues, operational decisions — and the resulting wait is 3 or more hours beyond the original scheduled departure time, Delta provides meal vouchers or meals for affected passengers.

**This does not apply to weather cancellations.** Weather is considered outside Delta's control, so the meal voucher obligation does not apply. However, Delta agents sometimes offer meal vouchers at their discretion even for weather events — ask directly.

Request meal vouchers from the gate agent as soon as the cancellation is announced. Do not wait for them to be offered proactively.

## Entitlement 4: Hotel Accommodation (Controllable Overnight Cancellations)

When a controllable cancellation results in an overnight disruption — meaning you cannot complete your journey the same day — Delta provides:

A complimentary hotel room at a partner property near the departure airport.
Round-trip ground transportation to and from the hotel.
Meal vouchers for dinner and breakfast.

**Weather cancellations do not trigger the hotel obligation.** If a storm cancels your flight overnight, Delta is not required to provide a hotel. The cash refund right still applies if you choose not to rebook.

Request hotel accommodation at the gate or by calling **+1-(866)-332-0756)**. Hotels fill quickly during large disruption events — request as early as possible.

## Entitlement 5: Goodwill Compensation (Discretionary)

US law does not require Delta to pay cash compensation beyond the ticket refund for flight cancellations. This contrasts with EU regulation EC 261/2004, which requires up to €600 in cash compensation for flights departing from EU airports.

However, Delta agents have discretion to offer goodwill eCredits, travel vouchers, or SkyMiles for significant disruptions, particularly controllable cancellations that cause major inconvenience. After your rebooking or refund is processed, ask the agent: "Is there any goodwill compensation available for this disruption?"

For EU-departing flights, file a separate EC 261 claim for the statutory cash compensation.

## What You Are Not Entitled To

Cash compensation purely for the inconvenience of a domestic US cancellation (beyond the ticket refund). Delta is not required by US law to pay you extra for the disruption itself.

Hotel accommodation for weather-caused cancellations.

Rebooking on non-SkyTeam airlines unless Delta specifically offers it.

Meals and hotel for cancellations below the 3-hour wait threshold (for controllable events).

## Documenting Your Entitlements

Keep records of everything: the cancellation notification, any agent names and reference numbers, receipts for any out-of-pocket expenses, and confirmation of any accommodations provided. Submit expense claims at delta.com/feedback if vouchers were not provided during the disruption.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What am I entitled to when Delta cancels my flight?**
Cash refund or free rebooking, plus meal vouchers and hotel for controllable overnight cancellations.

**Do I get a hotel if Delta cancels my flight due to weather?**
No. Hotel is only required for controllable cancellations. But the cash refund right still applies.

**Am I entitled to cash or just an eCredit?**
Cash, under the 2024 DOT rule. Ask explicitly for cash — Delta may present eCredit first.

**Does my fare type affect what I'm entitled to?**
No. DOT rights apply to all fare types including Basic Economy when Delta cancels.

**Can I get additional cash compensation beyond the refund?**
Not under US law. EU EC 261 may apply for EU-departing flights. Goodwill credits are discretionary.


**Am I entitled to cash or just an eCredit when Delta cancels my flight?**
You are entitled to cash under the 2024 DOT rule. Delta cannot default you into an eCredit. When Delta cancels, say explicitly that you want a full cash refund to your original payment method. Delta often presents eCredits or rebooking first — you must ask for cash to receive it.

**Does my fare type affect what I'm entitled to when Delta cancels?**
No. The DOT cash refund right and free rebooking entitlement apply to all fare types when Delta cancels, including Basic Economy. Basic Economy change and refund restrictions only apply to voluntary changes by the passenger, not to Delta-initiated cancellations.

**Can I get additional compensation beyond the refund for a Delta cancellation?**
For controllable cancellations (not weather), Delta agents have discretion to offer goodwill eCredits or SkyMiles compensation for significant disruptions. US law does not require cash compensation beyond the ticket refund for cancellations. EU regulation EC 261/2004 may require cash compensation for flights departing from EU airports.
