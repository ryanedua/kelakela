# Delta Basic Economy Baggage Rules 2026 (Carry-On & Checked)

One of the most common questions travelers ask before booking Delta Main Basic is whether the low price comes with baggage restrictions. Unlike some ultra-low-cost carriers that charge for carry-ons, Delta takes a different approach with its Main Basic fare. This guide clarifies exactly what baggage allowances apply and what fees you can expect.

## Delta Main Basic Carry-On Policy

### One Carry-On Bag and One Personal Item

Delta Main Basic passengers are allowed to bring:

- **One carry-on bag** — placed in the overhead bin
- **One personal item** — placed under the seat in front of you

There is no fee for either item on domestic flights. This is an important distinction from some other carriers' basic economy products, where overhead bin access may be restricted or charged extra.

### Carry-On Size Requirements

Delta's carry-on size limit applies to all passengers, including Main Basic:

- **Maximum dimensions:** 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 35 x 23 cm), including handles and wheels
- Items that do not fit within these dimensions may need to be checked

Personal items — such as a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack — must fit under the seat in front of you.

### Overhead Bin Access and Boarding Priority

While Main Basic passengers can bring a carry-on bag, there is a practical limitation: **Main Basic passengers board last**. By the time Main Basic passengers board, overhead bin space near their seats may already be full.

If the bins are full when you board, Delta gate agents may check your carry-on at the gate. This bag check is typically free of charge (since it happens at the gate), but your bag will arrive at baggage claim rather than being retrieved from the overhead bin.

Travelers who are particular about overhead bin access may want to consider Main Classic or Main Extra, which offer earlier boarding positions.

## Delta Main Basic Checked Bag Policy

### Checked Bag Fees Apply

Unlike some premium fare classes where checked bags are included, Delta Main Basic does not include free checked baggage. Checked bag fees apply and are charged at the same rates as other economy fare classes.

For most domestic U.S. routes in 2026, estimated checked bag fees are approximately:

| Bag | Approximate Fee |
|---|---|
| First checked bag | ~$35 |
| Second checked bag | ~$45 |
| Third+ bags (oversize/overweight) | $150–$200 |

Exact fees depend on your specific route, destination, and when you pay (fees paid at the airport may be higher than those paid online in advance). Always verify current rates on delta.com before your trip.

### Checked Bag Fees for International Flights

Checked bag policies vary more significantly on international routes. Some international itineraries include a free first checked bag based on the route. Check your specific itinerary's baggage allowance in your booking confirmation, as international baggage rules differ from domestic.

## How to Avoid or Reduce Checked Bag Fees on Main Basic

### Delta SkyMiles American Express Cards

Eligible Delta SkyMiles co-branded American Express cardholders can receive a first checked bag free for themselves and up to eight companions on the same reservation. This applies even when flying on Main Basic, provided the ticket was purchased with the eligible card.

Specific card products and their benefits include the Delta SkyMiles Gold, Platinum, and Reserve American Express cards, among others. Check the current terms of your card for confirmation.

### Medallion Status

Delta Medallion elite members (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) receive complimentary checked bags based on their status level:

- **Silver Medallion:** First checked bag free
- **Gold Medallion:** First two checked bags free
- **Platinum Medallion:** First three checked bags free
- **Diamond Medallion:** First three checked bags free

These benefits apply regardless of the fare class purchased, including Main Basic.

### Active Military

Active U.S. military personnel traveling on personal or official orders receive checked bag fee waivers on most Delta flights. Documentation may be required.

## Baggage on Delta Connecting Flights

If your Main Basic ticket involves a connection, baggage is checked through to your final destination. You do not need to retrieve and re-check your bag at the connecting airport for most itineraries. Verify this on your itinerary, particularly for international connections where customs clearance may require bag retrieval.

## Special Items and Oversized Baggage

Special items — sports equipment, strollers, musical instruments, oversized bags — are subject to additional fees and policies that apply to all Delta passengers, including Main Basic travelers. Check delta.com for the specific fee schedule for:

- Golf clubs
- Bicycles
- Musical instruments
- Ski equipment
- Strollers and car seats

Strollers and car seats for children are typically checked free of charge.

## Tips for Main Basic Passengers

- **Pack light to carry-on only.** Avoiding checked bags on Main Basic saves money and time.
- **Board early by paying for Main Extra or Comfort+.** If overhead bin access matters, upgrading your fare or buying a Comfort+ seat may be worth the extra cost.
- **Use a Delta AmEx card to cover checked bags.** If you have an eligible Delta credit card, your first bag is typically free.
- **Weigh your bag before arriving at the airport.** Overweight bags (over 50 lbs for the first bag) incur significant extra charges that apply to all fare classes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Delta Main Basic allow a carry-on bag?**

Yes, Delta Main Basic passengers are permitted one carry-on bag and one personal item on domestic flights at no additional charge. However, Main Basic passengers board in the last group, so overhead bin space may be limited.

**How much does a checked bag cost on Delta Main Basic?**

Delta Main Basic passengers pay the same checked bag fees as other economy fares. For most domestic U.S. routes in 2026, the first checked bag fee is around $35 and the second is around $45. Fees vary by route, so verify current rates at delta.com before your flight.

**Does having a Delta SkyMiles card waive bag fees on Main Basic?**

Yes. Eligible Delta SkyMiles co-branded American Express cardholders typically receive a first checked bag free for themselves and up to eight companions on the same reservation, even on Main Basic tickets. The card must be used to purchase the ticket.

**What are the size limits for a carry-on on Delta Main Basic?**

Delta's carry-on size limit is 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 35 x 23 cm), including handles and wheels. Personal items must fit under the seat in front of you. These limits apply to all fare classes, including Main Basic.
