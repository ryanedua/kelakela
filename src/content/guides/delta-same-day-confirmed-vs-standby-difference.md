# Delta Same-Day Confirmed vs Standby: Key Differences in 2026

Delta offers two ways to get on an earlier flight on the day of travel: Same-Day Confirmed and Same-Day Standby. They sound similar but work very differently. One guarantees your seat and costs $75. The other is free but depends entirely on availability at the gate. Choosing between them comes down to one question: how certain do you need to be that you get on the earlier flight? Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to request either option or check your eligibility before arriving at the airport.

## Same-Day Confirmed: Guaranteed Seat for $75

Same-Day Confirmed is a paid option that locks in a confirmed seat on a specific alternate Delta flight on the same travel day. When you select a flight through Same-Day Confirmed, you receive a new boarding pass immediately. Your original flight reservation is cancelled and replaced with the confirmed new booking.

**Cost**: $75 for general SkyMiles members, Silver Medallion members, and non-members. The fee is waived entirely for Gold Medallion, Platinum Medallion, and Diamond Medallion members on eligible routes.

**No fare difference**: As long as the new flight is in the same cabin class as your original booking, no fare difference is charged. You are not paying the price gap between your original departure and the new one — only the $75 service fee. This is one of the most underappreciated advantages of the same-day change system.

**Seat availability requirement**: A seat in your original fare class must be available on the new flight. If the flight is fully booked in your fare class, Confirmed is not available for that departure.

**How to request**: Open the Fly Delta app and go to Same-Day Change, select Confirmed for your preferred flight. Or request it at the airport check-in counter, gate agent, or by calling **+1-(866)-332-0756)**.

## Same-Day Standby: Free but No Guarantee

Same-Day Standby places you on the waitlist for an earlier Delta flight on the same travel day. You keep your original confirmed seat throughout the standby process. If a seat opens on the earlier flight before it departs, you are assigned and given a new boarding pass. If no seat opens, you board your original flight with no disruption to your booking.

**Cost**: Free for all eligible passengers. No fee is charged at any point — not when you join the list, not if you get a seat, and not if standby does not clear.

**No fare difference**: Same as Confirmed — no fare difference applies when the new flight is in the same cabin.

**How clearing works**: Your position on the standby list is determined by Medallion status and check-in order. Diamond members are processed first, then Platinum, Gold, Silver, and general members. Within the same tier, passengers who joined the standby list earlier have priority.

**Uncertainty is the defining characteristic**: Standby does not guarantee you get on the flight. You may wait at the gate and board your original departure if no seat opens. This is acceptable when you are truly flexible, less so when timing matters.

**How to request**: Open the Fly Delta app under Same-Day Change, select Standby. Or request at the airport counter or by calling **+1-(866)-332-0756)**.

## Side-by-Side Comparison

Guaranteed seat: Confirmed yes, Standby no.
Cost: Confirmed $75 (waived for Gold/Platinum/Diamond), Standby free.
Your original booking: Confirmed is cancelled when you switch, Standby is preserved until you clear.
Fare difference: Neither charges one when staying in the same cabin.
When seat must be available: Confirmed requires availability in original fare class. Standby requires any open seat.
Medallion benefit: Confirmed fee waived for Gold+. Standby is free for everyone — Medallion status only affects list priority.

## Which Option Is Right for You?

**Choose Confirmed when**:
- You need to be on a specific earlier flight with certainty.
- You have a connection, meeting, or other time-sensitive commitment at your destination.
- You are a Gold, Platinum, or Diamond Medallion member and the fee is waived anyway — always use Confirmed.

**Choose Standby when**:
- You are flexible about whether you get on the earlier flight.
- You are willing to wait at the gate for a seat to open.
- Saving the $75 matters more than certainty.
- You are a Silver or non-Medallion member and want to try for an earlier flight without paying.

## What Neither Option Covers

Both Same-Day Confirmed and Same-Day Standby apply primarily to domestic US routes. Long-haul international flights generally do not qualify.

Neither option is available for Delta Main Basic (Basic Economy) tickets. If you hold a Basic fare and want to be on an earlier flight, you would need to purchase a new ticket for the earlier departure.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between Same-Day Confirmed and Same-Day Standby?**
Confirmed guarantees a seat for $75 (waived for Gold/Platinum/Diamond). Standby is free but uncertain — you get a seat only if one opens.

**How much does Delta Same-Day Confirmed cost?**
$75 for most passengers. Waived for Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion members.

**Is Delta Same-Day Standby free?**
Yes, for all eligible passengers regardless of status.

**Does Same-Day Confirmed charge a fare difference?**
No. Neither option charges a fare difference when staying in the same cabin.

**Which should I choose?**
Confirmed for certainty, especially if the fee is waived by status. Standby to save $75 when flexibility is acceptable.
