# Which JetBlue Department to Contact for Your Specific Issue

Most JetBlue issues — flight changes, cancellations, eCredits, refunds, and disruption rebooking — are handled by the main customer service line at +1-(855)-234-9895. Refund status follow-up uses +1-(855)-234-9895. Baggage claims use the dedicated baggage line. Award ticket changes are also handled through the main line but state 'TrueBlue award ticket' when prompted.

JetBlue's customer service is primarily routed through one main line, but knowing how to direct your call within that system — and when to use a different number or channel — means reaching the right person without transfers. Call **+1-(855)-234-9895)** for most issues and use the routing guide below.

## Main Customer Service Line: +1-(855)-234-9895

This number handles the majority of JetBlue customer service requests:

Flight changes and date modifications on all fare types. Cancellations and eCredit processing. Rebooking after flight cancellations and disruptions. Travel waiver application and rebooking. Same-day confirmed changes and standby additions. TrueBlue award ticket changes and cancellations. Cash refund requests after qualifying JetBlue-initiated events. Supervisor escalation for all issue types. General booking inquiries and confirmation lookups.

When you reach the IVR, press 0 to route to the live agent queue. If the system asks you to describe your issue, saying "cancel" or "change" routes to the human queue faster.

## Refund Status and eCredit Follow-Up: +1-(855)-234-9895

For refund status checks on pending refund requests, eCredit balance and expiration date inquiries, escalation of refund requests not resolved on the main line, and eCredit reinstatement requests after expiration.

## Baggage Issues: In Person First, Then Online

For delayed, damaged, or missing baggage: go to the JetBlue baggage service office at the arrival airport immediately after your flight. Do not leave the airport without filing a report — the in-person report starts the claim process.

For follow-up after you have left the airport: use jetblue.com baggage tracking, or call +1-(855)-234-9895 and say "baggage" when the IVR prompts you.

## Written Complaints and Compensation: jetblue.com/contact-us

For formal complaints after a disruption, requests for goodwill compensation not resolved by phone, and documentation of incidents for record purposes. Responses typically arrive within 5 to 7 business days.

For DOT-mandated cash refund disputes — when JetBlue has not processed a refund within 7 business days of a qualifying cancellation — submit at jetblue.com/refunds and file at transportation.gov/airconsumer.

## Self-Service: Fly JetBlue App and jetblue.com

For tasks that do not require a human agent at all: flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets, eCredit application at checkout, cancellation processing, same-day standby additions, flight status, and boarding pass retrieval. Available 24 hours with no hold time.

## Frequently asked questions

**What number should I call for JetBlue flight changes and cancellations?**
Call +1-(855)-234-9895 for all flight changes, cancellations, rebooking after disruptions, and same-day changes.

**What number should I call for JetBlue eCredit and refund questions?**
Call +1-(855)-234-9895 for eCredit balance checks, expiration inquiries, and refund status follow-up.

**How do I reach JetBlue's baggage department?**
Go to the JetBlue baggage service office at the arrival airport immediately after your flight. For follow-up, call +1-(855)-234-9895 and say 'baggage' when prompted.

**Which JetBlue department handles TrueBlue award tickets?**
The main line at +1-(855)-234-9895. Say 'TrueBlue award' when the IVR asks for your reason.

**How do I reach JetBlue customer service for a complaint or compensation request?**
Call +1-(855)-234-9895 and ask for a customer satisfaction representative. For written complaints, submit at jetblue.com/contact-us.
