# Delta Weather Waiver: How to Rebook Online in 2026

When a Delta weather waiver is active for your flight, rebooking online through My Trips is the fastest option — faster than calling, faster than going to the airport, and available from anywhere. The key is doing it early. Alternative flights fill within hours once a major storm waiver is announced. This guide walks through the exact steps to rebook fee-free via delta.com or the Fly Delta app, and what to do if something goes wrong. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** if the online process is not reflecting the $0 fee or if your preferred flights are unavailable.

## Before You Start: Confirm the Waiver Covers Your Flight

Go to delta.com/travelinformation and find the active advisory. Look for an advisory that lists your departure or arrival airport within the specified travel date range. Each waiver includes:

The covered airports — your origin and/or destination must be listed.
The affected travel date range — your flight's departure date must fall within this window.
The rebooking deadline — the date by which your new travel must begin.
The ticket purchase window — some waivers only apply to tickets bought before a certain date.

If your flight qualifies, proceed to rebooking. If your airport is not listed, call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to ask whether any goodwill accommodation applies to your situation.

## Step-by-Step: Rebook on delta.com

**Step 1 — Log in**: Go to delta.com and sign in to your SkyMiles account. If you booked as a guest, use your booking confirmation number and last name to access My Trips.

**Step 2 — Go to My Trips**: Navigate to My Trips from your account menu or go directly to delta.com/mytrips.

**Step 3 — Select your affected booking**: Find the flight covered by the waiver and click on it to open the booking detail.

**Step 4 — Click Change Flight**: Look for the Change Flight option on the booking detail page. If this button is not visible, your ticket may not be eligible for online changes — call **+1-(866)-332-0756** instead.

**Step 5 — Search for available alternatives**: A flight search interface opens within your booking. The system filters results to show flights eligible under the waiver. Enter your preferred new departure date.

**Step 6 — Verify the fee shows $0**: When you select an eligible alternative flight, the summary should display $0 in change fees. This confirms the waiver is being applied. If any fee appears, do not confirm the booking. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to have an agent apply the waiver code manually.

**Step 7 — Confirm**: Review your new itinerary, confirm the change, and receive a new booking confirmation by email.

## Step-by-Step: Rebook on the Fly Delta App

**Step 1**: Open the Fly Delta app and tap My Trips at the bottom of the screen.

**Step 2**: Select your affected flight from the trip list.

**Step 3**: Tap Change Flight or Modify Trip. The app will show available alternatives within the waiver window.

**Step 4**: Verify the $0 fee on your selected alternative flight before confirming.

**Step 5**: Confirm the change. Your new boarding pass will be available in the app immediately.

The app experience is generally the same as the website. Some passengers find the app faster on mobile devices during high-traffic disruption periods when the website may be slower.

## What to Do If a Fee Appears

If the online system shows any fee other than $0 during an active waiver:

Do not confirm the booking. Confirming with a fee means you are paying out of pocket for something you should get for free.

Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** and tell the agent: "I am trying to rebook under the active [storm/weather] waiver for [airport] and the online system is showing a fee. Can you apply the waiver code and complete the rebooking without a fee?"

Agents can apply waiver codes manually and complete the rebooking by phone. A phone ticketing fee does not apply when the reason for calling is a waiver code issue that the online system is not handling correctly.

## Choosing the Best Available Alternative

When many passengers are rebooking simultaneously during a storm, the most desirable flights fill quickly. To maximize your options:

Act immediately when the waiver is announced rather than waiting until the day before or day of your original departure.

Target early morning departures and midweek days within the waiver window. These tend to have more open seats than Friday evenings, Sunday afternoons, or the day immediately after a storm.

Look at connecting options if direct flights are full. A connection through a less-affected hub may get you to your destination faster than waiting for a direct seat to open.

Stay in the same cabin class. The waiver covers same-cabin rebooking. Moving to a higher cabin requires paying the fare difference.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I rebook online under a Delta weather waiver?**
Log in to My Trips on delta.com, select your affected booking, click Change Flight, pick an alternative within the waiver window, and confirm at $0 fee.

**What if the online rebooking shows a fee?**
Do not confirm. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to have the waiver applied manually.

**How do I know which flights qualify under the waiver?**
The online system filters eligible flights. You must stay in the same cabin class. Flights outside the waiver date range show standard pricing.

**Can I rebook using the Fly Delta app?**
Yes. Go to My Trips, select your flight, and follow the same steps.

**What if my preferred flight is full during the waiver?**
Try off-peak times. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** if all visible options are unavailable.


**What if the online rebooking shows a fee during an active waiver?**
Do not confirm the booking. A fee appearing during an active waiver usually means the waiver code has not been applied to your booking. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 and ask an agent to apply the waiver code manually and complete the rebooking without the fee.

**How do I know which flights qualify for rebooking under the waiver?**
The online interface filters available flights to those within the waiver's allowed rebooking window. You must stay in the same cabin class as your original booking. Flights outside the waiver date range will show standard pricing.

**Can I rebook a Delta weather waiver using the Fly Delta app?**
Yes. Open the app, go to My Trips, select your affected flight, and tap the change or rebook option. The app applies the waiver the same way as the website. The same $0 fee should appear for eligible alternatives within the waiver window.

**What if my preferred flight is full during the weather waiver?**
Try flights at off-peak times — early morning departures and midweek dates tend to have more open seats. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 if all visible options are full, as agents can sometimes access inventory not displayed in the standard online search.
