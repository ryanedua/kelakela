# What Is a Delta Travel Waiver? (2026 Explanation)

A Delta travel waiver is a temporary policy Delta activates when a significant disruption — typically a major weather event, an international security situation, or a major operational failure — is expected to affect flight operations at specific airports. When a waiver is active, passengers with tickets to, from, or through the covered airports within the specified travel date range can rebook on alternative Delta flights without paying change fees or fare differences, or cancel for an eCredit with the standard cancellation fee waived. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to rebook under an active waiver or check delta.com/travelinformation for the list of covered airports.









## Key Rules to Know

Each waiver specifies covered airports, affected travel date ranges, and a rebooking deadline. You must rebook or cancel within the waiver window to use it. Voluntary cancellations under a waiver produce an eCredit — if Delta cancels your flight entirely, you are entitled to a full cash refund instead under DOT rules.
