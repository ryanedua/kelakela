# Delta Same-Day Standby Policy 2026: Complete Guide

Same-Day Standby is Delta's way of letting you try for an earlier flight without giving up your confirmed seat or paying a fee. If you arrive at the airport ahead of schedule and want to catch an earlier departure, standby lets you take that shot with zero downside — your original booking stays intact the entire time. Here is exactly how it works, who qualifies, and how to get on the list fast. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to add yourself to standby by phone or use the Fly Delta app for the fastest self-service option.

## How Same-Day Standby Works

Same-Day Standby is a request to be placed on a waiting list for an earlier Delta flight on the same travel day. You are not confirmed on the earlier flight. Your original booking remains active and your seat on the original flight is preserved.

If a seat opens on the earlier flight before it departs, Delta assigns it to you and issues a new boarding pass. You board the earlier flight and your original seat is released to another standby passenger or sold. If no seat opens, you board your original flight as planned. No fee is charged in either scenario.

This is the key distinction from Same-Day Confirmed. Standby is a bet on availability with no cost and no certainty. Confirmed is a $75 guarantee of a seat on your chosen alternative flight.

## Who Is Eligible for Same-Day Standby

Same-Day Standby is available for passengers on the following ticket types for domestic US routes:

Main Cabin Classic, Main Cabin Extra, Comfort+, Delta First, and Delta One.

**Not eligible**: Delta Main Basic (Basic Economy) fares are excluded from Same-Day Standby. If you booked the lowest Main Basic fare, you cannot join the standby list for an earlier flight. Your only option would be to purchase a new ticket separately.

**Medallion priority**: Diamond, Platinum, Gold, and Silver Medallion members are placed higher on the standby list than non-Medallion passengers. Among non-Medallion passengers, standby priority follows check-in order — earlier check-in generally means higher placement on the list.

## How to Request Same-Day Standby

**Via the Fly Delta app** is the fastest method. Open the app, tap My Trips, select your booking, and choose Same-Day Change. Browse available earlier flights and select Standby for the flight you want. Your name is added to the list immediately. You can monitor your position in real time through the app as the flight approaches departure.

**At the airport**: Approach the Delta check-in counter or gate agent and ask to be added to the standby list for an earlier flight. Give your booking confirmation and preferred alternative departure time. The agent adds you to the list and notes your original confirmed booking.

**By phone**: Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** and request standby addition. Provide your booking confirmation and the earlier flight you want to target. Phone standby requests are useful if you want to be listed before arriving at the airport.

## When to Add Yourself to Standby

Add yourself to the standby list as early as possible on your travel day. Earlier standby requests typically receive higher priority on the list, particularly for non-Medallion passengers where list order matters.

Standby requests can generally be made starting at midnight on the day of travel through the departure time of the earlier flight you want. The earlier you request relative to the target flight's departure, the more time Delta has to process your placement and the better your position on the list.

At airports with multiple earlier departures to your destination, you can target multiple flights — ask the agent or check the app for the option to add yourself to more than one standby list simultaneously.

## Standby Seat Assignment and Notification

If a seat opens on the earlier flight, Delta sends a push notification through the Fly Delta app and issues a new boarding pass electronically. Check the app regularly as departure approaches, particularly in the 30 to 60 minutes before the target flight.

If you are at the airport, you may also receive a gate announcement or an update from the gate agent. Gate agents manage the final standby list as the boarding door closes.

A seat is not guaranteed regardless of your position on the list. If the flight is full with confirmed passengers and other higher-priority standby passengers ahead of you, you board your original flight.

## Same-Day Standby vs Same-Day Confirmed: Which to Choose

Choose Standby when: you want to try for an earlier flight without cost, you are flexible about whether you get on the earlier departure, and saving the $75 fee matters more than certainty.

Choose Confirmed when: you need to be on a specific earlier flight with certainty, you have a time-sensitive connection or appointment at your destination, and the $75 fee is worth the guarantee. For Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion members, the confirmed fee is waived — making Confirmed the clear choice since it costs nothing and guarantees a seat.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Delta same-day standby free?**
Yes. No fee is charged for Same-Day Standby, even if you successfully get a seat on the earlier flight.

**Who is eligible for Delta Same-Day Standby?**
Main Cabin Classic, Extra, Comfort+, First Class, and Delta One on domestic routes. Main Basic (Basic Economy) is excluded.

**How do I add myself to the Delta Same-Day Standby list?**
Via the Fly Delta app under Same-Day Change, at the airport counter, or by calling **+1-(866)-332-0756**.

**What happens to my original seat if I don't get off the standby list?**
Your original booking remains active. If standby does not clear, you board your original flight as planned.

**Can I do Same-Day Standby on an international Delta flight?**
Generally no. Same-Day Standby applies primarily to domestic US routes. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to check your specific route.
