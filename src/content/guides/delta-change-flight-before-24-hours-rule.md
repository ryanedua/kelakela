# Changing a Delta Flight Within 24 Hours of Departure: What's Available

Changing a Delta flight close to departure is not just possible — for most fare types, the same no-change-fee policy that applies when you change months out also applies hours before departure. The only costs are fare differences on standard changes, or the $75 Same-Day Confirmed fee for same-day schedule adjustments. The 24-hour period before a flight is when same-day change options become available in addition to standard change capabilities. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for any last-minute change request.

## Standard Changes: No Special Fee Within 24 Hours

For Main Classic and Main Extra tickets, the standard no-change-fee policy applies at any point before the flight departs, including within the last 24 hours. If you want to move your travel to a different date, a different flight tomorrow, or a completely different route, the change process works the same way it did months earlier.

Log in to My Trips on delta.com or the Fly Delta app, select your booking, and click Change Flight. The system shows available alternatives. Fare difference applies if the new option is more expensive. If the new option is cheaper, the difference becomes an eCredit (Classic) or cash refund (Extra).

There is no "last-minute fee" or surcharge for changing close to departure on Classic or Extra tickets.

## Same-Day Change Options: Unique to the Day of Travel

Within 24 hours of departure — specifically for changes to a different flight on the same calendar day — two additional options become available that have different cost structures from standard changes.

**Same-Day Confirmed**: Move to an earlier or later Delta flight on the same day. Cost: $75 for non-Medallion and Silver Medallion passengers. Waived for Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion. **No fare difference** — this is the key advantage. Even if the earlier flight is priced $200 higher in the market, you pay only the $75 same-day fee.

**Same-Day Standby**: Join the waitlist for an earlier flight on the same day at no cost. Keep your original booking active throughout. Seat only guaranteed if one opens before departure. **No fare difference** if you get a seat.

These options are available via the Fly Delta app under Same-Day Change, at the airport check-in counter, or by calling **+1-(866)-332-0756)**.

## The 24-Hour Purchase Window vs 24 Hours Before Departure

There are two different "24-hour" rules in Delta's policy and they are easy to confuse:

**24 hours after purchase (risk-free cancellation window)**: Cancel any fare type for a full cash refund within 24 hours of buying the ticket.

**24 hours before departure (same-day change window)**: Same-day change options (Confirmed and Standby) become available for eligible fares within 24 hours of the flight's scheduled departure.

These are independent time references. A passenger might be 20 hours before their flight (within the same-day change window) and also 3 months past their purchase date (the 24-hour purchase window closed long ago).

## Basic Economy: No Changes After Purchase Window

Main Basic tickets cannot be changed after the 24-hour purchase window closes. This rule applies regardless of how close to departure you are. Whether you are 6 months before the flight or 2 hours before the flight, the outcome is the same: cancel for an eCredit minus the cancellation fee, or board as planned.

If you hold a Basic ticket and want to move to an earlier flight on the same day, same-day change options are not available. The only recourse is to purchase a new ticket for the earlier flight separately.

## Last-Minute Changes by Phone

If you need to make a last-minute change and the online tool is not working, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** immediately. Phone agents can complete changes that the self-service interface cannot handle, including complex routing changes, multi-segment modifications, and same-day confirmed requests when app connectivity is limited.

When calling last-minute, have your booking confirmation number ready and know your preferred alternative flight (airline, flight number, departure time) if you have a specific one in mind. This speeds up the process significantly.

## International Flights and Last-Minute Changes

Standard no-change-fee rules for Classic and Extra apply to international flights right up to departure as well. A fare difference applies based on current market pricing for the new routing.

Same-day change options (Confirmed and Standby) are primarily designed for domestic US routes. Long-haul international last-minute changes follow standard change rules rather than same-day protocols. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for any last-minute international change.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change a Delta flight within 24 hours of departure?**
Yes for Classic and Extra — same no-change-fee rules apply. Same-day options also become available.

**Is there a special fee for changing a Delta flight last minute?**
No for Classic and Extra. Same-day Confirmed costs $75. Same-day Standby is free.

**Can I change a Delta Basic Economy ticket within 24 hours of departure?**
No. Basic cannot be changed after the 24-hour purchase window. The flight is either taken or cancelled for an eCredit minus fee.

**What same-day change options are available within 24 hours of departure?**
Same-Day Confirmed ($75, waived for Gold/Platinum/Diamond Medallion) and Same-Day Standby (free). Both avoid fare differences.

**Can I change my international Delta flight within 24 hours of departure?**
Yes for Classic and Extra under standard change rules. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for assistance.


**What is Delta's same-day change policy within 24 hours of departure?**
Same-Day Confirmed ($75, waived for Gold/Platinum/Diamond Medallion) guarantees a seat on a different same-day flight with no fare difference. Same-Day Standby is free and places you on a waitlist with no guarantee. Both are available via the Fly Delta app or by calling +1-(866)-332-0756.

**Can I change my Delta international flight within 24 hours of departure?**
Yes for Classic and Extra — the no-change-fee policy applies right up to departure for standard changes. Same-day change options (Confirmed and Standby) are primarily for domestic routes. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 for last-minute international changes.
