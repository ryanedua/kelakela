# How to Contact Southwest Customer Service on a Holiday

Southwest customer service is available on all holidays at +1-(855)-321-3191 with no reduced hours or holiday closures. However, holiday periods — particularly Thanksgiving week, Christmas week, and the day before July 4 — see the highest call volume of the year. Call early in the morning before travel disruptions compound, use the callback option, or use the Southwest app for self-service.

Southwest does not close for holidays. Agents are staffed on Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day, July 4, and every other day of the year. What changes during holidays is call volume — and it changes dramatically. The days surrounding major US travel holidays are some of the highest-volume periods of the entire year. Call **+1-(855)-321-3191)** early in the morning before disruptions compound the queue.

## No Holiday Closures or Reduced Hours

Southwest customer service at **+1-(855)-321-3191)** operates 24 hours on every holiday. There is no holiday message, no reduced-staff skeleton crew that only handles emergencies, and no redirection to an online form. Live agents are available to process rebooking, refunds, eCredits, and all standard requests on Christmas morning the same as any other morning.

## Holiday Call Volume: When It Peaks

**Thanksgiving period**: The Wednesday before Thanksgiving and the Sunday after are the two busiest travel days of the year. Customer service volume on these days is among the highest of any non-weather-event period. Thanksgiving Day itself is typically quieter — most travelers are at their destination.

**Christmas and New Year's period**: December 22 through 26 and December 30 through January 2 see sustained high volume. Winter weather disruptions frequently compound the seasonal surge during this window.

**July 4 period**: The day before July 4 and the day after see elevated volume. Summer weather disruptions — thunderstorms at East Coast hubs — frequently coincide.

**Memorial Day and Labor Day weekends**: Moderate volume increase, particularly on the Friday before and Monday after.

## How to Reach Southwest Faster on Holidays

**Call before 7 AM Eastern**: Holiday morning volume is the lowest point of the holiday surge. Most holiday travelers have not yet encountered their disruptions. Calling before 7 AM on a holiday typically means a shorter wait than calling at noon.

**Accept the callback**: When the wait is long, the callback option on **+1-(855)-321-3191)** holds your place without keeping you on hold. On high-volume holiday days, accepting the callback and doing something else while you wait is significantly better than holding for 45 minutes.

**Use the Southwest app**: Holiday or not, the app handles self-service tasks with no hold time. Voluntary flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets, standby additions, eCredit application, and cancellations all work through the app on Christmas Day the same as any Tuesday.

**Go to the gate agent**: If you are already at the airport during a holiday disruption, the gate agent at your target departure is often faster than the phone queue, which is under maximum pressure during holiday travel peaks.
