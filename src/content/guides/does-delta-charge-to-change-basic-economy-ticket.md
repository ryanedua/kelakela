# Does Delta Charge a Fee to Change a Basic Economy Ticket in 2026?

This question comes up constantly and the answer is technically no — but not for the reason most people expect. Delta does not charge a change fee on Main Basic (Basic Economy) tickets because there is no change option to apply a fee to. After the 24-hour risk-free window closes, changes simply are not permitted on Basic fares. The option does not exist in the booking system for standard changes. What you can do is cancel before departure for an eCredit minus the applicable cancellation fee. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to understand exactly what you will receive before taking any action on your Basic ticket.

## Why There Is No Change Fee — But You Still Can't Change

Delta eliminated standalone change fees for Main Cabin and above tickets in 2020. That policy applies to Main Classic, Main Extra, Comfort+, and premium cabin Classic and Extra fares. It does not apply to Basic tickets because Basic Economy operates under a fundamentally different rule: the ticket is non-changeable, not just fee-free to change.

The distinction matters. A Main Classic ticket has no change fee — meaning you can change it freely, paying only a fare difference. A Main Basic ticket has no change option — meaning the system will not allow you to select a new flight date or time after the 24-hour window, regardless of whether a fee would apply.

If you try to change a Basic ticket through My Trips on delta.com, the Change Flight button either does not appear or is greyed out.

## What You Can Actually Do With a Basic Economy Ticket

**Within 24 hours of purchase**: Cancel for a full cash refund to your original payment method with no fee. This is your best option if you need to change fares or dates. After the refund processes, rebook on Main Classic or whatever fare you actually want.

**After 24 hours and before departure**: Cancel for an eCredit minus the applicable cancellation fee. The fee varies by route. What remains after the fee deduction is issued as an eCredit valid for one year from your original purchase date. You can apply that credit toward a new booking in any fare class.

**During an active weather waiver**: If Delta issues a travel waiver covering your airport and travel dates, the Basic Economy restrictions are temporarily suspended. You can rebook within the waiver window without paying the cancellation fee. Check delta.com/travelinformation for active waivers.

**If Delta cancels or significantly changes your flight**: When Delta initiates a cancellation or makes a schedule change of 3 or more hours domestically or 6 or more hours internationally, the Basic restrictions are overridden entirely. You are entitled to free rebooking or a full cash refund regardless of fare type.

## The Cancellation Fee by Route

When you cancel a Basic Economy ticket after the 24-hour window, Delta deducts the applicable fee before issuing the eCredit:

US and Canada originations: fee deducted from ticket value per fare rules.
Caribbean routes (to/from US, Canada, Mexico, Central America): $99.
Asia-Pacific routes (tickets issued on or after September 26, 2024): $199.
South America routes (tickets issued on or after June 25, 2025): $330.
Korea: 450,000 KRW deducted before refund to original payment.
Indonesia: 4,000,000 IDR deducted.

The residual value after the fee becomes your eCredit. If you paid $89 for a domestic Basic ticket and the fee is $89, you receive no eCredit. Always confirm the fee amount before cancelling by calling **+1-(866)-332-0756**.

## Accidentally Booked Basic Economy? Here's Your Best Move

The most common scenario is booking Basic Economy to save $40 to $80, then realizing the lack of seat selection, miles earning, and change flexibility makes it the wrong choice.

If you are within 24 hours of booking, cancel immediately for a full refund and rebook in Classic. This costs nothing and gives you full Classic flexibility going forward.

If 24 hours have passed, weigh the cancellation fee against the cost of buying a new Classic ticket. Sometimes the eCredit minus fee plus the cost of a new Classic ticket is less than what you would lose by sticking with the Basic ticket. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** for a calculation before deciding.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Delta charge a change fee for Basic Economy tickets?**
No, because changes are not permitted — there is no fee because there is no change option. Cancel for an eCredit minus the cancellation fee instead.

**What can I do if I need to change a Basic Economy Delta ticket?**
Cancel before departure for an eCredit minus the fee, then rebook. Or cancel within 24 hours of booking for a full cash refund.

**Is there any way to change a Basic Economy ticket without losing money?**
Within 24 hours of purchase (full refund) or during an active weather waiver (no cancellation fee).

**How much is the Delta Basic Economy cancellation fee?**
Varies by route: $99 Caribbean, $199 Asia-Pacific, $330 South America. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** for your specific route.

**What if I accidentally booked Basic Economy?**
Cancel within 24 hours for a full refund and rebook in Classic. After 24 hours, cancel for an eCredit and weigh the cost of rebooking.


**Is there any way to change a Basic Economy Delta ticket without losing money?**
Within 24 hours of purchase, you can cancel for a full cash refund and rebook. During an active Delta weather waiver covering your airports and travel dates, Basic Economy can be rebooking without the cancellation fee. Outside these windows, some value loss is unavoidable.

**What if I accidentally booked Basic Economy and want to switch to Classic?**
Within 24 hours of booking, cancel for a full cash refund and rebook in Classic. After 24 hours, cancel for an eCredit minus the cancellation fee and use that credit toward a Classic ticket. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 to process the cancellation.
