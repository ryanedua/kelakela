# What is Delta's refund policy 2026? — 2026 Answer

Delta's 2026 refund policy: Main Extra is fully refundable to original payment any time before departure. Main Classic gives a full eCredit with no fee. Main Basic gives an eCredit minus a cancellation fee. All fares get a full cash refund within 24 hours of purchase. If Delta cancels your flight, full cash refund under DOT rules regardless of fare type. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 for your specific situation.
