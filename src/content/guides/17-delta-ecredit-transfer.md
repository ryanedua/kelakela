# Delta eCredit: Can You Transfer It to Another Person?

If you have a Delta eCredit but cannot use it for your own travel, you might wonder whether you can pass it along to a family member or friend who can make use of it. Unfortunately, the answer is clear: Delta eCredits are non-transferable. This guide explains why, what it means in practice, and what options you do have when you cannot use an eCredit yourself.

## Delta eCredits Are Non-Transferable

Delta's eCredit policy is explicit: an eCredit is tied to the **passenger named on the original ticket** and can only be used by that person. You cannot:

- Transfer an eCredit to a spouse, partner, or child
- Give an eCredit to a friend or colleague
- Use your eCredit to purchase a ticket for another traveler
- Sell or give away an eCredit

This policy applies regardless of the reason the eCredit was issued — whether from a voluntary cancellation, a Delta-issued disruption credit, or a goodwill gesture after a difficult travel experience.

## Why Delta Does Not Allow eCredit Transfers

The non-transferability rule exists for several reasons:

**Identity and booking security.** eCredits are linked to the booking history and identity of the original ticketed passenger. Allowing transfers would create security and accountability gaps in the system.

**Revenue management.** If eCredits were freely transferable, they would function more like gift cards, which Delta sells separately as a distinct product. The eCredit is specifically designed to bring the original passenger back for future travel.

**Fare integrity.** Transferability would allow secondary markets to develop around eCredit resale, which airlines typically seek to prevent.

## What "Named Passenger Only" Means in Practice

When you apply an eCredit at checkout on delta.com, the system checks whether the eCredit matches the passenger's name on the booking being made. If the names do not match, the eCredit cannot be applied.

This means even if you are paying for a family vacation for multiple people, your eCredit can only be applied to your own ticket in that group booking — not to the tickets of your travel companions.

## Scenarios Where You Cannot Use Your eCredit

Here are common situations where passengers discover they cannot use their eCredit:

- **You are not planning to fly Delta** in the foreseeable future.
- **Your eCredit is close to expiration** and you have no travel plans.
- **A family member's flight was canceled** but the eCredit is in the other parent's name.
- **You received an eCredit** for a business trip and cannot use it for personal travel within the expiration window.

In all of these cases, the non-transferable rule prevents gifting the credit to someone who could use it.

## What You Can Do If You Cannot Use Your eCredit

While you cannot transfer an eCredit, you do have some options for making the most of a credit you might not otherwise use.

### Book Travel You Might Take

Even if you have no firm plans, use the eCredit to book a refundable ticket to a destination you might visit before the credit expires. A refundable ticket can be canceled if needed, keeping your options open without losing the credit to expiration.

### Use It for a Business Trip

If you have work travel coming up, apply the eCredit toward a business trip ticket in your name. Even if the company typically reimburses travel expenses, using an eCredit reduces out-of-pocket costs that you then recover.

### Book a Flight for Yourself and Pay for Others Separately

If a group is traveling and you hold an eCredit, you can apply your eCredit to your own ticket while other group members book separately or through a shared itinerary where each person pays their own portion.

### Contact Delta for Exceptions

In very limited and exceptional circumstances — serious illness, death of the account holder, or other unusual situations — Delta may exercise discretion. Call **++1-866-332-0756** to explain your specific situation. While Delta's standard policy does not permit transfers, customer service representatives occasionally have the ability to make accommodations in documented extraordinary cases.

## Delta Gift Cards as an Alternative

If your goal is to give someone else travel funds for Delta, consider purchasing a **Delta Gift Card** instead of attempting to transfer an eCredit. Delta Gift Cards can be purchased at delta.com and can be used by the recipient to book their own flights. They are designed for exactly this purpose and do not carry the name-specific restrictions that eCredits do.

## eCredits from Group or Multi-Passenger Bookings

When multiple passengers are on the same itinerary and a cancellation occurs, each passenger receives their own eCredit in their own name. You cannot pool multiple passengers' eCredits into one account, and you cannot use another traveler's eCredit even if you paid for their ticket originally.

## What If the Named Passenger Has Passed Away

If an eCredit is in the name of a passenger who is deceased, the standard non-transferability rule still applies by default. Families dealing with this situation should contact Delta directly at **++1-866-332-0756** with documentation. Delta may make an exception to allow estate representatives to apply the credit in appropriate circumstances, though this is handled on a case-by-case basis.

## Contact Delta Customer Support

For questions about eCredit eligibility, exceptions in extraordinary circumstances, or help applying a credit to your own future booking, contact Delta Air Lines at **++1-866-332-0756** or visit delta.com.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I transfer a Delta eCredit to another person?**

No. Delta eCredits are non-transferable and are tied to the passenger named on the original ticket. They cannot be transferred to a spouse, family member, friend, or any other traveler. Only the named passenger can apply the eCredit to a future Delta booking.

**Can I use a Delta eCredit for someone else's flight?**

No. Delta eCredits can only be used by the original passenger for whom the ticket was issued. You cannot apply your eCredit to a ticket for a different person, even if you are paying for their travel.

**What can I do if I can't use my Delta eCredit?**

If you cannot use your eCredit yourself, your options are limited. You can book a flight for yourself in any future travel window before the eCredit expires, or contact Delta at ++1-866-332-0756 to explore any exceptions that may apply to your specific situation.

**Can I use a Delta eCredit to buy a ticket for my child?**

Only if the eCredit was issued in the child's name, matching the original ticket holder. If the eCredit is in your name, you cannot apply it to a ticket purchased for your child. eCredits are name-specific and non-transferable.
