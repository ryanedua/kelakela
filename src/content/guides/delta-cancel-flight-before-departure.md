# How to Cancel a Delta Flight Before Departure in 2026

Cancelling a Delta flight before departure is straightforward once you know which fare type you booked and what you will receive in return. The outcome ranges from a full cash refund on an Extra ticket to a partial eCredit on a Basic ticket. The key rule that applies to every fare type is the same: cancel before departure or lose everything. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to cancel by phone or confirm your options before proceeding.

## What You Get by Fare Type When You Cancel

**Main Basic (Basic Economy)**: Cancel before departure to receive an eCredit minus the applicable cancellation fee. The fee varies by route — typically a set amount for US/Canada routes, $99 for Caribbean routes, $199 for Asia-Pacific routes on newer tickets, and $330 for South America routes on tickets issued on or after June 26, 2025. If you do not cancel before departure, the entire ticket value is forfeited.

**Main Classic**: Cancel any time before departure to receive a full eCredit with no cancellation fee deducted. You recover the entire ticket value as future travel credit valid for one year from the original purchase date.

**Main Extra**: Cancel any time before departure to receive a full cash refund to your original payment method. No cancellation fee, no conversion to eCredit. The money goes back to your card within 7 business days.

**All fare types within 24 hours of purchase**: Cancel within 24 hours of booking for a full cash refund regardless of fare type. This is Delta's 24-Hour Risk-Free Cancellation policy and applies to tickets purchased directly from Delta.

## How to Cancel Online

**Via delta.com**: Log in to your account, go to My Trips, and locate your booking. Click on the flight you want to cancel, select Cancel Flight, and review the refund or eCredit amount displayed before confirming. The system calculates your outcome automatically based on fare type. Confirm the cancellation and a confirmation email will be sent.

**Via the Fly Delta app**: Open the app, tap My Trips, select the booking, and tap Cancel. Follow the prompts. The app shows your cancellation outcome before you confirm.

The online cancellation process is available 24 hours a day for most ticket types. Some tickets with special fare rules or those purchased through third-party agencies cannot be cancelled online and require a phone call.

## How to Cancel by Phone

Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to cancel by phone. Have your booking confirmation number ready. An agent will confirm your fare type, calculate your refund or eCredit amount, and process the cancellation during the call. Phone cancellation is useful when the online system is not recognizing your ticket, when you have a complex multi-segment booking, or when you want to confirm your options before committing to the cancellation.

If your ticket was purchased through a third-party travel agency or platform, you must contact that platform to cancel. Delta cannot process refunds for tickets issued outside its own sales channels.

## Cancelling the Day of Departure

You can cancel a Delta flight on the day of departure as long as you do so before the scheduled departure time. The same fare type rules apply — Basic gets eCredit minus fee, Classic gets full eCredit, Extra gets full cash refund.

If you are at the airport and need to cancel the same day, go to a Delta customer service counter or call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** from anywhere. Do not simply not show up. A no-show results in full forfeiture regardless of fare type and also cancels all remaining segments on your itinerary.

## Cancelling a Round-Trip Ticket

If you cancel before using any portion of a round-trip ticket, the entire booking is cancelled and you receive a refund or eCredit for the full ticket value based on your fare type.

If you have already completed the outbound flight and want to cancel the return leg, contact Delta at **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to cancel the remaining segment. The refund or eCredit will reflect the prorated value of the unused return portion.

## What Happens When You Cancel vs No-Show

The difference between cancelling before departure and not showing up is significant.

When you cancel before departure, you receive whatever your fare type entitles you to — eCredit minus fee, full eCredit, or full cash refund.

When you no-show without cancelling, the entire remaining ticket value is forfeited for Basic and most non-refundable tickets, all subsequent segments including return flights are automatically cancelled, and no eCredit is issued.

Even a last-minute cancellation seconds before the flight is better than a no-show. Cancel before the plane departs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I cancel my Delta flight before departure?**
Log in to My Trips on delta.com or the Fly Delta app and select Cancel, or call **+1-(866)-332-0756)**.

**What do I get when I cancel a Delta flight before departure?**
Extra: full cash refund. Classic: full eCredit. Basic: eCredit minus cancellation fee. Within 24 hours of purchase: full cash refund regardless of fare type.

**Is there a deadline to cancel a Delta flight before departure?**
Yes. You must cancel before the scheduled departure time. No-shows forfeit the entire ticket value.

**Can I cancel a Delta flight the day of departure?**
Yes, before the flight departs. The same fare type rules apply.

**What happens if I cancel a round-trip Delta ticket before the outbound flight?**
The entire round-trip is cancelled and you receive a refund or eCredit for the full unused value.
