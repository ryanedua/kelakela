# Delta Trip Delay Reimbursement: How to Claim & What's Covered

A long flight delay costs more than just time. Meals, hotel stays, and ground transportation can add up quickly, and knowing who pays for what makes a meaningful difference in your out-of-pocket costs. Delta's approach to delay reimbursement depends on the cause of the delay, the duration, and the documentation you provide.

## What Delta Will and Won't Reimburse

Delta's reimbursement commitment is tied directly to whether the delay was within the airline's control.

### Controllable Delays (Delta's Responsibility)

When a delay is caused by something within Delta's control — aircraft maintenance, crew availability, cleaning issues, or other operational factors — Delta's customer commitment policies may cover:

- **Meal vouchers** for delays of approximately 3 hours or more
- **Hotel accommodations** for overnight controllable delays
- **Ground transportation** to and from an airport hotel

These benefits are not always offered automatically. You may need to ask at the gate or contact Delta Customer Care after your trip.

### Uncontrollable Delays (Not Delta's Responsibility)

When a delay is caused by weather, Air Traffic Control restrictions, a security incident, or other events outside Delta's control, the airline's obligation to reimburse out-of-pocket expenses is significantly reduced. In these situations:

- Delta is not required to provide hotel accommodations
- Meal vouchers may or may not be offered at Delta's discretion
- Your best reimbursement path is through travel insurance

## How to Claim Reimbursement from Delta

### During the Delay: At the Gate

The fastest way to receive any available assistance is to speak with a Delta gate agent or airport customer service representative during the delay. Ask directly:

- Is this delay controllable or weather-related?
- Are meal vouchers being distributed?
- Is Delta providing hotel accommodations if the delay is overnight?

If meal or hotel vouchers are available, agents distribute them at the gate, at the customer service counter, or through the Fly Delta app.

### After Your Trip: Customer Care Submission

For reimbursement not received during the delay — including situations where you paid out of pocket for meals, transportation, or lodging during a controllable delay — you can submit a claim to Delta after your trip.

**Steps to submit a reimbursement request:**

1. Go to delta.com and navigate to **Customer Care** under the "Need Help" menu.
2. Submit a written description of your experience, including:
   - The flight number and travel date
   - The nature and cause of the delay (as communicated by Delta)
   - The expenses incurred with dates and amounts
3. Attach copies of receipts for all expenses claimed.
4. Submit the form and note your case number.

Alternatively, call Delta Customer Care at **++1-866-332-0756** to initiate a claim over the phone.

### What to Include in Your Claim

- **Original receipts** for all claimed expenses — restaurants, hotels, taxis, ride-shares
- **Documentation of the delay** — a screenshot of the flight status page showing the delay time, or a gate announcement printout
- **Your boarding pass** showing you were on the affected flight
- **Delta's communications** — any text, email, or app notification that indicated the nature of the delay

Delta reviews each case individually. The more documentation you provide, the stronger your claim.

## How Much Will Delta Reimburse?

Delta does not publish a fixed maximum reimbursement per delay. Outcomes depend on:

- The length and nature of the delay
- Whether the delay was controllable
- The specific expenses claimed and whether they are deemed reasonable

Reasonable expenses generally include economy-level dining, standard airport-area hotel rates, and basic ground transportation. First-class restaurant meals or luxury hotels during a delay are less likely to be fully reimbursed.

## Credit Card Trip Delay Insurance as a Supplement

Many travel credit cards offer trip delay insurance that can reimburse delay expenses independently of what Delta provides. This coverage typically:

- Activates after a minimum delay period (often 4 to 6 hours, depending on the card)
- Covers eligible expenses up to a set daily limit (often $200 to $500 per day)
- Applies regardless of whether the delay was controllable or weather-related
- Requires that the ticket was purchased with the eligible card

If you paid for your Delta ticket with a credit card that includes trip delay insurance, you may be able to claim delay expenses through the card's benefit even when Delta does not cover them. Review your card's benefits guide for specific terms.

**Important:** You cannot claim the same expense from both Delta and a credit card benefit. If Delta reimburses a meal, you cannot also claim that meal through your card's trip delay coverage.

## Delta Proactive Customer Credits

Separately from formal reimbursement claims, Delta has implemented a system of proactive customer service credits for disrupted passengers. Through the Fly Delta app, Delta sometimes deposits SkyMiles or travel credits into the accounts of passengers affected by significant controllable delays, sometimes before those passengers even ask.

If you experienced a significant controllable delay and did not receive a proactive credit, contact Delta Customer Care after your trip through the app or by calling **++1-866-332-0756**.

## Tips for Maximizing Your Delay Reimbursement

- **Keep every receipt.** Even small meal or coffee purchases add up and all are potentially reimbursable for controllable delays.
- **Document the delay cause.** If a gate agent or Delta notification indicates the cause, note it or take a screenshot. This helps establish whether the delay was controllable.
- **File your claim promptly.** Do not wait months after the delay to submit a claim. Filing within a few weeks of the incident generally results in faster and more successful outcomes.
- **Know your credit card benefits.** If your card includes trip delay insurance, you have a parallel reimbursement path that does not depend on Delta's goodwill.

## Contact Delta Customer Support

To submit a reimbursement claim or inquire about delay compensation, contact Delta Air Lines at **++1-866-332-0756** or use the Customer Care section at delta.com.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Delta reimburse expenses for flight delays?**

Delta may provide meal vouchers and hotel accommodations for significant controllable delays, but does not guarantee reimbursement for all delay-related expenses. Keep receipts and contact Delta Customer Care at ++1-866-332-0756 after your trip to request reimbursement for documented expenses related to a controllable delay.

**How do I submit a claim to Delta for delay expenses?**

After your trip, visit delta.com's Customer Care section and submit a written claim describing the delay, the expenses incurred, and attach receipts. You can also call Delta at ++1-866-332-0756 to initiate a claim. Delta reviews each case individually.

**Does Delta cover hotel costs for weather delays?**

Delta generally does not cover hotel costs for weather-related delays, as these are considered outside the airline's control. For weather delays, trip delay insurance through a travel credit card or standalone travel insurance policy is typically the more appropriate reimbursement path.

**What is the difference between Delta trip delay reimbursement and credit card trip delay insurance?**

Delta's reimbursement is discretionary and typically limited to controllable delays. Credit card trip delay insurance is a contractual benefit that reimburses eligible expenses after a set delay threshold (usually 4 to 6 hours) regardless of the delay cause. Both can be pursued, but you cannot double-claim the same expense.
