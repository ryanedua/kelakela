# What to Do When United Cancels Your Flight

When United cancels your flight, check the United app immediately for automatic rebooking. If the new itinerary works, accept it. If not, call +1-(855)-235-1859 right away — you are entitled to free rebooking or a full cash refund under DOT rules. For controllable overnight cancellations, request hotel accommodation from the gate agent.

A United cancellation triggers specific rights under DOT rules and United's own customer commitment. Knowing them before you call — or before you tap Accept on the automatic rebooking — means you get the outcome you are entitled to instead of whatever United presents first. Open the United app and call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** simultaneously.

## The First Five Minutes

**Step 1 — Check the app**: Open the United app and go to My Trips. United often rebooks cancelled passengers automatically within minutes of the cancellation. Check whether a new itinerary is already there.

**Step 2 — Evaluate the rebooking**: Is the new flight getting you to your destination at a time that works? Same day? Next day? Via an acceptable connection? If yes, accept it immediately to lock in the seat.

**Step 3 — Reject and call if needed**: If the automatic rebooking does not work, reject it and call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** immediately. Every minute you wait is a minute another passenger takes the seat on the alternative you want.

**Step 4 — At the airport**: If you are at the airport, go to the departure gate of the flight you want to board. Gate agents have direct system access and can add you to standby or confirm a seat for a flight that is boarding now — faster than any phone queue during a disruption.

## Your Two Rights After a United Cancellation

**Option 1 — Free rebooking**: United places you on the next available flight to your destination at no charge. No fare difference, no change fee. Ask specifically for the flight and time you want.

**Option 2 — Full cash refund**: You decline to travel on any alternative and receive a full refund to your original payment method. Say this explicitly: "United cancelled my flight. I choose not to travel on an alternative. I want a full cash refund to my original payment method under DOT rules."

United may present the eCredit option first. An eCredit is not cash. Ask for cash before accepting anything.

## Controllable Overnight Cancellations: Hotel, Meals, Transport

When a United-initiated cancellation — mechanical, crew, or operational — causes an overnight disruption, United's Customer Commitment requires:

A complimentary hotel room at a partner property near the departure airport. Round-trip ground transportation between the airport and hotel. Meal vouchers for dinner and breakfast.

These are not given automatically. Ask the gate agent: "This is a controllable overnight cancellation — can you arrange hotel accommodation, ground transportation, and meal vouchers?"

Weather cancellations do not trigger hotel accommodation requirements, but free rebooking and cash refund rights still apply.

## Frequently asked questions

**What should I do first when United cancels my flight?**
Open the United app immediately and check My Trips for automatic rebooking. If it works, accept it. If not, call +1-(855)-235-1859 right away.

**Am I entitled to a cash refund when United cancels my flight?**
Yes. Under the 2024 DOT final rule, United must provide a full cash refund when it cancels and you choose not to accept the alternative. Call +1-(855)-235-1859 and request cash explicitly.

**Does United provide a hotel when it cancels a flight overnight?**
Yes for controllable cancellations. Hotel, ground transportation, and meal vouchers. Ask the gate agent — not given automatically. Not required for weather cancellations.

**Can United give me an eCredit instead of cash after cancelling my flight?**
Only with your affirmative consent. Ask for cash before accepting anything.

**What if United rebooks me on an unacceptable flight?**
Reject it in the app or call +1-(855)-235-1859 and request an alternative. Ask about Star Alliance partner rebooking if no adequate United option exists.
