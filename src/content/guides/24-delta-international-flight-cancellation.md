# Delta International Flight Cancellation: Cash Refund vs eCredit

International travel involves more planning and often more money than domestic flights, which makes cancellations particularly stressful. Whether Delta cancels your international flight or you need to cancel yourself, understanding what you are entitled to receive — a cash refund or an eCredit — helps you navigate the situation with confidence.

## The Core Distinction: Who Cancelled the Flight?

The single most important factor in determining your refund outcome for an international Delta flight is whether Delta cancelled the flight or whether you cancelled it voluntarily.

- **Delta cancels the flight** → You are entitled to a full cash refund (all fare types)
- **You cancel voluntarily** → Outcome depends on your fare class (refundable vs non-refundable)

This distinction is straightforward, but it is important to be aware that Delta will often present the rebooking option first, making it easy to inadvertently accept a rebooked itinerary when you are actually entitled to a refund.

## When Delta Cancels Your International Flight

### Your Right to a Cash Refund

Under U.S. Department of Transportation regulations, when an airline cancels a flight, passengers are entitled to a **full cash refund to their original payment method** regardless of:

- Fare type (refundable or non-refundable)
- How far in advance the cancellation is announced
- The reason for the cancellation (weather, mechanical, operational)

This right exists independently of Delta's own policies. It is a federal consumer protection that no fine print or ticket terms can override.

### Accepting Rebooking vs Requesting a Refund

When Delta cancels an international flight, it typically:

1. Automatically rebooks affected passengers on the next available Delta flight
2. Sends a notification with the new itinerary
3. Offers options to accept, rebook differently, or request a refund

You are not required to accept the automatic rebook. If the new itinerary does not work for you, or if you simply prefer your money back, you can request a refund instead.

**Important:** If you accept the new itinerary through the app or by clicking a confirmation link in Delta's email, you may be giving up your active refund right. Always review your options before confirming anything.

## How to Request a Cash Refund for a Delta-Cancelled International Flight

### Online

1. Log in to delta.com and go to **My Trips**.
2. Locate the affected international booking.
3. Look for the option to decline the rebook and **request a refund**.
4. Submit the refund request and save the confirmation number.

### By Phone

Call **++1-866-332-0756** and state clearly that your international flight was cancelled by Delta and that you are requesting a full cash refund rather than the rebooked itinerary. This is a clear, unambiguous right, and the agent should process it without resistance.

### Refund Timeline

For international flight refunds:

- **Credit card purchases** — 7 business days
- **Other payment methods** — up to 20 business days

For complex international bookings where multiple payment methods or partial miles were used, the timeline may vary per component.

## When You Cancel an International Delta Flight Voluntarily

### Non-Refundable International Tickets

If you hold a non-refundable international ticket and cancel voluntarily:

- You receive a **Delta eCredit** for the unused ticket value
- No cash is returned
- The eCredit expires one year from the original purchase date
- The eCredit can only be used by the named passenger

Delta eliminated change fees on most international tickets, so there is generally no additional penalty beyond losing your refund eligibility. The eCredit reflects the full ticket value.

### Refundable International Tickets

If you purchased a refundable international fare and cancel voluntarily:

- You receive a **full cash refund** to your original payment method
- No penalties apply
- You can cancel at any point before departure

Refundable fares cost more but provide this flexibility, which is valuable for international trips where plans may change due to visa issues, health concerns, or other factors.

### Award Ticket Cancellations

If you cancel an international SkyMiles award ticket voluntarily:

- **Miles are redeposited** to your SkyMiles account (minus any redeposit fee)
- **Cash taxes and fees** are refunded to your original card
- Redeposit fees vary by Medallion status (Diamond and Platinum members typically pay no fee)

## International Schedule Changes and Refund Rights

A significant schedule change to an international Delta flight — such as a departure or arrival shift of 6 hours or more, an airport change, or a cabin downgrade — also triggers the right to a cash refund. This is true even for non-refundable tickets.

The same process applies: go to My Trips to review the changed itinerary and select the refund option, or call **++1-866-332-0756**.

## Partner Airline Segments on International Itineraries

Some Delta international itineraries involve partner airline segments operated by SkyTeam or other allies. The refund rights for the overall ticket (booked through Delta) follow Delta's policies and DOT regulations, even if the individual flight is operated by a partner.

Contact Delta directly at **++1-866-332-0756** for any international booking involving partner segments when you need to cancel or request a refund.

## Tips for International Cancellations

- **Do not assume the rebooked itinerary is your only option.** Delta is required to offer a refund if it cancels your flight. Ask explicitly.
- **Act quickly.** Rebooking slots fill up after mass cancellations. If you want a rebooked flight rather than a refund, secure it early.
- **Check for travel insurance.** If you purchased travel insurance, a cancellation — especially a voluntary one — may be covered depending on the reason. Review your policy terms.
- **Understand eCredit terms before canceling voluntarily.** eCredits for international tickets have the same name-specific and expiration restrictions as domestic eCredits.

## Contact Delta Customer Support

For international cancellation assistance, refund requests, or help understanding your options after a Delta schedule change, contact Delta Air Lines at **++1-866-332-0756** or visit the Refunds section at delta.com.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**If Delta cancels my international flight, do I get a cash refund?**

Yes. Under U.S. Department of Transportation rules, if Delta cancels your international flight, you are entitled to a full cash refund to your original payment method regardless of fare type. You may also choose to rebook on an alternative Delta flight at no additional cost.

**What happens if I cancel an international Delta flight myself?**

If you cancel a non-refundable international Delta ticket voluntarily, you receive a Delta eCredit for the unused ticket value, not a cash refund. The eCredit expires one year from the original purchase date and can only be used by the named passenger.

**Do international Delta flights have different cancellation fees?**

Delta eliminated change and cancellation fees on most international tickets, though fare classes still affect your outcome. Refundable international fares yield cash on voluntary cancellation. Non-refundable fares yield eCredits. Award tickets result in miles redeposit.

**How do I request a cash refund for a Delta-cancelled international flight?**

Go to 'My Trips' on delta.com and select the cancelled flight. Look for the refund option rather than accepting the automatic rebook. Alternatively, call Delta at ++1-866-332-0756 to request your cash refund directly.
