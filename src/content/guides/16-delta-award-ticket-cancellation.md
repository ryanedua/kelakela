# Delta Award Ticket Cancellation: Miles Redeposit Fee & Rules

Booking a Delta SkyMiles award ticket is one of the best ways to use your accumulated miles, but life does not always cooperate with travel plans. Understanding how to cancel a Delta award ticket — and what fees or processes are involved in getting your miles back — helps you make informed decisions before and after booking.

## How Delta Award Ticket Cancellation Works

When you cancel a Delta SkyMiles award ticket, the process works differently than canceling a cash ticket. Instead of receiving a cash refund or eCredit, the following happens:

- **Miles are redeposited** to your SkyMiles account (minus any applicable redeposit fee)
- **Taxes and fees paid in cash** are refunded to your original payment card
- Any **carrier-imposed surcharges** paid in cash are also refunded

The key variable is the redeposit fee, which depends on your Medallion status.

## Delta Miles Redeposit Fee by Medallion Status

Delta's redeposit fee structure rewards loyalty by reducing or eliminating fees for elite members.

| Medallion Status | Redeposit Fee |
|---|---|
| Diamond Medallion | No fee |
| Platinum Medallion | No fee |
| Gold Medallion | No fee |
| Silver Medallion | No fee |
| Non-elite SkyMiles member | Fee applies (check delta.com for current amount) |

Delta has periodically adjusted redeposit fee amounts, so always verify the current fee on delta.com or by calling **++1-866-332-0756** before canceling, especially if you are a non-elite member.

## How to Cancel a Delta Award Ticket

### Online via Delta.com

1. Log in to your SkyMiles account at delta.com.
2. Go to **My Trips** and locate your award booking.
3. Select the booking and click **Cancel Flight**.
4. Review the cancellation summary, which will show the miles to be redeposited and any applicable fee.
5. Confirm the cancellation.

You will receive a confirmation email, and miles typically appear back in your SkyMiles account within 24 to 72 hours.

### Via the Fly Delta App

Open the app, navigate to **My Trips**, select your award flight, and tap the cancel option. The app mirrors the website process and shows redeposit details before you confirm.

### By Phone

Call **++1-866-332-0756** to cancel an award ticket by phone. Phone cancellation is particularly useful for:

- Complex itineraries with multiple passengers or award tickets from different bookings
- Situations where only one leg of an award needs to be canceled
- Cases where you want to confirm redeposit fee amounts before committing to the cancellation

## The 24-Hour Free Cancellation Window for Award Tickets

Like cash tickets, award tickets are subject to Delta's 24-hour risk-free cancellation policy. If you cancel an award ticket **within 24 hours of booking** and the travel date is at least **7 days away**, the miles redeposit fee is waived and all miles are returned in full.

This is the most important window to be aware of. If you book an award ticket and immediately have second thoughts, cancel within 24 hours to avoid any fees.

## What Happens to Taxes and Fees

All government taxes and carrier fees paid in cash when you booked the award ticket are refunded to your original credit card or payment method. The refund timeline is:

- **Credit card refunds** — typically 7 business days
- **Other payment methods** — up to 20 business days

You do not lose the cash portion of your award booking when you cancel, even if a redeposit fee applies to the miles portion.

## Canceling Award Tickets When Delta Changes Your Flight

If Delta cancels your award flight or makes a significant schedule change, the redeposit fee is typically waived. This is consistent with the broader passenger protection principle: if the change is Delta's doing, you should not bear a financial penalty for adjusting your booking.

In this situation, contact Delta at **++1-866-332-0756** to confirm that the fee waiver applies and to request your miles be returned without charge.

## Partial Cancellations: Canceling One Leg of an Award

If your award ticket covers a round trip or multi-city itinerary, you may want to cancel only one leg rather than the entire booking. This is possible but requires contacting Delta directly at **++1-866-332-0756** in most cases, as the online self-service tool may default to canceling the entire trip.

When canceling a partial award, the miles for the unused segment are redeposited, and the redeposit fee (if applicable) covers that cancellation.

## Award Ticket No-Show Policy

The same no-show rules that apply to cash tickets apply to award tickets. If you fail to cancel before departure and do not board, your miles are forfeited rather than redeposited. This is especially costly for high-value awards.

Always cancel before departure — even minutes before — to preserve your miles.

## Can You Change an Award Ticket Instead of Canceling?

Yes. Delta allows you to change award tickets rather than canceling them outright. Flight changes on award tickets are subject to:

- Award availability on the new date and route
- A change fee may apply for non-elite members (similar to the redeposit fee structure)
- Fare differences in the number of miles required if the new award costs more

If the new flight requires fewer miles, the difference is redeposited to your account. If it requires more, you pay the additional miles.

## Tips for Award Ticket Holders

- **Use the 24-hour window freely.** There is no downside to booking an award and canceling within 24 hours if plans are not finalized.
- **Achieve Medallion status to eliminate redeposit fees.** All Medallion tiers currently avoid redeposit fees, which adds significant value to elite status for frequent award travelers.
- **Cancel, do not no-show.** The difference between a cancellation and a no-show is the difference between getting your miles back and forfeiting them entirely.
- **Book refundable awards when available.** Some Delta award tickets are issued on refundable fare bases, which may provide additional flexibility.

## Contact Delta Customer Support

For award ticket cancellations, redeposit fee questions, or partial cancellation requests, contact Delta Air Lines at **++1-866-332-0756** or visit the SkyMiles section at delta.com.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I cancel a Delta SkyMiles award ticket?**

Log in to delta.com, go to 'My Trips,' select your award booking, and choose 'Cancel Flight.' Your miles will be redeposited to your SkyMiles account. Any taxes and fees paid in cash will be refunded to your original payment card.

**What is the Delta miles redeposit fee?**

Delta charges a redeposit fee to return miles to your SkyMiles account after canceling an award ticket. The fee varies by Medallion status: Diamond members pay no fee, while Platinum, Gold, Silver, and non-elite members may be charged. Check delta.com for current fee amounts.

**How long does it take for Delta miles to be redeposited after cancellation?**

Miles are typically redeposited to your SkyMiles account within 24 to 72 hours after canceling an award ticket. Cash refunds for taxes and fees take 7 business days for credit card refunds.

**Can I cancel a Delta award ticket within 24 hours of booking?**

Yes. If you cancel a Delta award ticket within 24 hours of booking and the award flight departs at least 7 days later, the redeposit fee is waived and miles are returned to your account. This is the risk-free cancellation window.
