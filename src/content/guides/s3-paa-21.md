# Can I cancel a Delta extra ticket for a full refund? — 2026 Answer

Yes. Delta Main Extra and all Extra tier fares in premium cabins (Comfort+ Extra, First Class Extra, Delta Premium Select Extra, Delta One Extra) are fully refundable to your original payment method at any time before the scheduled departure. No cancellation fee applies. Cancel via My Trips on delta.com or call +1-(866)-332-0756. The refund processes within 7 business days for credit cards.
