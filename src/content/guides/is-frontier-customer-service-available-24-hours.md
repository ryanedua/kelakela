# Is Frontier Customer Service Available 24 Hours a Day?

Yes, Frontier customer service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week at +1-(866)-227-2948. There is no closing time. However, wait times vary significantly by hour — under 5 minutes during off-peak windows (Tuesday through Thursday, 5 to 7 AM Eastern) and up to 45 minutes during peak periods. The Frontier app is also available 24 hours with no hold time for self-service tasks.

Frontier customer service operates around the clock with no closing time. Whether you are calling at 3 AM after a red-eye cancellation or at 11 PM before an early morning departure, agents are available. The question is not whether Frontier is open — it is how long you will wait when you call. Call **+1-(866)-227-2948** at any hour.

## 24/7 Availability: What It Actually Means

Frontier staffs its customer service line at **+1-(866)-227-2948)** around the clock, every day of the year including weekends and holidays. There is no voicemail-only period, no overnight message directing you to call back during business hours, and no seasonal shutdown.

Live agents are available at 2 AM on a Tuesday, 6 AM on Christmas Day, and 11 PM on a Sunday. The line does not distinguish between these hours in terms of availability — only in terms of wait time.

## Wait Times Still Vary Significantly

24/7 availability does not mean 24/7 short waits. Staffing levels are lower during overnight hours (midnight to 4 AM Eastern), which means that while agents are available, a call at 1 AM might have a moderate wait if a disruption event has driven up volume.

The genuinely shortest wait windows are:
- Tuesday through Thursday, 5 to 7 AM Eastern — under 5 minutes typical
- Tuesday through Thursday evenings, 8 to 11 PM Eastern — generally under 15 minutes
- Saturday mid-morning, 9 to 11 AM Eastern — moderate volume

The longest waits occur during Monday mornings, Friday afternoons, and the first two hours after any major weather announcement or mass cancellation.

## The 24/7 Alternative: Fly Frontier App

For tasks that do not require a human agent, the Frontier app has no staffing constraints and no wait time at any hour. Flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets, eCredit application at checkout, cancellations, same-day standby additions, boarding passes, and flight status all resolve through the app in under 2 minutes — at 3 AM or 3 PM.
