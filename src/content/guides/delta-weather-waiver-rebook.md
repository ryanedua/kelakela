# Delta Weather Waiver: How to Find It, Use It & Rebook in 2026

A Delta weather waiver is your ticket to fee-free rebooking when a storm disrupts your flight. Delta issues these waivers whenever significant weather is expected to affect operations at specific airports, typically hurricanes, nor'easters, severe thunderstorm systems, and major ice or snowstorms. Acting quickly when a waiver is announced is critical because alternative seats fill fast when hundreds of passengers rebook simultaneously. Check delta.com/travelinformation right now for active waivers, or call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to confirm whether your flight qualifies.

## What a Delta Weather Waiver Covers

When a weather waiver is active, passengers with tickets to, from, or through the covered airports within the specified travel date range can do three things at no cost.

They can rebook on a different Delta flight within the waiver window, moving to an earlier or later date without paying a change fee or fare difference as long as the new flight is in the same cabin.

They can postpone travel to any available date within the rebooking window stated in the waiver, which typically extends several days to a few weeks beyond the weather event.

They can cancel and receive an eCredit for the unused value of the ticket, with no cancellation fee deducted. This applies even to Main Basic fares, which normally would carry a cancellation charge.

Each waiver has its own specific terms. The waiver page lists which airports are covered, which travel dates qualify, the ticket purchase window, and the deadline by which new travel must begin. Read the specific waiver terms before rebooking to make sure your flight falls within the coverage.

## How to Check If Your Flight Is Covered

Go to delta.com/travelinformation and look for an active advisory that mentions your departure or arrival airport. Active waivers are listed with the covered city pairs, affected travel date range, and rebooking window.

If you cannot find the page or are unsure whether your specific routing qualifies, call **+1-(866)-332-0756** and give the agent your booking confirmation number. They can check your itinerary against active waivers in real time.

For large storm systems, check the waiver page multiple times in the 48 hours before your departure. Delta updates advisories frequently as storm tracks shift, and airports can be added to or removed from coverage as conditions change.

## How to Rebook Under a Waiver

Rebooking under a weather waiver is time-sensitive. Move as soon as the waiver is announced rather than waiting. Flights on alternative dates fill quickly when a storm affects a major hub.

**Via the Fly Delta app**: Open the app, tap My Trips, select your affected booking, and look for the change option. Available alternative flights within the waiver window should display with a $0 change fee.

**Via delta.com**: Log in to My Trips, select your flight, and choose to change. The rebooking interface should reflect the waiver and show no fee. If a fee appears, do not proceed. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** and ask the agent to apply the waiver code manually.

**Via phone**: Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** and give your booking confirmation number. The agent will identify your waiver, walk through available alternatives, and complete the rebooking without any fee.

When choosing your new flight, prioritize off-peak times such as early mornings or midweek departures within the waiver window. These tend to have more available seats when large numbers of passengers are all rebooking during the same storm window.

## Rebooking Rules Within the Waiver

Delta weather waivers allow rebooking in the same cabin of service as your original ticket. If you booked a Main Cabin Classic seat, you can rebook in Main Cabin at no additional cost. Moving to Comfort+ or First Class would require paying the fare difference even under a waiver.

If the same class of service is unavailable on your preferred alternative flight, Delta agents can rebook you in the next available class within the same cabin at no charge. Ask the agent about this option if your preferred flight is showing full in your fare class.

Award tickets booked with SkyMiles may also qualify under weather waivers. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to confirm the terms for SkyMiles bookings during an active waiver period.

## Weather Waiver vs Delta Cancellation: Know the Difference

This distinction is worth repeating because it directly affects whether you get an eCredit or a cash refund.

If you rebook or cancel voluntarily under an active weather waiver, the outcome is an eCredit. You chose to act under the waiver rather than waiting to see whether Delta cancels.

If Delta cancels your flight due to the weather event, the DOT cash refund right applies. You are entitled to a full refund to your original payment method, not just an eCredit. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** after a Delta-initiated cancellation and specifically request the cash refund.

If you are close to your departure and uncertain whether to rebook proactively under the waiver or wait for a potential Delta cancellation, the decision depends on your flexibility. Proactive rebooking under the waiver gives you more control over timing. Waiting risks losing seat availability on alternative flights but preserves the cash refund option if Delta cancels.

## What If Your Airport Is Not in the Waiver

Delta waivers are issued for specific airports. If a storm is affecting your area but your airport is not listed in the active waiver, you have two options.

Call **+1-(855)-321-3528** and request a goodwill accommodation. Delta agents sometimes extend waiver-like flexibility to passengers in airports adjacent to the covered zone, particularly when a storm is widespread. This is not guaranteed but is worth asking about, especially if your departure airport feeds into a covered hub.

Alternatively, do not cancel voluntarily. Wait to see whether Delta ends up cancelling your specific flight. If it does, even without a waiver covering your airport, you become entitled to a full cash refund or free rebooking. Voluntarily cancelling early for an eCredit locks you into a less favorable outcome.
