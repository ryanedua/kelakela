# How to Reach Frontier Customer Service From Outside the US

Frontier customer service can be reached from outside the US by calling the US line +1-(866)-227-2948 using international dialing (add your country's exit code before the +1). Frontier also maintains country-specific customer service numbers for major markets. The Frontier app and flyfrontier.com work in all countries with no call required for self-service tasks.

Frontier customer service is reachable from any country in the world — either through the US number with international dialing, through country-specific numbers for major markets, or through the Frontier app which works globally with no phone charges. Call **+1-(866)-227-2948)** from outside the US using your country's international dialing prefix, or use the methods below.

## Calling the US Number From Abroad

The most direct way to reach Frontier from outside the US is to call the US customer service number with your country's international exit code.

**Format**: [Your country's exit code] + 1-866-332-0756

Examples:
- From the UK: 001-866-332-0756 (or +1-866-332-0756 on a mobile)
- From Australia: 0011-866-332-0756 (or +1-866-332-0756 on a mobile)
- From Mexico: 00-1-866-332-0756
- From Japan: 010-1-866-332-0756
- From Germany: 00-1-866-332-0756

On most modern mobile phones, entering +1-(866)-227-2948 directly works from any country without needing to know the specific exit code. The + replaces the exit code automatically.

International calling charges may apply depending on your carrier plan. Check whether your plan includes international calls to US toll-free numbers.

## Frontier's Country-Specific Numbers

Frontier maintains dedicated customer service numbers for many international markets. These avoid international calling charges and may offer service in the local language for select countries.

To find your country's number: go to flyfrontier.com, scroll to the footer or go to Contact Us, and select your country from the dropdown menu. Major markets with dedicated numbers include the United Kingdom, Canada, Mexico, Japan, South Korea, Australia, Germany, France, China, and Brazil.

## The Fly Frontier App: No International Charges

The most cost-effective way to handle most Frontier issues from outside the US is through the Frontier app. It works on any internet connection — hotel Wi-Fi, mobile data, airport Wi-Fi — in any country.

Self-service tasks available globally through the app: voluntary flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets, eCredit application at checkout, cancellation processing, same-day standby additions, flight status and gate checks, boarding pass retrieval.

These tasks have no phone charges, no international rates, and no hold time.

## Time Zone Considerations for International Callers

Frontier's lowest wait times are Tuesday through Thursday, 5 to 7 AM Eastern time. From international time zones:

- UK (BST, UTC+1): 6 to 8 AM BST
- Central Europe (CEST, UTC+2): 11 AM to 1 PM CEST
- Japan (JST, UTC+9): 6 to 8 PM JST
- Australia Eastern (AEST, UTC+10): 7 to 9 PM AEST

Timing your call to align with the US off-peak window reduces wait times even when calling from abroad.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I call Frontier customer service from outside the United States?**
Yes. Dial +1-(866)-227-2948 using your country's international dialing prefix. On most mobile phones, entering +1-(866)-227-2948 directly works from any country.

**Does Frontier have customer service numbers for international callers?**
Yes. Visit flyfrontier.com under Contact Us and select your country for local or regional numbers. Available for UK, Canada, Japan, Mexico, Australia, Germany, and many others.

**Can I use the Frontier app to contact Frontier from outside the US?**
Yes. The Frontier app works in any country with internet access. Self-service tasks have no phone charges or international rates.

**Does Frontier customer service support multiple languages for international callers?**
English is the primary language. Country-specific numbers at flyfrontier.com may offer local language service for select markets.

**What is the best way to contact Frontier from Europe?**
Frontier app for self-service (no charges). For live agent issues, call +1-(866)-227-2948 or find a European regional number at flyfrontier.com. Time calls for 11 AM to 1 PM Central European time to hit the US off-peak window.

- Country numbers: flyfrontier.com → Contact Us → select your country
- No-charge option: Frontier app (works globally)
- US off-peak in UTC: 09:00 to 11:00 UTC (Tuesday through Thursday)
