# How Long Does a Delta eCredit Last in 2026?

Delta eCredits last exactly one year from the original ticket purchase date. Not the cancellation date. Not the travel date. The purchase date. Understanding this single rule prevents the most common reason passengers lose eCredit value. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to confirm the exact expiration on your specific credit.

## The One-Year Rule: Purchase Date, Not Cancellation Date

Delta eCredits expire on the anniversary of your original ticket purchase. If you bought a ticket on April 15, 2025, for a trip in July 2025, and then cancelled in June 2025, your eCredit expires April 15, 2026 — not June 2026 and not July 2026.

This catches passengers off guard. A ticket purchased in January that is cancelled in October still expires in January — nine months away from when the credit was generated. The credit does not get a fresh one-year window from cancellation.

The good news is that you only need to book a new flight before the expiration date — travel can happen after. Delta opens its schedule 331 days in advance, so you can book nearly 11 months of future travel before a credit expires.

## Checking Your eCredit Expiration

To find your exact expiration date: log in to delta.com and go to My Wallet under your SkyMiles profile, then Credits and Certificates. Each active eCredit shows its value and expiration date. On the Fly Delta app, go to Account, then My Wallet, then Certificates and eCredits.

Alternatively, go to delta.com/redeem and search by your 13-digit ticket number (starting with 006, from your original booking confirmation email) to find the credit and its expiration date without logging in.

If you cannot find your credit or the expiration date is unclear, call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** with your original ticket number for confirmation.

If a credit is approaching expiration and you have no travel plans, book a placeholder Classic or Extra flight on any future date. You only need to complete the booking before expiration — the travel can happen after.

If a credit has already expired, call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** immediately. Extensions are not standard policy, but Delta occasionally considers reinstatement for extenuating circumstances or when the credit originated from a Delta-initiated cancellation that should have been a cash refund.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does a Delta eCredit last?**
One year from the original ticket purchase date — not the cancellation date. If you bought a ticket in January 2025 and cancelled it in August 2025, the eCredit expires in January 2026. You only need to book by the expiration date; travel can occur after.

**Do I have to travel before my Delta eCredit expires?**
No. You only need to book a new flight before the expiration date. The actual travel can occur after the credit expires. Delta opens its schedule 331 days in advance, giving you nearly 11 months of future travel to book before an expiring credit runs out.

**What happens if I partially use a Delta eCredit?**
The remaining balance is reissued as a new eCredit with the same original expiration date. The expiration clock does not reset from the date of partial use.
