# How to Reach Delta Customer Service From Outside the US

Delta customer service can be reached from outside the US by calling the US line +1-(866)-332-0756 using international dialing (add your country's exit code before the +1). Delta also maintains country-specific customer service numbers for major markets. The Fly Delta app and delta.com work in all countries with no call required for self-service tasks.

Delta customer service is reachable from any country in the world — either through the US number with international dialing, through country-specific numbers for major markets, or through the Fly Delta app which works globally with no phone charges. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** from outside the US using your country's international dialing prefix, or use the methods below.

## Calling the US Number From Abroad

The most direct way to reach Delta from outside the US is to call the US customer service number with your country's international exit code.

**Format**: [Your country's exit code] + 1-866-332-0756

Examples:
- From the UK: 001-866-332-0756 (or +1-866-332-0756 on a mobile)
- From Australia: 0011-866-332-0756 (or +1-866-332-0756 on a mobile)
- From Mexico: 00-1-866-332-0756
- From Japan: 010-1-866-332-0756
- From Germany: 00-1-866-332-0756

On most modern mobile phones, entering +1-(866)-332-0756 directly works from any country without needing to know the specific exit code. The + replaces the exit code automatically.

International calling charges may apply depending on your carrier plan. Check whether your plan includes international calls to US toll-free numbers.

## Delta's Country-Specific Numbers

Delta maintains dedicated customer service numbers for many international markets. These avoid international calling charges and may offer service in the local language for select countries.

To find your country's number: go to delta.com, scroll to the footer or go to Contact Us, and select your country from the dropdown menu. Major markets with dedicated numbers include the United Kingdom, Canada, Mexico, Japan, South Korea, Australia, Germany, France, China, and Brazil.

## The Fly Delta App: No International Charges

The most cost-effective way to handle most Delta issues from outside the US is through the Fly Delta app. It works on any internet connection — hotel Wi-Fi, mobile data, airport Wi-Fi — in any country.

Self-service tasks available globally through the app: voluntary flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets, eCredit application at checkout, cancellation processing, same-day standby additions, flight status and gate checks, boarding pass retrieval.

These tasks have no phone charges, no international rates, and no hold time.

## Time Zone Considerations for International Callers

Delta's lowest wait times are Tuesday through Thursday, 5 to 7 AM Eastern time. From international time zones:

- UK (BST, UTC+1): 6 to 8 AM BST
- Central Europe (CEST, UTC+2): 11 AM to 1 PM CEST
- Japan (JST, UTC+9): 6 to 8 PM JST
- Australia Eastern (AEST, UTC+10): 7 to 9 PM AEST

Timing your call to align with the US off-peak window reduces wait times even when calling from abroad.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I call Delta customer service from outside the United States?**
Yes. Dial +1-(866)-332-0756 using your country's international dialing prefix. On most mobile phones, entering +1-(866)-332-0756 directly works from any country.

**Does Delta have customer service numbers for international callers?**
Yes. Visit delta.com under Contact Us and select your country for local or regional numbers. Available for UK, Canada, Japan, Mexico, Australia, Germany, and many others.

**Can I use the Fly Delta app to contact Delta from outside the US?**
Yes. The Fly Delta app works in any country with internet access. Self-service tasks have no phone charges or international rates.

**Does Delta customer service support multiple languages for international callers?**
English is the primary language. Country-specific numbers at delta.com may offer local language service for select markets.

**What is the best way to contact Delta from Europe?**
Fly Delta app for self-service (no charges). For live agent issues, call +1-(866)-332-0756 or find a European regional number at delta.com. Time calls for 11 AM to 1 PM Central European time to hit the US off-peak window.

## Quick Reference

- International dial: **+1-(866)-332-0756** (use your country's exit code or + prefix)
- Country numbers: delta.com → Contact Us → select your country
- No-charge option: Fly Delta app (works globally)
- US off-peak in UTC: 09:00 to 11:00 UTC (Tuesday through Thursday)
