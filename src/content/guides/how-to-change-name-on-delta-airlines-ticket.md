# How to Change Name on Delta Airlines Ticket

A misspelled name on your Delta ticket can become a serious problem at the airport. TSA agents and airline staff will match the name on your boarding pass against your government-issued ID. If they don't match, you could be denied boarding. Fortunately, Delta has a process in place to handle name corrections. Call the Delta booking line at **+1-(866)-332-0756** as soon as you notice the error and get it resolved before your departure date.

One important thing to understand upfront: Delta draws a clear line between a **name correction** and a **ticket transfer**. Delta does not allow you to transfer a ticket to a different person. What is allowed is correcting the spelling of the existing passenger's name to match their official ID. Knowing this distinction will save you time and confusion when you reach out.

## Name Correction vs Legal Name Change

Delta handles two types of name-related requests differently.

**Minor spelling corrections** cover small typos such as transposed letters, extra characters, or slight misspellings. Examples include "Jhon" instead of "John" or "Christin" instead of "Christine." For these cases, Delta typically processes the correction at no charge as long as the change involves no more than three characters and is reported promptly after booking.

**Legal name changes** apply to passengers who have officially changed their name due to marriage, divorce, or a court order. Delta permits this type of change but requires supporting documentation. You will need to provide a marriage certificate, divorce decree, or court document showing the legal name change. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to get guidance on exactly which documents to submit and how to send them so the process goes smoothly.

## How to Request a Name Change with Delta

There are several ways to get a name correction processed.

**By phone** is the fastest and most reliable method, especially if your departure is within 72 hours. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** and have your booking confirmation number ready, the name currently on the ticket, the correct name as it appears on your ID, and your passport or government ID number. The agent will check your booking in real time and tell you whether the correction can be made and whether any fee applies.

**Online via delta.com** is sometimes available for minor spelling errors. Log in to My Trips, find your booking, and check whether an edit option appears for the passenger name. Not all tickets support self-service name edits, particularly promotional fares or tickets that have already been modified.

**At the airport** is a last resort if you only notice the problem after arriving. A Delta check-in agent can help with minor corrections, though the process takes longer and not every counter can handle complex name change requests under time pressure.

Regardless of which method you use, you will need to go through Delta directly. There is no automated way to change a name on a Delta ticket without speaking to an agent or using the official customer portal.

## How Much Does a Delta Name Change Cost?

Delta does not charge a flat fee for all name corrections. Several factors determine whether you pay anything at all.

Minor typo corrections are generally free if you report them quickly after booking. The sooner you catch the error, the less likely you are to incur any charge.

Legal name changes due to marriage or divorce are also typically processed without a fee when supported by the right documentation, though this can vary depending on your fare type.

Your ticket type plays a major role. Basic Economy tickets are the most restrictive and offer almost no flexibility for changes of any kind, including name corrections. Main Cabin, Comfort+, First Class, and Delta One tickets have more room for adjustments. Tickets purchased through a third-party travel agency must be changed through that agency rather than directly with Delta.

To find out the exact cost for your specific booking, call **+1-(855)-321-3528**. An agent can pull up your reservation and give you a real-time cost estimate before you commit to anything.

## What Happens If You Leave the Name Wrong?

Many passengers assume a one or two letter difference will not matter. In practice, it can cause serious problems.

At domestic US airports, TSA checks your boarding pass name against your ID at every security checkpoint. A name that deviates too much from your passport or driver's license can result in you being denied entry to the boarding area.

For international flights, the stakes are even higher. Immigration authorities at your destination country may deny entry if the name on your ticket does not match your passport, even for what seems like a minor spelling difference.

If you realize there is a name error after completing online check-in, call **+1-(855)-321-3528** before you get to the airport. It is much easier to resolve this remotely than to deal with it at the check-in counter with a long line behind you and a departure clock ticking.

## Name Changes on Codeshare and Delta Partner Flights

Delta operates within the SkyTeam alliance and runs many codeshare flights with partner airlines including Air France, KLM, Korean Air, and Virgin Atlantic. If your itinerary involves a codeshare flight, the name correction process can be more complex because the change needs to be reflected in two airline systems simultaneously.

In these situations, Delta will typically coordinate with the partner airline, but you still need to contact Delta first as the ticket issuer. When you call **+1-(866)-332-0756**, let the agent know your flight is a codeshare so they can immediately take the appropriate steps on both sides.

## Tips to Avoid Name Problems on Future Bookings

Prevention is always easier than a correction. A few habits can save you a lot of trouble.

Always enter your name exactly as it appears on the passport or government ID you plan to use at the airport. Avoid nicknames, shortened versions, or middle name variations that differ from your official document.

Review the spelling before clicking the payment button. Many booking platforms offer a short window after purchase during which changes can be made at no cost. Take advantage of this if you catch a mistake right after booking.

If you are planning to get married or change your name legally in the near future, consider booking your ticket under the name on your new official ID once it has been issued. Alternatively, use your current legal name and update after the change is finalized.

For families booking multiple tickets at once, check each passenger name individually. Errors are most common with children's names or middle names that are rarely used day to day.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can Delta transfer a ticket to a different person?**
No. Delta does not allow ticket ownership to be transferred to another person. Changes are only permitted to correct the spelling of the original ticketed passenger's name.

**How long does a Delta name change take?**
Minor spelling corrections processed by phone are typically completed within minutes. Legal name changes requiring document verification can take one to two business days. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** for a more specific timeline based on your situation.

**Can I change the name on a Delta ticket after checking in online?**
Once a boarding pass has been issued, name changes become significantly harder to process. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528** immediately if you discover an error after completing online check-in.

**What if I bought my Delta ticket through Expedia, Traveloka, or another third-party platform?**
You need to contact the platform where you made the purchase to request a name correction. Delta cannot directly modify tickets issued by third-party agencies. You can still call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to confirm the right steps for your booking.

**Will a name change affect my SkyMiles account?**
Not as long as the corrected name still belongs to the same passenger. If you have gone through a legal name change, make sure to update your SkyMiles account as well so your miles continue to accumulate correctly.
