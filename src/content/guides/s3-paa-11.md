# Does Delta compensate for delayed flights? — 2026 Answer

Under US law Delta is not required to pay cash compensation for flight delays. For controllable delays of 3+ hours, Delta provides meal vouchers. For overnight controllable delays, Delta provides hotel accommodation and ground transportation. If Delta cancels your flight you get a full cash refund. EU law may require cash for flights departing Europe. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 for assistance.
