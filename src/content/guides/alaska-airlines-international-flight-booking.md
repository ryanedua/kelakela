Alaska Airlines' international flight offering in 2026 is fundamentally different from what it was two years ago. The 2025 merger with Hawaiian Airlines and the April 2026 Sabre Passenger Service System unification transformed Alaska from a primarily West Coast domestic and near-international carrier into a significantly expanded network with genuine transpacific reach. Travelers who haven't booked Alaska internationally since the merger may find the available routes, cabin products, and booking processes have changed substantially. This guide covers everything you need to know to book an Alaska Airlines international flight correctly in 2026. For personalized booking assistance, call **+1-(855)-321-3341**.

## Alaska Airlines' International Network in 2026

The combined Alaska and Hawaiian network following the Sabre PSS unification in April 2026 now operates international flights across several distinct geographic regions.

**Hawaii** is now fully integrated into Alaska's booking system, with service from Seattle, Portland, San Francisco, Los Angeles, San Diego, and other West Coast cities to Honolulu, Maui (OGG), Kona, Lihue, and Hilo. Inter-island flights within Hawaii are also bookable under a single Alaska itinerary for the first time, making multi-island Hawaii trips straightforward to construct in a single reservation.

**Transpacific routes** inherited from Hawaiian Airlines now operate as Alaska flights, including service to Tokyo Haneda, Osaka, Seoul Incheon, Sydney, and Auckland. These are among the longest routes in the combined network and are served by widebody aircraft — primarily the Airbus A330 previously operated by Hawaiian — with a full international cabin configuration.

**Canada** is served by Alaska's own operations, with direct flights from Seattle, Portland, San Francisco, and Los Angeles to Vancouver, Calgary, and Victoria.

**Mexico** receives direct service from multiple West Coast cities to Cabo San Lucas, Puerto Vallarta, Cancun, Mexico City, and Guadalajara.

**Central America and the Caribbean** are served with direct flights to Costa Rica, Belize, and select Caribbean destinations, primarily from Los Angeles and Seattle.

For current route availability and scheduling on any of these destinations, call **+1-(855)-321-3341**. The April 2026 Sabre integration introduced new scheduling structures, and some routes and frequencies may differ from pre-merger patterns.

## Cabin Classes on Alaska International Flights

Alaska's international cabin configuration varies significantly by route type and aircraft, particularly following the integration of Hawaiian Airlines' widebody fleet.

**First Class** on Alaska's narrowbody Boeing 737 fleet — used on shorter international routes to Canada, Mexico, and some Hawaii routes — features a 2-2 recliner seat configuration with enhanced meal service, priority boarding, and dedicated overhead bin access. This is a domestic-style First Class product, not a lie-flat business class.

**Lie-flat business class** is available on select transpacific routes operated by the Airbus A330 fleet inherited from Hawaiian Airlines, including service to Japan, South Korea, Australia, and New Zealand. These widebody aircraft carry a true international business class product with fully reclinable seats, multi-course dining, premium amenity kits, and priority access. For lie-flat availability on your specific transpacific route, call **+1-(855)-321-3341**.

**Premium Class** provides extra legroom seating in a dedicated section forward of Main Cabin on select routes, including some Hawaii and Mexico services on Boeing 737 aircraft. Premium Class includes enhanced snack and beverage service and priority boarding but does not include a lie-flat bed.

**Main Cabin** is the base fare class on all Alaska international routes, with complimentary snack and beverage service on most flights and full meal service on longer transpacific routes operated by widebody aircraft.

## How to Book Alaska Airlines International Flights

**Booking online at alaskaair.com** works well for straightforward round-trip or one-way itineraries on direct routes within Alaska's own network. The website was updated following the April 2026 Sabre PSS unification to reflect the combined Alaska and Hawaiian inventory, so Hawaii routes, inter-island connections, and transpacific departures are now bookable directly. The flexible date calendar shows fare variation across a 30-day window for identifying the lowest-cost travel dates.

**Booking by phone at +1-(855)-321-3341** is the more effective option for:

**Multi-city itineraries** combining mainland U.S., Hawaii, and transpacific destinations in a single trip. The online search handles point-to-point queries but does not always construct the most efficient open-jaw or multi-destination international routing.

**Inter-island connections combined with transpacific segments** — for example, a routing from Seattle to Honolulu followed by an inter-island connection to Maui, then a return from Kahului to Seattle. These multi-segment Hawaii itineraries are easier to construct and ticket correctly by phone.

**Mileage Plan award bookings** on oneworld partner airlines, where availability may not be fully visible through alaskaair.com.

**Group bookings** of ten or more passengers requiring coordinated seating and ticketing across international routes.

**Post-merger routing questions** — if you are unsure which routes are now operated under Alaska flight numbers versus legacy Hawaiian numbers, a phone agent can clarify the current operating structure and book accordingly.

## Checked Baggage on Alaska International Flights

Alaska's international baggage policy varies by route, cabin class, and Mileage Plan elite status.

**Main Cabin on Hawaii routes:** First checked bag fees apply for most standard Main Cabin fares. Mileage Plan MVP members and above receive the first checked bag free. Alaska Airlines Visa credit card holders receive the first bag free for themselves and up to six companions on the same reservation.

**Main Cabin on transpacific routes (Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand):** One or two free checked bags are typically included depending on the specific fare class, consistent with international long-haul norms. Confirm the specific rule attached to your fare before packing.

**First Class and lie-flat business class:** Two free checked bags at no charge, with a per-bag weight allowance of 70 pounds on most international routes.

**Mileage Plan MVP Gold and MVP Gold 75K:** One and two additional free checked bags respectively on international itineraries beyond the standard allowance.

For confirmation of the exact baggage allowance on your specific fare and route, call **+1-(855)-321-3341**.

## Mileage Plan Awards for International Travel

Alaska Mileage Plan miles can be redeemed for international flights on Alaska's own network and on oneworld alliance partners, which Alaska joined formally in 2021. The oneworld partnership gives Mileage Plan members access to award space on British Airways, Qantas, Japan Airlines, Cathay Pacific, Iberia, Finnair, and other member carriers.

**Alaska Mileage Plan awards** use a partner chart for redemptions on Alaska-operated flights. Award pricing is competitive on transpacific and Hawaii routes, particularly in premium cabins where Alaska's award rates are frequently lower than comparable programs at other carriers.

**Atmos Rewards** — the post-merger rebrand of Alaska's combined loyalty platform integrating both Mileage Plan and the former HawaiianMiles program — is the current program name as of 2026. Legacy HawaiianMiles balances were converted to Mileage Plan miles under the merger integration timeline.

**Oneworld partner awards** allow Mileage Plan redemption for flights on Japan Airlines — particularly useful for transpacific routing to Japan beyond Alaska's own service — as well as Qantas for Australian destinations and British Airways for connecting European routes. Partner award availability requires phone confirmation in many cases. For oneworld partner award assistance, call **+1-(855)-321-3341**.

## Entry Requirements and Documentation

International travel on Alaska's expanded network requires destination-specific documentation beyond the ticket itself.

**Japan and South Korea:** U.S. passport holders can enter Japan and South Korea visa-free for short stays (90 days and 90 days respectively) but must ensure passport validity of at least six months beyond the return date. Japan also requires a Visit Japan Web registration for smooth customs processing.

**Australia and New Zealand:** U.S. passport holders require an Electronic Travel Authority (ETA) for Australia and a New Zealand Electronic Travel Authority (NZeTA) before departure. These are online applications processed within minutes but must be completed before check-in.

**Canada:** U.S. citizens can enter Canada with a valid U.S. passport. Canadian citizens flying to the U.S. require a passport and, if applicable, a U.S. visa or ESTA.

**Mexico and Central America:** U.S. passport holders enter most Alaska-served Mexico and Costa Rica destinations without a visa. Tourist cards are typically required and issued at the airport.

For any destination-specific documentation questions, confirm requirements with the relevant country's embassy or consulate well before your travel date.
