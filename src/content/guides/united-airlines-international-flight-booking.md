United Airlines operates one of the largest international route networks available to U.S. travelers in 2026, connecting passengers to destinations across Europe, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, the Middle East, Africa, and Oceania through its own aircraft and the Star Alliance partnership network. The booking decisions made before departure — cabin class, hub routing, fare type, and whether to pay cash or redeem MileagePlus miles — directly determine the cost and quality of the entire journey. For personalized booking assistance or help with complex international itineraries, call **+1-(844)-578-2245**.

## United's International Hub Network in 2026

United organizes its international operations around five primary hubs, each serving a distinct geographic region of the world.

**Newark Liberty International (EWR)** is United's dominant transatlantic hub on the East Coast, offering nonstop service to London Heathrow, London Gatwick, Dublin, Amsterdam, Frankfurt, Zurich, Paris, Lisbon, Madrid, Rome, Tel Aviv, and Johannesburg among others. For East Coast travelers heading to Europe, Africa, or the Middle East, Newark provides the densest United-operated schedule of direct departures.

**San Francisco International (SFO)** anchors United's transpacific operations, with nonstop flights to Tokyo Narita, Tokyo Haneda, Osaka, Shanghai, Beijing, Hong Kong, Singapore, Sydney, Melbourne, Auckland, and Seoul. SFO is the optimal departure point for any Asia-Pacific or Oceania itinerary on United.

**Houston George Bush Intercontinental (IAH)** is the primary Latin America hub, offering direct service to more than 20 countries including Mexico City, Bogota, Lima, Santiago, Buenos Aires, São Paulo, and Panama City. For Central and South America routes, IAH provides the widest United-operated schedule of any hub.

**Chicago O'Hare International (ORD)** serves as a secondary transatlantic and transpacific hub with direct service to London, Frankfurt, Tokyo, and Shanghai, as well as extensive domestic connections feeding into United's international network.

**Los Angeles International (LAX) and Washington Dulles (IAD)** provide additional long-haul departure capacity, particularly for transpacific and select European routes, and serve as connection gateways for West Coast and Mid-Atlantic travelers.

Beyond United's own aircraft, the **Star Alliance network** — the world's largest airline alliance with 26 member carriers — extends United's effective global reach to destinations not served by United metal. Lufthansa, Swiss, Austrian, ANA, Air Canada, Singapore Airlines, Turkish Airlines, TAP Air Portugal, Copa Airlines, and others provide connecting service bookable on a single United ticket with coordinated baggage transfer. For Star Alliance routing options for your destination, call **+1-(844)-578-2245**.

## United's International Cabin Classes

United operates four distinct cabin classes on international routes. Matching the right product to your trip length, budget, and comfort requirements before booking saves both money and post-purchase regret.

**United Economy** is the base international fare class on all United international routes. On long-haul widebody aircraft, Economy seats include personal in-seat entertainment screens, USB charging, adjustable headrests, and complimentary meals and non-alcoholic beverages on most flights. Seat configuration and pitch vary by aircraft — the Boeing 787 Dreamliner and Boeing 777 offer different Economy layouts with varying degrees of comfort on identical routes.

**United Economy Plus** adds 3 to 6 inches of seat pitch above standard Economy and includes dedicated overhead bin access on most aircraft. On flights exceeding eight hours, the additional legroom meaningfully reduces fatigue. Economy Plus is bookable as an upgrade from Economy at the time of booking or at check-in, and pricing varies by route and demand. United Premier Silver members and above may receive complimentary Economy Plus access at check-in based on availability and fare class.

**United Business** is available on select shorter international routes — typically transatlantic flights under six hours or certain Latin American routes — where the Polaris product is not deployed. Business class on these routes features a standard recliner seat configuration rather than a lie-flat bed, with enhanced meal service, priority boarding, and dedicated overhead bin space. It is a meaningful upgrade over Economy Plus on routes where Polaris is unavailable.

**United Polaris** is United's flagship international business class, deployed on long-haul widebody routes on Boeing 787 Dreamliner, Boeing 777, and Boeing 767 aircraft. Polaris seats convert to fully lie-flat beds of 180 degrees with direct aisle access for every passenger — no climbing over a seatmate during an overnight flight. The product includes Saks Fifth Avenue bedding and pillows, a premium multi-course dining service, a dedicated amenity kit, priority security and boarding, and access to United Polaris Lounges at hub airports in Chicago, Newark, San Francisco, Houston, Los Angeles, and Washington Dulles. For Polaris availability and pricing on your specific route, call **+1-(844)-578-2245**.

## How to Book United International Flights

**Booking online at united.com** is best suited for straightforward round-trip or one-way itineraries on United-operated flights between two cities. The flexible date calendar shows fare variation across a 30-day window, useful for identifying the lowest-cost travel dates within a flexible window.

**Booking by phone at +1-(844)-578-2245** is the more effective option for:

**Multi-city itineraries** involving three or more destinations. United's online search handles point-to-point queries but does not always surface the most efficient or cost-effective routing for complex open-jaw or multi-destination trips.

**Mixed-cabin bookings** where you want Polaris on the long transoceanic leg and Economy on a short domestic connection. Mixed-cabin tickets require manual construction not always available through the standard online flow.

**Star Alliance partner award redemptions** where partner availability is not fully visible or bookable on united.com. Many partner Saver awards require phone confirmation and manual ticketing.

**Group bookings** of ten or more passengers requiring coordinated seating and ticketing.

**Last-minute international bookings** within 48 to 72 hours of departure where seat assignments, meal pre-orders, and special service requests need to be confirmed simultaneously with the ticket.

## Checked Baggage on United International Flights

United's international baggage policy varies by cabin class, MileagePlus Premier status, destination region, and specific fare class purchased.

**United Economy:** First checked bag fees vary by route. Many transatlantic Economy fares include one free checked bag. Transpacific and Latin American routes vary by fare type — confirm the specific rule attached to your fare before packing.

**United Economy Plus:** Matches the checked baggage allowance of the Economy fare on your ticket unless modified by status or credit card benefits.

**United Business and Polaris:** Two checked bags included at no charge, with a per-bag weight allowance of 70 pounds on most international routes.

**MileagePlus Premier status:** Premier Silver and Gold members receive one additional free checked bag on international itineraries. Premier Platinum and 1K members receive two additional free checked bags.

**United-affiliated credit cards:** The United Explorer Card and United Club Infinite Card each provide first checked bag free for the cardholder and one companion on the same reservation.

Before finalizing any international booking, confirm the exact baggage allowance for your specific fare by calling **+1-(844)-578-2245**, as rules differ significantly across fare buckets within the same cabin.

## MileagePlus Awards for International Travel

United MileagePlus miles can be redeemed for international flights on United-operated services and across all Star Alliance member carriers. The program uses dynamic pricing in 2026, meaning mileage costs fluctuate based on demand, route, cabin class, and booking lead time.

**Saver awards** represent the lowest mileage tier and deliver the best value per mile. Saver space is limited and tends to open either 11 to 12 months before departure when airlines first load their schedules, or within 14 to 30 days of departure as unsold inventory is released.

**Everyday awards** offer broader availability with significantly more miles required. On United Polaris long-haul routes, the gap between a Saver and Everyday award can represent tens of thousands of miles.

**Star Alliance partner awards** allow MileagePlus redemption on Lufthansa, ANA, Singapore Airlines, Air Canada, Turkish Airlines, and other alliance members — particularly valuable for reaching destinations where United doesn't operate its own aircraft. Partner award availability is not always visible on united.com and frequently requires a phone call to confirm and ticket. For Star Alliance award assistance, call **+1-(844)-578-2245**.

## Entry Requirements and Travel Documentation

Booking the ticket is the first step. Documentation requirements for international travel must be confirmed separately before departure.

**Passport validity:** Many countries require your passport to remain valid for six months beyond your return date, not just your departure date. Check destination-specific requirements well before booking.

**Visa requirements:** U.S. passport holders have visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to the majority of United destinations, but China, India, Brazil, Vietnam, and several African nations require advance visa applications with processing times of four to six weeks or more.

**APIS data:** United collects Advance Passenger Information System data — passport number, nationality, date of birth, and destination address — during online check-in. Have your passport and accommodation details available when checking in online.
