# Can I transfer my Delta eCredit to someone else? — 2026 Answer

No. Delta eCredits are non-transferable. They can only be used by the original ticketed passenger whose name is on the credit. You cannot give, sell, or transfer an eCredit to another person. However, you can use your eCredit to book a flight that includes both yourself and a companion on the same reservation. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 for multi-passenger eCredit bookings.
