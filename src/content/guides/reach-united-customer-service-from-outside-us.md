# How to Reach United Customer Service From Outside the US

United customer service can be reached from outside the US by calling the US line +1-(855)-235-1859 using international dialing (add your country's exit code before the +1). United also maintains country-specific customer service numbers for major markets. The United app and united.com work in all countries with no call required for self-service tasks.

United customer service is reachable from any country in the world — either through the US number with international dialing, through country-specific numbers for major markets, or through the United app which works globally with no phone charges. Call **+1-(855)-235-1859)** from outside the US using your country's international dialing prefix, or use the methods below.

## Calling the US Number From Abroad

The most direct way to reach United from outside the US is to call the US customer service number with your country's international exit code.

**Format**: [Your country's exit code] + 1-866-332-0756

Examples:
- From the UK: 001-866-332-0756 (or +1-866-332-0756 on a mobile)
- From Australia: 0011-866-332-0756 (or +1-866-332-0756 on a mobile)
- From Mexico: 00-1-866-332-0756
- From Japan: 010-1-866-332-0756
- From Germany: 00-1-866-332-0756

On most modern mobile phones, entering +1-(855)-235-1859 directly works from any country without needing to know the specific exit code. The + replaces the exit code automatically.

International calling charges may apply depending on your carrier plan. Check whether your plan includes international calls to US toll-free numbers.

## United's Country-Specific Numbers

United maintains dedicated customer service numbers for many international markets. These avoid international calling charges and may offer service in the local language for select countries.

To find your country's number: go to united.com, scroll to the footer or go to Contact Us, and select your country from the dropdown menu. Major markets with dedicated numbers include the United Kingdom, Canada, Mexico, Japan, South Korea, Australia, Germany, France, China, and Brazil.

## The Fly United App: No International Charges

The most cost-effective way to handle most United issues from outside the US is through the United app. It works on any internet connection — hotel Wi-Fi, mobile data, airport Wi-Fi — in any country.

Self-service tasks available globally through the app: voluntary flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets, eCredit application at checkout, cancellation processing, same-day standby additions, flight status and gate checks, boarding pass retrieval.

These tasks have no phone charges, no international rates, and no hold time.

## Time Zone Considerations for International Callers

United's lowest wait times are Tuesday through Thursday, 5 to 7 AM Eastern time. From international time zones:

- UK (BST, UTC+1): 6 to 8 AM BST
- Central Europe (CEST, UTC+2): 11 AM to 1 PM CEST
- Japan (JST, UTC+9): 6 to 8 PM JST
- Australia Eastern (AEST, UTC+10): 7 to 9 PM AEST

Timing your call to align with the US off-peak window reduces wait times even when calling from abroad.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I call United customer service from outside the United States?**
Yes. Dial +1-(855)-235-1859 using your country's international dialing prefix. On most mobile phones, entering +1-(855)-235-1859 directly works from any country.

**Does United have customer service numbers for international callers?**
Yes. Visit united.com under Contact Us and select your country for local or regional numbers. Available for UK, Canada, Japan, Mexico, Australia, Germany, and many others.

**Can I use the United app to contact United from outside the US?**
Yes. The United app works in any country with internet access. Self-service tasks have no phone charges or international rates.

**Does United customer service support multiple languages for international callers?**
English is the primary language. Country-specific numbers at united.com may offer local language service for select markets.

**What is the best way to contact United from Europe?**
United app for self-service (no charges). For live agent issues, call +1-(855)-235-1859 or find a European regional number at united.com. Time calls for 11 AM to 1 PM Central European time to hit the US off-peak window.

## Quick Reference

- International dial: **+1-(855)-235-1859** (use your country's exit code or + prefix)
- Country numbers: united.com → Contact Us → select your country
- No-charge option: United app (works globally)
- US off-peak in UTC: 09:00 to 11:00 UTC (Tuesday through Thursday)
