## How Priceline's Cheap Flight Tools Actually Work

Priceline gives travelers three distinct ways to find below-market fares, and knowing which tool to use can make the difference between a decent deal and a genuinely cheap ticket.

**Express Deals** are the fastest route to a discounted fare. Priceline negotiates bulk inventory with airlines and passes the savings to you, but the airline name is only revealed after purchase. You'll see the price, approximate departure time window, and number of stops before committing. Express Deals typically run **20-40% below standard fares** on the same route.

**Name Your Own Price** lets you enter a bid for a flight. If an airline in Priceline's network accepts your offer, the booking is confirmed immediately. Tickets booked this way are non-refundable and non-changeable, so only use this tool when your schedule is fixed.

**Standard flight search** with the **flexible date calendar** is the safest starting point. The calendar view color-codes fares from cheapest to most expensive across a full month, making it easy to spot the lowest fare window without manually checking each date.

## Strategies to Book the Lowest Fare on Priceline in 2026

Getting a cheap flight on Priceline is less about luck and more about using the right combination of filters at the right time.

**Search mid-week, fly mid-week.** Tuesdays and Wednesdays consistently show the lowest fares on Priceline's calendar, both for domestic and transatlantic routes. Avoid Friday and Sunday searches for the best rates.

**Set a Price Alert.** Priceline allows you to track a route and receive notifications when the fare drops. This is especially useful for trips booked 3-8 weeks out, where fares tend to fluctuate the most.

**Layer Express Deals with the lowest-price filter.** After running a standard search, toggle to the Express Deals tab and sort by price. Compare the Express Deal price against the standard result for the same route. If the gap is over 15%, the Express Deal is usually worth the trade-off in flexibility.

**Bundle with a hotel.** Priceline's Flight + Hotel packages frequently unlock additional savings that are not available when booking flights separately. Even a one-night hotel add-on can reduce the total flight cost in a bundle.

## What to Know Before You Book a Cheap Priceline Flight

Cheap fares on Priceline come with conditions that matter. Before confirming any booking, check these details.

**Refundability.** Express Deals and Name Your Own Price tickets are non-refundable. Standard fares vary by airline and fare class. Always read the fare rules before paying, especially if your travel dates could change.

**Baggage fees are separate.** Priceline displays the base fare, but airlines add baggage fees on top. A $79 Express Deal fare on a budget carrier can easily reach $130+ once a checked bag is included. Factor this in before comparing against a higher base fare that includes a bag.

**Seat selection.** Most discounted fares on Priceline do not include advance seat selection. If sitting together with a travel companion is important, check whether the airline allows seat selection after booking and what the fee is.

**Travel protection.** Priceline offers optional travel insurance at checkout through a third-party provider. For cheap non-refundable tickets, this is worth considering if there is any chance your plans could change.

**What is Priceline's Name Your Own Price for flights?**

Name Your Own Price is a bidding feature where you submit a fare you're willing to pay for a given route and travel window. If an airline in Priceline's network accepts your bid, the booking is confirmed immediately and charged to your payment method. You won't know the airline or exact departure time until after the bid is accepted. **Does Priceline charge fees for flight bookings?**

However, if the airline charges fees for changes, cancellations, or seat selection, those apply on top of what Priceline displays.
