# How to Reach Southwest Customer Service From Outside the US

Southwest customer service can be reached from outside the US by calling the US line +1-(855)-321-3191 using international dialing (add your country's exit code before the +1). Southwest also maintains country-specific customer service numbers for major markets. The Southwest app and southwest.com work in all countries with no call required for self-service tasks.

Southwest customer service is reachable from any country in the world — either through the US number with international dialing, through country-specific numbers for major markets, or through the Southwest app which works globally with no phone charges. Call **+1-(855)-321-3191)** from outside the US using your country's international dialing prefix, or use the methods below.

## Calling the US Number From Abroad

The most direct way to reach Southwest from outside the US is to call the US customer service number with your country's international exit code.

**Format**: [Your country's exit code] + 1-866-332-0756

Examples:
- From the UK: 001-866-332-0756 (or +1-866-332-0756 on a mobile)
- From Australia: 0011-866-332-0756 (or +1-866-332-0756 on a mobile)
- From Mexico: 00-1-866-332-0756
- From Japan: 010-1-866-332-0756
- From Germany: 00-1-866-332-0756

On most modern mobile phones, entering +1-(855)-321-3191 directly works from any country without needing to know the specific exit code. The + replaces the exit code automatically.

International calling charges may apply depending on your carrier plan. Check whether your plan includes international calls to US toll-free numbers.

## Southwest's Country-Specific Numbers

Southwest maintains dedicated customer service numbers for many international markets. These avoid international calling charges and may offer service in the local language for select countries.

To find your country's number: go to southwest.com, scroll to the footer or go to Contact Us, and select your country from the dropdown menu. Major markets with dedicated numbers include the United Kingdom, Canada, Mexico, Japan, South Korea, Australia, Germany, France, China, and Brazil.

## The Fly Southwest App: No International Charges

The most cost-effective way to handle most Southwest issues from outside the US is through the Southwest app. It works on any internet connection — hotel Wi-Fi, mobile data, airport Wi-Fi — in any country.

Self-service tasks available globally through the app: voluntary flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets, eCredit application at checkout, cancellation processing, same-day standby additions, flight status and gate checks, boarding pass retrieval.

These tasks have no phone charges, no international rates, and no hold time.

## Time Zone Considerations for International Callers

Southwest's lowest wait times are Tuesday through Thursday, 5 to 7 AM Eastern time. From international time zones:

- UK (BST, UTC+1): 6 to 8 AM BST
- Central Europe (CEST, UTC+2): 11 AM to 1 PM CEST
- Japan (JST, UTC+9): 6 to 8 PM JST
- Australia Eastern (AEST, UTC+10): 7 to 9 PM AEST

Timing your call to align with the US off-peak window reduces wait times even when calling from abroad.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I call Southwest customer service from outside the United States?**
Yes. Dial +1-(855)-321-3191 using your country's international dialing prefix. On most mobile phones, entering +1-(855)-321-3191 directly works from any country.

**Does Southwest have customer service numbers for international callers?**
Yes. Visit southwest.com under Contact Us and select your country for local or regional numbers. Available for UK, Canada, Japan, Mexico, Australia, Germany, and many others.

**Can I use the Southwest app to contact Southwest from outside the US?**
Yes. The Southwest app works in any country with internet access. Self-service tasks have no phone charges or international rates.

**Does Southwest customer service support multiple languages for international callers?**
English is the primary language. Country-specific numbers at southwest.com may offer local language service for select markets.

**What is the best way to contact Southwest from Europe?**
Southwest app for self-service (no charges). For live agent issues, call +1-(855)-321-3191 or find a European regional number at southwest.com. Time calls for 11 AM to 1 PM Central European time to hit the US off-peak window.

- Country numbers: southwest.com → Contact Us → select your country
- No-charge option: Southwest app (works globally)
- US off-peak in UTC: 09:00 to 11:00 UTC (Tuesday through Thursday)
