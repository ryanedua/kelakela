# Does Delta Charge a Cancellation Fee? (2026 Rules)



Delta eliminated standalone cancellation fees for Main Classic and Extra tickets in 2020. In 2026, this policy remains fully in place. Cancelling a Main Classic ticket before departure produces a full eCredit with no deduction. Cancelling a Main Extra ticket produces a full cash refund. Main Basic (Basic Economy) tickets carry a cancellation fee that varies by route, deducted from the eCredit before it is issued. All fare types cancel free within 24 hours of purchase. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to confirm the outcome for your specific ticket.







## Key Rules to Know



The cancellation fee on Basic tickets varies by route. For Caribbean routes: $99. For Asia-Pacific: $199 for tickets issued on or after September 26, 2024. For South America: $330 for tickets issued on or after June 25, 2025. If Delta cancels your flight, all fees are waived and a full cash refund is owed regardless of fare type.
