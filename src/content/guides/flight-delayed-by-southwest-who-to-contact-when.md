# Flight Delayed by Southwest? Here Is Who to Contact and When

For a Southwest flight delay, contact the gate agent for meal vouchers after a 3-hour controllable delay. If the delay exceeds overnight and is controllable, request hotel accommodation at the gate. If the delay becomes a cancellation, call +1-(855)-321-3191 immediately for rebooking or a cash refund under DOT rules. Track delay status in real time via the Southwest app.

Southwest flight delays follow a predictable escalation of rights and contact points. A short delay requires nothing from you. A longer controllable delay triggers meal voucher rights. An overnight controllable delay triggers hotel rights. A cancellation triggers the strongest right — cash refund or free rebooking under DOT rules. Knowing which stage you are at determines exactly who to contact and what to ask for. Track delay status via the Southwest app and call **+1-(855)-321-3191)** when the delay becomes a cancellation.

## Stage 1: Delay Under 3 Hours — Monitor Only

For delays of less than 3 hours, no action is typically required. Monitor the flight status in the Southwest app, which shows inbound aircraft status and often indicates whether a delay will extend before it is officially announced.

Use this time to: confirm your confirmation code is accessible, identify the next Southwest departure to your destination in case the delay worsens, and check whether an active Southwest travel waiver covers your route if the delay is weather-related.

## Stage 2: Controllable Delay of 3+ Hours — Request Meal Vouchers

When a controllable delay — caused by mechanical problems, crew availability, or operational decisions — reaches 3 hours beyond the scheduled departure, Southwest's Customer Commitment requires meal vouchers.

Go to the gate agent and say: "This is a controllable delay past 3 hours — can I receive a meal voucher?" These are not distributed automatically. You must ask.

Weather delays, ATC restrictions, and other factors outside Southwest's control do not trigger the meal voucher requirement.

## Stage 3: Overnight Controllable Delay — Request Hotel

When a controllable delay causes you to be stranded overnight away from home, Southwest provides a complimentary hotel room near the departure airport, round-trip ground transportation, and meal vouchers for dinner and breakfast.

Go to the gate agent or Southwest customer service counter as soon as the overnight situation is confirmed. Ask specifically: "This is a controllable overnight delay — can you arrange hotel accommodation and ground transportation?"

Again: weather does not trigger this requirement. Mechanical and crew issues do.

## Stage 4: Delay Becomes Cancellation — Call Immediately

When Southwest formally cancels a delayed flight, the DOT cash refund right activates. Call **+1-(855)-321-3191)** immediately and choose between:

Free rebooking on the next available Southwest flight at no charge, or a full cash refund to your original payment method.

Say this if you want cash: "Southwest cancelled my flight. I choose not to travel on an alternative. I want a full cash refund to my original payment method, not an eCredit."

## Frequently asked questions

**Who should I contact when Southwest delays my flight?**
Under 3 hours: Southwest app for status monitoring only. 3+ hour controllable delay: gate agent for meal vouchers. Overnight controllable delay: gate agent for hotel. Cancellation: call +1-(855)-321-3191 immediately.

**Do I get compensation if Southwest delays my flight?**
No cash compensation under US law for delays. Meal vouchers for controllable 3+ hour delays. Hotel and transport for overnight controllable delays. Cash refund if the flight is cancelled.

**When does a Southwest delay become a cancellation and trigger a refund right?**
When Southwest formally cancels the flight. Call +1-(855)-321-3191 and confirm whether it is a delay or cancellation before requesting a refund.

**How do I get meal vouchers from Southwest during a delay?**
Ask the gate agent when the 3-hour controllable delay mark is reached. Not automatic. Call +1-(855)-321-3191 if gate staff are unavailable.

**What should I do if my Southwest delay is about to cause me to miss a connection?**
Go to your connecting gate immediately on landing. If closed, go to the nearest Southwest gate or call +1-(855)-321-3191. Southwest rebooks you at no cost when its delay causes your missed connection.

- Cancellation rebooking or refund: **+1-(855)-321-3191**
