# How do I apply a Delta eCredit to a new booking? — 2026 Answer

During checkout on delta.com, click Use eCredits in the Payment section and select the credit to apply. Alternatively, go to delta.com/redeem first, load your credit using the 13-digit ticket number starting with 006, and proceed to flight search with the credit pre-loaded. A credit card is still required on file even when the eCredit covers the full fare. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 if the credit is not appearing.
