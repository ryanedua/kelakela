# How much is Delta's same-day confirmed change fee? — 2026 Answer

The Same-Day Confirmed change fee is $75 for general SkyMiles members, Silver Medallion members, and non-members. The fee is waived for Gold Medallion, Platinum Medallion, and Diamond Medallion members on eligible domestic routes. No fare difference applies when staying in the same cabin class. Same-Day Standby is free for all eligible passengers. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 to request.
