# Delta Basic Economy Change Fee 2026: What You Can and Cannot Do

Delta Basic Economy, now officially called Main Basic, is designed around a simple premise: a lower price in exchange for minimal flexibility. Once you are past the 24-hour risk-free window, the ticket cannot be changed. Understanding what you can do, what you cannot do, and when exceptions apply is essential before booking or attempting to modify a Main Basic fare. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** if you have a Main Basic ticket and need to understand your current options.

## Can You Change a Delta Basic Economy Ticket?

No. Delta Main Basic fares do not allow standard ticket changes after the 24-hour risk-free cancellation period. You cannot move to a different departure date, a different flight on the same day, or a different routing using the normal change process.

The only modification available on a Main Basic ticket after the 24-hour window is cancellation. When you cancel, Delta deducts the applicable cancellation fee from the ticket value and issues the remainder as an eCredit, which you can apply toward a future booking.

There is no standard change fee on a Basic Economy ticket because the option to change simply does not exist.

## What You Can Do Instead

**Cancel for an eCredit**: Cancel your Main Basic ticket before the scheduled departure time. Delta deducts the cancellation fee and issues an eCredit for the remaining value. That eCredit can be used toward any future Delta flight for the same passenger. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to confirm your fee and process the cancellation.

**Wait for a Delta schedule change**: If Delta moves your flight by 3 or more hours domestically or 6 or more hours internationally, the change restriction does not apply. You can request a full cash refund or free rebooking on a different flight, even if you originally booked Main Basic. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528** to check whether a qualifying schedule change has been made to your itinerary.

**Use a travel waiver**: If an active Delta weather waiver covers your airport and travel dates, the cancellation fee is waived and you can rebook in the same cabin without charge. Check delta.com/travelinformation for active waivers.

**Wait for Delta to cancel**: If Delta cancels your flight for any reason, the Basic Economy restrictions are lifted entirely. You are entitled to free rebooking or a full cash refund under DOT rules.

## Same-Day Changes on Basic Economy

Same-Day Confirmed and Same-Day Standby changes are both excluded for Main Basic fare holders. If you arrive at the airport early and want to move to an earlier flight, you cannot use the same-day change system on a Basic ticket. You would need to purchase a new ticket separately for the earlier flight.

This is one of the most important practical limitations of the Main Basic fare for travelers who frequently want flexibility on the day of travel.

## When Are Basic Economy Change Restrictions Waived?

There are three scenarios where the standard change restriction on a Main Basic ticket does not apply.

**Delta cancels the flight**: When Delta initiates the cancellation, the Basic restriction is suspended. You get free rebooking or a cash refund.

**Significant schedule change**: A Delta-initiated change of 3 or more hours domestically or 6 or more hours internationally qualifies you for a full cash refund or alternate rebooking regardless of fare type.

**Active weather waiver**: During an active waiver for your covered airports and travel dates, the cancellation fee is typically waived and rebooking within the waiver window is permitted at no charge.

Outside these three exceptions, the Main Basic change restriction is firm.

## The Cancellation Fee: What It Actually Costs You

When you cancel a Main Basic ticket after the 24-hour window, the fee varies by route origin.

For flights originating in the US and Canada, the fee is deducted from the ticket value before the eCredit is issued. The exact amount is built into your fare rules.

For Caribbean routes (US, Canada, Mexico, Central America destinations), the fee is $99.

For Asia-Pacific routes on tickets issued on or after September 26, 2024, the fee is $199.

For South America routes on tickets issued on or after June 26, 2025, the fee is $330.

The eCredit you receive after the fee deduction is valid for one year from the original purchase date. If the fee exceeds the ticket value, no eCredit is issued and the ticket is fully forfeited.

If you are unsure how much will remain after the fee on your specific ticket, call **+1-(855)-321-3528** before cancelling. An agent can calculate the residual value so you know what you will receive.

## Basic Economy in Premium Cabins

As of July 8, 2026, Delta extended Basic fares to Comfort+, Delta First, Delta Premium Select, and Delta One (branded as Basic Business). The same general logic applies: Basic fares in premium cabins have restricted changes and cancellations with fees, while Classic and Extra in the same cabin have no change fees.

If you booked a Basic Business or Delta First Basic ticket, call **+1-(844)-833-1535** to understand the specific change and cancellation rules that apply to your premium cabin Basic fare, as some terms differ from Main Basic.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change a Delta Basic Economy ticket?**
No. Changes are not permitted after the 24-hour window. Only cancellation for an eCredit minus a fee is available. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to process.

**What is the Delta Basic Economy change fee?**
There is no change fee because changes are not allowed. The applicable charge is the cancellation fee deducted from your eCredit. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528** for the fee on your specific route.

**Can I do a same-day change on a Delta Basic Economy ticket?**
No. Same-Day Confirmed and Same-Day Standby are excluded for Main Basic fares.

**Does Delta waive the Basic Economy restriction during weather waivers?**
Yes. An active weather waiver covering your airports and travel dates typically waives the cancellation fee and permits rebooking within the waiver window.

**What happens to my Basic Economy ticket if Delta significantly changes my schedule?**
You are entitled to a full cash refund or free rebooking regardless of Basic Economy restrictions if Delta changes your schedule by 3 or more hours domestically or 6 or more hours internationally.


**Does Delta waive the Basic Economy change restriction during weather waivers?**
Yes. During an active Delta weather waiver, Main Basic passengers in covered markets can rebook without paying the cancellation fee. This is one of the rare exceptions where the Basic change restriction is lifted.

**What happens to my Basic Economy ticket if Delta significantly changes my flight schedule?**
If Delta changes your schedule by 3 or more hours domestically, or 6 or more hours internationally, you are entitled to a full cash refund or free rebooking regardless of Basic Economy restrictions. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 to claim your rights.
