# How to Cancel a Delta Flight (Step-by-Step, All Ticket Types)

Canceling a Delta flight is a process most travelers will face at some point. Whether your plans changed, you bought a refundable ticket, or you need to understand what happens to your Main Basic fare, this guide walks you through every cancellation path for 2026, including which outcomes to expect based on your ticket type.

## Before You Cancel: Know Your Ticket Type

The outcome of your cancellation depends largely on the type of ticket you purchased.

| Ticket Type | Cancellation Outcome |
|---|---|
| Refundable | Full cash refund to original payment |
| Non-refundable (Main Basic, Classic, Extra) | eCredit for unused ticket value |
| Within 24-hour booking window | Full cash refund (all fare classes) |
| SkyMiles award ticket | Miles redeposited; taxes refunded |
| Group booking | Contact Delta group desk |

## How to Cancel a Delta Flight Online (Step-by-Step)

This is the fastest and most common method for most travelers.

### Step 1: Log In to Delta.com

Go to delta.com and log in to your SkyMiles account. If you did not book with a SkyMiles account, you can retrieve your reservation using your confirmation number and last name.

### Step 2: Go to My Trips

Click on **My Trips** from the main navigation menu. This shows all your upcoming reservations.

### Step 3: Select the Flight to Cancel

Find the trip you want to cancel and click on it to open the trip details.

### Step 4: Click "Cancel Flight"

On the trip details page, look for the option to **Cancel Flight**. Click it and review the cancellation details presented on screen, including what refund or eCredit you will receive.

### Step 5: Confirm the Cancellation

Read the cancellation summary carefully, then confirm. You will receive a confirmation email with your cancellation reference number and details of any eCredit or refund issued.

## How to Cancel via the Fly Delta App

1. Open the Fly Delta app and log in.
2. Tap **My Trips** and select your upcoming flight.
3. Tap the flight options menu and choose **Cancel Flight**.
4. Review the refund or eCredit summary and confirm.
5. You will receive a push notification and email confirming the cancellation.

## How to Cancel by Calling Delta

If you prefer to speak with someone or have a complex itinerary, call Delta customer support at **++1-866-332-0756**. An agent can:

- Look up your booking and walk you through cancellation options
- Apply the 24-hour cancellation rule if eligible
- Process an exception refund if Delta has disrupted your travel
- Handle award ticket cancellations and miles redeposit

Phone lines can be busy during peak travel periods, so online cancellation is recommended when time matters.

## Canceling a Delta Main Basic Ticket

Main Basic is Delta's most restrictive economy fare. Cancellation options are limited:

- **Voluntary cancellation** — You receive an eCredit for the unused ticket value. No cash refund is issued.
- **Within 24 hours of booking** — You receive a full cash refund if the flight departs at least 7 days out.
- **Delta-cancelled flight** — You are entitled to a full cash refund under DOT rules.

Main Basic passengers cannot change their flights for voluntary reasons on most domestic routes, but they can cancel and receive an eCredit.

## Canceling a Refundable Delta Ticket

If you purchased a refundable fare (identifiable in your booking details), canceling is simple and results in a full cash refund to your original payment method. There are no fees or penalties. Follow the same online steps above and select the refund option when prompted.

Refundable tickets can be canceled at any time before departure, including on the day of travel.

## Canceling a Delta SkyMiles Award Ticket

Award ticket cancellations work similarly to cash ticket cancellations in terms of process, but the refund takes a different form.

- **Miles are redeposited** to your SkyMiles account typically within 24 to 72 hours.
- **Taxes and fees** paid in cash are refunded to the original credit card.
- **Redeposit fees** — Delta may charge a miles redeposit fee depending on your Medallion status. Diamond members pay no redeposit fee. Lower-tier members and non-elite flyers may pay a fee.

To cancel an award ticket, follow the same My Trips process on delta.com or the Fly Delta app and confirm the miles redeposit.

## Canceling Within the 24-Hour Window

If you cancel within 24 hours of booking and your flight departs at least 7 days from the booking date, Delta's risk-free cancellation policy gives you a full cash refund on any fare class, including Main Basic. This is one of the most valuable and underused passenger protections available.

This rule applies whether you booked directly through Delta or via a participating travel agency. Third-party bookings may have additional processing requirements.

## What Happens to Add-On Purchases

If you paid for extras such as seat upgrades, in-flight Wi-Fi, or checked bag fees, what happens to those payments depends on the timing and reason for your cancellation:

- **Paid seat selections** — Generally refunded if canceled before the flight.
- **Checked bag fees** — Typically refunded if the flight is canceled by Delta; may not be refunded for voluntary cancellations in all cases.
- **Upgrades to Comfort+** — Treated separately and refundable policies vary.

If you are unsure about a specific add-on, contact Delta at **++1-866-332-0756** to confirm refund eligibility.

## Tips for a Smooth Cancellation

- **Cancel as early as possible.** The sooner you cancel before departure, the more options you have and the less chance of complications.
- **Use the 24-hour window aggressively.** If there is any doubt about your trip right after booking, cancel within 24 hours for a penalty-free cash refund.
- **Save your eCredit certificate number.** Once your cancellation is confirmed, record the eCredit number for future use.
- **Check for travel waivers before canceling.** If a weather event or major disruption is affecting your route, a travel waiver may give you more flexible options than a standard cancellation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I cancel a Delta flight online?**

Log in to delta.com, go to 'My Trips,' select the flight you want to cancel, and click 'Cancel Flight.' Follow the prompts to confirm your cancellation. You will receive a confirmation email and any applicable eCredit or refund.

**Can I cancel a Delta flight within 24 hours of booking?**

Yes. Delta's 24-hour risk-free cancellation policy allows you to cancel any ticket within 24 hours of booking and receive a full cash refund, as long as the flight departs at least 7 days after the booking date.

**What happens when I cancel a non-refundable Delta ticket?**

When you cancel a non-refundable Delta ticket voluntarily, you receive a Delta eCredit equal to the value of the unused portion of your ticket. The eCredit can be used toward a future Delta flight and expires one year from the original purchase date.

**How do I cancel a Delta SkyMiles award ticket?**

Log in to delta.com, go to 'My Trips,' and select your award booking. Choose 'Cancel Flight.' Miles are redeposited to your SkyMiles account. Award redeposit fees may apply depending on your Medallion status.
