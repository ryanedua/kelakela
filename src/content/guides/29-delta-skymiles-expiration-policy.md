# Delta SkyMiles Expiration: Does It Expire & How to Keep Active

One of the most common concerns for infrequent travelers with Delta SkyMiles is whether those hard-earned miles will vanish over time. The good news is that Delta has one of the more generous expiration policies among major U.S. carriers. Here is what the current rules say and what you need to do to protect your balance.

## Do Delta SkyMiles Expire?

The straightforward answer is: **Delta SkyMiles do not expire, provided your account remains active.**

Unlike some other airline loyalty programs that expire miles after a set period regardless of activity, Delta ties the lifetime of your miles to account activity. As long as you make at least one qualifying earn or redeem transaction within a 24-month window, your miles remain intact.

This policy has been in place for some time and reflects Delta's commitment to keeping loyalty miles valuable for both frequent and infrequent flyers.

## What Counts as Account Activity

Keeping your SkyMiles account "active" does not require flying. A wide range of activities qualify:

### Earning Miles

Any transaction that earns SkyMiles counts as account activity:

- **Flying Delta** — Every Delta flight earns miles based on the distance flown and your fare class.
- **Delta co-branded American Express card purchases** — Using a Delta SkyMiles Amex card for everyday purchases earns miles and keeps your account active.
- **SkyMiles Dining** — Dining at participating restaurants through the SkyMiles Dining program earns miles.
- **SkyMiles Shopping** — Purchasing from partners via the SkyMiles Shopping portal earns miles.
- **Hotel and car rental partners** — Booking stays or rentals with SkyMiles hotel and car partners earns miles.
- **Non-airline partner activities** — Other SkyMiles ecosystem partners may offer earning opportunities.

### Redeeming Miles

Any redemption transaction also counts as activity:

- Booking an award flight
- Using miles for upgrades
- Redeeming miles through partner programs
- Any other eligible miles redemption

## How Often Do You Need to Be Active?

While Delta's program terms describe the miles as not expiring for active accounts, maintaining account activity at least **once every 24 months** is a practical guideline to ensure continuity. Even a small transaction — a single coffee purchase with a Delta Amex card, for example — resets the activity clock.

## How to Check Your Delta SkyMiles Balance

### Online at Delta.com

1. Go to delta.com and log in to your SkyMiles account.
2. Your miles balance is displayed prominently on the account dashboard.
3. Click into your account activity to see a history of recent earnings and redemptions.

### Fly Delta App

Open the app and navigate to your SkyMiles summary. The balance and recent activity are visible without navigating to a separate section.

### By Phone

Call Delta at **++1-866-332-0756** and a representative can confirm your current balance and recent account activity.

## What Happens If Your Account Becomes Inactive

While Delta's published policy centers on account activity rather than hard expiration dates, extremely long periods of inactivity could put an account at risk of closure under Delta's general program terms. The SkyMiles program terms reserve the right to close accounts and forfeit miles for extended inactivity or violations of program terms.

In practice, Delta has generally been lenient about inactive accounts, but the safest approach is to maintain at least minimal account activity.

## Low-Effort Ways to Keep Your Account Active

If you are not flying frequently, here are easy ways to maintain account activity without significant effort or spending:

- **Link a Delta co-branded Amex card** to everyday spending. Even a single small purchase per month keeps your account active.
- **Shop the SkyMiles Shopping portal** when making normal online purchases. Many major retailers participate.
- **Dine at SkyMiles Dining partners.** Register your credit card with SkyMiles Dining at delta.com; you earn miles automatically when you dine at registered restaurants.
- **Book a hotel or car rental through Delta's partners.** Even on a non-Delta trip, using a partner can earn a few miles and reset your activity.

## Can You Reinstate Expired or Lost Miles?

If an account has been closed due to inactivity and the miles forfeited, reinstatement is generally not available as a standard service. However, if you believe your miles were lost due to an error or unusual circumstance, contacting Delta at **++1-866-332-0756** to explain the situation is worth trying.

For active accounts where miles have been reduced due to account sharing violations, fraud, or other program terms violations, a separate review process applies.

## SkyMiles Activity for Non-Flyers

Even if you rarely or never fly Delta, maintaining a SkyMiles account with low-level activity makes sense. The miles accumulated through credit card spending and partner purchases can build to a meaningful balance over time, particularly with a Delta co-branded Amex card that earns miles on everyday purchases.

When you are ready to use those miles, they will be waiting — as long as you have kept the account active.

## Contact Delta Customer Support

For questions about your SkyMiles balance, account activity, or how to keep your miles from being at risk, contact Delta Air Lines at **++1-866-332-0756** or visit the SkyMiles section at delta.com.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do Delta SkyMiles expire?**

Delta SkyMiles do not expire as long as your SkyMiles account remains active. Account activity includes any qualifying earn or redeem transaction, such as a flight, credit card purchase, or partner activity. Accounts with no activity may eventually be subject to closure policies.

**How do I keep my Delta SkyMiles from expiring?**

Maintain account activity by earning or redeeming SkyMiles at least once every 24 months. Actions that count include flying Delta, making a purchase with a Delta co-branded credit card, using a SkyMiles shopping or dining partner, or redeeming miles for any eligible reward.

**How do I check my Delta SkyMiles balance?**

Log in to your SkyMiles account at delta.com or the Fly Delta app. Your current miles balance and a summary of recent activity appear on your account dashboard. You can also call Delta at ++1-866-332-0756 for balance information.

**What happens to my SkyMiles if my Delta account is closed?**

If Delta closes an account due to extended inactivity or violation of program terms, the SkyMiles balance in that account may be forfeited. Keeping your account active with any qualifying transaction prevents closure due to inactivity.
