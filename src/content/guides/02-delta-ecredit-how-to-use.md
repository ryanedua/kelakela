# Delta eCredit: How to Use, Check Balance & Expiration Rules

If you have canceled a Delta flight or had a trip disrupted, you may have received a Delta eCredit — sometimes called a travel certificate. These credits represent real monetary value that can be applied toward future Delta tickets, but using them is not always immediately obvious. This guide explains exactly how to redeem a Delta eCredit, how to check your balance, and what you need to know about expiration rules so your travel funds do not go to waste.

## What Is a Delta eCredit?

A Delta eCredit is a digital travel voucher issued when you cancel a non-refundable ticket, receive compensation for a disrupted flight, or voluntarily change a booking. Instead of receiving a cash refund, the value of your original ticket is held as an eCredit that can be applied to a future Delta flight.

eCredits are sometimes issued automatically and sometimes require you to request them. They are linked to the passenger's SkyMiles account or to the original ticket number, depending on how the credit was generated.

## How to Use a Delta eCredit

Redeeming a Delta eCredit is a straightforward process when you know where to look during checkout.

### Step 1: Log In to Your SkyMiles Account

Navigate to delta.com or open the Fly Delta app. Log in to your SkyMiles account before or during the booking process. While some eCredits can be used without logging in by entering the certificate number manually, having your account linked makes the process smoother and ensures all available credits are visible.

### Step 2: Search and Select Your Flight

Search for your desired flight as you normally would. Choose your fare class and proceed through the booking flow until you reach the payment screen.

### Step 3: Apply Your eCredit at Checkout

On the payment page, look for the option labeled **"Certificate, eCredit, or Gift Card."** Select this option and enter your eCredit certificate number. The system will display the available balance and apply it toward your total.

If the eCredit covers the full cost of the ticket, your booking is complete with no additional payment required. If the ticket costs more than the eCredit balance, you can pay the remaining amount with a credit card or another payment method.

### Step 4: Confirm Your Booking

Review the itinerary and payment details, then confirm your booking. You will receive a confirmation email showing the eCredit was applied.

## How to Check Your Delta eCredit Balance

### Online via Delta.com

1. Go to delta.com and log in to your SkyMiles account.
2. Navigate to **My Trips** or look for **My Wallet** in your account dashboard.
3. Any eCredits linked to your account will appear with their remaining balance and expiration date.

### Using the Check eCredit Tool

Delta's website includes a dedicated eCredit lookup tool. You can enter your eCredit certificate number directly to view its value and expiration date without logging in. Look for this tool under the "Travel Credit" section of delta.com.

### By Phone

If you are unable to locate your eCredit balance online, call Delta customer support at **++1-866-332-0756**. A representative can look up your eCredit by your ticket number or SkyMiles account and provide your current balance.

## Delta eCredit Expiration Rules

Understanding expiration timelines is critical to ensuring you do not lose the value of your eCredit.

### Standard Expiration Policy

Most Delta eCredits expire **one year from the date the original ticket was purchased**, not from the date the credit was issued. This is an important distinction. If you purchased a ticket in January and canceled it in March, your eCredit may expire the following January, not the following March.

The expiration date is visible on the eCredit certificate itself and in your Delta wallet online. Always check this date before assuming you have more time.

### What Happens When an eCredit Expires

Once a Delta eCredit reaches its expiration date, the credit is typically forfeited. Delta does not automatically extend eCredits, though there have been exceptions during extraordinary circumstances such as pandemic-era travel disruptions.

If your eCredit has recently expired, it may be worth contacting Delta at **++1-866-332-0756** to inquire whether any extension or reissuance options are available. Outcomes vary by situation, but customer service representatives can occasionally assist in edge cases.

### Keeping Your eCredit Active

There is no action required to prevent an eCredit from expiring — you simply need to use it before the stated date. Setting a calendar reminder well in advance of the expiration date is a practical way to ensure you book travel in time.

## Using Multiple eCredits on One Booking

Delta allows you to apply multiple eCredits to a single booking. At the payment step, you can enter each certificate number sequentially. This is useful if you have received several small credits over time from fare adjustments, compensation vouchers, or partial refunds.

## Can You Use a Delta eCredit for Fees and Extras?

Delta eCredits are primarily intended for ticket purchases. They may not be applicable to ancillary fees such as checked bag fees, seat upgrades, or in-flight purchases. If you are unsure whether your eCredit can be applied to a specific charge, check the terms printed on your certificate or call Delta at **++1-866-332-0756** before booking.

## Tips for Getting the Most from Your eCredit

- **Book before the expiration date, not just travel before it.** The credit must be applied to a booking made before expiration, not necessarily for travel before that date.
- **Keep your certificate number in a safe place.** eCredits are identified by their certificate number. If you lose it, Delta can usually look it up via your SkyMiles account, but having the number handy speeds things up.
- **Log in before searching for flights.** When you are logged in, available eCredits may appear automatically at checkout, reducing the chance of forgetting to apply them.
- **Compare fares across dates.** Your eCredit has a fixed value, so finding a lower-priced flight lets you stretch it further or cover the entire ticket.

## Contact Delta Customer Support

For personalized help with your Delta eCredit, including balance inquiries, expired credits, or assistance applying a credit to a booking, contact Delta Air Lines at **++1-866-332-0756** or visit delta.com.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I use a Delta eCredit when booking a flight?**

Log in to your Delta SkyMiles account on delta.com or the Fly Delta app, search for a flight, and proceed to the payment step. Select 'Certificate, eCredit, or Gift Card' as your payment method, enter the eCredit certificate number, and apply it to your booking.

**How do I check my Delta eCredit balance?**

Visit delta.com and log in to your SkyMiles account. Go to 'My Wallet' or use the Check eCredit tool on the Delta website. You can also call Delta at ++1-866-332-0756 for assistance checking your eCredit balance.

**When does a Delta eCredit expire?**

Most Delta eCredits expire one year from the date the original ticket was purchased. The expiration date is printed on the eCredit certificate and is also visible in your Delta wallet online.

**Can I use a Delta eCredit for someone else's ticket?**

Delta eCredits are generally non-transferable and must be used by the passenger named on the original ticket. In most cases, you cannot apply your eCredit to a ticket purchased for a different traveler.
