# Delta eCredit vs Travel Credit: What's the Difference?

Delta issues multiple types of credits to passengers in different situations, and the terminology can be confusing. You may see references to "eCredits," "travel credits," "compensation credits," or "travel certificates" — and each carries slightly different terms. This guide clarifies the distinctions so you know exactly what you have and how to use it.

## What Is a Delta eCredit?

A Delta eCredit is the standard form of credit issued when you cancel a non-refundable ticket voluntarily. It represents the unused value of your original ticket and is the primary way Delta returns value to passengers who cannot travel as planned.

**Key characteristics of a Delta eCredit:**

- Equal to the unused ticket value (full or partial, depending on whether any travel occurred)
- Tied to the named passenger on the original ticket — non-transferable
- Expires one year from the **original ticket purchase date**
- Applied at checkout on delta.com or the Fly Delta app
- Can be used for any future Delta flight booking by the same passenger

eCredits are the default outcome when a non-refundable ticket is canceled before the flight departs. They are a standard transactional tool rather than a compensation or goodwill measure.

## What Is a Delta Travel Credit?

The term "travel credit" is used more broadly and can refer to several types of credits that are not tied to a canceled ticket. In practice, Delta travel credits often arise from:

- **Goodwill gestures** after a significantly disrupted flight (long delay, lost baggage, service failure)
- **Compensation offers** for inconvenience during controllable disruptions
- **Proactive customer service credits** deposited via the Fly Delta app after disruptions
- **Promotional credits** issued as part of a marketing offer

Unlike eCredits that have a clear monetary value equal to a canceled ticket, travel credits may be issued in specific amounts (such as "$150 credit" or "5,000 SkyMiles") and may have different expiration terms.

## Key Differences: eCredit vs Travel Credit

| Feature | eCredit | Travel Credit / Voucher |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Canceled ticket | Compensation, goodwill, promotion |
| Value | Equals unused ticket amount | Set by Delta based on situation |
| Named passenger | Yes | Varies |
| Expiration | 1 year from original purchase | Varies by credit type |
| Redemption | delta.com / Fly Delta app | delta.com / Fly Delta app |
| Transferable | No | Usually no |

## How to Identify What Type of Credit You Have

When you receive a Delta credit, look at the certificate or email to identify the type:

- If the credit amount exactly matches the value of a ticket you canceled, it is an eCredit.
- If the credit was issued following a delay, a complaint, or proactively by Delta without you canceling anything, it is likely a travel or compensation credit.
- The certificate will typically include a certificate number, the value, and an expiration date.

If you are unsure, log in to delta.com and check **My Wallet** — active eCredits and travel credits should appear in your account dashboard.

## How to Redeem a Delta eCredit or Travel Credit

The redemption process is the same for both:

1. Log in to your SkyMiles account at delta.com.
2. Search for and select your desired flight.
3. Proceed to the payment step.
4. Choose **"Certificate, eCredit, or Gift Card"** as your payment method.
5. Enter the certificate number from your eCredit or travel credit.
6. The credit is applied. Pay any remaining balance with another payment method.

If the credit does not appear automatically when you log in, entering the certificate number manually at checkout should work.

## Using Multiple Credits on One Booking

Delta allows you to apply multiple credits — whether eCredits or travel credits — to a single booking. Enter each certificate number separately at the payment step. This is useful if you have accumulated multiple smaller credits over time and want to consolidate their value toward a single ticket purchase.

## What If Your Credit Does Not Work at Checkout?

If a credit does not apply successfully at checkout:

- Verify the certificate number is entered exactly as shown (no extra spaces or characters)
- Confirm the credit has not expired by checking the date in your wallet or on the certificate
- Ensure the credit is in the name of the passenger being booked
- Call Delta at **++1-866-332-0756** for assistance if the issue persists

## SkyMiles Credits Are Different

A SkyMiles compensation credit — where Delta deposits bonus miles into your account after a disruption — is separate from both eCredits and travel credits. These miles are added directly to your SkyMiles balance and are redeemed through the standard "Use Miles" process at checkout, not through the eCredit/voucher flow.

## Tips for Managing Delta Credits

- **Check expiration dates for all credits regularly.** Different credits expire at different times, and losing track is the most common way travel value is forfeited.
- **Use the oldest credit first.** When you have multiple credits, apply the one closest to expiration before spending the one with more time remaining.
- **Contact Delta if you received a compensation credit and are unsure of its terms.** The specific terms of goodwill credits can vary and are worth confirming before you plan travel around them.

## Contact Delta Customer Support

For questions about a specific eCredit or travel credit, including how to apply it, when it expires, or whether it can be used on a specific fare type, contact Delta Air Lines at **++1-866-332-0756** or visit the My Wallet section at delta.com.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between a Delta eCredit and a travel credit?**

A Delta eCredit comes from a canceled ticket and equals the unused ticket value. Travel credits are typically issued as compensation or goodwill gestures — such as after a significant delay — and may have different validity periods and terms than standard eCredits.

**How do I use a Delta travel credit or eCredit?**

Both are applied at checkout on delta.com or the Fly Delta app. At the payment step, select 'Certificate, eCredit, or Gift Card' and enter the certificate number. The credit is applied to the total booking cost.

**Do Delta travel credits expire?**

Most Delta eCredits expire one year from the original ticket purchase date. Goodwill travel credits or compensation vouchers may have different expiration terms. Always check the specific terms on the certificate you received or in your Delta wallet.

**Can Delta travel credits be transferred to another person?**

Delta eCredits are non-transferable and name-specific. Some goodwill or compensation travel credits may also be non-transferable, though terms vary by the type of credit issued. Check the specific certificate or contact Delta at ++1-866-332-0756 to confirm.
