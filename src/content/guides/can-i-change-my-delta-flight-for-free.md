# Can You Change a Delta Flight for Free in 2026?

For most Delta passengers, the answer is yes. Delta permanently eliminated change fees for Main Cabin and above tickets originating in the US, Canada, and Caribbean in 2020, and that policy remains fully in effect in 2026. If you booked a Main Classic or Main Extra ticket, you can change to a different flight, date, or routing at any time before departure without paying any standalone change fee. The only cost you may face is a fare difference if the new flight is priced higher than the original. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to change your flight by phone or for help if the online change flow is not working.

## Which Tickets Can Be Changed for Free

**Main Classic**: Free to change at any time before departure. No change fee. If the new flight costs more, you pay the fare difference. If it costs less, the difference is returned as an eCredit.

**Main Extra**: Free to change at any time before departure. No change fee. If the new flight costs less, the difference is returned as a cash refund since Extra is fully refundable.

**Comfort+, First Class Classic/Extra, Delta Premium Select Classic/Extra, Delta One Classic/Extra**: No change fee. Same fare difference rules apply as Main Classic and Extra.

**Award tickets (non-Basic, US/Canada-originating)**: No change fee. Any mileage difference applies if the new flight costs more in miles.

## Which Tickets Cannot Be Changed for Free

**Main Basic (Basic Economy)**: Cannot be changed after the 24-hour risk-free window. No standard change option exists. Cancel for an eCredit minus the cancellation fee, then rebook separately.

**Basic experience fares in premium cabins**: Delta First Basic, Delta Premium Select Basic, Comfort Basic, Basic Business — same restrictions as Main Basic. Changes not permitted after 24 hours.

**Same-day confirmed changes**: A $75 fee applies for non-Medallion passengers. This is a same-day-specific rule and not a standard change fee.

## How to Change Your Delta Flight for Free Online

**Step 1**: Log in to delta.com and go to My Trips.

**Step 2**: Select your booking and click Change Flight.

**Step 3**: Search for the new flight you want on your preferred date.

**Step 4**: The system displays the fare difference. If the new flight costs the same as your original ticket, no payment is required and the change is free.

**Step 5**: If the new flight costs more, pay the fare difference to confirm the change.

**Step 6**: Receive a new confirmation email with your updated itinerary.

## What If the New Flight Costs Less?

Changing to a cheaper flight is always allowed and never penalized. The fare difference between your original ticket and the new lower-priced flight becomes a credit.

For Main Classic tickets, this credit is issued as an eCredit valid for one year from the original ticket purchase date.

For Main Extra tickets, this credit is refunded to your original payment method since Extra is fully refundable.

The credit or refund is generated automatically after the change is confirmed. No separate request is needed.

## The 24-Hour Risk-Free Window

Even Main Basic tickets can be changed or cancelled for a full refund within 24 hours of purchase, regardless of fare type. This applies to all tickets purchased directly from Delta on delta.com, the Fly Delta app, or by phone.

The 24-hour window starts the moment your purchase is confirmed and ends exactly 24 hours later. Use this window if you booked a Basic ticket and then realized you wanted more flexibility — cancel and rebook in Classic within the window and receive a full refund on the Basic ticket.

## Active Weather Waivers

During active Delta weather waivers, the restrictions on Main Basic and other Basic fare types are temporarily lifted. All passengers in the covered markets and travel date ranges can rebook without paying the cancellation fee that normally applies to Basic tickets.

Check delta.com/travelinformation for active waivers or call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to confirm whether a waiver applies to your flight.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change my Delta flight for free?**
Yes for Main Classic and Extra. No change fee — only fare difference if the new flight costs more. Basic Economy cannot be changed after 24 hours.

**Does Delta charge a fee to change a flight in 2026?**
No standalone change fee for Classic and Extra. Basic cannot be changed. Same-day confirmed costs $75 for non-Medallion passengers.

**What if my new flight is cheaper?**
The difference is returned as an eCredit (Classic) or cash refund (Extra).

**Can I change a Basic Economy Delta flight for free?**
No after the 24-hour window. Cancel for an eCredit minus the fee and rebook separately.

**How do I change my Delta flight online for free?**
Log in to My Trips on delta.com, select your flight, click Change Flight, and pick a new departure.


**What if my new flight is cheaper than my original ticket?**
If you change to a cheaper flight, the difference is issued as an eCredit for Classic tickets. For Extra tickets, the difference is refunded as cash since Extra is fully refundable. The remaining credit or refund uses the same expiration terms as the original ticket.

**How do I change my Delta flight for free online?**
Log in to My Trips on delta.com, select your booking, and click Change Flight. Choose your new flight and confirm. If the new flight costs the same or less, no payment is required. If it costs more, pay only the fare difference.
