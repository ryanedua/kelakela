# How to Check Delta eCredit Balance Online in 2026: Step-by-Step

Checking your Delta eCredit balance takes under a minute if you know where to look. The location differs depending on whether you have a SkyMiles account linked to your booking or whether you purchased as a guest. Both paths work — they just require different information to get started. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** if the credit is not appearing where it should be or if you cannot locate it using any of the methods below.

## Method 1: My Wallet on delta.com (SkyMiles Members)

This is the fastest method if you have a SkyMiles account and the credit is linked to it.

**Step 1**: Go to delta.com and log in with your SkyMiles number and password.

**Step 2**: Click your name or profile icon in the upper right corner.

**Step 3**: Select My Profile from the dropdown menu.

**Step 4**: In the left sidebar under My Profile, click Certificates, eCredits & Vouchers. This section shows all active credits associated with your account, including values and expiration dates.

Alternatively, you may see a shortcut path: click your profile, look for My Wallet, then Credits and Certificates. Delta has restructured the profile navigation periodically — if Certificates and eCredits does not appear in one location, check My Wallet or Wallet in your profile section.

## Method 2: My Wallet on the Fly Delta App (SkyMiles Members)

**Step 1**: Open the Fly Delta app and log in to your SkyMiles account.

**Step 2**: Tap Account at the bottom of the screen.

**Step 3**: Tap My Wallet.

**Step 4**: Tap Certificates and eCredits. Your active credits appear with balances and expiration dates.

## Method 3: delta.com/redeem (Guest Bookers and Non-Linked Credits)

If you purchased as a guest without a SkyMiles account, or if a credit is not appearing in your SkyMiles wallet, use this path.

**Step 1**: Go to delta.com/redeem.

**Step 2**: Choose how you want to look up the credit from the three options provided:

**Option A — Ticket number**: Enter the 13-digit ticket number that starts with 006. Find this in your original booking confirmation email. Open the email, scroll to the Flight Receipt section, and look for the first item listed — that is your ticket number.

**Option B — Credit card**: Enter the card number used to purchase the original ticket. This retrieves any eCredits associated with that card.

**Option C — SkyMiles number**: Enter your SkyMiles account number to retrieve credits linked to that account.

**Step 3**: After entering your lookup information, enter the first and last name of the original ticket holder and click Look Up.

**Step 4**: Your eCredit or credits appear with the current balance and expiration date.

## What the Balance Information Shows

For each eCredit in your wallet or at delta.com/redeem, the information displayed includes:

The current dollar value remaining on the credit.
The original ticket number or booking reference.
The expiration date — this is the date by which you must complete a new booking (not travel) to use the credit.
The passenger name the credit is tied to.

## Why the Credit Might Not Be Appearing

**Credit not linked to your SkyMiles account**: If you purchased using a guest checkout and later created or linked a SkyMiles account, the credit may not have transferred automatically. Use delta.com/redeem to find it by ticket number.

**Credit has expired**: Expired credits do not appear in the active credits section. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** to check whether a recently expired credit can be located and whether any recovery options exist.

**Credit is in a different passenger's name**: If you are looking for a credit from a ticket you cancelled for a travel companion, the credit is in their name, not yours. Use delta.com/redeem with their ticket number.

**Credit not yet issued**: eCredits typically appear in the system within minutes of a cancellation being processed but may occasionally take a few hours. If you just cancelled and do not see the credit, wait 1 to 2 hours before calling.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I check my Delta eCredit balance online?**
Log in to delta.com, go to My Wallet or Certificates, eCredits & Vouchers in your profile. Or use delta.com/redeem for guest bookings.

**What if I don't have a SkyMiles account?**
Use delta.com/redeem with your 13-digit ticket number (starting with 006) from your booking confirmation email.

**Why is my Delta eCredit not showing in my wallet?**
May not be linked to your account, may be expired, or may be in a different passenger's name. Call **+1-(855)-321-3528)** with your ticket number.

**Can I check my Delta eCredit balance without logging in?**
Yes via delta.com/redeem using your ticket number or original credit card number.

**What information do I need to look up a Delta eCredit?**
SkyMiles account login, OR your 13-digit ticket number starting with 006, OR the original purchasing credit card number.


**What if I don't have a SkyMiles account — how do I find my eCredit?**
Go to delta.com/redeem and search using one of three methods: your 13-digit ticket number (starts with 006, found in your original booking confirmation email under Flight Receipt), the credit card used to purchase the original ticket, or your SkyMiles account number if you have created one since.
