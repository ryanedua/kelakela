# Delta Same-Day Flight Change Policy 2026: Everything You Need to Know

Delta's same-day flight change policy gives you two options on the day of travel when you want to move to an earlier or later departure. Same-Day Confirmed guarantees a seat for $75. Same-Day Standby is free but puts you on a waitlist. Both avoid fare differences when you stay in the same cabin. Neither is available for Basic Economy. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756** to request either option or use the Fly Delta app.

## Same-Day Confirmed: Guaranteed Seat for $75

Same-Day Confirmed locks in a seat on a specific alternate Delta flight on the same travel day. You receive a new boarding pass immediately and your original booking is replaced.

**Cost**: $75 for general SkyMiles members and Silver Medallion. Waived entirely for Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion members.

**No fare difference**: As long as the new flight is in the same cabin class, no price gap is charged — only the $75 service fee.

## Same-Day Standby: Free but No Guarantee

Same-Day Standby places you on the waitlist for an earlier Delta flight at no cost. Your original booking stays active. If a seat opens before that flight departs, you board. If not, you board your original flight.

**Cost**: Free for all eligible passengers. No fee regardless of outcome.

**No fare difference**: Same as Confirmed — no price gap when staying in the same cabin.

## Eligibility

Both options are available for Main Classic, Extra, Comfort+, First Class, and Delta One on domestic US routes. Main Basic (Basic Economy) is excluded from both.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Delta's same-day flight change policy?**
Delta offers two same-day change options for eligible tickets on domestic routes. Same-Day Confirmed costs $75 (waived for Gold/Platinum/Diamond Medallion) and guarantees a seat. Same-Day Standby is free but places you on a waitlist. No fare difference for either when staying in the same cabin. Basic Economy is excluded.

**Is Same-Day Standby free on Delta?**
Yes. Same-Day Standby is free for all eligible passengers regardless of Medallion status. No fee is charged even if you successfully clear standby and board the earlier flight.

**Does Delta same-day change charge a fare difference?**
No. Neither Same-Day Confirmed nor Same-Day Standby charges a fare difference when the new flight is in the same cabin as the original booking. This is the key financial advantage over making a standard advance change.

**Who is eligible for same-day changes on Delta?**
Main Classic, Extra, Comfort+, First Class, and Delta One ticket holders on domestic US routes are eligible. Main Basic (Basic Economy) is excluded from both Same-Day Confirmed and Same-Day Standby.

**How do I request a same-day change on Delta?**
Open the Fly Delta app, go to My Trips, select your flight, and tap Same-Day Change. Choose Confirmed or Standby. You can also request at the airport counter or by calling **+1-(866)-332-0756**.
