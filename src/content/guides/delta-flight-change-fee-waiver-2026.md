# Delta Flight Change Fee Waiver 2026: When Is the Fee Waived?

In October 2020, Delta permanently eliminated change fees for most economy and premium cabin tickets originating in the US and Canada. In 2026, this remains the policy. For the vast majority of Delta passengers, changing a flight costs nothing in standalone fees — you pay only a fare difference if the new flight is more expensive. There are still specific situations where fees apply, and specific situations where even Basic Economy restrictions are lifted. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to confirm the rules for your specific ticket before initiating any change.

## The Permanent Elimination of Change Fees

Delta eliminated change fees for Main Cabin and above tickets originating from the United States, Canada, and Caribbean in 2020, and the policy has remained unchanged through 2026. This covers Main Classic, Main Extra, Comfort+, Delta First, Delta Premium Select, and Delta One tickets on routes originating in those markets.

When you change a Classic or Extra ticket, the only cost is the fare difference between your original ticket and the new flight. If the new flight costs the same, you pay nothing. If it costs more, you pay the difference. If it costs less, the difference is credited back as an eCredit (Classic) or a cash refund (Extra).

No penalty, no administrative fee, no reissue charge. The change fee that used to be $200 to $500 per ticket simply no longer exists for qualifying fare types.

## When Change Fees Still Apply

**Main Basic (Basic Economy)**: There is no change fee on Basic Economy because changes are not permitted at all after the 24-hour risk-free window. The option to change simply does not exist. The only modification available is cancellation for an eCredit minus the applicable cancellation fee. A waiver code can unlock change flexibility during active weather waivers.

**Same-day confirmed changes**: A $75 fee applies to Same-Day Confirmed changes for general SkyMiles members, Silver Medallion members, and non-members. This fee is waived for Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion members.

**Award tickets originating outside the US and Canada**: Non-Medallion members pay a $150 redeposit fee when cancelling award tickets on routes originating outside North America. Award changes may also carry fees on some international-origin routes.

**Partner airline changes**: Award tickets booked on SkyTeam partner airlines through Delta SkyMiles may carry change or reissue fees set by the individual partner carrier. Always confirm partner-specific terms before changing.

## When Active Waivers Lift All Restrictions

During an active Delta travel waiver — issued for weather events, international security situations, or major operational disruptions — the standard change fee rules are suspended for passengers in covered markets.

Under an active waiver, Main Basic passengers can rebook without paying the cancellation fee. Classic and Extra passengers can rebook on any alternative within the waiver window. All fare classes effectively become changeable at no cost during the waiver period.

Check delta.com/travelinformation for active waivers or call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to confirm whether an active waiver applies to your specific itinerary.

## How the Fare Difference Works

The fare difference is the only cost when changing a qualifying Delta ticket in 2026. Understanding how it is calculated helps you plan changes effectively.

The fare difference compares the price of your original ticket to the current price of the new flight you want. If you paid $250 for a Main Classic ticket and the new flight you want is currently priced at $320, you pay a $70 fare difference.

The fare difference can be zero if the new flight is the same price or cheaper. If it is cheaper, the difference ($250 - $200 = $50 in this example) is returned as an eCredit for Classic or cash for Extra.

One important nuance: the comparison is against the current market price of the new flight at the time you make the change, not a locked-in original price. This means changing to a flight that has increased in price since you first booked requires paying the full difference based on today's price.

## Medallion Status Benefits for Change Fees

Medallion status affects the same-day confirmed change fee specifically.

Diamond, Platinum, and Gold Medallion members have the $75 same-day confirmed change fee waived entirely. They can move to an earlier or later flight on the same day at no cost as long as a seat in their original fare class is available.

Silver Medallion members pay the same $75 as general members for same-day confirmed changes.

All Medallion levels receive free Same-Day Standby regardless of status.

For standard advance changes on Classic and Extra tickets, Medallion status provides no additional benefit because the change fee is already zero for all passengers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Delta charge a change fee in 2026?**
No for Main Classic and Extra from the US, Canada, and Caribbean. You pay only fare difference. Basic Economy cannot be changed. Same-day confirmed costs $75 for non-Medallion passengers.

**When is the Delta same-day confirmed change fee waived?**
For Gold, Platinum, and Diamond Medallion members on eligible routes.

**Does Delta waive change fees during weather waivers?**
Yes. Active waivers suspend standard restrictions for all fare types including Main Basic.

**Is there a fee to change a Delta award ticket?**
No fee for US/Canada-originating non-Basic awards. $150 redeposit fee for some international-origin award cancellations.

**What is the fare difference?**
The price gap between your original ticket and the new flight's current price. No standalone change fee — only this fare difference applies.


**What is the fare difference and how is it calculated?**
The fare difference is the price gap between your original ticket and the new flight you want. If the new flight costs $50 more than what you paid, you pay $50. If the new flight costs less, the difference is issued as an eCredit for Classic tickets or a cash refund for Extra tickets.
