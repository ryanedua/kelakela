# Is Delta basic economy refundable? — 2026 Answer

Delta Main Basic (Basic Economy) is not refundable to cash after the 24-hour risk-free window. Cancel before departure for an eCredit minus the applicable cancellation fee. Full cash refund applies only within 24 hours of purchase or when Delta cancels your flight. During active weather waivers, the cancellation fee is typically waived. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 for help.
