# Delta SkyMiles Award Cancellation Fee 2026: Full Breakdown

Delta's SkyMiles award cancellation policy in 2026 is one of the most flexible among US frequent flyer programs. For the vast majority of members booking US or Canada-originating non-Basic awards, there is simply no fee — miles come back in full when you cancel before departure. Knowing the specific exceptions — Basic awards, international-origin routes, and the no-show rule — prevents you from being caught off guard. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to cancel an award ticket or to confirm which rules apply to your specific booking.

## The Core Rule: No Fee for US/Canada Non-Basic Awards

Delta's SkyMiles Program Rules state that members may cancel Award tickets for travel originating in the US or Canada without a redeposit fee, excluding Basic experience fares. This applies to all Medallion tiers and to general SkyMiles members with no elite status.

When you cancel a qualifying award ticket before departure, your miles are redeposited to your SkyMiles account within 24 to 72 hours. Cash taxes and fees paid at the time of booking are refunded to your original payment method within 7 business days.

The no-fee redeposit policy makes Delta SkyMiles awards particularly flexible to book in advance. You can lock in scarce award space and cancel later at no financial penalty if your plans change.

## Exception 1: Basic Experience Award Fares

Delta Basic experience award fares carry the same restrictions as Basic revenue tickets. A mileage cancellation charge is deducted before any miles are redeposited. The amount of the deduction depends on the specific fare rules attached to the Basic award.

If you want full cancellation flexibility on an award ticket, avoid booking Basic experience award fares. Classic and Extra award fares in any cabin offer free cancellation with full redeposit.

## Exception 2: International-Origin Routes for Non-Medallion Members

For award tickets originating outside the US and Canada, Delta's policy includes a $150 per-person redeposit fee for non-Medallion members on cancellations. This fee applies to routes where the travel begins at a non-North American airport — for example, a Tokyo to New York award or a London to Atlanta award.

Medallion members (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) have this fee waived regardless of where the award originates. If you hold any Medallion status, you can cancel an international-origin award without paying the redeposit fee.

The $150 fee for international-origin cancellations is not consistently enforced on all routes, but it is the stated policy. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to confirm whether the fee applies to your specific booking before cancelling.

## Exception 3: No-Show After Departure

If you miss your award flight without cancelling before the scheduled departure time, your miles are forfeited. No redeposit occurs for no-shows on award tickets.

Cancel before departure — even one minute before the flight departs — to preserve your miles. Cancellations made before departure always result in full redeposit (minus any applicable fee) even at the last second. No-shows result in complete mile forfeiture.

## What Happens When Delta Cancels Your Award Flight

When Delta cancels your award flight, the process is different: your miles are fully redeposited at no charge, and all cash taxes and fees are refunded. This is an automatic process in most cases.

Delta sends a notification when an award booking is affected by a cancellation. Miles typically redeposit within a few hours to 24 hours of the cancellation. If you have not received the redeposit notification within 72 hours, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** and ask an agent to manually process the redeposit.

## Partner Airline Award Cancellations

Award tickets booked through Delta SkyMiles for travel on SkyTeam partners — Air France, KLM, Korean Air, etc. — may have different cancellation rules and fees set by the individual carrier. Partner award cancellations generally require a phone call and cannot be processed online.

Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** to cancel a partner airline award. Confirm the partner's specific cancellation terms before initiating the cancellation, as fees and timelines vary.

## Redeposit Timeline and Tracking

Miles redeposit to your SkyMiles account within 24 to 72 hours of a cancelled award booking.

To check whether miles have redeposited: log in to delta.com, go to your SkyMiles account, and check the recent activity tab. The redeposit will appear as a positive mileage transaction.

Cash taxes and fees refund separately within 7 business days for credit card payments.

If either does not arrive within the expected window, call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** with your booking confirmation number and ask for a status update on the redeposit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a fee to cancel a Delta SkyMiles award ticket in 2026?**
No fee for US/Canada-originating non-Basic awards. Basic awards face a mileage deduction. Non-Medallion members pay $150 on some international-origin routes.

**How long does Delta take to redeposit miles after cancelling?**
24 to 72 hours. Cash taxes refund within 7 business days.

**Do Medallion members pay an award cancellation fee?**
No. All Medallion tiers have redeposit fees waived on all routes.

**What happens if I no-show on a Delta award ticket?**
Miles are forfeited with no redeposit. Cancel before departure to preserve miles.

**Can I get my miles back if Delta cancels my award flight?**
Yes, full redeposit at no charge. Automatic in most cases. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** if not received within 72 hours.


**How long does Delta take to redeposit miles after cancelling an award?**
Miles redeposit within 24 to 72 hours of cancellation. Cash taxes and fees refund within 7 business days. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 if miles have not redeposited after 72 hours.

**Do Medallion members pay an award cancellation fee on Delta?**
No. All Medallion tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) have award redeposit fees waived on all routes including international-origin bookings. Non-Medallion members pay $150 redeposit fee on some international-origin cancellations.

**What happens to my miles if I no-show on a Delta award ticket?**
Miles are forfeited with no redeposit. Always cancel before the scheduled departure time to preserve your miles — even one minute before departure is better than a no-show.
