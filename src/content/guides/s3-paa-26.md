# Does Delta waive change fees for weather? — 2026 Answer

Yes. When Delta issues an active travel waiver for a weather event covering your airports and travel dates, standard change fees and Basic Economy cancellation fees are suspended. All eligible passengers can rebook within the waiver window at no cost. Check delta.com/travelinformation for active waivers. Call +1-(866)-332-0756 to rebook under an active waiver.
