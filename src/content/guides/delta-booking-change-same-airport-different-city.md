# Changing a Delta Booking to a Different Airport or City in 2026

Changing your Delta booking to a completely different destination, a different departure airport, or a different city entirely is permitted on Classic and Extra fares — and it costs nothing in standalone fees. Only the fare difference matters: if the new routing costs more than the original, you pay the difference. If it costs less, you get the difference back. This flexibility is one of the most practical aspects of the no-change-fee policy Delta implemented in 2020. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for any routing change that involves a different city or airport, as complex changes often require agent assistance rather than the standard online change flow.

## What "Change to a Different City" Actually Means

When Delta says you can change the destination, it means you can modify your booking to fly to a completely different city than originally booked, using Delta's network. A New York to Los Angeles booking can be changed to New York to Seattle. A Chicago to Miami booking can be changed to Chicago to Atlanta.

The same principle applies to changing the departure airport. If you booked from JFK and now want to depart from EWR or LGA, that is a departure airport change — permitted on Classic and Extra with no change fee.

These are treated the same way as date and time changes: no standalone fee, only a fare difference.

## How Fare Differences Work for City Changes

The fare difference on a city or airport change compares the current market price of the new routing to the price you originally paid.

If you paid $320 for New York to Los Angeles and the new routing (New York to Seattle) currently costs $380, you pay a $60 fare difference.

If the new routing (New York to Atlanta) currently costs $180, you receive the $140 difference back. For Classic tickets this becomes an eCredit. For Extra tickets this becomes a cash refund.

The critical thing to understand is that the comparison is against today's price on the new route, not what you originally paid for a similar distance. A short hop may cost more than a cross-country flight if demand and timing differ.

## How to Change to a Different City or Airport

**Simple routing changes (same origin, different destination)**: Log in to My Trips on delta.com, select your booking, click Change Flight, and search for the new routing. Many destination changes can be completed through the standard online change flow.

**Complex changes (different origin and destination, or multi-city)**:  Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)**. Routing changes that involve both a new origin and a new destination, or that create a multi-city itinerary, often cannot be completed through the self-service online tool and require an agent.

**Award ticket destination or origin changes**: These must always be done by phone. Go to My Trips online for date changes on award tickets, but call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** for any routing or destination change on an award booking.

## What Cannot Be Changed

**Main Basic tickets**: After the 24-hour risk-free window, no changes of any kind are permitted. This includes city changes, airport changes, date changes, and routing changes. Cancel for an eCredit minus the cancellation fee if you need to change your destination.

**Tickets purchased through third-party platforms**: If you bought your ticket through Expedia, Traveloka, or a travel agency, changes must be made through that platform. Delta cannot modify third-party-issued tickets through My Trips.

**Changes to destinations not served by Delta**: You cannot change a Delta ticket to fly to a city not served by Delta or Delta Connection on the new routing. Delta's change policy applies to Delta-network routes only.

## When an Airport Change Qualifies as a Significant Schedule Change

If Delta itself changes your flight to depart from or arrive at a different airport than what was originally booked, this qualifies as a significant schedule change — entitling you to a full cash refund or free rebooking regardless of fare type. This is a Delta-initiated change and the rules are different from a voluntary passenger-initiated change.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change my Delta flight to a different destination?**
Yes for Classic and Extra. No change fee, only fare difference. Basic cannot be changed after 24 hours.

**Can I change my Delta flight to depart from a different airport?**
Yes for Classic and Extra. Same rules apply.

**Is there a fee to change to a different city on Delta?**
No standalone change fee. Only the fare difference between the original ticket and the new routing.

**Can I change a Delta award ticket to a different destination?**
Yes for US/Canada-originating non-Basic awards. Mileage difference applies. Call **+1-(866)-332-0756)** — cannot be done online.

**What if I want to change to a city not served by Delta?**
Cancel and rebook separately. Delta's change policy applies only to Delta-network routes.


**What if I want to change my Delta flight to a completely different airline's hub city?**
Delta's change policy applies to routing on Delta and Delta Connection flights only. If you want to travel to a city not served by Delta, you would need to cancel and rebook rather than change. The cancellation outcome depends on your fare type.
