# Which Alaska Department to Contact for Your Specific Issue

Most Alaska issues — flight changes, cancellations, eCredits, refunds, and disruption rebooking — are handled by the main customer service line at +1-(855)-321-3341. Refund status follow-up uses +1-(855)-321-3341. Baggage claims use the dedicated baggage line. Award ticket changes are also handled through the main line but state 'Mileage Plan award ticket' when prompted.

Alaska's customer service is primarily routed through one main line, but knowing how to direct your call within that system — and when to use a different number or channel — means reaching the right person without transfers. Call **+1-(855)-321-3341)** for most issues and use the routing guide below.

## Main Customer Service Line: +1-(855)-321-3341

This number handles the majority of Alaska customer service requests:

Flight changes and date modifications on all fare types. Cancellations and eCredit processing. Rebooking after flight cancellations and disruptions. Travel waiver application and rebooking. Same-day confirmed changes and standby additions. Mileage Plan award ticket changes and cancellations. Cash refund requests after qualifying Alaska-initiated events. Supervisor escalation for all issue types. General booking inquiries and confirmation lookups.

When you reach the IVR, press 0 to route to the live agent queue. If the system asks you to describe your issue, saying "cancel" or "change" routes to the human queue faster.

## Refund Status and eCredit Follow-Up: +1-(855)-321-3341

For refund status checks on pending refund requests, eCredit balance and expiration date inquiries, escalation of refund requests not resolved on the main line, and eCredit reinstatement requests after expiration.

## Baggage Issues: In Person First, Then Online

For delayed, damaged, or missing baggage: go to the Alaska baggage service office at the arrival airport immediately after your flight. Do not leave the airport without filing a report — the in-person report starts the claim process.

For follow-up after you have left the airport: use alaskaair.com baggage tracking, or call +1-(855)-321-3341 and say "baggage" when the IVR prompts you.

## Written Complaints and Compensation: alaskaair.com/customerrelations

For formal complaints after a disruption, requests for goodwill compensation not resolved by phone, and documentation of incidents for record purposes. Responses typically arrive within 5 to 7 business days.

For DOT-mandated cash refund disputes — when Alaska has not processed a refund within 7 business days of a qualifying cancellation — submit at alaskaair.com/refunds and file at transportation.gov/airconsumer.

## Self-Service: Fly Alaska App and alaskaair.com

For tasks that do not require a human agent at all: flight changes on Classic and Extra tickets, eCredit application at checkout, cancellation processing, same-day standby additions, flight status, and boarding pass retrieval. Available 24 hours with no hold time.

## Frequently asked questions

**What number should I call for Alaska flight changes and cancellations?**
Call +1-(855)-321-3341 for all flight changes, cancellations, rebooking after disruptions, and same-day changes.

**What number should I call for Alaska eCredit and refund questions?**
Call +1-(855)-321-3341 for eCredit balance checks, expiration inquiries, and refund status follow-up.

**How do I reach Alaska's baggage department?**
Go to the Alaska baggage service office at the arrival airport immediately after your flight. For follow-up, call +1-(855)-321-3341 and say 'baggage' when prompted.

**Which Alaska department handles Mileage Plan award tickets?**
The main line at +1-(855)-321-3341. Say 'Mileage Plan award' when the IVR asks for your reason.

**How do I reach Alaska customer service for a complaint or compensation request?**
Call +1-(855)-321-3341 and ask for a customer satisfaction representative. For written complaints, submit at alaskaair.com/customerrelations.

## Quick Reference

- Flight changes, cancellations, rebooking: **+1-(855)-321-3341**
- eCredit and refund follow-up: **+1-(855)-321-3341**
- Baggage: airport baggage office, then +1-(855)-321-3341
- Written complaints: alaskaair.com/customerrelations
- DOT refund disputes: alaskaair.com/refunds and transportation.gov/airconsumer
